- **C3** [anchor: §6 · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-14-an-economy-answerable-to-life-beyond-gdp · pdf-p15] **Degrowth reinterpreted as differentiated life-coherent convergence** — NOT uniform shrinkage but *"differentiated convergence: high-extraction Global North reduces ecological/material throughput while expanding life-capacity; high-deprivation Global South expands material throughput to meet universal life-necessities while protecting life-coherent thresholds."* Escapes the degrowth-vs-growth binary by substituting life-capacity-expansion as both criterion and direction.

- **C4** [anchor: §Appendix B · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-14-an-economy-answerable-to-life-beyond-gdp · pdf-p32] **Hickel-to-Life-Coherent translation table** — explicit per-concept translation from Hickel's Marxist-political-economy vocabulary (use-value vs exchange-value / capitalist law of value / ecological unequal exchange / metabolic rift / commodity frontier) to Sahely's life-coherence vocabulary (life-capacity / money-value sequencing / cross-border life-drain / life-ground rupture / claim-territorialization). Operationalizes the substrate-integration at vocabulary layer.

- **C5** [anchor: §10 · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-14-an-economy-answerable-to-life-beyond-gdp · pdf-p24] **Small Island States as diagnostic sites** — Caribbean + Pacific SIDS as *"concentrated diagnostic sites where unequal-exchange + climate-overshoot + monetary-financial dependency + cultural memory of colonial extraction converge."* Empirical anchor for the framework; Sahely's own diagnostic location.

- **C6** [anchor: §8 · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-14-an-economy-answerable-to-life-beyond-gdp · pdf-p21] **Dashboards need power (institutional embedding)** — *"an indicators dashboard without institutional power to act on its signals is ornamental. Life-coherent progress requires dashboard + governance authority + de-implementation capacity as a coupled triad."* Without all three, performative transformation is the predicted outcome.

- **C7** [anchor: §3 · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-14-an-economy-answerable-to-life-beyond-gdp · pdf-p(§3)] **GDP as symptom of a deeper inversion** — GDP is *"the accounting form of a deeper inversion"* (life justifying itself before the economy, rather than the economy serving life); the master measure conserved beyond the living relations it was meant to track.

## 4. Substrate-resonance map (descriptive)

Read against the post-Bundle-α canon (14 foundation docs / 9 primitives / **4 cross-cutting doctrines** / concepts-yaml v24 / 40 canon-decisions). Descriptive resonance observations, not admission proposals.

### 4.1 Fourfold Synthesis ↔ life-value-doctrine (ADR-0086)

C1 places McMurtry's life-value criterion as one of four foundational substrates. McMurtry life-value / universal human life necessities / life-capacity is exactly the territory Spore admitted at **ADR-0086** as `life-value-doctrine` — the **4th cross-cutting doctrine**, sibling to `reproductive-commoning`, `boundary-commoning`, and `care-commoning`. The resonance is direct: Sahely's paper supplies the same McMurtrian life-value criterion the doctrine encodes, now braided with Hickel's political-economy mechanism. Because Sahely is a single AI-co-authored corpus, this is **reinforcement-within-an-already-resolved-admission**, not independent convergence evidence; the doctrine stands as admitted, and this paper is one citation-bearing articulation of the McMurtrian substrate beneath it.

### 4.2 GDP-as-symptom ↔ golden-calf-trap + recursive-audit-method (ADR-0085)

C7's GDP-as-symptom-of-inversion (the master measure conserved beyond the living relations it was meant to track) is the **SHAPE-OF** the `golden-calf-trap` vocab admitted at **ADR-0085** — and, per the consistency map, `golden-calf-trap` is the SHAPE-OF (not equivalent-to) ADR-0048's `substitution-trap`. GDP is the master-substitution case: a proxy elevated to the thing-it-stood-for. C6's corrective triad (dashboard + governance authority + **de-implementation capacity**) parallels `recursive-audit-method` (also ADR-0085): the discipline of turning the measuring grammar back on itself and acting on what it reveals, rather than letting the proxy ossify. No canon-shift — both slugs are admitted; Sahely supplies an economics-domain instantiation.

### 4.3 Cross-border life-drain ↔ reproduction-continuity (ADR-0049) + maintenance-economics (ADR-0079)

C2 reframes Hickel's unequal exchange as net flows of embodied labour + ecological materials + life-substance from periphery to core. Structurally this is `reproduction-continuity` (ADR-0049) operating at cross-federation scale: the periphery loses reproductive/maintenance capacity to sustain core consumption. The political-economy **mechanism** Hickel adds is named in canon as `asymmetric-commitment` (ADR-0047 Layer 2 power-multi-layer-decomposition). The `maintenance-economics` foundation doc (ADR-0079) carries the provisioning/maintenance-labour layer this drains. Descriptive cross-domain isomorphism; no canon-shift.

### 4.4 Differentiated convergence ↔ Field rule-stratification (ADR-0046)

C3's differentiated convergence — one constitutional life-coherence rule admitting opposite throughput trajectories (North contracts, South expands) per starting condition — maps cleanly to `rule-in-use` under **ADR-0046** Field rule-stratification: the same constitutional rule-level hosts divergent rule-in-use trajectories across local conditions. This is the cleanest single-ADR resonance in the post; it is a framing observation, not pressure.

### 4.5 Dashboards-need-power ↔ structural-legitimacy (ADR-0042)

C6's claim that an indicators dashboard without power-to-act is ornamental restates, at the institutional layer, the canon discipline that **persistence ≠ legitimacy** (`structural-legitimacy`, ADR-0042): a measurement arrangement may persist (be published, be cited) while failing the legitimacy test that would make it act on its own signals. Spore canon already carries this distinction at the federation-governance layer; Sahely supplies a beyond-GDP-economics instantiation. No canon-shift.

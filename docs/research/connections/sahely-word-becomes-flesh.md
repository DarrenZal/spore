## 2. C-claims (verbatim from extraction record)

All quotes verbatim from the extraction record; RID `orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-25-when-the-word-becomes-flesh-organized-re`.

- **C1** [anchor: §Abstract · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-25-when-the-word-becomes-flesh-organized-re · pdf-p3] Maturanan central question: *"The paper's central question is Maturanan: What way of living does this institution conserve? Drawing on Maturana's biology of cognition and biology of love, the paper interprets institutions as conserving patterns of coexistence."*

- **C2** [anchor: §Abstract · pdf-p3] Babel/Nehemiah definitions: *"Babel names any institutional pattern that conserves control without communion; Nehemiah names shared repair in living relation. ... AI is not a living observer or moral subject, but it can become a moral environment when institutions delegate classification, visibility, prediction, decision-making, and authority to non-living systems."*

- **C3** [anchor: §Abstract · pdf-p3] Life-coherence definition: *"By life-coherence, this paper means the alignment of persons, institutions, technologies, and systems with the preservation, restoration, and expansion of life-capacity across embodied, relational, social, ecological, and spiritual dimensions."*

- **C4** [anchor: §Executive Summary · pdf-p4] Primordial corrective: *"Governance, regulation, doctrine, law, and institutional reform are necessary, but they are not primordial enough. Before governance, there is living. Before policy, there is relation. Before doctrine, there is embodied encounter. Before AI deployment, there is a way of living already being conserved."*

- **C5** [anchor: §Executive Summary · pdf-p4] The life-coherent institutional test: *"Does this institution expand or restrict the domain in which the other appears as a legitimate other?"*

- **C6** [anchor: §Executive Summary · pdf-p4] Love as relational ground, not sentimentality: *"Love, in Maturana's sense, is not sentimentality but the relational domain in which the other appears as a legitimate other in coexistence."*

- **C7** [anchor: §Executive Summary · pdf-p5] Institutional repentance as intelligence: *"Institutional repentance is therefore not weakness; it is a form of life-coherent intelligence. The same memory must now be directed toward digital colonialism, data extraction, hidden labor, and new forms of slavery."*

- **C8** [anchor: §9 Digital Civil Commons · pdf-p18] Universal destination of goods extended to digital: *"the goods universally intended for everyone must now include not only land, water, air, and natural resources, but also patents, algorithms, digital platforms, technological infrastructure, and data."*

- **C9** [anchor: §9 · pdf-p19] The commons question: *"the life-coherent technological question is: Does AI become a digital commons of participation, or a digital enclosure of life?"*

- **C10** [anchor: §10 Civilization of Love · pdf-p19] Civilization of love is operational, not sentimental: *"The civilization of love must not be reduced to moral aspiration or religious poetry. It is a way of living that must be conserved through recurrent practices, institutions, designs, policies, rituals, habits, and relationships. ... The civilization of love is not sentimental. It is operational."*

- **C11** [anchor: §10 · pdf-p19] Babel and Nehemiah internal to every institution: *"The master diagram's deepest insight is that Babel and Nehemiah are not two external options. They are two conserved ways of living that can appear inside every institution."*

- **C12** [anchor: §14 Conclusion · pdf-p23] Final criterion (canonical statement of the test): *"An institution is life-coherent when its word, code, doctrine, law, ritual, policy, economy, or system becomes flesh as embodied love, justice, mercy, mutual legitimacy, and shared repair. It becomes life-blind when living persons are made to serve abstraction, control, reputation, efficiency, profit, purity, or power."*

- **C13** [anchor: §14 · pdf-p23] No system is life-coherent by claim alone: *"no system is life-coherent simply because it claims truth, efficiency, authority, intelligence, legality, tradition, orthodoxy, or progress. Every institution must be judged by the way of living it conserves."*

## 4. Substrate-resonance map (descriptive)

Read against Spore's post-Bundle-α canon (14 foundation docs / 9 primitives / **4** cross-cutting doctrines / 2 modes / 2 properties / concepts-yaml v24). Descriptive only.

### 4.1 Life-coherence test ↔ `life-value-doctrine` (ADR-0086)

C3's definition of life-coherence as *"the preservation, restoration, and expansion of life-capacity across embodied, relational, social, ecological, and spiritual dimensions"* and the C12/C13 final criterion (*"judged by the way of living it conserves"*, not by claim) are the **institutional-test instantiation** of McMurtry's life-value onto-axiology that Spore admitted as the fourth cross-cutting doctrine `life-value-doctrine` (ADR-0086). The doctrine is a lens-over-primitives evaluating whether coordination outcomes serve life-value vs money-value; Sahely's §11 ten-institution Babel/Nehemiah matrix is exactly such a lens applied to ten institution-classes. This is reinforcement of an already-admitted doctrine from the same McMurtry-Maturana substrate that admitted it — not independent-tradition evidence, and not new pressure.

### 4.2 Digital Civil Commons ↔ `civil-commons` (ADR-0087)

C8 (universal destination of goods *"must now include ... patents, algorithms, digital platforms, technological infrastructure, and data"*) and C9 (*"Does AI become a digital commons of participation, or a digital enclosure of life?"*) are a **domain-instantiation** of the `civil-commons` derived-glossary slug (ADR-0087), which names socially-constructed life-good provisioning systems as primary economic infrastructure. The "digital civil commons" here extends the same McMurtry/Ostrom/Bollier substrate that grounded the slug admission into the digital-goods domain. The slug already exists; this post adds a domain example, not a new concept.

### 4.3 Legitimate-other / biology of love ↔ care-cluster scope-condition (ADR-0088)

C5/C6 — *"the relational domain in which the other appears as a legitimate other in coexistence"* — is Maturana's operational definition of love. ADR-0088 already scope-conditioned the care-cluster (`care-commoning` ADR-0045) to acknowledge the Maturana biology-of-love substrate alongside the original care-feminist substrate. Sahely's legitimate-other test sits squarely inside that scope-condition. The §11 matrix's recurring *"make the other more visible as a legitimate other"* question is the care-cluster relational-discipline applied as an institutional rubric. No canon-shift.

### 4.4 Persistence ≠ legitimacy / primordial corrective ↔ structural-legitimacy (ADR-0042)

C13 (*no system is life-coherent simply because it claims truth, efficiency, authority ...*) and C4 (*"Before governance, there is living. Before policy, there is relation."*) restate at civilizational scale what Spore's `structural-legitimacy` foundation doc (ADR-0042) does at federation-governance scale: operational persistence is not the same as legitimacy. C7's institutional-repentance-as-intelligence (remember-harm → confess-complicity → repair-way-of-living) further resonates with the external-validation-loop F8 (meta-canon receiving external feedback) and actor-governance F3 (actor-capture remediation / governance-response). Reinforcing across domains; no shift.

### 4.5 Babel/Nehemiah internal to every institution ↔ failure-modes F6 (and trap-shape ADR-0085)

C11's central insight — *"Babel and Nehemiah are not two external options. They are two conserved ways of living that can appear inside every institution"* — is structurally the F6 `failure-modes` discipline: failure is not an external category but an internal mode every institution can occupy. The Babel form (control-without-communion; persons reduced to abstraction/data/efficiency per C12) is readable as a `substitution-trap` (ADR-0048) / `golden-calf-trap` (ADR-0085) shape — a corrective distinction (doctrine, code, law) captured and hardened until living persons are made to serve it. Sahely's framework is the Maturanan articulation of the same trap-shape Spore already named; recurrence within his single corpus is not independent confirmation.

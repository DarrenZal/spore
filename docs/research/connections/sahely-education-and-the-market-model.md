- **C8 (Woodhouse 2001 — expunging counter-evidence)** [html-section:Woodhouse-2001-abstract] "Statements made by leading advocates of the market model exhibit a habitual tendency to expunge all evidence that does not serve the overriding goal of maximizing private money profits."

- **C9 (Woodhouse 2001 — totalizing moment to be opposed)** [html-section:Woodhouse-2001-abstract] "When taken together, these characteristics suggest that the market model of education has become a totalizing moment in human affairs, which Canadian faculty and students must oppose if the university as a public institution is to survive."

## 4. Substrate-resonance map (descriptive)

This section reads the source's abstract-level substrate against Spore's post-Bundle-α canon (14 foundation docs / 9 primitives / **4 cross-cutting doctrines** / 2 modes / 2 properties / concepts-yaml **v24** / 40 canon-decisions through ADR-0090). Layer-distinctness preserved — these are descriptive resonance observations, not admission proposals.

### 4.1 Life-knowledge vs market command ↔ `life-value-doctrine` (ADR-0086)

C3's conclusion (*"the long-term development of education and of civilization itself requires the autonomy of education from market command"*) and the *"TOWARD LIFE-KNOWLEDGE"* banner (C11) are an **early-McMurtry statement of the life-value-vs-money-value contrast** that Spore admitted at ADR-0086 as `life-value-doctrine` — the fourth cross-cutting doctrine, sibling to `reproductive-commoning` (ADR-0002), `boundary-commoning` (ADR-0003), and `care-commoning` (ADR-0045, care-cluster scope-conditioned at ADR-0088). The doctrine reads coordination outcomes through a life-value lens (do they serve life-capacity, or cumulative monetary indicators decoupled from life-coherence?); McMurtry's education-vs-market thesis is one operational instance of exactly that distinction. This 1991 paper is one early point in the multi-book multi-decade McMurtry corpus the doctrine's C1 cluster already cites. **No canon-shift needed; the doctrine is resolved and this is an upstream anchor.**

### 4.2 Market subordinating educators' self-conceptualization ↔ perception-as-power on F4 (ADR-0090)

C1's most distinctive move is that the market goal subordinates *"the language and self-conceptualization of educators themselves"* — i.e., the dominant frame does not merely redirect resources, it **changes what becomes visible and sayable**, including how educators describe their own work. That is precisely the *perception-as-power / what-becomes-visible* phenomenon Spore scope-conditioned onto `representation-authority` (F4) at **ADR-0090**: the authority to set the representational frame is a form of power, and Galtung-style structural+cultural framing controls what counts. C8's "expunge all evidence that does not serve … private money profits" is the same concern at the evidence-admission layer. **Descriptive resonance; the resolved canon-locus is F4 representation-authority + its perception-as-power scope-condition; no canon-shift.**

### 4.3 Education / university as public good ↔ `civil-commons` slug (ADR-0087)

C7 (*"shared knowledge as a public good"*; *"the university as a public institution"*) and McMurtry's defense of education as a non-market social institution point at education-as-**civil-commons** — the socially-constructed systems (public health, sanitation, education, legal frameworks) enabling universal access to life-goods that Spore's `civil-commons` derived-glossary slug (ADR-0087) names. The slug's yaml already cites the McMurtry *Cancer Stage of Capitalism* (2013) + Ostrom + Bollier-Helfrich-Linebaugh cluster; this 1991 education paper is an **earlier point in the same McMurtry corpus**, predating *Cancer Stage* by 22 years. **No canon-shift; reinforcing the resolved slug at an early academic-anchor point.**

### 4.4 Totalizing-but-illegitimate market frame ↔ `structural-legitimacy` (ADR-0042)

C9 (*"totalizing moment in human affairs, which … must oppose if the university … is to survive"*) and C7's *"enveloping"* describe an arrangement that is **operationally dominant and persistent** yet, on McMurtry/Woodhouse's argument, **not legitimate** — it should be opposed precisely because its persistence is not the same as its life-grounded warrant. Spore's `structural-legitimacy` foundation doc (ADR-0042) does the same canon-work at the federation-governance layer: operational persistence of an arrangement is distinct from its legitimacy. **Resolved-canon reinforcement at the political-philosophy layer; no new pressure.**

### 4.5 Knowledge-provisioning maintenance (light) ↔ `maintenance-economics`

C7's concern for the *"advancement and dissemination of shared knowledge as a public good"* lightly touches the provisioning/maintenance labour `maintenance-economics` (F9) carries — the reproductive infrastructure that keeps a knowledge institution alive across time (`reproductive-commoning`, ADR-0002 / `reproduction-continuity`, ADR-0049). The touch is genuine but **secondary**: this post's substrate is education-vs-market *critique* (life-value + civil-commons + representation-authority), not maintenance-economic-primary. Recorded descriptively so the cross-reference is legible; not load-bearing for this note.

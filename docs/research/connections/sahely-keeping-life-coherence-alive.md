### 4.1 The structural identity: Golden Calf Trap ↔ substitution-trap (ADR-0048)

ADR-0048 (Power Expressive + Constructed Modes — Johar Three-Form Integration, 2026-04-22) admitted `substitution-trap` as a canon-legible failure mode complementing ADR-0005 (decentralization-myth bundle). The original Johar formulation from "Power Cannot Be Allocated" (2026), cited in ADR-0048 §Context and §Consequences, is: *"One can assign authority without generating comprehension. One can define responsibility without producing commitment."* ADR-0048 §Consequences names this as the failure mode *"where decentralization-theater fails by appearing to distribute power while concentrating it, the substitution-trap fails by assigning structural capacity (authority/responsibility) without generating the grammar-dynamics (comprehension/commitment) that would animate it."*

Sahely's Golden Calf Trap (C-6 + C-13) names *"the transformation of life-coherence itself into slogan, doctrine, institution, identity, orthodoxy, or moral weapon"* / *"the idolization of the symbol in place of the living reality it was meant to serve."* The two formulations articulate the same operational concern at the same canon-shape layer: a coherence-grammar's *use* — not its *content* — is the operational site of its life-coherence-or-incoherence. The symbol substitutes for the living reality; the allocational form substitutes for the animating dynamic; the doctrine substitutes for the discipline of practice that the doctrine was introduced to scaffold.

Per `feedback_surface_vocabulary_vs_operational_concern.md`, surface vocabulary alone is necessary but not sufficient for substrate-relevance — operational-concern match is the gating criterion. The operational-concern match here is **strong and explicit**: both Johar and Sahely identify *corrective-distinction-becomes-the-domination-it-was-introduced-to-correct* as the failure shape. Sahely's "Trojan Horse Trap" (C-13: *"capture hidden inside gifts, reforms, or corrective language"*) names exactly this operational concern again from a second angle within the same paper — the corrective is the carrier of the capture.

### 4.3 The recursive life-coherence audit as methodological extension

C-7 + C-14 articulate a **recursive audit methodology** — 11 reflexive questions (*What life-capacity does this reveal? What does it obscure? Who gains voice? Who loses voice? What practices does it authorize? What practices does it prohibit? What violence could be justified in its name? What institutional interests might capture it? What living consequences would require its revision? Does it still serve life, or has it begun to serve itself?*) applied to any candidate distinction.

ADR-0048's substitution-trap admission names the failure mode but does not specify operationally how a coordination-grammar should detect-and-respond to the substitution-trap firing. ADR-0048 §"Honest limit acknowledgment" explicitly flags this gap: *"If operational evidence shows expressive/constructed modes being systematically collapsed into allocational framings... a dedicated amendment ADR may be required. Naming the modes is necessary but not sufficient."*

Sahely's recursive life-coherence audit is **a candidate methodological complement** to ADR-0048's named-but-undetected substitution-trap. The 11 reflexive questions are interrogatives over operational concerns Spore canon already names at different sites:

- *What does it reveal / obscure?* — F4 representation-authority observable-vs-hidden discipline
- *Who gains / loses voice?* — ADR-0048 expressive-power distribution analysis
- *What practices does it authorize / prohibit?* — ADR-0046 rule-in-use vs rule-in-form mapping
- *What violence could be justified in its name?* — F6 failure-modes §"meta-pattern / composition" Golem-Calf-shaped substitution alert
- *What institutional interests might capture it?* — ADR-0005 decentralization-myth bundle capture analysis
- *What living consequences would require its revision?* — F8 external-validation-loop trigger-shape
- *Does it still serve life, or has it begun to serve itself?* — ADR-0048 substitution-trap canonical question

The methodology is **distributed across Spore canon** at present without being **named as a methodology**. Sahely's contribution is the integration: the 11 questions as a single canon-method discipline applied recursively. ### 4.4 Central thesis ↔ parsimony-as-earning-test-outcome (methodological isomorphism)

Per the extraction record's "Spore canon engagement notes" section, the operator-emphasized observation: Sahely's C-11 central thesis — *"a distinction is not life-coherent because it names life; it is life-coherent only while its use preserves, restores, and expands life-capacity in actual relations of coexistence"* — is **structurally identical** to Spore's ADR-0048 parsimony-as-earning-test-outcome-not-axiom discipline at the methodological layer.

ADR-0048 §"What the primitives earn their place" framing (cited in §Decision): *"Parsimony at 7 primitives is not an axiom.

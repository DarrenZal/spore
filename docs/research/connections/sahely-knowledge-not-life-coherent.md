## 4. Substrate-resonance map (descriptive)

Read against post-Bundle-α canon (concepts-yaml v24; 4 cross-cutting doctrines; 40 canon-decisions through ADR-0090). Descriptive only. All concept-claims attribute to **John McMurtry** (REPOST), not Sahely.

### 4.1 Life-coherence-as-criterion-of-knowledge ↔ life-value-doctrine (ADR-0086)

C2's exact move — "knowledge is not knowledge if it is not life coherent" — and C10's "felt, thought, action for all universal human life necessities" are the compressed kernel of McMurtry's **life-value onto-axiology**: value (and here, knowledge-validity) measured by its coherence with the conditions of life and the satisfaction of universal human life-needs. Spore already metabolized this as `life-value-doctrine`, the **4th cross-cutting doctrine** (ADR-0086), sibling to `reproductive-commoning` (ADR-0002), `boundary-commoning` (ADR-0003), and `care-commoning` (ADR-0045). This foundation-era repost is an **early, pre-AI** statement of the same doctrine the later (2025–2026) AI-co-authored anchors articulate at length; it is independent in *time* within McMurtry's own corpus but is **not** independent *evidence* (one author; a repost of one text). No canon-shift.

### 4.2 Universal human life necessities ↔ civil-commons substrate (ADR-0087)

C10's "universal human life necessities in their enjoyment" is the **life-needs** stratum from which McMurtry's *civil commons* (the unpriced social provisioning of universal life-needs) is built in his broader work. Spore carries `civil-commons` as a **derived-glossary slug** (ADR-0087), NOT a doctrine — the slug-vs-doctrine boundary is load-bearing per the v24 yaml notes (`civil-commons` is operationally identical to contraction/expansion of life-value but admitted at the derived-glossary layer, with civil-commons-as-fifth-doctrine explicitly rejected as a canon-object-class conflation). This thin post names only the life-needs limb verbatim, not "civil commons" itself; the resonance is descriptive substrate, cited against the resolved slug.

### 4.3 Felt-knowledge / emotions-moored-in-action ↔ care-cluster scope-condition (ADR-0088 on ADR-0045)

C3 ("the felt side of being, of which emotions are key, or it is inconsistent and life-blind") and C4 (emotions "moored in life coherent action") put the **felt/emotional** dimension at the center of valid knowledge. This is the same territory ADR-0088 scope-conditioned onto `care-commoning` (ADR-0045) — care / love-as-structural-coupling / the felt-relational ground of coordination. Per the Bundle α map, care themes cite **ADR-0088**, not the pre-Bundle-α ADR-0045 parent. McMurtry's "felt knowledge cannot go wrong … all the way across and down" (C8) is the affective-evaluation limb the care-cluster already holds. No canon-shift.

### 4.4 Independent-inspection verifiability ↔ recursive-audit-method (ADR-0085) + representation-authority (F4)

C9 — knowledge "verifiable by independent inspection of words, facts, conclusions" — is the **audit/verification** discipline: validity is not self-asserted but exposed to independent inspection across the words → facts → conclusions chain. This reinforces ADR-0085's `recursive-audit-method` (the method-side slug of the trap-shape-vocab bundle) and, at the representation layer, F4 `representation-authority`'s fact-vs-specification + independent-inspection-of-representations discipline (ADR-0090 later scope-conditions perception-as-power onto F4, but **no perception-as-power content appears in this thin post**, so ADR-0090 is not cited here). Descriptive reinforcement; no canon-shift.

### 4.5 Policy-or-it-dies ↔ structural-legitimacy (ADR-0042)

C11 — "beyond song alone … when the rubber hits the road … policy decides / it dies on the vine" — is McMurtry's operational test: knowledge that never becomes life-coherent *policy* is "helpless in gestural moments." This parallels the structural-legitimacy discipline (ADR-0042): **persistence (or eloquence) is not legitimacy** — a knowledge-claim that cannot survive independent inspection and operational enactment lacks standing. Descriptive only.

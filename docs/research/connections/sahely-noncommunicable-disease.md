## 2. C-claims (verbatim from extraction record)

All quotes verbatim from the extraction record §"Verbatim key claims"; all carry RID `orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-27-noncommunicable-disease-and-the-worlds-w` and `pdf-p<N>`.

- **C-1** [anchor: §Title-page · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-27-noncommunicable-disease-and-the-worlds-w · pdf-p2] Core thesis: "Core thesis: NCDs are best understood not only as chronic failures inside individual bodies, but as conserved organism–niche miscouplings — patterns of living in which biological, emotional, relational, commercial, ecological, and institutional conditions recursively stabilize disease-producing trajectories."

- **C-5** [anchor: §Executive-Summary · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-27-noncommunicable-disease-and-the-worlds-w · pdf-p5] Diseases of conserved maladaptive coherence: "From this standpoint, many NCD patterns are diseases of conserved maladaptive coherence. The organism is not merely malfunctioning. It may be surviving under constraint... [these] may be adaptive responses that become pathological when the organism remains locked into a disease-producing niche."

- **C-6** [anchor: §Executive-Summary · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-27-noncommunicable-disease-and-the-worlds-w · pdf-p5] The clinical-question shift: "The clinical question shifts from 'Why is this patient non-compliant?' to 'What organism–niche pattern is this person conserving, and what new relational conditions would allow a healthier way of living to become possible?'"

- **C-11** [anchor: §4-Maturanas-Biology-of-Living · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-27-noncommunicable-disease-and-the-worlds-w · pdf-p7] Structural coupling, health- or disease-generating: "Through recurrent interactions, organism and environment become fitted to each other. Coupling can be health-generating or disease-generating. Eating patterns, sleep rhythms, stress responses, movement habits, and emotional coping strategies emerge through repeated coupling with family, school, work, culture, economy, ecology, and care systems."

- **C-12** [anchor: §4-Maturanas-Biology-of-Living · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-27-noncommunicable-disease-and-the-worlds-w · pdf-p7] Emotioning specifies domains of action: "Emotions are bodily-relational dispositions that specify domains of possible action. A person in shame, fear, grief, exhaustion, trust, dignity, or love inhabits different worlds of possible conduct. Information alone rarely changes behavior unless the emotional field of possibility changes."

- **C-13** [anchor: §4-Maturanas-Biology-of-Living · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-27-noncommunicable-disease-and-the-worlds-w · pdf-p7] Languaging can close or open possibility: "Human beings live in language as a domain of coordination. Phrases such as 'I am non-compliant,' 'I have no willpower,' or 'nothing works' are not neutral; they can close possibility. Clinical language can either deepen shame or open a new domain of action."

- **C-14** [anchor: §4-Maturanas-Biology-of-Living · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-27-noncommunicable-disease-and-the-worlds-w · pdf-p7] Love as relational ground: "Love, in Maturana's biological sense, is the relational domain in which the other is allowed to appear as a legitimate other in coexistence. In NCD care, dignity, respect, and trust are not soft extras; they widen the patient's domain of possible action."

- **C-17** [anchor: §6-Organism-Niche-Coherence-and-Miscoupling · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-27-noncommunicable-disease-and-the-worlds-w · pdf-p8] **Coherence is not comfort (life-capacity test):** "Coherence is not mere comfort, stability, or adaptation. A person may be adapted to a harmful situation. A family may be stable but oppressive. A food system may be efficient but pathogenic. Coherence must therefore be judged by whether the conserved pattern expands or narrows life-capacity."

- **C-18** [anchor: §6-Organism-Niche-Coherence-and-Miscoupling · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-27-noncommunicable-disease-and-the-worlds-w · pdf-p8] Definition of miscoupling: "Organism–niche miscoupling occurs when recurrent relations conserve patterns that allow short-term adaptation but progressively narrow the organism's range of viable action, producing chronic physiological, behavioral, emotional, and relational strain."

- **C-20** [anchor: §9-From-Compliance-to-Co-Creation · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-27-noncommunicable-disease-and-the-worlds-w · pdf-p12] Patient as participant; clinician as observer-participant: "This reframes 'non-compliance.' The patient is not a passive recipient of medical instruction. The patient is a participant in reconfiguring a way of living. The clinician becomes an observer-participant who helps open a new domain of action."

- **C-21** [anchor: §11-From-Pathogenic-Niches-to-Life-Coherent-Societies · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-27-noncommunicable-disease-and-the-worlds-w · pdf-p16] Public goods as life-supporting commons: "A Maturana-informed public health asks: What way of living is this society conserving? Which institutions stabilize disease-producing patterns? Which commercial actors benefit from maladaptive coupling? Which communities lack the conditions for healthier living? Which public goods must be rebuilt as life-supporting commons?"

- **C-23** [anchor: §13-Caribbean-SIDS-Application · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-27-noncommunicable-disease-and-the-worlds-w · pdf-p20] Small-society commons-infrastructure: "Churches, schools, workplaces, sports clubs, primary-care teams, community kitchens, gardens, and family networks can become infrastructures for conserving healthier ways of living."

- **C-26** [anchor: §16-Conclusion · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-27-noncommunicable-disease-and-the-worlds-w · pdf-p23] The central question: "The central question for NCD care is therefore not only 'How do we reduce risk factors?' but also 'What kind of world must we bring forth so that healthier ways of living can be conserved?'"

## 4. Substrate-resonance map (descriptive)

Read against Spore's post-Bundle-α canon (14 foundation docs / 9 primitives / **4 cross-cutting doctrines** / 2 modes / 2 properties / derived glossary slugs incl. `civil-commons`, `golden-calf-trap`, `recursive-audit-method`; concepts-yaml v24; 40 canon-decisions). Descriptive only.

**4.1 Life-capacity test ↔ life-value-doctrine (ADR-0086).** C-17 is the load-bearing resonance: "Coherence must be judged by whether the conserved pattern expands or narrows life-capacity," and C-18 defines miscoupling as conserving patterns that "progressively narrow the organism's range of viable action." This is the clinical-domain instantiation of the **life-value-doctrine** (the 4th cross-cutting doctrine admitted at ADR-0086 — a doctrine sitting *across* primitives, sibling to `reproductive-commoning` / `boundary-commoning` / `care-commoning`, not a glossary slug). The McMurtry-lineage move — judge an arrangement by whether it expands or narrows life-capacity rather than by mere persistence — is exactly the doctrine's shape. Sahely arrives via Maturanan organism-niche biology rather than McMurtry's life-value onto-axiology; the convergence is real but tradition-distinct, and per single-source rigor it is *reinforcement-from-another-tradition* of an already-admitted doctrine, not new pressure.

**4.2 Biology of love ↔ care-commoning under ADR-0088 scope-condition.** C-14 names love as "the relational domain in which the other is allowed to appear as a legitimate other in coexistence" and insists "dignity, respect, and trust are not soft extras." This is the same canon-layer work as **care-commoning** (ADR-0045), now carrying the **care-cluster scope-condition** of ADR-0088 (which scope-conditions ADR-0045 to admit the love-as-structural-coupling / legitimacy-of-the-other substrate alongside the care-feminist substrate). C-20's "observer-participant" clinician and C-6's compliance→co-creation shift operationalize this dignity ground in the clinical encounter. Already canonical; no shift.

**4.3 Life-supporting commons ↔ civil-commons (ADR-0087).** C-21 ("Which public goods must be rebuilt as life-supporting commons?") and C-23 (churches, schools, community kitchens, gardens, primary-care teams as "infrastructures for conserving healthier ways of living") map to the **`civil-commons`** derived-glossary slug (ADR-0087; McMurtry/Ostrom/Bollier lineage; alias `civil-commons-substrate`). Note the discipline: `civil-commons` is a *derived slug*, not a doctrine — the doctrine carrying the value-test is `life-value-doctrine` (4.1). Sahely's "life-supporting commons" is the substrate the slug names; no shift.

**4.4 Structural coupling, languaging, emotioning ↔ already-canonical scope-conditions.** Three Maturanan primitives map to existing Spore scope-conditions without pressure: (a) C-11/C-18 organism–niche **structural coupling** reinforces Membrane-as-self-produced (ADR-0062) and Holon-irreducibility (ADR-0050) — the organism is neither reducible to its parts nor determined by its medium; (b) C-13 **languaging** ("clinical language can close or open possibility") sits inside Signal sense-making-mode (ADR-0063 participatory-sense-making); (c) C-12 **emotioning** ("emotions specify domains of possible action") resonates with Johar `expressive-power`/`constructed-power` modes (ADR-0048) — the emotional field as the pre-reaction substrate conditioning which actions are available. None requires canon-shift.

**4.5 Persistence ≠ legitimacy + recursive safeguards ↔ structural-legitimacy + recursive-audit-method.** C-17's "a family may be stable but oppressive; a food system efficient but pathogenic" is the persistence-is-not-legitimacy discipline of `structural-legitimacy` (ADR-0042) in clinical-domain dress. Distinctly, the paper's 7 §15 safeguards ("do not romanticize adaptation"; "do not blame the niche in a way that removes agency"; "do not treat coherence as simple harmony") are a domain-specific instance of the **recursive-audit-method** (ADR-0085) — the framework auditing its own use to avoid becoming the instrument it critiques (the trap-shape family: cf. `golden-calf-trap`, the SHAPE-OF ADR-0048's substitution-trap, not equivalent-to). The mythic/substitution-trap vocabulary is fully resolved at ADR-0085; nothing re-opens.

## 2. C-claims (verbatim from extraction record)

All claims carry RID `orn:source:bsahely-2026-02-10-the-self-as-a-viability-stack-from` and `pdf-p<N>` per intake protocol. Quotes are verbatim from the extraction record §"Verbatim key claims".

- **C-1** [anchor: §Abstract · orn:source:bsahely-2026-02-10-the-self-as-a-viability-stack-from · pdf-p5] — Central thesis: "This white paper proposes a unifying framework in which the self is understood as a **stacked architecture of viability-regulating interfaces**, rather than a unitary entity or representational construct."

- **C-2** [anchor: §Abstract · orn:source:bsahely-2026-02-10-the-self-as-a-viability-stack-from · pdf-p5] — Damasio-grounded architecture: "Building on Antonio Damasio's typology of proto-self, core self, and autobiographical self, the paper anchors the proto-self in mitochondrial psychobiology, identifies molecular signaling … as the primary translation layer from cellular viability to felt experience, and situates consciousness in brainstem–hypothalamic affective circuits."

- **C-3** [anchor: §Abstract · orn:source:bsahely-2026-02-10-the-self-as-a-viability-stack-from · pdf-p5] — Life-regulating-itself-from-inside: "The result is a rigorously naturalistic, non-reductive account of the self as **life regulating itself from the inside** …"

- **C-4** [anchor: §Executive Summary · orn:source:bsahely-2026-02-10-the-self-as-a-viability-stack-from · pdf-p6] — Central organizing claim: "the self is not a thing but a viability stack — a layered architecture through which life regulates itself under uncertainty, with each layer solving a distinct regulatory problem and inheriting constraints from below."

- **C-5** [anchor: §Executive Summary · orn:source:bsahely-2026-02-10-the-self-as-a-viability-stack-from · pdf-p6] — Consciousness-as-felt-enforcement: "Consciousness, on this account, is not cortical awareness but the **felt enforcement of viability constraints**."

- **C-6** [anchor: §Executive Summary · orn:source:bsahely-2026-02-10-the-self-as-a-viability-stack-from · pdf-p6] — Trauma as interface injury: "It reframes trauma and psychopathology as **interface injuries** — persistent constraints on regulatory flexibility — rather than as failures of insight or will."

- **C-7** [anchor: §1.1 · orn:source:bsahely-2026-02-10-the-self-as-a-viability-stack-from · pdf-p7] — Structural-error diagnosis: "It is a structural error: the self is repeatedly approached as a *thing* to be located, rather than as a **process that spans interfaces**."

- **C-8** [anchor: §1.2 · orn:source:bsahely-2026-02-10-the-self-as-a-viability-stack-from · pdf-p7] — Layered descriptions of one architecture: "These are not competing theories. They are *layered descriptions of the same architecture*, seen from different observational standpoints. At each interface: intrinsic constraints must be satisfied, information is compressed, and global control variables become *felt*."

- **C-9** [anchor: §1.2 · orn:source:bsahely-2026-02-10-the-self-as-a-viability-stack-from · pdf-p8] — Single organizing principle: "What philosophers call subjectivity, neuroscientists call affect, clinicians call agency, and traditional systems call vitality are different projections of a single organizing principle: **viability from the inside**."

- **C-10** [anchor: §2.1 · orn:source:bsahely-2026-02-10-the-self-as-a-viability-stack-from · pdf-p9] — Viability precedes representation: "Any account of the self that begins with representation begins too late. Long before a system can model the world … it must *remain viable*. Viability here does not mean mere persistence. It means maintaining a bounded, coherent organization in the face of perturbation, uncertainty, and limited information."

- **C-11** [anchor: §2.1 · orn:source:bsahely-2026-02-10-the-self-as-a-viability-stack-from · pdf-p9] — Value implicit in continued existence: "Value is implicit in the very act of continued existence. States are already better or worse for the system before they are named, categorized, or understood. … This is the first unifying move: **viability precedes representation**."

- **C-12** [anchor: §2.4 · orn:source:bsahely-2026-02-10-the-self-as-a-viability-stack-from · pdf-p10] — Qualia as interface variables: "These experienced variables are what we call *qualia*. … They are **felt global control signals** that encode the system's current relationship to its own constraints."

## 4. Substrate-resonance map (descriptive)

This reads the pp 1-11 substrate against Spore's post-Bundle-α canon (14 foundation docs / 9 primitives / 4 cross-cutting doctrines / concepts-yaml v24; canon-rebuild arc at 40 canon-decisions through ADR-0090). Descriptive observations, not admission proposals.

### 4.1 "Value is implicit in continued existence" ↔ reproduction-continuity (ADR-0049)

C-11 — *"Value is implicit in the very act of continued existence. States are already better or worse for the system before they are named …"* — is a biological-scale articulation of the substrate Spore canonized as the **reproduction-continuity** primitive (ADR-0049): the verb of conserving viability across episodes, operating at a time-scale prior to the within-episode intent→commitment→evidence→signal cycle. Sahely's claim that value precedes representation (C-10/C-11) sits underneath the reproduction-continuity intuition that keeping-the-system-alive-over-time is a coordination operation in its own right, not a derivative of cognition. The resonance is cross-scale (cellular/organismic vs federation-of-instances) and cross-tradition (affective neuroscience vs social-reproduction theory) — independent reinforcement, not new pressure.

### 4.2 "Self is not a thing but a process" ↔ Holon-irreducibility + relational-identity (ADR-0050)

C-4 and C-7 reject the self-as-locatable-thing in favor of *"a process that spans interfaces"*. ADR-0050 ratified that Spore Holons are irreducible to their parts (Side-B+ joint-commitment substrate), and the `relational-identity` slug captures identity as relationally-constituted rather than thing-like. Sahely's anti-reification of the self lives on the same anti-reductionist axis: the whole (self) is not the sum of, nor reducible to, any single layer. No canon-shift — ADR-0050 already admits this substrate; Sahely adds an affective-neuroscience articulation.

### 4.3 "Life regulating itself from the inside" ↔ Membrane-as-self-produced (ADR-0062)

C-3 and C-10 frame viability as *"life regulating itself from the inside"* and *"maintaining a bounded, coherent organization in the face of perturbation."* This is the autopoietic reading ADR-0062 already co-admits as a scope on the Membrane primitive: for living-system instance-classes, the boundary is constituted by the ongoing operations of the system itself, not given exogenously. Sahely's viability-stack is structural-coupling-language at the cellular scale. No canon-shift; ADR-0062 already scope-conditions exactly this reading.

### 4.4 Qualia as "felt global control signals" ↔ Signal sense-making-mode (ADR-0063)

C-5 (consciousness as *"felt enforcement of viability constraints"*) and C-12 (qualia as *"felt global control signals that encode the system's current relationship to its own constraints"*) describe signalling that is not one-way send-and-receive but constitutively-interactive emergence at the cellular/affective scale. The extraction record itself flags this as engaging Spore's Signal sense-making-mode discipline (ADR-0063). Held descriptive: at biological scale the signal *is* the felt enforcement, which is the constitutively-interactive limb ADR-0063 already scope-conditions. No canon-shift; framing-note value for any future Signal scope-conditioning that wants independent-tradition substrate.

### 4.5 Layered constraint-inheritance ↔ rule-in-use nested stratification (ADR-0046)

C-4's *"each layer solving a distinct regulatory problem and inheriting constraints from below"* and C-8's *"layered descriptions of the same architecture"* resonate with the nested-constraint stratification of ADR-0046 (`rule-in-use` vs `rule-in-form`; the Ostrom action-situation stack of operational / collective-choice / constitutional levels). Both share the discipline of *operational regulation as the locus of identity* across nested levels. Cross-tradition convergence (affective neuroscience + institutional economics) on layered-constraint-inheritance; no canon-shift needed.

### 4.6 "Viability ≠ mere persistence" ↔ structural-legitimacy (ADR-0042)

C-10 explicitly separates viability from *"mere persistence."* Spore's `structural-legitimacy` foundation doc (ADR-0042) does the same canon-work at the federation-governance layer: a federation arrangement may operationally persist while failing the legitimacy test that makes persistence meaningful. The two articulate persistence≠legitimacy at different scales (organismic viability vs federation governance). Reinforcing cross-domain convergence; no canon-shift.

## 2. C-claims (verbatim from extraction record)

All quotes verbatim from the extraction record; RID `orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-16-life-coherent-discernment-and-repair`.

- **C1** [anchor: §Abstract · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-16-life-coherent-discernment-and-repair · pdf-p3-4] **Sacred insecurity** as central diagnostic: "Sacred insecurity [is] a condition in which collective trauma, identity, land, religion, sovereignty, memory, and survival become fused into an ultimate concern that makes compromise appear as betrayal and violence appear as protection."

- **C2** [anchor: §Abstract · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-16-life-coherent-discernment-and-repair · pdf-p3] Discernment as spiritual analogue of measurement: "the spiritual analogue of measurement is discernment. Measurement asks what counts as progress. Discernment asks what is worthy of ultimacy. Both can reveal or conceal life. Both can become instruments of repair or mechanisms of distortion."

- **C3** [anchor: §Abstract + Part IV · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-16-life-coherent-discernment-and-repair · pdf-p4, pdf-p82-104] Eight failure-modes of sacred incoherence (enumerated): "weaponized victimhood / redemptive violence / enemy absolutization / institutional idolatry / spiritual bypass / selective legality / metric and narrative capture / peace without life-conditions."

- **C4** [anchor: §Abstract + Part IV · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-16-life-coherent-discernment-and-repair · pdf-p4] Nine-step life-coherent discernment and repair cycle (verbatim): "recognize the wound; name the ultimate concern; expose the sacred distortion; distinguish life-protection from life-destruction; de-implement harmful patterns; restore the commons of coexistence; repair life-capacity; and conserve the conditions of peace."

- **C5** [anchor: §Abstract closing · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-16-life-coherent-discernment-and-repair · pdf-p4] Middle East stress-test stance: "It stress-tests the framework against the Middle East, arguing that no people's wound should be denied and no people's wound should be allowed to sanctify the destruction of another."

- **C6** [anchor: §Acknowledgements · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-16-life-coherent-discernment-and-repair · pdf-p2] Substrate integration (excerpt): "Maturana's biology of love and legitimate coexistence; McMurtry's life-value onto-axiology and critique of life-incoherent value systems; Galtung's analysis of direct, structural, and cultural violence; Peil Kauffman's understanding of emotion as embodied moral-spiritual guidance; Wilber's distinctions between waking up, growing up, cleaning up, and showing up…"

- **C7** [anchor: §11 Core Architecture · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-16-life-coherent-discernment-and-repair · pdf-p82-89] Discernment-question stack (excerpt of the 11): "1. What is being treated as sacred? / 2. What is being protected? / 3. What is being sacrificed? / 4. Whose grief is recognized? / 5. Whose fear is legitimized? / 6. What distinction is being blurred? / 7. What life-destroying pattern is being reproduced? / 8. What must be de-implemented? / 9. What must be restored? / 10. What would repair life-capacity? / 11. What would conserve peace?"

## 4. Substrate-resonance map (descriptive; cites admitted slugs/ADRs)

1. **Trap-shape vocabulary at the sacred-political layer (ADR-0085).** "Sacred insecurity" (C1) is the fusion of finite concerns (trauma, land, sovereignty, memory) into an ultimate concern such that "violence appear[s] as protection" — a sacred-political instance of the `golden-calf-trap` shape (the SHAPE-OF ADR-0048's `substitution-trap`, not equivalent-to). Several of the eight failure-modes (C3) read directly onto trap-shape vocabulary: *institutional idolatry* and *metric and narrative capture* are corrective-capture / grammar-used-to-dominate forms; *weaponized victimhood* is a finite-good elevated to ultimacy. The 11-question stack's recursive self-interrogation (C7: "What life-destroying pattern is being reproduced? / What must be de-implemented?") is the `recursive-audit-method` shape — a discipline that turns the diagnostic instrument back on its own use. Already admitted; no shift.

2. **McMurtry life-value as the 4th cross-cutting doctrine (ADR-0086).** C6 cites "McMurtry's life-value onto-axiology and critique of life-incoherent value systems" by name, and C4's "repair life-capacity" + "conserve the conditions of peace" are life-value-doctrine operations. `life-value-doctrine` is a CROSS-CUTTING DOCTRINE (the 4th, sibling to `reproductive-commoning` / `boundary-commoning` / `care-commoning`), not a glossary slug — and this paper's life-capacity / life-conditions framing is squarely inside it.

3. **Maturana biology of love inside the care-cluster scope-condition (ADR-0088).** C6's "Maturana's biology of love and legitimate coexistence" is the love-as-structural-coupling / legitimacy-of-the-other substrate that ADR-0088 scope-conditioned onto `care-commoning` (ADR-0045). C4's "restore the commons of coexistence" reads as care-doctrine work. Reinforcing only.

4. **Perception-as-power inside the F4 scope-condition (ADR-0090).** C2 frames discernment as the spiritual analogue of measurement — "Both can reveal or conceal life" — which is precisely the perception-as-power / standpoint concern (what-becomes-visible determines what gets protected) that ADR-0090 scope-conditioned onto `representation-authority` (F4). C7's questions 4–6 ("Whose grief is recognized? / Whose fear is legitimized? / What distinction is being blurred?") are Galtung-structural-violence-shaped: legitimacy and visibility distributed asymmetrically. Reinforcing only.

5. **Failure-modes and persistence≠legitimacy (ADR-0075 F6; ADR-0042).** C3's eight failure-modes are a domain-specific catalog at the spiritual-political layer that F6 failure-modes (ADR-0075) already provides the substrate for. The eighth mode — "peace without life-conditions" — plus C5's stress-test stance ("no people's wound should be allowed to sanctify the destruction of another") enact the `structural-legitimacy` discipline (ADR-0042): an arrangement may persist (a ceasefire, a sacred order) without being legitimate by the test that makes its persistence meaningful.

## 2. C-claims (verbatim from extraction record)

All quotes verbatim from the extraction record §"Verbatim key claims". RID `orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-27-living-coherence-and-neuropsychiatric-di`; `pdf-p<N>` per intake protocol.

- **C-1** [anchor: §Abstract · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-27-living-coherence-and-neuropsychiatric-di · pdf-p2] Central derivational thesis: "This white paper proposes such a framework by deriving neuropsychiatric disease from disturbances in living coherence across five foundational layers: autopoiesis, structural coupling, emotioning, felt interiority, and emotional sentience."

- **C-2** [anchor: §Abstract · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-27-living-coherence-and-neuropsychiatric-di · pdf-p2] Five-layer definitions: "Autopoiesis defines the living organism as a materially open, organizationally closed, self-producing unity. Structural coupling situates this unity within histories of viable or destructive relation with its medium. Emotioning specifies bodily domains of possible action through which organisms meet the world. Felt sense and interoception disclose the organism's living condition from within. Emotional sentience names the evaluative self-regulatory dimension by which life feels how life is going."

- **C-3** [anchor: §Abstract · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-27-living-coherence-and-neuropsychiatric-di · pdf-p2] De-moralizing reframe (incoherence ≠ moral failing): "In this usage, incoherence is not a personal failing or moral deficit; it is a sign that life conditions, regulatory capacity, or organism-world coupling have become nonviable."

- **C-4** [anchor: §6 Third Principle · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-27-living-coherence-and-neuropsychiatric-di · pdf-p13] Love as legitimate-other-in-coexistence (Maturana): "Following Maturana, emotions are not merely private feelings. They are dynamic bodily dispositions that specify domains of possible action. ... Love, in Maturana's distinctive biological sense, opens coexistence with the other as a legitimate other (Maturana, 1988)."

- **C-5** [anchor: §6 Third Principle · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-27-living-coherence-and-neuropsychiatric-di · pdf-p13] Rationality operates within emotion-opened domains: "Emotioning also explains why reasoning is never emotionally neutral. Rationality operates within a domain of action already opened by emotion. A proposal heard in trust is not the same proposal heard in humiliation. Evidence encountered in curiosity is not the same evidence encountered in terror."

- **C-6** [anchor: §5 Second Principle · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-27-living-coherence-and-neuropsychiatric-di · pdf-p12] Organism-medium co-development: "Structural coupling names the recurrent history through which organism and medium become sufficiently congruent for living to continue (Maturana & Varela, 1980; Varela et al., 1991). ... The person is not simply located in an environment; the person and world co-develop through histories of coupling."

- **C-7** [anchor: §4 First Principle · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-27-living-coherence-and-neuropsychiatric-di · pdf-p11] Autopoiesis definition (organizationally closed / materially open): "Autopoiesis names the minimal organization of a living system as a self-producing unity. A living being is materially open ... Yet it is organizationally closed: its processes recursively produce the components and boundary conditions through which the system remains the kind of unity it is."

- **C-8** [anchor: §Figure 3 Seven-Primitives Clinical Grammar · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-27-living-coherence-and-neuropsychiatric-di · pdf-p16] The clinical grammar's **Margin** primitive: clinical question "What reserve remains?"; repair direction (grammar table, pdf-p17): "Restore safety, sleep, nourishment, pacing, support, medication when indicated."

- **C-9** [anchor: §Figure 3 Seven-Primitives Clinical Grammar · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-27-living-coherence-and-neuropsychiatric-di · pdf-p16] The clinical grammar's **Perception** primitive: clinical question "What is salient?"; repair direction (grammar table, pdf-p17): "Recalibrate salience through safety, testing, dialogue, embodiment, and reality contact."

- **C-10** [anchor: §13 Limitations and Safeguards · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-27-living-coherence-and-neuropsychiatric-di · pdf-p25] Social-use caution (autopoiesis does not transfer cleanly to societies): "The social use of autopoiesis requires caution; societies, institutions, and economies are not organisms in the same sense as biological living systems."

- **C-11** [anchor: §13 Limitations and Safeguards · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-27-living-coherence-and-neuropsychiatric-di · pdf-p25] Recursive moralizing-safeguard: "The language of coherence can become moralizing if misused. Lack of coherence should be read as a sign of unmet life conditions, not personal failure."

- **C-12** [anchor: §11.13 Cross-cutting process logics · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-27-living-coherence-and-neuropsychiatric-di · pdf-p21] Process-logic over diagnostic-box-stacking: "A patient with depression, trauma, addiction, and chronic pain may not be best understood by stacking four diagnostic boxes. The better formulation may be that the person's margin has collapsed ... The diagnosis remains important, but the process logic guides the repair plan."

- **C-13** [anchor: §About the Author · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-27-living-coherence-and-neuropsychiatric-di · pdf-p32] Author's named lineage (life-value + civil-commons): "His writing brings together clinical experience, biological theory, autopoiesis, structural coupling, emotional sentience, life-value onto-axiology, peace theory, and civil-commons thinking ..."

## 4. Substrate-resonance map (descriptive)

Read against Spore's post-Bundle-α canon (14 foundation docs / 9 primitives / **4** cross-cutting doctrines / concepts-yaml v24 / 40 canon-decisions through ADR-0090). All points descriptive; no admission proposed.

### 4.1 Maturanan love ↔ care-commoning (ADR-0045 + ADR-0088)

C-4's "Love, in Maturana's distinctive biological sense, opens coexistence with the other as a legitimate other" is the **same biology-of-love lineage** the 2026-05-21 viability-grammar paper carried, and is exactly the care-cluster that ADR-0088 scope-conditioned onto `care-commoning` (ADR-0045). The Bundle α arc already resolved this: love-as-legitimate-other-in-coexistence and Spore's `care-commoning` doctrine do the same canon-layer work (relational doctrine across primitives). This paper is a clinical instantiation, not new pressure.

### 4.2 Life-value + civil-commons lineage ↔ ADR-0086 + ADR-0087

C-13's About-the-Author paragraph explicitly names "life-value onto-axiology ... and civil-commons thinking" as Sahely's working lineage. These are the two McMurtry-adjacent threads the Bundle α arc admitted: `life-value-doctrine` as the **4th cross-cutting doctrine** (ADR-0086, sibling to `reproductive-commoning` / `boundary-commoning` / `care-commoning`) and `civil-commons` as a **derived-glossary slug** (ADR-0087, alias `civil-commons-substrate`). Here both appear only as named-in-back-matter author-context, not as load-bearing argument inside the clinical framework — so the resonance is citation-level, reinforcing the already-resolved admissions without surfacing fresh operational pressure.

### 4.3 Clinical Margin ↔ margin-as-reserve on F9 (ADR-0089)

The Seven-Primitives clinical grammar's **Margin** primitive (C-8: "What reserve remains?"; repair = "restore safety, sleep, nourishment, pacing, support") is the *margin-as-reserve* sense — depleted reserve as the locus of breakdown, restoration of slack as the locus of repair. ADR-0089 scope-conditioned exactly that reserve-sense onto the F9 maintenance-economics foundation doc. The clinical reading (organismic reserve: sleep / nourishment / pacing) and the canonical reading (federation maintenance reserve) are **cross-domain**; the operational shape (reserve depletion ⇒ nonviability; reserve restoration ⇒ repair) is shared. Descriptive only.

### 4.4 Clinical Perception ↔ perception-as-power on F4 (ADR-0090)

The clinical **Perception** primitive (C-9: "What is salient?"; repair = "recalibrate salience through safety, testing, dialogue, embodiment, and reality contact") resonates with the perception-as-power / what-becomes-visible scope-condition ADR-0090 placed on F4 representation-authority. The shared core is *what becomes salient/visible governs what can be acted on*. The clinical version is intra-organismic salience (threat-saturation, altered salience in psychosis/mania per the cross-cutting-logics table); the canonical version is representational authority over what a federation makes visible. Genuinely adjacent, not coextensive — `concepts:` deliberately omits a perception slug because neither `perception-as-power` is a registered glossary slug nor is the clinical salience the canon's representational concern.

### 4.5 Clinical grammar vs Spore grammar — cross-layer, not 1:1

The paper's Seven-Primitives Clinical Grammar (Constraint / Margin / State / Disturbance / Perception / Regulation / Options) is a **diagnostic rubric** — questions a clinician poses to locate where living coherence has broken down (C-12 process-logic-over-box-stacking). Per the extraction record's own cross-corpus note, it is a clinical specialization of the same author's general 8-primitive viability-grammar (shared: Constraint, Margin, Disturbance, Regulation; specialized: State / Perception / Options). Spore's 9 primitives are **operational coordination acts and structures**, not reflective questions. As in the Phase-2 anchor (`sahely-maturana-viability-grammar.md` §4.1), these grammars occupy adjacent-but-cross-layer canon-object classes: Sahely's clinical grammar is closest in Spore terms to a diagnostic-rubric pattern (M4 design-criteria-pattern / catalog-pattern shape per ADR-0065), not a primitive roster. No primitive-roster pressure.

### 4.6 Recursive moralizing-safeguard ↔ recursive-audit-method (ADR-0085)

C-11 ("the language of coherence can become moralizing if misused") plus C-10 (caution against transferring biological autopoiesis to societies) are author-internal safeguards against the grammar capturing into domination. This is exactly the discipline the Bundle α arc named in ADR-0085: `recursive-audit-method` (the grammar applies its own audit to itself) and `golden-calf-trap` (the SHAPE-OF, not equivalent-to, ADR-0048's `substitution-trap` — a corrective vocabulary hardening into the thing it was meant to dissolve). Sahely's C-10 social-use caution is a useful guard for any Spore bridge tempted to import the clinical grammar wholesale: organisms and federations are not the same kind of unity (cf. C-10 verbatim), so the clinical grammar should compose-over, not collapse-into, Spore's operational primitives.

### 4.7 Structural coupling + autopoiesis ↔ Holon-irreducibility + Membrane-as-self-produced

C-6 (organism and world co-develop through histories of coupling; "the person is not simply located in an environment") and C-7 (organizationally closed / materially open self-producing unity) sit underneath ADR-0050 (Holon-irreducibility) and ADR-0062 (Membrane-as-self-produced scope-conditioning). Both ADRs already admit this substrate; Sahely's clinical derivation adds same-lineage articulation, not new pressure. C-5 (rationality operates within emotion-opened domains) likewise sits inside ADR-0063 Signal participatory-sense-making scope. Descriptive reinforcement only.

## 2. C-claims (verbatim from extraction record)

All quotes verbatim; anchors carry RID `orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-30-the-poetics-of-life-coherence-beauty-ritua` and `pdf-p<N>`. **LOAD-BEARING** marks the claims most engaged by §3.

- **C1** [anchor: §Scholarly Positioning Statement · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-30-the-poetics-of-life-coherence-beauty-ritua · pdf-p2] **LOAD-BEARING — transmission-layer thesis:** "This white paper extends the Life-Coherence framework by articulating its poetic transmission layer: the aesthetic, ritual, grief-bearing, developmental, temporal, and commons-based forms through which right relation becomes perceptible, embodied, inherited, and shared."

- **C2** [anchor: §Scholarly Positioning Statement · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-30-the-poetics-of-life-coherence-beauty-ritua · pdf-p2] **LOAD-BEARING — McMurtry life-value grounding:** "From McMurtry, it receives the life-value criterion: that social formations, institutions, and systems must be judged by whether they enable or disable life-capacity (McMurtry, 2013). From Galtung, it draws the distinction between negative peace and positive peace, emphasizing that life-coherence requires not only the absence of direct harm but the presence of life-enabling conditions (Galtung, 1969)."

- **C3** [anchor: §Abstract · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-30-the-poetics-of-life-coherence-beauty-ritua · pdf-p3] "This paper argues that life-coherence is not only a principle of living organization, nor only an ethical criterion for action; it is also a poetics: a lived pattern of recognition through which beings perceive right relation, honor thresholds, grieve loss, and act in time."

- **C4** [anchor: §Executive Summary · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-30-the-poetics-of-life-coherence-beauty-ritua · pdf-p4] "Yet a framework of life-coherence cannot survive as a concept alone. It must become perceptible, repeatable, mournable, beautiful, and livable. This paper proposes that the missing layer is poetics."

- **C5** [anchor: §2 Beauty as the Felt Appearance of Right Relation · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-30-the-poetics-of-life-coherence-beauty-ritua · pdf-p13] **LOAD-BEARING — captured vs living beauty:** "Captured beauty narrows attention while pretending to enrich it. It is surface without responsibility. … It becomes a mask over incoherence. Living beauty does something different. It widens attention. It restores proportion. … It does not merely please the eye; it reorganizes perception toward care."

- **C6** [anchor: §2 Figure 2 · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-30-the-poetics-of-life-coherence-beauty-ritua · pdf-p15] **LOAD-BEARING — form must not contradict content:** "Its forms of presentation must not contradict its content. If the visual language is dry, mechanical, extractive, or over-controlled, it may inadvertently reproduce the very incoherence the framework seeks to heal. The form must carry the message."

- **C7** [anchor: §3 Ritual as Cultural Physiology · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-30-the-poetics-of-life-coherence-beauty-ritua · pdf-p19] **LOAD-BEARING — living vs dead ritual:** "A living ritual restores relation. A dead ritual enforces conformity. A living ritual invites participation. A dead ritual protects hierarchy. … The difference is not whether the form is old or new, religious or secular, formal or informal. The difference is whether the ritual remains answerable to life."

- **C8** [anchor: §4 Grief as the Deep Test of Coherence · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-30-the-poetics-of-life-coherence-beauty-ritua · pdf-p21] "Grief reveals whether a world is life-coherent. … It is exposed in how it holds loss." And: "But grief is not a defect in life. Grief is love encountering finitude."

- **C9** [anchor: §6 Tempo, Rhythm, and Non-Forcing Action · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-30-the-poetics-of-life-coherence-beauty-ritua · pdf-p32] **LOAD-BEARING — temporal violence:** "Living systems have tempo. To ignore tempo is to miscouple with life. … Temporal violence occurs when living processes are forced into rhythms that violate their conditions of coherence. … when ecosystems are exploited faster than they can regenerate, and when workers are treated as if human attention were an endlessly renewable resource."

- **C10** [anchor: §6 Tempo, Rhythm, and Non-Forcing Action · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-30-the-poetics-of-life-coherence-beauty-ritua · pdf-p35] **LOAD-BEARING — non-forcing action / wu-wei:** "Non-forcing action, or wu-wei, is often misunderstood as passivity. But in life-coherent terms, it means action in right relation to the field. … It is intervention that listens before it acts, acts without domination, and stops before care becomes control. … It is the disciplined refusal to add force where relation, timing, and field conditions are asking for attunement."

- **C11** [anchor: §6 Tempo, Rhythm, and Non-Forcing Action · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-30-the-poetics-of-life-coherence-beauty-ritua · pdf-p36] **LOAD-BEARING — living judgment exceeds protocol:** "This is why living judgment cannot be fully replaced by protocol. Protocols are necessary. They preserve memory, reduce avoidable error, and coordinate action. But protocols cannot exhaust the intelligence required by living situations. Life always exceeds the script."

- **C12** [anchor: §6 Tempo, Rhythm, and Non-Forcing Action · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-30-the-poetics-of-life-coherence-beauty-ritua · pdf-p38] **LOAD-BEARING — grammar must not become an instrument of force:** "These questions move life-coherence from principle to practice. They help prevent the framework itself from becoming another instrument of force. Even good ideas can become harmful when imposed without timing, humility, or participation. This concern also recalls Illich's warning that institutional systems can become counterproductive when tools and structures cease to serve human-scale flourishing (Illich, 1973)."

- **C13** [anchor: §8 The Knowledge Commons as a Poetic Vessel · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-30-the-poetics-of-life-coherence-beauty-ritua · pdf-p49] **LOAD-BEARING — platform vs commons:** "A platform captures attention. A commons cultivates attention. A platform monetizes engagement. A commons deepens participation. … A platform asks: how do we keep users here? A commons asks: what does this person need in order to re-enter life more coherently?" Grounded: "This understanding draws from commons theory and civil commons thinking … (McMurtry, 2013; Ostrom, 1990)."

- **C14** [anchor: §9 Toward Worlds That Teach Us How to Live · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-30-the-poetics-of-life-coherence-beauty-ritua · pdf-p55] **LOAD-BEARING — life-blindness; never-final coherence:** "But living coherence is never final. It is maintained through ongoing relation, repair, response, and renewal. It is dynamic, not static. … Its opposite is not imperfection. Its opposite is life-blindness: the persistent organization of relation against the conditions of life."

## 4. Substrate-resonance map (descriptive)

1. **Life-value lineage is exact, not analogical.** C2 cites McMurtry's life-value criterion ("enable or disable life-capacity") as a grounding tradition. This is the **same** source canonized as `life-value-doctrine` (ADR-0086, the 4th cross-cutting doctrine, sibling to reproductive-commoning / boundary-commoning / care-commoning). The paper is downstream of the doctrine's source, so it confirms the doctrine's grounding without adding independent weight (single-author corpus).

2. **Civil-commons is the literal slug-source.** C13's "commons theory and civil commons thinking (McMurtry, 2013; Ostrom, 1990)" is precisely the McMurtry/Ostrom cluster behind the resolved `civil-commons` derived-glossary slug (ADR-0087; alias `civil-commons-substrate`). Note the discipline boundary the resolved state preserves: `civil-commons` is a **derived-glossary slug**, NOT a doctrine — the doctrine is `life-value-doctrine`.

3. **Form-carries-message ↔ representation-authority + perception-as-power.** C6 ("the form must carry the message"; incoherent presentation "reproduces the very incoherence the framework seeks to heal") is a poetics-layer cousin of F4 representation-authority (ADR-0074) and the perception-as-power scope-condition on F4 (ADR-0090): what a system's surface makes perceptible shapes what becomes actionable. Descriptive resonance only; Spore's representation-precedence is about text-vs-graph-vs-attestation authority, not aesthetics.

4. **Recursive safeguard ↔ trap-shape vocab.** C12's "prevent the framework itself from becoming another instrument of force" (with the Illich counterproductivity warning) is Sahely's recurring recursive-audit move (`recursive-audit-method`, ADR-0085). The `golden-calf-trap` slug names the **shape-of** ADR-0048's `substitution-trap` (shape-of, not equivalent-to, per the resolved ADR-0085 reconciliation). "Even good ideas can become harmful when imposed" is the trap a recursive audit is built to catch.

5. **Temporal violence + non-forcing ↔ margin-as-reserve + rule-in-use.** C9's regeneration-rate framing ("exploited faster than they can regenerate") composes with the margin-as-reserve scope-condition on F9 maintenance-economics (ADR-0089). C10/C11 (wu-wei; "life always exceeds the script") parallel `rule-in-use` carrying authority over rule-in-form (ADR-0046): both insist living judgment in-situation cannot be exhausted by the protocol-in-form.

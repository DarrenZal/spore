### 4.1 Civil Commons as social immune system ↔ `civil-commons` slug (ADR-0087)

C-4's *"Life-Ground of socially constructed rules and institutions that protect the shared requirements of human existence"* is the McMurtry articulation of exactly the territory Spore admitted as the `civil-commons` derived-glossary slug under ADR-0087. The slug's `one_line_definition` already names *"socially-constructed systems (public water, sanitation, public health, education, legal frameworks, environmental protections) that enable universal access to life-goods and constitute primary economic infrastructure."* This post supplies the substrate prose for that admission; it does not press for anything new. C-3's "Junk-Food Claws" and "Austerity (Bleeding the Host)" pathways are the *attack-on-civil-commons* mechanisms the slug's capture-mechanism evaluation already anticipates.

### 4.2 Life-Capital + three life-requirements ↔ `life-value-doctrine` (ADR-0086)

C-6's *"Life-Capital is the wealth that creates more life-wealth without loss"* and C-5's three pillars (Income Security / Human Vocation / Environmental Life-Host) are McMurtry's Life-Value Onto-Axiology (LVOA) — the **named substrate** of Spore's 4th cross-cutting doctrine `life-value-doctrine` (ADR-0086, sibling to `reproductive-commoning` / `boundary-commoning` / `care-commoning`). The doctrine evaluates whether coordination outcomes serve life-value or money-value; this post is the canonical economic statement of that life-value/money-value distinction (M-M' money-sequence vs life-serving function). **Reinforcement of an already-admitted doctrine** from its own named substrate — not independent-cluster evidence (single-source rigor).

### 4.3 Autonomous M-M' money-sequence ↔ `golden-calf-trap` shape (ADR-0085)

C-1 + C-2 — the money-sequence becoming *"autonomous, uninhibited, and entirely disconnected from any productive or life-serving function"*, ungrounded in 1973 — is the civilizational-scale instance ADR-0085 explicitly maps: McMurtry's *Cancer Stage of Capitalism* maps to the **Golden Calf class-trap at civilizational scale** (the false-idol substitution of money-value for life-value). Note the canon discipline: `golden-calf-trap` is the **shape-of** ADR-0048's `substitution-trap` mode, **not** equivalent-to it — and McMurtry-via-Sahely is one of the named layer-instantiations of that shape. `recursive-audit-method` is the companion discipline that surfaces such traps; both are already in canon at v24.

### 4.4 Public Banking / Zero-Bad-Growth ↔ recovery-mechanism layer (descriptive, no doctrine)

C-9 (Public Banking, Life-Principled Investment) and C-10 (Zero-Bad-Growth) are McMurtry's *cure* mechanisms. Spore canon deliberately does **not** author a money-creation or banking doctrine — these sit adjacent to F9 `maintenance-economics` (reproductive/provisioning/maintenance labour) and to the `civil-commons` economic-infrastructure substrate, but Spore reads them descriptively, not as canon objects. ### 4.5 Persistence-≠-legitimacy ↔ structural-legitimacy (ADR-0042)

The cancer-stage thesis as a whole is a persistence-without-legitimacy diagnosis: a money-sequence economy can metastasize and persist (C-3 global symptoms) while negating its life-host. This parallels Spore's `structural-legitimacy` foundation doc (ADR-0042) at the civilizational scale — operational persistence is not legitimacy. Cross-domain reinforcement; no canon-shift.

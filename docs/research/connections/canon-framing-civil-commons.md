# Canon Framing — Civil Commons (ADR-0087, Sahely Bundle α #3)

Shared framing for the canon-decision admitting `civil-commons` as a derived-glossary slug per the C6 R-Civil-Commons-substrate standalone disposition (DECISION-BRIEF §9 + §17.5 Bundle α architecture + §17.8 (3) operator final dispositions). This note coordinates one ADR (`spore:ADR-0087-civil-commons-derived-glossary-slug-admission`), one new derived-glossary slug (`civil-commons`), and the cross-ADR composition substrate that the slug anchors at glossary-vocabulary layer (5+ ADR-relationships + BKC peer-instance-family β-evidence). Spore-only scope at admission; ic + pm + BKC + bregion Wave-N+1 alignment EXPLICITLY DEFERRED to operator-elective separate-session work.

## 1. The slug in one paragraph

McMurtry's Civil Commons articulation (cited verbatim by Sahely 2026-01-10 §9): *"socially constructed systems that enable universal access to life goods and cannot function if subordinated to private profit extraction. These include: public water systems, sanitation and waste management, public health infrastructure, education systems, legal and institutional frameworks that secure rights and trust, environmental protections that preserve shared life conditions. Orthodox economics typically treats these as 'public expenditures' or 'externalities.' McMurtry shows that this framing is inverted. Markets and private enterprise presuppose the civil commons. They cannot exist without stable ecosystems, educated populations, healthy workers, enforceable contracts, and social trust. Yet orthodox models treat these enabling conditions as peripheral rather than foundational. In a life-coherent economics, the civil commons are recognized as primary economic infrastructure — the base layer upon which all market activity depends. Their erosion is therefore not a fiscal choice among others; it is a direct contraction of life capital."* The `civil-commons` slug anchors this substrate at Spore's derived-glossary layer — a substrate-noun rather than a coordination operation, named for use by future Spore canon work as a substrate-content the `life-value-doctrine` (ADR-0086) operates over without re-routing through bridge-note narrative each time.

## 2. Scope decision and rationale

**Scope: Spore-only derived-glossary admission.** Slug-layer admission is internal-to-Spore vocabulary-promotion, not a doctrine-layer admission (the doctrine `life-value-doctrine` already landed via ADR-0086; this admission anchors one operational-substrate the doctrine operates over). IC and PM do not require coordinated grammar-shape updates because:

- IC's seven intelligence primitives at the domain layer above Spore's nine coordination primitives (per ic:ADR-0018) are not contradicted by adding a derived-glossary substrate-noun. IC's H2-decline of concepts-p2p-wiki.yaml (per ic:ADR-0019 + ic:ADR-0021) means no slug propagation is forced.
- PM's four protocol-objects (pm:Intent / pm:CommitmentBundle / pm:TrustAttestation / pm:MatchProposal) at the protocol layer are not contradicted by adding a substrate-noun at glossary layer. PM's H2-decline of concepts-p2p-wiki.yaml (per pm:ADR-0015 + pm:ADR-0018) means no slug propagation is forced.
- The Q5 discipline established in Phase 3 (ADR-0044) is preserved: primitive-layer or grammar-shape changes coordinate across repos; doctrine-layer or vocabulary-layer changes can be Spore-internal without breaking cross-repo coherence. Slug-layer admissions are even lighter than doctrine-layer admissions.
- BKC peer-instance-family bridge candidate (`bkc.connection.mcmurtry-civil-commons-as-economic-foundation` or operator-named): preserved as operator-elective separate-session work; the Spore-side `sahely-ruddick-civil-commons-bridge.md` (Phase 2 closure Task A) already articulates the 3-layer McMurtry → Ruddick → BKC composition.
- bregion peer-instance-family composes with civil-commons at planetary-scale + life-ground territory layer (per ADR-0086 Open Question on `field` primitive planetary-scale composition); descriptive cross-reference only; no alignment ADR.

Alternative scopes considered and rejected:

- **Bundle with C3 life-value-doctrine (triple-bundle vocab admission per DECISION-BRIEF §9.4 Option A)**: rejected at §17.8 (3) ratification because life-value-doctrine admission required canon-object-class expansion (3 → 4 cross-cutting doctrines) at ADR-0086, while civil-commons admission is derived-glossary-only at slug layer. Bundling would conflate canon-object-class shifts with vocabulary-layer additions, obscuring the distinct ceremony-shapes the two admissions require.

- **No framing-note (matches ADR-0084 vocab-only precedent)**: considered but rejected at Step 2 operator gate (E.1 ratified over E.3) — cross-ADR composition substrate density (5+ ADR-relationships + BKC peer-instance-family β-evidence) exceeds §Consequences scope-bleed threshold; dedicated framing-note layer is the appropriate scope-discipline shape for articulating composition without crowding the ADR §Consequences.

## 5. Three substantive Civil-Commons layer-instantiations

McMurtry's Civil Commons enumeration (W4.1 §9 verbatim) operationalizes across three layers, each with operational-concern match per ADR-0064 discipline:

- **Public-infrastructure layer**: water systems, sanitation, public health, education. McMurtry's explicit canonical enumeration. Operationally, these are non-substitutable life-support systems (no private substitute can provide universal access at scale without itself becoming a quasi-public infrastructure). Erosion produces immediate measurable life-capital contraction (water-system collapse → epidemics; public-health collapse → demographic crisis; education collapse → loss of operationalized knowledge across generations).
- **Legal-institutional layer**: legal frameworks securing rights and trust; constitutional protections; enforceable contracts as civil-commons. The slug names the *framework that provisions trust* enabling contracts — NOT private-property-as-civil-commons (the contracts themselves are private; the framework is civil-commons). This distinction matters: it prevents the slug from being captured into private-property-rights-as-civil-commons reframings (per ADR-0085 Sacralized Trojan-Horse trap-shape variant — sacralized preservation-via-reverence of the framework that resists recursive audit).
- **Environmental-protection layer**: ecological protections preserving shared life conditions; climate-protection regime; biodiversity protection. At planetary-scale, this layer composes with ADR-0086 Open Question on `field` primitive planetary-scale composition. McMurtry's life-ground concept (preserved as Open Question in ADR-0086) maps to civil-commons at planetary-scale; future ADR may operationalize the composition if planetary-scale Field instantiation accumulates operational substrate.

These three layers do not exhaust civil-commons scope — they are the three with sufficient Sahely-paper substrate at admission-time to merit naming. Other layer-instantiations (telecommunications infrastructure, transportation infrastructure, food-systems infrastructure, knowledge-commons) are accessible to the slug's substrate-noun framing but not separately operationalized in the current substrate.

# Canon Framing — Recursive Audit Method + Trap-Shape Taxonomy (Sahely Bundle α — ADR-0085 Option D triple-move)

Shared framing for the Sahely corpus Bundle α first admission (ADR-0085) carrying the triple-move composition's framing-note layer per DECISION-BRIEF §4.5 Option D + §17.8 (2) operator ratification 2026-05-22. This note articulates three components: (a) **trap-shape taxonomy** naming canon-legible variants of ADR-0048 substitution-trap mode (Golden-Calf-class simple-substitution + Sacralized Trojan-Horse preservation-via-reverence); (b) **recursive-audit-method canon-method articulation** composable with existing Spore canon-method discipline feedback memories; (c) **folded substrate documentation** for C11 R-Civilization-extended-niche + C14 R-Externalization-as-deferred-collapse + C15 R-Sacred-memory-transformation as Trojan-Horse-class trap subtype per §17.8 (2).

## Trap-shape taxonomy

ADR-0048 admits `substitution-trap` as a canon-legible failure mode at the modes-across-primitives layer (per Phase 3b.3b integration of Johar's three-form framework). The mode names the failure-class: "One can assign authority without generating comprehension. One can define responsibility without producing commitment." (Johar 2026, *Power Cannot Be Allocated*, per ADR-0048 derivation).

This framing-note names **two canon-legible trap-shape variants** of that mode. Both are operationally distinct shapes the mode takes; both are independently citable via dedicated slugs (`golden-calf-trap`) or substrate-resonance documentation (Sacralized Trojan-Horse class via C15 fold).

### Golden-Calf class — simple substitution

**Canonical 5-stage shape**: corrective distinction → captured → hardened → ritualized → selection-mechanism.

A corrective distinction is introduced to address a coordination problem (e.g., legibility for state administration; categorization for governance; metric for performance evaluation). The distinction works, addressing the original problem. Over time the distinction becomes captured — separated from its corrective function and elevated as worship-worthy artifact. It hardens — its specific form becomes resistant to revision. It ritualizes — the practices around it become rote performance. Finally it becomes a selection-mechanism — actors who fail to perform fidelity to the now-captured distinction are filtered out, regardless of whether the distinction still addresses the original problem.

Sahely W2.4 names this with the Golden Calf metaphor (Exodus 32:1-8): Aaron's calf was made from gold the Israelites donated as an offering; what was offered became what was worshipped; what was worshipped became the criterion for who was destroyed (Exodus 32:25-28). The corrective distinction (offering-as-gratitude) was captured (offering-as-substance), hardened (calf-as-form), ritualized (worship-of-calf), and became selection-mechanism (Moses's purge).

Six full cross-tradition clusters per ADR-0085 §C cluster-counting articulate this trap-shape: Johar/Sufi (corrective-language-becomes-capture) + Sahely-Maturana (languaging-becomes-domination via C-12 recursive safeguard) + Post-Marxist critical theory (reification + symbolic violence; tightly genealogical lineage Marx-Lukacs-Frankfurt-Gramsci-Foucault-Bourdieu-Brown) + Postcolonial critique (colonized adopting colonizer's categories per Fanon; coloniality of power per Quijano-Mignolo) + State-legibility / bureaucratic-form critique (legibility-imposition destroying local knowledge per Scott; institutional inversion per Illich) + Galtung Norwegian-peace-research (direct/structural/cultural-violence triad operationally identical to substitution-trap shape at peace-civilization scale).

**Three substantive layer-instantiations** (per Sahely W4.2 manifesto + DECISION-BRIEF §17.3 (vi)):
- **Security Trap** (W4.2 C-9; security-policy layer) — security-as-full-spectrum-dominance permanent-emergency destroying the life-support systems it claims to protect. The corrective distinction (protect-the-population) is captured (population-as-threat-vector), hardened (permanent-emergency posture), ritualized (surveillance-and-clearance theater), and becomes selection-mechanism (who is "secure" is filtered through who performs fidelity to the security apparatus).
- **Growth Illusion** (W4.2 C-10; economic layer) — money-sequence multiplication blind to productive-vs-parasitic distinction. The corrective distinction (productive-investment-generates-life-support) is captured (any-money-sequence-multiplication-is-good), hardened (GDP-as-master-metric), ritualized (growth-imperative governance), and becomes selection-mechanism (institutions failing to multiply money sequences are filtered out, regardless of life-support generation).
- **Cancer Stage of Capitalism** (W4.2 C-5; McMurtry 1999 canonical; civilizational scale) — military-industrial money sequences multiplying at the expense of the life-support systems they inhabit. The corrective distinction (capital-as-life-supporting-coordination) is captured (capital-as-self-replicating-end-in-itself), hardened (financialization), ritualized (shareholder-value supremacy), and becomes civilizational selection-mechanism.

### Sacralized Trojan-Horse class — preservation-via-reverence

**Canonical shape**: sacralized-captured-distinction; the corrective distinction is preserved and revered specifically to prevent its critique.

Where Golden-Calf class names simple substitution (the captured distinction is worshipped AS the corrective function), Sacralized Trojan-Horse class names a subtler variant: the captured distinction is preserved AS sacred memory specifically so it cannot be examined, revised, or transcended. The trap-shape inverts: rather than substituting the captured form for the corrective function, the sacred preservation prevents the corrective function from operating at all.

Sahely W3.1 (toward-life-coherent-peace) articulates this via sacred-memory-transformation (C15 substrate per DECISION-BRIEF §17.2 (ii) + §17.8 (2)). Grievance-possession-as-sacred-memory traps cycles of trauma: the wound is preserved with reverence specifically because acknowledging its transformability would be experienced as betrayal of the sacred bond with those who suffered. The corrective distinction (remember-the-wound-to-prevent-recurrence) is captured (the-wound-as-identity-anchor), hardened (sacred-status), and ritualized (commemorative practices that prevent transcendence rather than enabling it).

Per §17.8 (2) operator ratification: C15 R-Sacred-memory-transformation FOLDS into this framing-note as Trojan-Horse-class trap subtype rather than receiving separate slug admission. Honest cluster-counting (≥3rd tradition would need to articulate sacred-memory-transformation operationally) does not yet support independent slug admission; the substrate-resonance with Golden-Calf class is substantive and earns canon-legible documentation at framing-note layer.

## Recursive-audit-method canon-method articulation

ADR-0085 admits `recursive-audit-method` as the canonical Spore-side anchor for the discipline of surfacing captured distinctions by applying a viability-grammar's own question to itself. Primary source Sahely W2.4 (recursive life-coherence audit method) + W1.1 C-12 ("A viability grammar becomes life-incoherent if it is used to dominate, accuse, or negate. Therefore, the grammar must apply its own question to itself").

**The method's canonical form** (per Sahely W1.1 C-12 + W2.4 substantive articulation):
1. Surface the viability-grammar's load-bearing distinctions (the corrective categorizations the grammar relies on to coordinate)
2. Apply each distinction's audit question to the distinction itself (e.g., if the grammar asks "does this practice support life-coherence?", ask "does THIS DISTINCTION'S use in our coordination support life-coherence?")
3. Surface any distinction that fails its own audit — the captured distinctions
4. Distinguish trap-shape (which variant per taxonomy above) + earning-test the distinction's continued canonical status (per parsimony-as-earning-test-outcome discipline)
5. Either rehabilitate the distinction (restore corrective function via scope-conditioning or narrative-extension) OR decline-with-trigger (acknowledge the captured distinction; document re-opening conditions; route through governance per F3 actor-governance discipline)

**Two full cross-tradition clusters support the method specifically** (per ADR-0085 §C honest-rigor reconciliation per Codex round 2 item 3):
- **Sahely-Maturana school**: W2.4 recursive-audit explicit articulation + W1.1 C-12 recursive-safeguard naming the self-applying discipline + adjacent Maturana / Verden-Zöller / Bunnell / Dávila Yáñez substrate
- **Johar/Sufi internal-recursive-audit**: existing ADR-0048 substrate via Johar's "the grammar must turn on itself" mandate per derivation primary-inspiration

Adjacent traditions (Post-Marxist + Postcolonial + State-legibility + Galtung) articulate trap-shapes substantively but do NOT name a reflexive method that the discipline turns on itself. This is the honest cluster-strength asymmetry within bundle: `golden-calf-trap` carries territory-wide 6-cluster substrate; `recursive-audit-method` carries 2-cluster method-specific substrate. Bundle-symmetric admission is honest because shared substrate (Sahely corpus) + shared verdict-shape (ADMIT-likely under ≥2-cluster derived-glossary threshold) + complementary operational roles (shape-the-method-surfaces + method-that-surfaces-shapes) hold without forcing cluster-count parity per ADR-0052 reciprocity-stronger-than-trust precedent + ADR-0084 route-graph-stronger-than-settlement-operator precedent.

### Composition with ADR-0001 pluriversal-incommensurability held-tension

Recursive-audit-method's "the grammar must apply its own question to itself" operates within Western-deliberative-rationality frame. Pluriversal traditions carry alternate recursive-audit shapes:

- **Indigenous ceremonial accountability** — recursive-audit operates through ceremony rather than deliberative reasoning; the grammar's audit-question is asked through ritual that includes more-than-human kin + ancestral consultation
- **Sufi muraqaba / muhasaba** — recursive-audit operates through contemplative practice rather than analytical articulation; the grammar's audit-question is asked through inner observation of the soul's relationship to the distinction
- **Buddhist vipassana / inquiry** — recursive-audit operates through meditative inquiry rather than logical reasoning; the grammar's audit-question dissolves into observation of the distinction's arising and passing
- **African kgotla / palaver tradition** — recursive-audit operates through extended communal deliberation rather than time-bounded review; the grammar's audit-question is asked across multiple generations of consensus

ADR-0001 held-tension UNCHANGED by ADR-0085 admission. Recursive-audit-method admitted at substrate-deepening layer per Johar/Sahely Western-deliberative-rationality lineage WITHOUT claiming pluriversal-resolution. Per ADR-0001 honest acknowledgment: alternate recursive-audit shapes are equally legitimate within their own ontological frames; Spore's admission of `recursive-audit-method` does not subsume or invalidate them. Future ADR may admit alternate-frame recursive-audit articulations if Spore federation operationally encounters them at canon-pressure layer.

## Folded substrate documentation

### C11 R-Civilization-extended-niche (framing-note-only per §17.4)

Sahely articulates civilization-as-extended-niche substrate-resonance: civilizations are extended ecological niches that organisms construct, maintain, and inhabit; civilizational-scale trap-shapes operate on this extended-niche substrate. The Cancer Stage layer-instantiation of Golden-Calf class composes with this substrate: when civilizational coordination distinctions become captured (e.g., financialization at civilizational scale), they destroy the extended-niche they were introduced to maintain (planetary life-support).

Substrate-resonance is substantive: extended-niche construction theory (Odling-Smee-Laland-Feldman 2003) + civilizational-collapse literature (Diamond / Tainter / Greer) + niche-construction-via-canalization-and-decanalization literature articulate civilization-as-extended-niche; Sahely's recursive-audit-method applied at civilizational scale = niche-audit-method.

Not admitted as separate slug per parsimony-elegance dual discipline: civilization-as-extended-niche substrate-resonance is substantive but the slug-admission pressure does not exceed Golden-Calf class layer-instantiation (Cancer Stage carries the civilizational-scale articulation honestly without requiring separate civilizational-substrate slug).

### C14 R-Externalization-as-deferred-collapse (framing-note-only folded into C1 per §17.4)

Sahely W4.1 articulates externalization-as-deferred-collapse at economic layer: money-sequence multiplication blind to productive-vs-parasitic distinction externalizes the parasitic component to environment + future generations + colonized populations. The externalization defers collapse temporally (future-generations) + spatially (colonized-populations) + ontologically (environment-as-externality), but the deferred collapse compounds.

This composes with Golden-Calf class as Growth Illusion layer-instantiation (per W4.2 C-10): externalization-as-deferred-collapse is the operational mechanism by which Growth Illusion captures coordination — the corrective distinction (productive-investment-generates-life-support) is captured (any-money-sequence-multiplication-is-good) because the parasitic component is externalized off-balance-sheet.

Substrate-resonance with ecological economics literature (Daly / Costanza / Røpke) + post-growth literature (Hickel / Raworth / Jackson) + ecological-imperialism literature (Crosby / Hornborg) substantive but folded into Golden-Calf class economic layer-instantiation rather than admitted as separate slug per parsimony.

## 4. Substrate-resonance map (descriptive)

Read against post-Bundle-α canon (14 foundation docs / 9 primitives / **4** cross-cutting doctrines / concepts-yaml v24 / 40 canon-decisions through ADR-0090). Descriptive observations, not admission proposals.

### 4.1 Perception-as-power / projection (ADR-0090 on F4) — load-bearing

Galtung's entire diagnostic is a perception-as-power argument: a collective's *psychogram* — what it makes visible (its myths, its chosenness) and what it makes invisible (its own violence, repressed and projected onto the Other via the RP-complex of C3) — governs its conduct in the world. C6 projection-as-stabilizer is the operational mechanism: the Self stabilizes its self-image by relocating its violence onto an external enemy, which then *becomes visible* as the threat that justifies the Self's force. This is exactly the operational concern ADR-0090 scope-conditioned onto F4 representation-authority: **what becomes representable / authoritative is itself an exercise of power**, and standpoint determines what enters the record as legitimate fact. Galtung's structural-violence lineage is one of the substrate clusters perception-as-power names. No canon-shift: ADR-0090 already carries this; Galtung is independent-tradition (peace studies) reinforcement of a resolved scope-condition.

### 4.2 Mythic-trap and grammar-used-to-dominate (ADR-0085 trap-shape vocabulary)

C1's myths (Manifest Destiny; the "Trinity" of Judeo-Christianity / Free Markets / Democracy) and C2's DMA syndrome (dichotomy → Manicheism → Armageddon) are textbook specimens of two ADR-0085 trap shapes: a **mythic-trap** (a glamorizing narrative substituting for honest self-account — the `golden-calf-trap` is the SHAPE-OF ADR-0048's substitution-trap, the worshipped-image standing in for the living thing) and **grammar-used-to-dominate** (Us-vs-Them carved into "watertight parts" then moralized Good-vs-Evil, a coordination grammar weaponized for domination rather than coordination). The DMA "Armageddon" limb — that tension *must* resolve through final violent confrontation — is the grammar's domination-mode made explicit. No new trap-shape is surfaced; ADR-0085's vocabulary already covers these.

### 4.3 The 21-archetype psychogram as a recursive-audit specimen (ADR-0085)

C3's "code / patriotic program" of 21 archetypes is, structurally, a collective's self-conserving self-narration — precisely the object ADR-0085's `recursive-audit-method` is designed to interrogate (turning the grammar's own question back on its user: *what manner of living does this self-account conserve?*). Galtung's monograph is, in effect, an external recursive audit of a polity that cannot perform the audit on itself (the RP-complex represses exactly the material the audit would surface). Descriptive resonance only; this note imports no method.

### 4.4 Isomorphism as relationally-constituted (pathological) identity (ADR-0051)

C5's isomorphism — "recognition of the Self in others", the U.S. identifying with Boers and Zionists as fellow "covenanted nations" — is identity constituted *relationally*, through recognition rather than as an atomic given. This is the same anti-atomic axis ADR-0051 admits (`relational-identity` as a Holon property). Galtung's specimen shows the axis operating in a pathological/projective register: relational self-recognition used to license shared violence against a constructed "pagan/inferior" Other. The axis is canonical; this is an instance of its misuse, not a canon-shift.

### 4.5 War Participation Index as persistence-is-not-legitimacy (ADR-0042 structural-legitimacy)

C4's WPI quantifies durable structural pattern: a polity averaging more than one military use abroad per year since 1798, with rising frequency. That a pattern persists — even ranks first in the world — confers no legitimacy. This is the civilizational-scale instance of what `structural-legitimacy` (ADR-0042) does at federation-governance scale: persistence and legitimacy are separable, and a long-conserved arrangement can be structurally illegitimate. Descriptive cross-scale parallel; no canon-shift.

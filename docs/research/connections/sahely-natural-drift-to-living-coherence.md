## 2. C-claims (verbatim from extraction record)

All quotes verbatim from the extraction record's 7-fact moderate-selective extraction. Anchors carry the RID above and `pdf-p<N>`.

- **C1** [anchor: §Core thesis · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-05-…evo-devo · pdf-p4] **LOAD-BEARING — reproduction-continuity bridge:** "Evolution is the historical conservation, transformation, and diversification of viable organism–niche relations through reproduction, development, behavior, plasticity, niche construction, symbiosis, inclusive inheritance, and population-level genetic sorting." Maturana provides "the evolutionary unit of meaning: the conserved way of living"; contemporary evolutionary biology provides the mechanisms.

- **C2** [anchor: §Three-synthesis integration · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-05-…evo-devo · pdf-p4 + pdf-p27] Three-synthesis integration: Modern Synthesis (allele-frequency change, selection, drift, mutation, recombination, gene flow, quantitative traits, pleiotropy, epistasis) + Extended Evolutionary Synthesis (evo-devo, plasticity, niche construction, inclusive inheritance, reciprocal causation) + Maturana–Mpodozis natural drift (organism-niche-relations as evolutionary unit). "Each addresses a different layer; together they form complete evolutionary biology."

- **C3** [anchor: §Natural drift as grammar · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-05-…evo-devo · pdf-p7 + pdf-p27] **LOAD-BEARING — conserved-through-reproduction question:** "Maturana and Mpodozis's theory of natural drift remains deeply relevant because it asks the right biological question: what is conserved through reproduction? Their answer — the ontogenic phenotype–ontogenic niche relation — provides a relational grammar for integrating recent developments in evolutionary biology."

- **C4** [anchor: §Eco-evo-devo bridge · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-05-…evo-devo · pdf-p11 + pdf-p15] Eco-Evo-Devo (Gilbert et al. 2015) brings development + symbiosis + plasticity into evolutionary explanation; "Developmental plasticity and genetic accommodation" + "Niche construction and reciprocal causation" named as central EES contributions.

- **C5** [anchor: §Holobionts · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-05-…evo-devo · pdf-p17] **LOAD-BEARING — multi-species individuality:** Symbiosis and holobiont theory holds that "organismic individuality can be multi-species" — the explicitly evolutionary version of the holonic-relational unity claim.

- **C6** [anchor: §Inclusive inheritance · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-05-…evo-devo · pdf-p19] **LOAD-BEARING — honest-rigor caution:** "Inclusive inheritance broadens inheritance but must be used cautiously." Extends inheritance beyond DNA (epigenetic, parental, ecological, cultural) while resisting over-extension.

- **C7** [anchor: §Population genetics · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-05-…evo-devo · pdf-p27] "Population genetics remains essential, especially as contemporary work reveals complex genotype–phenotype relations" (cites Mackay & Anholt 2024 on pleiotropy + epistasis). "Synthesis preserves rather than displaces population genetics" — additive-not-replacement discipline.

## 4. Substrate-resonance map (descriptive)

Read against Spore's post-Bundle-α canon (14 foundation docs / 9 primitives / 4 cross-cutting doctrines / 2 modes / 2 properties / derived glossary slugs; concepts-yaml v24; 40 canon-decisions through ADR-0090). Descriptive observations, not admission proposals.

### 4.1 "What is conserved through reproduction" ↔ reproduction-continuity (ADR-0049)

C3's central question — *what is conserved through reproduction?* — and C1's naming of reproduction as the first-listed mechanism of the conservation of "viable organism–niche relations" sit in the same canon-layer slot as Spore's `reproduction-continuity` primitive (ADR-0049: the coordination verb operating at cross-episode time-scale, below the within-episode intent→commitment→evidence→signal loop). Both name reproduction as a **foundational conserving layer**, not a derivative of within-episode mechanism. This is the resonance the extraction's R1 already flags, and it is the strongest in the paper.

**Lossy bits:** ADR-0049's `reproduction-continuity` was admitted from a Beer/VSM viability-over-time + governance-succession + pluriversal-cyclical + structured-disagreement-replication cluster — coordination labor that conserves the *verb-loop, field-substrate, and commitment-patterns* across actor turnover. Sahely's "conserved through reproduction" is biological-evolutionary (ontogenic phenotype–ontogenic niche relation). The two converge on *reproduction-as-conserving-layer* from genuinely different substrates; the convergence is cross-tradition reinforcement, not new pressure. Per single-source rigor, one Maturanan source does not move the cluster count.

### 4.2 Holobionts + organism-niche unit ↔ Holon-irreducibility (ADR-0050) + Membrane-as-self-produced (ADR-0062)

C5's holobiont claim that "organismic individuality can be multi-species" is the explicitly evolutionary version of the holonic-relational unity claim. It sits on the same anti-reductionist axis as ADR-0050 Holon-irreducibility (the whole is not reducible to the sum of its parts). C1's inclusion of *niche construction* and the EES *reciprocal causation* theme (C4) — organism actively co-constituting its medium rather than passively inhabiting an exogenous environment — is structural-coupling language that ADR-0062 Membrane-as-self-produced already admits as a co-scope (Membrane constituted by the ongoing operations of the living system itself). No canon-shift needed; both ADRs already admit the substrate Sahely imports from evolutionary biology.

### 4.3 Conservation-through-reproduction ↔ rule-in-use (ADR-0046)

C1+C3's framing of evolution as *conservation* of a viable way of living — identity persisting through transformation of its parts — parallels at substrate-philosophy layer the ADR-0046 distinction between `rule-in-use` (what is operationally conserved through the flow of action-situations) and rule-in-form (static-text declaration). Both locate identity in *operational conservation*, not in fixed form. Cross-tradition convergence (Maturanan evolutionary biology + Ostrom institutional economics); framing-note value only.

### 4.4 Natural drift ↔ structural-legitimacy persistence≠legitimacy (ADR-0042)

Maturana–Mpodozis natural drift (C2+C3) reframes evolution away from optimization-by-selection toward *conservation of whatever way of living is viable enough to persist* — a lineage persists without that persistence implying optimality or selection-for-fitness in the adaptationist sense. This rhymes with Spore's `structural-legitimacy` discipline (ADR-0042): a federation arrangement may operationally persist while failing the legitimacy test that would make its persistence meaningful. The shared shape is *persistence is not the same as the normative property one might read into it*. Reinforcing only; the domains differ (biological lineage-persistence vs federation-governance legitimacy).

### 4.5 Inclusive-inheritance caution ↔ honest-rigor boundary-conditions discipline

C6 ("inclusive inheritance … must be used cautiously") and C7 ("synthesis preserves rather than displaces population genetics") encode an additive-not-replacement, resist-over-extension discipline at the methodological layer. This is the same posture Spore canon practices as honest-rigor cluster-counting and parsimony-as-earning-test-outcome (the disposition that declined to over-extend at ADRs 0054/0069 and held parsimony at ADR-0048). It is a methodological rhyme worth noting descriptively; it is not canon-pressure, and `maintenance-economics` is named in `relates_to:` only because reproduction/maintenance is the nearest Spore territory to "what conserves a lineage's viability over time," not because the paper engages maintenance-economics directly.

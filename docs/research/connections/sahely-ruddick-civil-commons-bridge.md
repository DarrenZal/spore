## §2 Three-layer composition map (descriptive)

### §2.1 Foundation-doctrine layer (McMurtry-primary; cited via Sahely §9)

McMurtry's Civil Commons articulation, as cited verbatim by Sahely (2026-01-10) §9 (pdf-p19), is canonical:

> "He refers to these as the **civil commons**: socially constructed systems that enable universal access to life goods and cannot function if subordinated to private profit extraction. These include: public water systems, sanitation and waste management, public health infrastructure, education systems, legal and institutional frameworks that secure rights and trust, environmental protections that preserve shared life conditions. Orthodox economics typically treats these as 'public expenditures' or 'externalities.' McMurtry shows that this framing is inverted. Markets and private enterprise **presuppose** the civil commons. They cannot exist without stable ecosystems, educated populations, healthy workers, enforceable contracts, and social trust. Yet orthodox models treat these enabling conditions as peripheral rather than foundational. In a life-coherent economics, the civil commons are recognized as **primary economic infrastructure** — the base layer upon which all market activity depends. Their erosion is therefore not a fiscal choice among others; it is a **direct contraction of life capital**." `[Sahely 2026-01-10 §9 pdf-p19, citing McMurtry]`

Three structural moves are load-bearing for the cross-repo composition:

1. **Civil Commons as primary economic infrastructure** (not externality): the base layer upon which all market activity depends. McMurtry-primary; spans *Unequal Freedoms* (1998), *Value Wars: The Global Market versus the Life Economy* (2002), *Philosophy and World Problems* (UNESCO Encyclopedia of Life Support Systems, 2009), *The Cancer Stage of Capitalism* (2nd ed. 2013).

2. **Life Capital definition** (Sahely §8 pdf-p18, McMurtry verbatim attribution):

> "He defines **Life Capital** as: **The wealth of means of life and life goods that produce more without loss in cumulative yield through time.** This definition introduces a **temporal regeneration criterion** absent from orthodox theory. … What unites these is not ownership form but **regenerative function**." `[Sahely 2026-01-10 §8 pdf-p18, citing McMurtry]`

3. **Life-coherence constraint** (Sahely §5 pdf-p11, McMurtry verbatim attribution):

> "McMurtry's contribution is to identify the **life-coherence constraint** that economics has left implicit and therefore violated with impunity. An economy that undermines breathable air, potable water, ecological stability, public health, education, social inclusion, or meaningful work is not merely unjust. It is **malfunctioning as an economy**, because it is degrading the conditions of its own reproduction." `[Sahely 2026-01-10 §5 pdf-p11, citing McMurtry]`

These three moves jointly establish the foundation-doctrine layer: Civil Commons are not optional public expenditures; they are the regenerative substrate without which market activity cannot reproduce itself. Erosion of the Civil Commons is operationally identical to contraction of Life Capital, which violates the life-coherence constraint and degrades the conditions of economic reproduction.

## §3 Substrate-coherence between layers (descriptive)

### §3.1 Civil-Commons-as-primary-infrastructure ↔ commitment-pool-as-CVLE-stewardship

McMurtry's Civil Commons (foundation-doctrine layer) names **socially constructed systems that enable universal access to life goods**. Ruddick's commitment pool with its CVLE four-function decomposition (protocol layer) names **the operational mechanism** by which a community could steward exactly such systems: Registry (R) curates which commitments count as life-good-bearing; Value Index (V) values them in life-coherent terms (not money-proxy terms); Limiter (L) prevents extractive over-issuance that would erode the regenerative substrate; Fee Policy + Vault-Settlement (F) governs exchange consistent with life-capital regeneration; Governance (G) authorizes stewardship parameters.

The coherence is not metaphorical. It is operational at three points:

1. **Stewardship is governance, not market**: McMurtry's framing that Civil Commons "cannot function if subordinated to private profit extraction" maps exactly onto Ruddick's framing that Governance G is "stewardship environment, not a fifth protocol function" (C5 pdf-p9). Both insist that the governance layer is *constitutive of* not *adjacent to* the protocol's life-coherent operation.

2. **CVLE is the missing operational decomposition for what McMurtry names as foundation-doctrine**: Civil Commons (water systems, public health, education, legal infrastructure) require precisely curation (who counts as eligible recipient + what counts as life-good), valuation (what is the life-coherent unit), limitation (how to prevent extractive depletion), and exchange (how to coordinate across multiple pools/bioregions). McMurtry articulates the requirement; Ruddick formalizes the operational decomposition.

3. **Route-graphs realize multi-bioregion Civil Commons federation**: Ruddick's `G_P = (𝒫, E)` (route-graph) provides the formal machinery for cross-pool admission — exactly the protocol substrate required to enable multi-bioregion stewardship of shared Civil Commons (e.g., transboundary water systems, shared ecological protections, cross-jurisdictional legal infrastructure). BKC's Tier-2 Pool Federation ("multi-hop credit routing across pools via trust links") operationalizes this at bioregional layer.

The three-layer composition is **substrate-coherent**: McMurtry articulates the *what and why* (Civil Commons as life-capital regenerative infrastructure), Ruddick formalizes the *how at protocol layer* (CVLE four-function decomposition + route-graph multi-pool federation), and BKC operationalizes the *how at production layer* (TBFFSettler + SwapPool + Celo on-chain deployment).

### §3.2 Life Capital ↔ memory-based ROLA witness (Ruddick Appendix B)

McMurtry's Life Capital definition (foundation-doctrine layer) — "wealth of means of life and life goods that produce more without loss in cumulative yield through time" with regenerative-function-as-unifier — engages a particularly important coherence with Ruddick's memory-based ROLA witness (Appendix B pdf-p35-40, validated by Spore against ADR-0078 min-viable-spore-instance §"Minimum Viable Composition").

Three points of coherence:

1. **Regenerative-function-not-ownership-form**: McMurtry's "what unites these is not ownership form but regenerative function" (Sahely §8 pdf-p18). Ruddick's memory-based ROLA witness demonstrates that state-representation-form is similarly secondary: "recognized group memory + witness testimony + beneficiary acknowledgment + steward attestation may constitute relevant state and evidence variables, provided membership/contribution/obligation/dispute/remedy effects are observable at the chosen abstraction level" (C14 pdf-p23-24). Both insist that *function* (regenerative-yield-through-time / observable-evidence-of-stewardship) is canonically authoritative, not *form* (ownership-instrument / ledger-instrument).

2. **Temporal-regeneration criterion ↔ horizon-bounded observable preservation**: McMurtry's "temporal regeneration criterion absent from orthodox theory" maps onto Ruddick's bounded-horizon `≡_H` observational equivalence (Def 8 pdf-p12) at the meta-level: both require evaluation across time at a *macro-step boundary*, not at instantaneous-state-snapshot level. Life Capital is evaluated by cumulative yield through time; ROLA realization is evaluated by observable preservation at horizon H.

3. **Steward role**: McMurtry's framing implicitly requires stewards who maintain the regenerative substrate; Ruddick's stewarded-pool concept + Appendix B explicit `gsteward(e, s) : e ∈ E ∧ e has authority under G` formalizes the role with explicit authority constraints. F3 actor-governance (ADR-0077) maps onto this stewardship role via §4.1 admission/role-assignment + §4.7 governance-body-composition (per `spore.connection.ruddick-2026-commitment-pool-route-graphs` R5).

### §3.3 Selection-mechanism diagnostic ↔ realization-falsification criterion

McMurtry's selection-mechanism diagnostic (Sahely §11 pdf-p20, McMurtry-substrate):

> "By defining capital in monetary terms, defining efficiency as profit, defining development as transaction volume, the system **selects for activities that convert life capital into money claims**. This is not a moral accusation; it is a selection mechanism. Firms, investors, and institutions that extract value fastest from life systems outperform those that regenerate them — until the underlying systems fail. McMurtry's framework reveals that what appears as market success can be **systemic self-consumption**." `[Sahely 2026-01-10 §11 pdf-p20, citing McMurtry]`

This is structurally homologous to Ruddick's operational falsification criterion for realization (C12 pdf-p17):

> "A proposed in-scope mechanism falsifies direct-realization if any enabled source transition cannot be factorized into U, or required state variable cannot be represented through (R,V,L,F,H,G), or Σ cannot be operationalized through custody/payout/route/receipt logic, or priority/loss observables differ after projection, or decisive governance/repair step depends on discretion that cannot be represented as explicit state transition." `[Ruddick 2026 §3.5 pdf-p17]`

The coherence at structural level: both name **systemic-failure-by-mismatch-between-declared-purpose-and-operational-mechanism**. McMurtry diagnoses orthodox-economics as selecting for life-capital-conversion-into-money-claims rather than life-capital-regeneration; Ruddick formalizes when a proposed pool architecture fails to realize the institution it claims to instantiate. Both criteria are operational, not moral — they describe what the system does, not whether it ought to do otherwise.

This composition has implications for Spore canon at the F8 external-validation-loop layer (ADR-0081 5-category external-witness doctrine): McMurtry-via-Sahely operates as external-witness producing diagnostic-of-system-pathology; Ruddick operates as external-witness producing operational-falsification-criteria-for-proposed-realizations. Both surface as Spore-canon-receiving-feedback per F8 §4.1+§4.2 categories. (No admission-pressure; descriptive composition only.)

### §3.4 Regenerative-function-as-unifier ↔ stewarded-pool governance-G

McMurtry's regenerative-function-as-unifier framing ("what unites these is not ownership form but regenerative function") is the foundation-doctrine claim that Civil Commons share *operational character* (regenerative yield through time without depletion) across heterogeneous institutional forms (ecological systems, public health, education, social trust, knowledge systems).

Ruddick's stewarded-pool concept with Governance G as parameter-authorization environment provides the protocol-layer machinery to *enforce* regenerative-function as operational unifier across heterogeneous commitment forms. The pool's Limiter (L) prevents over-issuance that would erode regenerative substrate; the Value Index (V) can value commitments in life-coherent rather than money-proxy terms; the Registry (R) can curate eligibility by regenerative-function-criteria; Governance G authorizes parameter changes that maintain the regenerative-function-criterion across time.

BKC's TBFF (Threshold-Based Flow Funding) operationalizes this at production layer: threshold bands explicitly named as "pool safety limits" preventing extractive over-flow; CLC netting yield as proxy for reduced extractive metabolic dependency. The composition maps cleanly: McMurtry foundation-doctrine → Ruddick protocol substrate → BKC production threshold bands.

## 4. Substrate-resonance map (descriptive)

Thin substrate; the map is gestural and read off C1–C4 only.

- **"Reality and Language" → representation-authority (F4) + perception-as-power (ADR-0090).**
  Mitchell's title names the same concern F4 governs: which representation is *authoritative*
  over reality. The lecture's charge is that mainstream macro mistakes its *language* (models,
  aggregates) for *reality*. Spore's resolved posture is that representation does not
  automatically inherit authority over the fact it describes (fact-vs-specification
  distinction, ADR-0074/F4), and that *what a frame makes visible is itself a form of power*
  (perception-as-power scope-condition, ADR-0090). This is the strongest resonance in the
  post — but it is a one-sentence gesture, not an argument.
- **"fake knowledge" → structural-legitimacy (ADR-0042).** A frame that is dominant
  ("mainstream") is not thereby legitimate. Spore's persistence ≠ legitimacy discipline
  (structural-legitimacy) is the canonical home for "this is entrenched but unearned".
- **"fake knowledge" as self-displacing metric → golden-calf-trap (ADR-0085).** An economics
  that treats its own abstractions as the thing-itself has the *shape of* the golden-calf
  trap (a symbol displacing the reality it was meant to track). This is a **shape-of**
  mapping only — explicitly NOT an equivalence to ADR-0048 `substitution-trap` (per the
  trap-shape vocab discipline, ADR-0085).
- **"TOWARD LIFE-KNOWLEDGE" → life-value-doctrine (ADR-0086).** Sahely's category framing
  positions an economics critique as a step toward *life-grounded* knowledge — the gestural
  seed of what becomes, in his later AI-era papers, the McMurtry life-value through-line.
  Cite the doctrine (4th cross-cutting doctrine), not a slug. Here it is a category label,
  nothing more.

## 2. C-claims (verbatim from extraction record)

All quotes verbatim from extraction record §"Verbatim key claims" (pp 7-12). RID `orn:source:bsahely-2026-02-07-learning-to-read-what-keeps-us-alive`.

- **C1** [anchor: §Abstract · orn:source:bsahely-2026-02-07-learning-to-read-what-keeps-us-alive · pdf-p7] Viability-illiteracy thesis: "This white paper proposes that a central driver of today's metacrisis is **viability illiteracy** — a widespread inability to recognize, name, and protect the life-conditions upon which all social, economic, and institutional systems depend."

- **C2** [anchor: §Abstract · orn:source:bsahely-2026-02-07-learning-to-read-what-keeps-us-alive · pdf-p7] Reframe to loss-of-orientation: "Rather than attributing current failures to moral decline, technological insufficiency, or ideological conflict, the paper reframes the crisis as a loss of orientation: signals have drifted away from the realities they are meant to represent."

- **C3** [anchor: §Abstract · orn:source:bsahely-2026-02-07-learning-to-read-what-keeps-us-alive · pdf-p7] Universal grammar of viability: "the paper introduces a **universal grammar of viability**: a simple, humane framework that reconnects life-requirements, life-support systems, and the measurements that guide decision-making. Emphasis is placed on coherence, capacity, continuity, and care, rather than speed, scale, or abstract growth."

- **C4** [anchor: §Executive Summary · orn:source:bsahely-2026-02-07-learning-to-read-what-keeps-us-alive · pdf-p8] **LOAD-BEARING — life-ground definition:** "The paper introduces the concept of the **life-ground**: the universal conditions every human life requires — including clean air and water, nutritious food, care, learning, safety, time for rest and connection, and a living environment. These necessities are not ideological preferences or commodities; they are preconditions for any viable society."

- **C5** [anchor: §Executive Summary · orn:source:bsahely-2026-02-07-learning-to-read-what-keeps-us-alive · pdf-p8] **LOAD-BEARING — three-question diagnostic:** "the paper outlines a universal grammar of viability, organized around three simple questions that can be applied at any scale: 1. What does life actually require here to function and flourish? 2. What systems are meant to provide those conditions? 3. What are we measuring or rewarding instead?"

- **C6** [anchor: §Executive Summary · orn:source:bsahely-2026-02-07-learning-to-read-what-keeps-us-alive · pdf-p8] Repair-as-ongoing-re-anchoring: "Repair is framed not as total overhaul, but as ongoing re-anchoring — protecting capacity, preserving margins, and preventing unnecessary harm."

- **C7** [anchor: §Executive Summary · orn:source:bsahely-2026-02-07-learning-to-read-what-keeps-us-alive · pdf-p8] Fidelity-not-domination measure: "The ultimate measure of success is not domination of outcomes, but fidelity to life-conditions in an uncertain world."

- **C8** [anchor: §0.2 Why This Paper Exists · orn:source:bsahely-2026-02-07-learning-to-read-what-keeps-us-alive · pdf-p10] Literacy-not-moral problem: "This gap is not primarily moral. It is not the result of bad intentions or collective failure of character. It is a **literacy problem**. ... viability illiteracy can lead societies to optimize for signals that slowly erode the foundations they depend on."

- **C9** [anchor: §0.2 Why This Paper Exists · orn:source:bsahely-2026-02-07-learning-to-read-what-keeps-us-alive · pdf-p10] Three modest-and-serious purposes: "to restore attention to the basic conditions that allow human life to function and flourish, to help distinguish between symbols and realities, and to offer a common set of questions that can be asked in homes, workplaces, institutions, and communities alike."

- **C10** [anchor: §1.1 Why This Feeling Is Hard to Name · orn:source:bsahely-2026-02-07-learning-to-read-what-keeps-us-alive · pdf-p11] **LOAD-BEARING — systemic-misalignment-felt-at-human-scale:** "What people are sensing is not merely personal strain. It is **systemic misalignment felt at a human scale**. We are exquisitely sensitive beings, evolved to notice when the conditions that support life are becoming unstable. Long before collapse becomes visible in headlines or data, it is felt as: tension without a clear source, fatigue that rest alone does not resolve, difficulty trusting long-term promises, a subtle grief for futures imagined but no longer assumed."

- **C11** [anchor: §1.2 When Feeling Is Ahead of Language · orn:source:bsahely-2026-02-07-learning-to-read-what-keeps-us-alive · pdf-p12] Felt-dissonance as early recognition: "Sometimes what we are feeling is not confusion — it is **early recognition**. Recognition of: systems moving faster than the conditions they depend on, decisions optimized for short horizons colliding with long-lived consequences, signals of success drifting away from lived well-being. These perceptions do not arrive as neat conclusions. They arrive as **felt dissonance**."

- **C12** [anchor: §1.3 The Cost of Dismissing This Feeling · orn:source:bsahely-2026-02-07-learning-to-read-what-keeps-us-alive · pdf-p12] Diagnostic-not-pathologizing stance: "When this quiet feeling is dismissed or pathologized ... None of these responses are moral failures. They are coping strategies in the absence of shared meaning. But they come at a cost."

## 4. Substrate-resonance map (descriptive)

Read against Spore's post-Bundle-α canon (14 foundation docs / 9 primitives / **4 cross-cutting doctrines** / concepts-yaml v24 / 40 canon-decisions). Descriptive only.

### 4.1 Life-ground ↔ life-value-doctrine (ADR-0086) + civil-commons (ADR-0087)

C-4's **life-ground** — "the universal conditions every human life requires ... preconditions for any viable society" — is the McMurtry life-ground / universal-human-life-necessities framing that Spore admitted in Bundle α as the **4th cross-cutting doctrine `life-value-doctrine`** (ADR-0086, sibling to `reproductive-commoning` / `boundary-commoning` / `care-commoning`). The extraction record's §"Cited authors" explicitly names "John McMurtry 'civil commons' + 'life-ground' + 'life capital'" as the implicit uncited substrate — the same McMurtry lineage that grounded ADR-0086 and the `civil-commons` derived-glossary slug (ADR-0087, alias `civil-commons-substrate`). This is a **general-audience-register** restatement of substrate Spore canon already carries; no canon-pressure. Note the canon distinction the consistency map preserves: `life-value-doctrine` is a **cross-cutting doctrine** (the 4th), while `civil-commons` is a **derived-glossary slug** — not co-equal canon objects.

### 4.2 Signals-drift-from-reality ↔ perception-as-power (ADR-0090) + representation-authority (F4)

C-2 ("signals have drifted away from the realities they are meant to represent") and C-11 ("signals of success drifting away from lived well-being") name the gap between the representation and the represented. ADR-0090 scope-conditioned **perception-as-power** onto F4 representation-authority — the concern that *what becomes visible / measurable* shapes *what gets governed*. C-5 Q3 ("what are we measuring or rewarding instead?") and C-8 ("optimize for signals that slowly erode the foundations") are diagnostic-register articulations of that same representation-authority concern. Reinforcing; no canon-shift.

### 4.3 Preserving-margins ↔ margin-as-reserve (ADR-0089)

C-6 frames repair as "ongoing re-anchoring — protecting capacity, **preserving margins**, and preventing unnecessary harm." ADR-0089 scope-conditioned **margin-as-reserve** onto F9 maintenance-economics (reserve/slack/resilience as a maintained condition, not a static buffer). Sahely's "preserving margins / protecting capacity" sits squarely in that scope-condition's operational concern. Reinforcing; no canon-shift.

### 4.4 Felt-misalignment-as-sensor ↔ sensor-oracle-governance (F1) + Membrane sensing

C-10's claim that humans are "exquisitely sensitive beings, evolved to notice when the conditions that support life are becoming unstable" — felt *before* collapse "becomes visible in headlines or data" — treats felt-experience as a primary viability-sensor that runs ahead of formal instrumentation. This resonates with Spore's F1 sensor-oracle-governance (how sensed signals enter governance) and the Membrane-as-sensing framing in project-vision. Descriptive resonance only; Spore's sensor governance is about machine/human/AI-attestation sensors, not affective-felt-dissonance specifically.

### 4.5 Fidelity-not-domination + erosion-of-foundations ↔ structural-legitimacy (ADR-0042) + golden-calf-trap (ADR-0085)

C-7 ("the ultimate measure of success is not domination of outcomes, but fidelity to life-conditions") and C-8 (societies "optimize for signals that slowly erode the foundations they depend on") share canon-work with two resolved objects: (a) **structural-legitimacy** (F structural-legitimacy / ADR-0042) — operational persistence/optimization is not the same as legitimate viability; and (b) the **golden-calf-trap** (ADR-0085) — worshipping the proxy/metric over the life-condition it was meant to track. C-5 Q3 ("measuring or rewarding instead") is precisely the metric-substitution *shape*; per the consistency map, `golden-calf-trap` is the SHAPE-OF ADR-0048 `substitution-trap`, **not equivalent-to** it. Descriptive only.

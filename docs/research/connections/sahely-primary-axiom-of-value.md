## 2. C-claims (verbatim, anchored)

Drawn from the extraction record (claims attach to McMurtry except C-1, Sahely's endorsement frame).

- **C-1** [html-section:body] *"I endorse, outline and apply John McMurtry's life-value onto-axiology, which is in all probability the most articulate theory of value developed by any philosopher in the 21st century."*
- **C-2** [html-section:The Primary Axiom of All Value] *"X is value if and only if, and to the extent that, x consists in or enables a more coherently inclusive range of thought/feeling/action than without it."*
- **C-3** [html-section:The Primary Axiom of All Value] *"Conversely: x is disvalue if and only if, and to the extent that, x reduces/disables/destroys any range of thought/experience/action."*
- **C-4** [html-section:Definitions of the three ultimate fields of value] *"the true = progressive consistency with the P-Axiom, or the life coherence principle"*
- **C-5** [html-section:Principle 1] *"The ultimate organising principle of any life-coherent society or economy through generational time is secure access to means of life/life goods otherwise in short supply …"*
- **C-6** [html-section:Principle 2] *"A means of life is a means of life if and only if it enables life capacities/abilities not possible without it (eg., food, water, shelter and literacy education)."*
- **C-7** [html-section:Principle 3 Converse] *"Any priced commodity which does not directly or indirectly provide means of life/life goods for these needs is uneconomic, and anti-economic to the extent of life resources wasted on the commodity's production and consumption (eg., non-growth addictive commodities)."*
- **C-8** [html-section:Principle 4 Converse] *"Willingness or ability to pay prices for commodities does not measure their life requirement, but increasingly the opposite (e.g., fast-foods, leisure motor vehicles)"*
- **C-9** [html-section:Principle 5] *"The true measure of the overall performance of any society or economy is its civil commons development or the full access of its members to these life goods …"*
- **C-10** [html-section:Principle 5 Converse] *"Growth of aggregate commodities sold in a society (GDP) is never an accurate or reliable measure of a society or economy's development unless it is made to correspond to the access of its members to life goods as defined …"*
- **C-11** [html-section:Principle 6] *"The primary capital of any society or economy is Life Capital (LC→LC1→LC n) = the wealth of means of life/life goods that produce more without loss in cumulative yield through time …"*
- **C-12** [html-section:Principle 7] *"The efficiency of any product, tool or process increases, and only increases, to the extent that … i … Ecological Efficiency … ii … Physical Input-Output Efficiency … iii … Human Development Efficiency."*

## 4. Substrate-resonance map (descriptive)

- **life-value-doctrine (ADR-0086, F9-adjacent).** This 2018 post is arguably the cleanest single-source statement of the McMurtry axiom that grounds Spore's 4th cross-cutting doctrine: value = "a more coherently inclusive range of thought/feeling/action" (C-2). The doctrine, the three fields (T/F/A), the life-coherence principle (C-4), and the nine validity-grounds (self-evidence … sovereignty … measurability) are all present here in foundation-era form.
- **civil-commons (ADR-0087).** Principle 5 (C-9) is the canonical origin of the *civil commons development* substrate-noun — "the full access of its members to these life goods (including the work share required to provide them)." Note the canon-object-class discipline: `civil-commons` is a **derived-glossary slug** (one operational substrate the doctrine operates over), NOT the doctrine itself.
- **maintenance-economics / reproductive-commoning (F9, ADR-0086).** Life Capital (C-11) — "wealth of means of life/life goods that produce more without loss in cumulative yield through time … species/ecological, social, and knowledge capital which reproduce and grow" — is reproductive/maintenance-economics substrate; the "carcinogenic self-multiplication" of life-blind money growth predating life capital is the diagnosis the F9 doctrine sits against.
- **care-commoning (ADR-0088 scope-condition).** Principle 3's *caring means of life* ("intimate love, social inclusion, safety and healthcare") is care-cluster substrate, now scope-conditioned by ADR-0088.
- **golden-calf-trap / recursive-audit-method (ADR-0085).** The Converses (C-7/C-8) — priced commodities that disable life-capacities are anti-economic; willingness-to-pay is the *opposite* of a life-requirement measure — name the symbol-displaces-reality trap-shape (`golden-calf-trap`, shape-of `substitution-trap`); the widen-or-narrow axiom is a diagnostic instance of `recursive-audit-method`.
- **structural-legitimacy (ADR-0042).** The axiom's insistence that *enabling more range* is value (not persistence, not market success) reinforces the persistence≠legitimacy discipline.

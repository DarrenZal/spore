## 2. C-claims (verbatim from extraction record)

All quotes verbatim from the 9-fact hand-curated extraction record. RID `orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-05-from-biology-of-love-to-life-coherent-governance-maturanan-galtungian-mcmurtrian`; `pdf-p<N>` anchors per the extraction read-call log.

- **C1** [anchor: §1 Abstract · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-05-from-biology-of-love-to-life-coherent-governance-maturanan-galtungian-mcmurtrian · pdf-p3] Central thesis: *"Maturana's biology of love provides a biological-ethical foundation for rethinking politics as the **conservation of coexistence** rather than the management of populations by external control. For Maturana, love is not sentimentality but the relational domain in which the other arises as legitimate in coexistence."*

- **C2** [anchor: §2 Biology of Love · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-05-from-biology-of-love-to-life-coherent-governance-maturanan-galtungian-mcmurtrian · pdf-p7-8] Love as social-biological ground: *"Love is the relational domain in which another living being appears as **legitimate in coexistence**. Without such legitimacy, social life becomes organized through fear, control, exclusion, obedience, and domination."* A society organized against love is *"**biologically incoherent** because it conserves social relations that narrow the possible actions of its members."* Cites Maturana 2002.

- **C3** [anchor: §4 Life-Value · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-05-from-biology-of-love-to-life-coherent-governance-maturanan-galtungian-mcmurtrian · pdf-p11-12] McMurtry life-value criterion: *"**The good is not what maximizes price, profit, growth, or preference satisfaction; the good is what enables a more coherently inclusive range of life-capacity.**"* *"The decisive shift is from money-value to life-value… Life-value asks whether a system enables or disables the range of thought, felt being, and action of living beings."* Cites McMurtry 2011.

- **C4** [anchor: §4 Life-Value · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-05-from-biology-of-love-to-life-coherent-governance-maturanan-galtungian-mcmurtrian · pdf-p11-12] Universal life necessities: *"breathable air, clean water, food, shelter, care, education, health protection, meaningful participation, ecological stability, freedom from violence, symbolic worlds that do not degrade legitimacy. These are not subjective luxuries. They are conditions of life-capacity itself."*

- **C5** [anchor: §5 Money-Sequencing · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-05-from-biology-of-love-to-life-coherent-governance-maturanan-galtungian-mcmurtrian · pdf-p13-14] Institutional pathology: *"**Institutional pathology arises when organizations conserve financial viability by disabling biological, social, ecological, or intergenerational viability.**"* Framed as *"the political analogue of costly conserved drift."* Cites McMurtry 2011 critique of corporate-rights globalization.

- **C6** [anchor: §6 Civil Commons · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-05-from-biology-of-love-to-life-coherent-governance-maturanan-galtungian-mcmurtrian · pdf-p15-17] Civil commons (McMurtry) as *"the social-institutional infrastructures that conserve and enable living coherence across persons, communities, species, ecosystems, and generations."* Healthcare / education / water / knowledge each named as civil-commons; *"Distinct from market (price-signaled) and command (state-directed) — third governance mode."* Integrates Ostrom (1990): durable governance *"depends on participation, contextual fit, monitoring, feedback, and collective rule-making rather than abstract command."*

- **C7** [anchor: §8 Violence Triangle · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-05-from-biology-of-love-to-life-coherent-governance-maturanan-galtungian-mcmurtrian · pdf-p18-19] Violence-triangle ↔ life-coherence-triangle pairing (Galtung extension): direct violence ↔ direct care; structural violence ↔ structural justice; cultural violence ↔ cultural legitimacy. Final formulation: *"**Peace is not merely the absence of violence; it is the organized presence of life-enabling conditions.**"* Cites Galtung 1969 + Galtung 1990.

- **C8** [anchor: §9 Rights to Duties · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-05-from-biology-of-love-to-life-coherent-governance-maturanan-galtungian-mcmurtrian · pdf-p20] Rights require civil-commons grounding: *"**A right is socially real only when the civil commons required to exercise it is protected.**"* Right to health requires water+food+sanitation+care+ecological safety+housing+education+peace; shifts politics *"from abstract entitlement to institutional responsibility."*

- **C9** [anchor: §11 Diagnostic Dashboard · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-05-from-biology-of-love-to-life-coherent-governance-maturanan-galtungian-mcmurtrian · pdf-p22] Life-coherent governance 10-point dashboard. Core operational question: *"**Does this institution conserve and expand life-capacity, or does it conserve itself by disabling life?**"* The ten points include protect universal life necessities; reduce direct/structural/cultural violence; expand coherent ranges of thought/feeling/action; protect ecological life-support; prevent institutional capture by money-sequencing; strengthen civil commons; support local self-organization with accountability; protect future generations; repair harm without reproducing domination.

- **C10** [anchor: §12 Non-Forcing Politics · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-05-from-biology-of-love-to-life-coherent-governance-maturanan-galtungian-mcmurtrian · pdf-p24-25] Wu-wei governance: *"The political equivalent of wu-wei is: **Act so that persons, communities, and ecosystems regain their own capacity for viable self-organization, repair, and co-drift.**"* Grounded in Maturana structural-determination (living systems cannot be instructed from outside like machines) + Ostrom commons-work + McMurtry life-value criterion. Diagnostic questions begin: *"1. What life-capacity is being disabled? 2. What direct, structural, or cultural violence is being conserved? 3. What civil commons is missing, degraded, or captured? 4. What local capacities for self-organization already exist?"*

### 4.1 McMurtry life-value ↔ `life-value-doctrine` (ADR-0086, the 4th doctrine)

### 4.2 Civil commons ↔ `civil-commons` derived-glossary slug (ADR-0087)

C6 names civil-commons (McMurtry, integrated with Ostrom 1990) as a *third governance mode* distinct from market (price-signaled) and command (state-directed). ### 4.3 Maturana biology of love ↔ care-cluster scope-condition (ADR-0088 on ADR-0045)

ADR-0088 already scope-conditioned the care-cluster (ADR-0045 `care-commoning`) to acknowledge exactly this Maturanan legitimacy-of-the-other substrate alongside the original care-feminist cluster. **Lossy bit:** Maturanan love arrives from a biology-of-cognition substrate (structural coupling generating the relational domain), whereas `care-commoning` was admitted from the Federici / Tronto / Gibson-Graham / pluriversal cluster; the convergence on *legitimate-other-in-coexistence* is real, the substrate-traditions differ, and ADR-0088's scope-condition is the canon record of that dual-substrate acknowledgment.

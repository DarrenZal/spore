## 2. C-claims (verbatim from extraction record)

All quotes verbatim from the extraction record. RID `orn:source:bsahely-2017-05-06-is-our-credit-creation-system-the-father`.

- **C1** [html-section:Pathogenic selves] "We are told that we behave “as if” we are rationally competing with one another to maximize our own self-interests, although our real world experiences tell us that this assumption is totally fake and not in keeping with reality."

- **C2** [html-section:Pathogenic marketplace of virtual currencies] "…in virtual currencies of fake transfers now based on financial instruments such as credit, debt, bonds, bills, shares, futures, MBS, CDS, etc., now denominated not in energy equivalents, but on fiat money and digital book-keeping accounting."

- **C3** [html-section:The rule of law in thermodynamics] "In order to minimize energy wastage as entropy, living systems had to learn to cooperate in coherent ways so that energy is stored appropriately and transformed prudentially at every level of life organization."

- **C4** [html-section:The universal energy currency within living beings] "All of this is regulated by the mitochondria which are the equivalents of communal banks within our cells.  It is the guidance by the needs of the cells that determines how much of this ATP credit is produced and recycled per cell, thus embodying the principle of subsidiarity at the cellulr level."

- **C5** [html-section:The energy reserves] "They in turn release their energy stored deposits on demand as credit to the cells in times of ATP and creatinine phosphate deficiencies, so as to top-up their shortfalls."

- **C6** [html-section:Regulation of energy flows is managed locally at the cellular level] "As is evident from the workings of all life beings, the flow of energy (and information) is regulated from the bottom-up by the needs of the cells, and the behaviour of the organisms is such that those needs are met."

- **C7** [html-section:The gross bodily product of energy flows is not indicative of the health of the organism] "Although we can quantify the gross bodily product (GBP) of energy throughput in terms of cardiac output of the heart or the metabolic rate of the organism, these single indicators do not give an indication of the health of the organism."

- **C8** [html-section:The pathogenic credit creation process revealed] "Instead of credit being created free-of-charge on demand by public utilities for life-giving and life-supporting purposes to fulfill universal human life necessities and manage and maintain critical infrastructures and institutions of the society and planet, banks use this man-made social privilege to accumulate more money without end."

- **C9** [html-section:The pathogenic credit creation process revealed] "…unlike real energy that depreciates with its recirculation as waste heat that is radiated from the planet, this fake energy, which is created from nothing, appreciates and grows exponentially given the compounded interest rates and markups in the price value chains of transactions. As a result, the credit creation system is anti-thermodynamic in principle!!"

- **C10** [html-section:The pathogenic credit creation process revealed] "…guardians and stewards of the people of nation states have to be “captured and bribed” to waste resources to defend this cancerous system. Thus explaining the wasteful creation of police states and the prison-industrial complexes along with the military-intelligence-media industrial complexes."

- **C11** [html-section:Transforming our credit creation system to make it fully life-coherent] "These degenerative trends will only accelerate and get worse if we do not act now to create a credit creation system modelled on the wisdom of a healthy, thriving living body, based on the for-ever-giving nature of mitochondria and ultimately that of the sun, which gives of themselves in service free of interest and debt creation."

- **C12** [html-section:Transforming our credit creation system to make it fully life-coherent] "Hopefully, our new system would empower us to reduce, reuse, recycle, repair and redistribute our life goods in service from where they are unused to where they are most needed."

## 4. Substrate-resonance map (descriptive)

This section reads the post against the **resolved** post-Bundle-α canon (14 foundation docs; 9 primitives; **4 cross-cutting doctrines**; 2 modes; 2 properties; concepts-yaml **v24**; 40 canon-decisions through ADR-0090). All observations are descriptive; nothing is admitted.

### 4.1 Life-value vs "as if" rationality ↔ `life-value-doctrine` (ADR-0086) — the load-bearing resonance

The post's spine is the McMurtry life-value contrast a decade before Sahely formalizes it. C1 rejects the orthodox "as if" rational-self-interest-maximizer model as "totally fake"; C8/C11/C12 propose a credit system organized around "universal human life necessities", "life goods", and "life-giving and life-supporting purposes". This is exactly the territory Bundle α admitted as the **fourth cross-cutting doctrine**, `life-value-doctrine` (ADR-0086), sibling to `reproductive-commoning` / `boundary-commoning` / `care-commoning`. Per the consistency map this is a **doctrine** (a lens operating across all nine primitives without primitive-elevation), **not** a glossary slug, even though the slug-token is registered in concepts-yaml v24. No canon-shift: the doctrine already exists; this 2017 post is one early articulation in the same single-author corpus that ADR-0086 cites in its substrate-citation set.

### 4.2 Free public-utility credit for life-necessities ↔ `civil-commons` slug (ADR-0087)

C8's positive proposal — "credit being created free-of-charge on demand by public utilities for life-giving and life-supporting purposes to fulfill universal human life necessities and manage and maintain critical infrastructures and institutions of the society and planet" — is the McMurtry civil-commons provisioning frame: socially-constructed life-good provisioning systems. Bundle α admitted this as the `civil-commons` **derived-glossary slug** (ADR-0087). Per the consistency map this is a **slug**, NOT a doctrine — the doctrine is `life-value-doctrine`; `civil-commons` is one operational substrate the doctrine operates over at the monetary/infrastructure application-domain. No canon-shift.

### 4.3 "Captured and bribed" guardians + fake-currency displacement ↔ `golden-calf-trap` + `recursive-audit-method` + F6 actor-capture (ADR-0085 / ADR-0048)

C10 names "guardians and stewards of the people of nation states… captured and bribed to waste resources to defend this cancerous system" — institutional stewards co-opted into defending an extractive mechanism. This is the **`golden-calf-trap` shape** (ADR-0085): a corrective/protective role captured and turned into a defense-mechanism for the very thing it should guard against. C2's "virtual currencies of fake transfers" displacing "real currencies of universal entities such as energy and information transfers" is a symbol-displaces-reality move that composes with `substitution-trap` (ADR-0048) and Spore F6 actor-capture (`spore.failure-modes`) at the monetary layer. Per the consistency map, `golden-calf-trap` is the SHAPE-OF ADR-0048 `substitution-trap`, not equivalent-to it. The post's diagnostic stance — C9's "anti-thermodynamic in principle" and C7's "single indicators do not give an indication of health" — applies a system's own grounding question (thermodynamic accounting; organismic health) back to the system, which is the operational stance `recursive-audit-method` (ADR-0085) names. All already-canonical; this post supplies an early monetary-layer illustration, not new canon-pressure.

### 4.4 Reserves-as-credit and GBP-conceals-health ↔ F9 / F4 scope-conditions (ADR-0089 / ADR-0090)

C5's glycogen/fat reserves that "release their energy stored deposits on demand as credit to the cells… to top-up their shortfalls" is the margin/reserve-as-resilience frame inside the **margin-as-reserve scope-condition on F9 maintenance-economics** (ADR-0089). Note the Bundle-α discipline: the `reserve-margin` slug was **explicitly NOT admitted** (`reproductive-infrastructure` already covers the reserve substrate); this is a scope-condition, not a glossary slug. Separately, C7's argument that GBP/cardiac-output/metabolic-rate "single indicators do not give an indication of the health of the organism" — what a top-line metric makes visible vs. what it conceals (a high-throughput cancer patient looks "productive") — sits inside the **perception-as-power scope-condition on F4 representation-authority** (ADR-0090; what-becomes-visible-is-power), again a scope-condition, not a slug. Both already-resolved.

### 4.5 Cellular subsidiarity, mutual-credit analogy, conservation, legitimacy ↔ ADR-0046 / ADR-0042

C4/C6's "subsidiarity at the cellular level" + "regulated from the bottom-up by the needs of the cells… no central planning unit at the top" is Spore's polycentric `rule-in-use` / conservation-through-change frame (ADR-0046): coordination conserved through bottom-up local guidance rather than top-down central planning (which the post explicitly indicts as how the "fake energy" system must be "micro-managed from top-down"). C4's mitochondria-as-"communal banks" producing/recycling "ATP credit" and C11's interest-and-debt-free for-ever-giving credit is a **mutual-credit / commitment-pooling** source-domain analogy resonating with `mutual-credit` / `commitment-pooling` (PM-primary slugs) at the economics-substrate layer. And C9/C11's diagnosis that the extractive credit system's persistence-and-exponential-growth is precisely *not* health — "an out-of-control cancerous system that would eventually kill its planetary host" — is the civilizational sibling of Spore's `structural-legitimacy` (ADR-0042): operational persistence (even runaway growth) is not legitimacy. All framing-note resonances against existing canon.

## 6. Summary

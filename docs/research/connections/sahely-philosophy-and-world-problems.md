## 2. C-claims (verbatim from extraction record; concepts attributed to McMurtry substrate)

All quotes verbatim from the extraction record §"Verbatim load-bearing claims". RID `orn:source:bsahely-2026-02-24-philosophy-and-world-problems-life-value-civil`; page anchors as the record gives them. Each claim is **Sahely-abstracting-McMurtry**; the concept attribution is to **McMurtry**.

- **C1** [anchor: §Civil Commons · orn:source:bsahely-2026-02-24-philosophy-and-world-problems-life-value-civil · pdf-p8] **Civil Commons definition (McMurtry):** "The Civil Commons refers to the long-evolved social structures, life-protective norms, and organized human agency that allow life to be protected and developed. It is the social life-fabric—from public education and universal healthcare to common sewers and the rule of law—built through social cooperation to ensure universal access to the means of life."

- **C2** [anchor: §Civil Commons · orn:source:bsahely-2026-02-24-philosophy-and-world-problems-life-value-civil · pdf-p8-9] **Two-logic collision — Money-Sequence vs Life-Sequence (McMurtry M→M′ / L→M→L′ formalisms):** "At the heart of our global crisis is a collision between two irreconcilable logics. The dominant 'Money-Sequence System' (M → M') views money as the beginning and end of all value, seeking only to maximize private accumulation. In contrast, the 'Life-Sequence of Value' (L → M → L') uses means (including money) to serve the ultimate end: the protection and development of life itself."

- **C3** [anchor: §IV Deep Justice · orn:source:bsahely-2026-02-24-philosophy-and-world-problems-life-value-civil · pdf-p5] **Two-rights-systems war (McMurtry):** "There is an 'unseen war' between two rights systems: 1. Corporate Person Rights: Inhuman, money-focused, and increasingly unaccountable. 2. Living Person Rights: Grounded in human life development and biological requirements. The text argues that corporate rights currently override life-protective international norms and the 'Social State.'"

- **C4** [anchor: §V Reclaiming Rationality · orn:source:bsahely-2026-02-24-philosophy-and-world-problems-life-value-civil · pdf-p5-6] **Life-Coherence Principle, epistemic version (McMurtry):** "**Life-Coherence Principle**: Requirement: Any rational or scientific system must be consistent with the requirements of life support systems. Correction of 'Cooked Science': Scientific method must be released from 'private financial subjugation' and returned to the public purpose of understanding and protecting the life-ground. Epistemic Shift: Moving from 'analytical epistemology' (which abstracts away from life) to a 'life-responsible' scientific method."

- **C5** [anchor: §V Reclaiming Rationality · orn:source:bsahely-2026-02-24-philosophy-and-world-problems-life-value-civil · pdf-p5] **Self-Maximizing Fallacy (McMurtry):** "Modern rationality is often defined as 'self-maximizing choice with no life base.' This logic leads to the 'Prisoner's Dilemma' and other models where cooperative, life-coherent choice space is erased a priori."

- **C6** [anchor: §Civil Commons guide · orn:source:bsahely-2026-02-24-philosophy-and-world-problems-life-value-civil · pdf-p10] **Language-as-paradigm-of-the-commons (McMurtry):** "Language is the ultimate paradigm of the Civil Commons. It is a form of wealth that defies the logic of scarcity and exclusionary ownership, serving as a model for how all social resources should be governed. … Non-Depletable Life-Capital: Unlike the Money-Sequence, where resources are consumed or extracted, language grows more powerful the more it is shared. It is a 'social life-host' that expands the internal thought-field of every participant."

- **C7** [anchor: §VI Human Identity · orn:source:bsahely-2026-02-24-philosophy-and-world-problems-life-value-civil · pdf-p7] **Meta-choice framing (McMurtry):** "Humanity faces a choice between 'predatory' development and 'Civil Commons overcoming,' where the point of life is to live fully the capacities of one's being in a way that is life-coherent."

- **C8** [anchor: §Conclusion · orn:source:bsahely-2026-02-24-philosophy-and-world-problems-life-value-civil · pdf-p7] **Closing thesis (McMurtry's UNESCO essay conclusion):** "The synthesis concludes that the current global crisis—characterized by planetary ecocide, increasing life insecurity, and system blindness—cannot be solved by the same logic that created it. The 'Life-Value Compass' and the 'Life-Coherence Principle' are presented not merely as philosophical ideals, but as global system imperatives necessary to ensure the continued existence of life support systems and the advancement of human civilization."

### 4.2 Money-Sequence / Life-Sequence + meta-choice (C2 + C7 + C8) ↔ `life-value-doctrine` (ADR-0086)

C2's M→M′ (money-as-beginning-and-end) vs L→M→L′ (means-serving-life) collision is the McMurtry Life-Value Onto-Axiology substrate that ADR-0086 admitted as **`life-value-doctrine`**, the **4th cross-cutting doctrine** (sibling to `reproductive-commoning` ADR-0002, `boundary-commoning` ADR-0003, `care-commoning` ADR-0045). ### 4.3 Two-rights-war (C3) ↔ `structural-legitimacy` (ADR-0042) read through the life-value lens

C3's *"unseen war"* between Corporate-Person-Rights and Living-Person-Rights, with corporate rights *overriding* life-protective norms, is a persistence-is-not-legitimacy claim at civilizational scale: a rights-arrangement can operationally persist and dominate while failing the life-value test that would make its persistence legitimate. Spore's `structural-legitimacy` foundation doc (ADR-0042) does this canon-work at federation-governance layer; per ADR-0086 §Consequences, `structural-legitimacy` gained evaluative substrate so legitimacy of coordination outcomes is readable through the life-value lens *without primitive-grammar extension*. ### 4.4 System blindness + epistemic shift (C8 + C4) ↔ perception-as-power (ADR-0090)

C8 names *"system blindness"* as a defining feature of the crisis; C4's Life-Coherence Principle calls for an *"Epistemic Shift"* from *"analytical epistemology (which abstracts away from life)"* to a *"life-responsible scientific method,"* and for releasing science from *"private financial subjugation."* This is what-becomes-visible / what-becomes-invisible territory — which method makes life-coherence perceptible vs which method renders it invisible. ADR-0090's perception-as-power scope-condition on F4 (representation-authority) already covers the canon-shape: representational authority is partly a question of whose perception governs what is rendered visible. C4's "cooked science" is a McMurtry-flavored instance of the same concern. Descriptive reinforcement only.

### 4.5 Language-as-non-depletable-commons (C6) ↔ languaging (ADR-0063) and `recursive-audit-method` discipline on the Golden-Calf trap

C6's claim that *"language grows more powerful the more it is shared … a social life-host that expands the internal thought-field of every participant"* resonates with the participatory-sense-making scope ADR-0063 canonized on the Signal primitive (languaging as recursive coordination, not one-way send-and-receive).

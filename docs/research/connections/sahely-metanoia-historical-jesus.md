## 4. Substrate-resonance map (descriptive)

Read against post-Bundle-α canon (14 foundation docs / 9 primitives / **4** cross-cutting doctrines / 2 modes / 2 properties / concepts-yaml v24 / 40 canon-decisions). Descriptive observations only.

### 4.1 Metanoia-as-perception-shift ↔ perception-as-power (ADR-0090, F4)

C1's hypothesis that the central teaching was *"an interior reorientation of awareness rather than primarily moral repentance"*, and C6's claim that civilizational reforms were *"preceded by shifts in moral perception"*, both live in the territory ADR-0090 scope-conditioned onto F4 (representation-authority): **what becomes visible, meaningful, or legitimate is itself a site of power.** ADR-0090 declined a `perception-as-power` slug DESPITE a sufficient cluster-count, because F4's existing `epistemic-gap` (ADR-0076) + `external-witness` (ADR-0081 F8) slugs already cover the perception-authority territory. Sahely's metanoia is a first-person, individual-development articulation of perception-shift; ADR-0090's substrate is structural-violence/standpoint-epistemology (Galtung/Harding/Haraway). They share the *operational concern* (perception governs what is visible/legitimate) across very different layers. **No canon-shift; reinforcing observation only.**

### 4.2 Translation-drift + institutionalization ↔ trap-shape vocabulary (ADR-0085)

C2 (*metanoia → paenitentia* drift contributing to penitential emphasis) and C8 (institutional structures that *"preserved the tradition while also reshaping its conceptual framework"*) describe a **capture mechanism**: an experiential teaching progressively obscured as it is institutionalized. This has the *shape* of Spore's `golden-calf-trap` (ADR-0085) — the SHAPE-OF (not equivalent-to) ADR-0048's `substitution-trap`: a thing-pointed-at gets replaced by the form-that-points. The recursive-audit-method slug (ADR-0085) is the disciplined antidote-shape Sahely's own Appendix-G inversion gestures toward (re-perceiving rather than re-enacting the inherited form). **Descriptive resonance at the historiographic layer; no admission. This is one AI-co-authored paper, not cluster-evidence.**

### 4.3 Persistence ≠ legitimacy ↔ structural-legitimacy (ADR-0042)

C8's institution that "preserved the tradition while also reshaping" it is a clean instance of **persistence is not legitimacy**: the institutional form persists and even propagates while (on Sahely's thesis) negating the perception-transformation it claims to transmit. Spore's `structural-legitimacy` foundation doc (ADR-0042) does the same canon-work at the federation-governance layer — an arrangement may operationally persist while failing the legitimacy test that makes persistence meaningful. Cross-domain convergence; **no canon-shift.**

### 4.4 Scapegoat-inversion / re-perceiving the victim ↔ care-cluster legitimacy-of-the-other (ADR-0088)

C5's Metanoia-Scapegoat Inversion Model argues authentic metanoia *interrupts the scapegoat cycle by re-perceiving the victim* — the victim arises as legitimate rather than as sacrificeable. This touches the **legitimacy-of-the-other** axis that ADR-0088 scope-conditioned onto care-commoning (ADR-0045). The resonance is genuine but lives at the individual-spiritual / civilizational-ethics layer, not the coordination-grammar layer; ADR-0088's care-cluster scope-condition already holds the canon territory. **Descriptive only.**

### 4.5 Methodological caveat ↔ external-validation posture (F8 / ADR-0081)

C9 (§13.2) explicitly bounds the paper's epistemic reach: historical intentions "cannot be established with certainty." This author-side honesty-discipline rhymes with F8's `external-witness` / `validation-loop` posture (a canon that invites external feedback and marks its own uncertainty). It is a posture-resonance, not a coordination-mechanism; **no canon engagement beyond framing.**

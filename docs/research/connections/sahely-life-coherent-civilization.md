## 2. C-claims (verbatim from extraction record)

RID `orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-09-life-coherent-civilization-world-bringing-participatory-repair`; anchors carry `pdf-p<N>` per intake protocol. All quotes verbatim from the extraction record's 7-fact moderate-selective extraction.

- **C1** [anchor: §Executive Summary §3 · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-09-life-coherent-civilization-world-bringing-participatory-repair · pdf-p31] **the great inversion:** "The book names the dominant civilizational disorder as **the great inversion**. The great inversion occurs whenever systems originally justified as serving life come to require life to serve them. Economy no longer serves provisioning; provisioning becomes subordinate to growth, profit, and debt. Law no longer serves justice and repair; justice becomes subordinate to procedure, property, and institutional self-protection. Education no longer serves world-opening and human development; learning becomes subordinate to credentialing, competition, and labour-market sorting."

- **C2** [anchor: §Abstract · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-09-life-coherent-civilization-world-bringing-participatory-repair · pdf-p28] **Maturana + McMurtry + Galtung triad:** "The argument is built around three foundational correctives. From Humberto Maturana and Francisco Varela, it takes the insight that cognition is not passive representation of an independent world, but embodied, relational, and enacted world-bringing by living systems structurally coupled to their media. From John McMurtry, it takes the life-value principle… From Johan Galtung, it takes the expanded account of violence as direct, structural, and cultural life-disablement, and peace as the active creation of conditions for human and ecological flourishing."

- **C3** [anchor: §Abstract + §Executive Summary §4 · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-09-life-coherent-civilization-world-bringing-participatory-repair · pdf-p28 + pdf-p32] **life-coherent vessel as diagnostic, not blueprint:** "The proposed alternative is the **life-coherent vessel**: a framework for reordering civilization around the conditions that make life possible. The vessel is not a utopian blueprint, ideological program, or technocratic master plan. It is a diagnostic and practical architecture for asking: What world is being brought forth? Whose life is enabled or disabled? What forms of violence are hidden in normal arrangements? What civil commons are being protected or destroyed? What feedback is being ignored? What forms of repair are now required?"

- **C4** [anchor: §Executive Summary §2 · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-09-life-coherent-civilization-world-bringing-participatory-repair · pdf-p30] **three foundational claims:** "(1) **Worlds are brought forth.** (2) **Worlds must answer to life.** (3) **Avoidable life-disablement must be named as violence.**"

- **C5** [anchor: §1.1–1.10 ToC + Part I title · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-09-life-coherent-civilization-world-bringing-participatory-repair · pdf-p3] **world-bringing animal as foundational anthropology:** Chapter 1 title "**The World-Bringing Animal**" with 10 sub-sections incl. "1.2 Distinction as the beginning of a world / 1.3 Language and the coordination of worlds / 1.4 Institutions as stabilized distinctions / 1.9 The danger of world-blindness / 1.10 From world-bringing to responsibility" — human beings as participants-in-world-bringing, not observers of a pre-given world.

- **C6** [anchor: §Keywords + Conclusion · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-09-life-coherent-civilization-world-bringing-participatory-repair · pdf-p29] **anti-finalist conclusion:** "The book concludes that a life-coherent civilization is not one that abolishes conflict, uncertainty, plurality, suffering, or error. It is one that learns to organize its institutions around the continuous detection and repair of avoidable life-disablement."

- **C7** [anchor: §Abstract closing · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-09-life-coherent-civilization-world-bringing-participatory-repair · pdf-p29] **usable framework, not theoretical addition:** "**Its aim is not to add another theory to the existing archive of civilizational critique, but to offer a usable framework for seeing, judging, and acting differently: a way of bringing forth worlds that remain answerable to life.**"

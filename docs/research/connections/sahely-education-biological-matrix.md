## 2. C-claims (verbatim from extraction record)

All quotes verbatim from the extraction record; `[html-section:]` anchored; RID `orn:source:bsahely-2018-01-13-education-as-viewed-from-the-biological`. **These are Maturana & Dávila's words, reposted by Sahely.**

- **C1** [html-section:The responsibility for education] *"We live in a culture centered on relations of domination and submission."*
- **C2** [html-section:The responsibility for education] *"Control is the relational dynamic from which oneself and others arise denied capacities and talents, narrowing viewpoints, intelligence, and creativity, generating dependence, from which autonomy and self-respect are not possible."*
- **C3** [html-section:WHAT DO WE DO] *"The student does not learn a subject, but rather learns a way of living with others; a way of becoming a human being."*
- **C4** [html-section:WHAT DO WE DO] *"Genetics is the initial condition, a point of departure. From this point of departure it is possible to grow in one way or another, depending on the environment."*
- **C5** [html-section:WHAT DO WE DO] *"Children are not the future of the human community. It is we adults who are. We are the future of our children because how they will be depends upon how they live with us. The future lies in our present."*
- **C6** [html-section:DEMOCRACY] *"Collaboration exists only when relations are based on legitimacy of being, which implies respect for oneself and respect for others."*
- **C7** [html-section:DEMOCRACY] *"Love is the emotion that constitutes and makes possible living together democratically."*
- **C8** [html-section:DO WE EDUCATE FOR SELF-CONTROL] *"Well-being is the sensation of being in coherence with one's circumstances."*
- **C9** [html-section:DO WE EDUCATE FOR SELF-CONTROL] *"We are agents of change of the world in which we live, and we will generate a world of well-being or of unease according to whether we generate venues of autonomy or dependence in our students."*
- **C10** [html-section:WHERE DO WE EDUCATE] *"The biosphere is finished; now we live in the homosphere. We are destroying our environment and transforming it so that living in it will be impossible."*
- **C11** [html-section:VIRTUAL EMOTIONS] *"Emotions are not virtual because they correspond to what occurs internally in the organism as a relational basis."*
- **C12** [html-section:GLOBALIZATION] *"Listening consists of hearing and attending to what we hear and in which what the other says is valid."*
- **C13** [html-section:EPISTEMOLOGY] *"The enemy of reflection is the knowledge that we know. if I know, I don't look because I already know."*

## 4. Substrate-resonance map (descriptive)

Read against Spore's post-Bundle-α canon (14 foundation docs / 9 primitives / **4** cross-cutting doctrines / 2 modes / 2 properties / concepts-yaml v24 / 40 canon-decisions). Layer-distinctness preserved: descriptive resonance observations, not admission proposals. **All conceptual claims attribute to Maturana & Dávila (reposted by Sahely), not to Sahely.**

**4.1 Biology of love ↔ care-cluster scope-condition (ADR-0088).** C7's *"Love is the emotion that constitutes and makes possible living together democratically"* and C6's *"legitimacy of being"* are the precise Maturanan biology-of-love substrate — love as the relational domain in which the other arises as legitimate in coexistence. Bundle α used exactly this care-cluster substrate to scope-condition `care-commoning` via **ADR-0088**. This 2018 repost is the **historical primary-source root** of that substrate (the 2006 Maturana–Dávila essay), pre-dating Sahely's own later synthesis by ~20 years. Descriptive reinforcement only; the disposition is closed, and (single-source rigor) this is the same tradition ADR-0088 already weighed — not a second independent cluster.

**4.2 Legitimacy-of-being ↔ structural-legitimacy (ADR-0042).** C6 — collaboration exists *"only when relations are based on legitimacy of being"* — and C2's account of control as the dynamic that denies autonomy together parallel structural-legitimacy: a relational order grounded in legitimacy-of-being is distinct from one that merely persists through domination/control. The essay's patriarchal-matriarchal-control diagnosis is, in Spore terms, a persistence-without-legitimacy reading. Existing canon (ADR-0042); independent-tradition substrate-citation value; no shift.

**4.3 Languaging / listening / emotioning ↔ participatory-sense-making (ADR-0063).** C12's two-kinds-of-listening (hearing where the other is valid vs. hearing only oneself) and C3's *"learns a way of living with others"* are languaging-and-emotioning as constitutively-interactive sense-making, already dispositioned via ADR-0063's scope-conditioning on Signal (sender-receiver-transmission vs. constitutively-interactive-emergence). C11 (*"Only emotion changes emotion"*; emotions as the organism's relational basis) is the emotioning half of the same Maturanan pair. Descriptive; no shift.

**4.4 Structural coupling + organism-grown-in-community ↔ Holon / Membrane substrate (ADR-0050 / ADR-0062).** C4 (*genetics as initial condition, grown one way or another by environment*) and the lion/cheetah/gorilla "raised in its community, not by genes" example are structural-coupling / organism–medium co-determination, reinforcing Holon-irreducibility (ADR-0050), Membrane-as-self-produced (ADR-0062), and relational-identity (identity arises in relation). C5's *"the future lies in our present"* is conservation-through-change of the relational form — resonant with `rule-in-use` (ADR-0046). All existing canon; no shift.

**4.5 Well-being-as-coherence + control-as-trap ↔ life-value + trap-shape vocab (ADR-0086 / ADR-0085).** C8's *"Well-being is the sensation of being in coherence with one's circumstances"* and C9's agents-generate-well-being-or-unease read as a life-coherence/life-value framing (ADR-0086 `life-value-doctrine`). C1/C2's domination-and-control diagnosis — control as a self-defeating substitute for the living relation it claims to secure — maps to the trap-shape vocabulary: `golden-calf-trap` (shape-of, NOT equivalent-to, ADR-0048 `substitution-trap`; ADR-0085). C10's biosphere→homosphere touches the `civil-commons` ecological substrate (ADR-0087) thinly. C13's certainty-that-blinds touches perception-as-power on F4 (ADR-0090). All already-resolved; descriptive only.

**No graph-substrate-of-canon pressure.** This extraction surfaces no mathematical/topological substrate (no sheaf/Fano/E8/octonion/topology), so the C7-class graph-substrate DECLINE-with-triggers future-ADR-shape is not even touched. The essay is wholly a relational-biology-of-cognition text.

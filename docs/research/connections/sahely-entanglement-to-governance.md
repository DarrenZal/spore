## 2. C-claims (verbatim from Phase 1 extraction; load-bearing claims; pdf-p anchors)

Anchors carry RID `orn:source:bsahely-2026-04-02-from-entanglement-to-governance-the-geo` and pdf-p<page>. Page-ranges per Phase 1 extraction `read_call_log`.

- **C1** [anchor: Abstract · pdf-p15] Central derivation thesis: *"This work develops a unified framework for understanding persistence and failure in complex systems by deriving, rather than assuming, the minimal structures required for relational coherence."*

- **C2** [anchor: Abstract · pdf-p15] Triadic-closure-as-minimal-unit + Fano-plane uniqueness: *"Beginning from the requirement that viable systems must resolve interactions beyond pairwise relations, it is shown that triadic closure is the minimal unit of consistency. The unique finite structure satisfying this requirement is the Fano plane, which organizes seven irreducible relational roles into a closed configuration."*

- **C3** [anchor: Abstract · pdf-p15] Lifting chain (Fano → octonion → Jordan J3(O) → Freudenthal → E7): *"When these relations are required to support directed interaction, the structure lifts necessarily to the octonion algebra, introducing non-associativity as a measure of contextual inconsistency. The need to represent structured states leads to the exceptional Jordan algebra J3(O), whose cubic norm captures minimal global consistency. Further lifting to the Freudenthal triple system introduces symplectic duality and yields a quartic invariant preserved by the exceptional group E7, providing the first candidate for a global coherence measure across relational transformations."*

- **C4** [anchor: Abstract · pdf-p15] **Sheaf-substrate explicit declaration (the operator-signal target quote)**: *"To account for the distinction between observable variables and underlying structure, the framework incorporates fiber bundle theory, where measured states are projections of higher-dimensional relational configurations. Sheaf theory and cohomology formalize the transition from local consistency to global coherence, with failure arising as obstruction to the existence of a global section. This yields a structural interpretation of early warning signals as the accumulation of unresolved inconsistencies prior to observable collapse."*

- **C5** [anchor: Abstract · pdf-p15] Cross-domain mapping (medicine / ecology / economics / governance): *"The resulting framework is shown to apply across domains. In physics, it aligns with relational interpretations of quantum mechanics and entanglement. In medicine, disease is reinterpreted as loss of relational coherence preceding measurable dysfunction. In ecology, collapse emerges from breakdown of interaction networks before changes in indicators. In economics, crises reflect incoherence between financial and real systems. In governance, policy failure arises from optimizing projections rather than preserving structural integrity."*

- **C6** [anchor: Abstract · pdf-p15] Central claim: viability = coherence-of-relations, not properties-of-components: *"The central result is that viability is not a property of components but of the coherence of their relations, and that this coherence is governed by invariant structures arising from minimal mathematical constraints. Action within such systems must therefore shift from control of variables to preservation of relational coherence under constraint."*

- **C8** [anchor: Exec Summary §5 · pdf-p18] Failure mechanism three-stage chain:
  > *"Failure occurs in three stages: 1. Local inconsistency (captured by non-associativity). 2. Cross-domain incompatibility (failure of relational 'gluing'). 3. Global obstruction (loss of coherent structure). Collapse is the final projection of accumulated hidden incoherence."*

- **C9** [anchor: Exec Summary §6 · pdf-p18] Early warning = cohomological obstruction: *"Early warning signals are not primarily changes in variables but: loss of adaptive capacity, increasing variability, divergence between subsystems, delayed recovery from disturbance. Formally, these correspond to rising cohomological obstruction."*

- **C10** [anchor: Ch 3.2 · pdf-p38] Fano plane formal definition: *"The Fano plane is the smallest possible projective plane. It consists of: 7 points; 7 lines; Each line contains exactly 3 points; Each point lies on exactly 3 lines. Most importantly: Every pair of points is contained in exactly one line. Translated into our language: Points → relational roles; Lines → triadic closure relations. This is precisely the structure we required. No redundancy. No missing relations. Complete closure."*

- **C11** [anchor: Ch 3.4 · pdf-p39-40] Seven Fano-roles + functional triads: *"Each of the seven roles: Constraint (C), Margin (M), State (X), Disturbance (D), Perception (P), Regulation (R), Options (O) maps onto one point of the Fano plane. Each line (triad) corresponds to a functional channel. For example: (C, M, X) → Geometry of viability; (X, D, R) → Response channel; (X, P, O) → Possibility mapping; (M, O, R) → Adaptive capacity; (C, D, O) → Pressure vs possibility; (C, P, R) → Governance; (M, D, P) → Early warning. Each role participates in exactly three triads. Each triad intersects others in a structured way. Nothing is arbitrary. Everything is constrained."*

- **C13** [anchor: Ch 5.2 · pdf-p53] Octonions encode Fano-plane multiplication: *"Octonions are an extension of number systems: Real numbers (R), Complex numbers (C), Quaternions (H), Octonions (O). They consist of: One real unit. Seven imaginary units. Each imaginary unit corresponds to a point in the Fano plane. Multiplication is defined by the oriented triads: If (A → B → C) follows the arrow, then A · B = C. Reversing the order introduces a sign change. This structure is: Non-commutative (order matters). Non-associative (grouping matters). And crucially: It is the largest normed division algebra that exists. There is nothing beyond it of the same kind."*

- **C14** [anchor: Ch 5.7 · pdf-p57] Triality as perspective-rotation: *"Each triad can be viewed in three ways: As state-centered (X-focused). As process-centered (R or P focused). As constraint-centered (C or M focused). These are not different systems. They are different projections of the same structure. Triality allows us to rotate between them. Thus: The system is not fixed. It is perspectival."*

- **C15** [anchor: Ch 7.1 · pdf-p68] Sheaf substrate motivation — local-to-global problem: *"How do we determine whether a system is globally coherent when we only have local information? In every real system: We observe parts, not wholes. We measure locally, not globally. We intervene in fragments, not totalities. Thus, coherence must be inferred from local consistency. But local consistency does not guarantee global coherence. To formalize this distinction, we introduce the concept of a sheaf."*

- **C16** [anchor: Ch 7.2-7.3 · pdf-p68-69] Sheaf operational machinery: *"A sheaf provides a disciplined way of tracking: What exists locally. How local pieces relate on overlaps. Whether they can be combined into a global whole. In simple terms: A local section is what holds in a region. An overlap is where regions meet. Gluing is the process of combining local sections. A global section is a fully coherent system. A sheaf answers one question: Do locally consistent pieces form a globally coherent structure?"*

- **C17** [anchor: Ch 7.4 · pdf-p70] Sheaf reinterpretation of Fano triads: *"We can now reinterpret our structure in sheaf terms: Each triad (Fano line) → a local section. Each shared element between triads → an overlap. The entire system → a candidate global section. For coherence: Triads must agree on shared elements. Their relations must be compatible. The system must 'glue' consistently. If this holds, the system is viable. If not, something deeper is wrong."*

- **C18** [anchor: Ch 7.5-7.10 · pdf-p71-74] Cohomology = measure of obstruction: *"When local sections cannot be combined into a global whole, we encounter: An obstruction. This is not a visible failure. It is a structural incompatibility. Each part may appear correct. But their combination is impossible. This is the key insight: Failure begins as an inability to glue. To quantify this, mathematics introduces: Cohomology. Cohomology measures: The degree to which local consistency fails to produce global coherence. The presence of structural 'holes'. The impossibility of extending local solutions. In our framework: A zero cohomology class → full coherence. A non-zero class → obstruction present. Thus: Cohomology is a measure of hidden structural failure. [...] Viability is the ability of a system to maintain a globally coherent section across all local relational domains. And: Failure is the emergence of non-trivial cohomology. This is not metaphorical. It is structural."*

## 4. Substrate resonance against Spore canon (descriptive map)

This section maps Sahely's six-layer mathematical-substrate hierarchy onto Spore canon layers. **Spore is operational-coordination-grammar; Sahely's paper is mathematical-formal-substrate.** The two grammars operate at different abstraction layers; compositional comparison is honest, 1:1 mapping is not. Where Spore has nothing analogous, the section says so honestly rather than inventing a forced mapping.

### 4.1 Six-layer mathematical-substrate hierarchy (Sahely §8.9, pdf-p81)

Sahely chains the framework as: *"Fano plane → defines triadic closure. Octonions → encode relational multiplication. Associator → detects local inconsistency. Sheaves → track local-to-global coherence. Cohomology → measures obstruction. Quartic invariant → measures total coherence. Each layer builds on the previous. The invariant sits at the top: It integrates all structural information into a single quantity."*

| Sahely layer | Spore canon engagement | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| Fano plane (triadic closure; 7 minimal-relational-roles) | Compositionally-comparable to Spore's 9-primitive grammar at primitive-substrate layer; structurally distinct (closure-uniqueness vs cross-tradition-convergence) | DESCRIPTIVE-only |
| Octonion algebra (oriented Fano triads encoding multiplication; non-associative) | No Spore-canonical analog; Spore primitives are operational not algebraic; descriptive resonance via C14 triality ↔ ADR-0058 perspective-shifts | DESCRIPTIVE-only |
| Associator (algebraic measure of local non-associativity / contextual inconsistency) | Adjacent to F6 `coupling-breakdown` (ADR-0075) as local-failure-shape but at algebraic-substrate layer; F6 names operationally, Sahely names algebraically | DESCRIPTIVE-only |
| **Sheaf (local-section / overlap / gluing / global-section)** | **Direct engagement candidate with ADR-0058 3-primary graph-projection fragment (constitutional / commitment / epistemic) + ADR-0036; the operator-signal target** | **CANON-PRESSURE** |
| **Cohomology (measure of obstruction; non-trivial class = hidden structural failure)** | **Direct engagement candidate with F6 failure-modes; specifically F6.3 sensor-attestation-evidence-integrity + F6.4 scale-transition as cross-scale-gluing-failure shapes** | **CANON-PRESSURE** |
| Quartic invariant + E7 (candidate global-coherence scalar) | **No Spore analog; Spore does not claim to need a canonical viability scalar** | NONE |

### 4.2 The sheaf layer (Ch 7, pp68-74) — the operator-signal target

C15-C18 articulate sheaves at *exactly* the abstraction layer Spore's graph-projection canon (ADR-0036, ADR-0058) engages: **local sections** are the per-instance / per-bioregion / per-pool views over which Spore's federation operates; **overlaps** are the cross-instance contact-zones where two local views meet (federation interfaces, cross-bioregion commitment routing); **gluing** is the discipline by which locally-consistent views combine into a federation-coherent state; **global sections** are what the federation aspires to maintain; **cohomological obstruction** is what quantifies the *failure* to glue when individual sections look correct but cannot combine.

Spore canon currently engages this territory as follows:

- **ADR-0036 graph-projections-tiering-and-structure** (since deprecated-on-primary-set via ADR-0058): named eight projections; preserved on disk; deprecated-by-prose on primary-set per ADR-0058.
- **ADR-0058 phase-2c-graph-projections-dual-axis-bundle**: realigned to three primaries (constitutional / commitment / epistemic) per Opus-4-7 earning-test reading; five view-templates (Roadmap DAG / Intent hypergraph / Event / Routing-flow / Discourse) as one-line pattern-level references at `docs/patterns/view-template.md`.
- **ADR-0084 route-graph-settlement-operator-vocab-admission**: admitted `route-graph` slug (yaml v20→v21) as the multi-pool federation-coordination substrate surface where pools are nodes and admissible directed edges satisfy overlap + guard constraints. **This is Spore's most operationally-explicit substrate-shape adjacent to Sahely's sheaf framing.**
- **ADR-0068 federation-encounter-composition-pattern**: composition-pattern admit, with `federation-encounter` slug; composes Signal + Joint-commitment + Intent + Evidence within bounded Field-conditions at federation interface.

What Sahely-sheaf adds-or-might-add to this canon picture:

- A **disciplined formal vocabulary** (local section / overlap / gluing / global section / cohomological obstruction) for what Spore currently articulates more operationally and less algebraically.
- A **structural reason** why gluing-failure can be invisible — *each part appears correct but their combination is impossible* (C18). This is the algebraic counterpart to F6.5 membrane-boundary + F6.4 scale-transition failures.
- A **viability framing** at the global-section layer: *"viability is the ability of a system to maintain a globally coherent section across all local relational domains"* (C18). This is compositionally-aligned with Spore's C6 framing ("viability is coherence of relations, not properties of components") and with project-vision Core Thesis coordination-grammar framing.

What Sahely-sheaf does NOT add to Spore canon:

- **Concrete operational mechanics for federation coordination.** Sheaf framing is formal-substrate; it does not specify *which* commitments to pool, *which* roles to grant standing, *which* response-doctrine to fire when gluing fails. Spore canon (ADR-0046 Field rule-stratification + ADR-0050 joint-commitment + F3 actor-governance + F5 actuator-logic) carries that operational substrate.
- **Cross-tradition admission evidence.** Sahely-alone (or Sahely + the rich mathematical-tradition cluster sheaves themselves draw from) is single-source for the *application of sheaf-substrate to coordination-grammar*. Honest-rigor cluster-counting per ADR-0064 (`feedback_surface_vocabulary_vs_operational_concern.md`) would distinguish (i) tradition-support for sheaves-themselves (RICH: algebraic topology, Goresky-MacPherson, Joyal-Tierney, presheaf semantics, applied-sheaves Spivak / Fong-Spivak / Robinson) from (ii) tradition-support for *sheaf-substrate-as-coordination-grammar-foundation* (THIN: Sahely + perhaps category-theory-for-systems literature partial). DECISION-BRIEF carries this assessment.

### 4.3 The failure-modes layer (C8 three-stage chain + C9 cohomological-obstruction-as-early-warning)

Sahely's 3-stage chain (local inconsistency → cross-domain incompatibility → global obstruction) is **narrower-but-stronger** than Spore's F6 8-category taxonomy (per ADR-0075):
- F6.1 representation / F6.2 protocol / F6.3 sensor-attestation-evidence-integrity / F6.4 scale-transition / F6.5 membrane-boundary / F6.6 commitment-break / F6.7 actor-capture / F6.8 meta-pattern-composition.

Sahely's framing is *temporally-staged* (failure progresses through stages); F6 is *typologically-structured* (categories of failure-shape). The two are **compositionally compatible**, not in competition.

Where Sahely substantively adds substrate:

- **F6.3 sensor-attestation-evidence-integrity** could benefit from cohomological-obstruction framing as a precise formal measure of when sensor-attestation-evidence inputs *cannot consistently glue* into a coherent global picture (C18). The `coupling-breakdown` slug (ADR-0075; sibling-doctrine to structural-legitimacy) names operationally what Sahely names algebraically as obstruction.
- **F6.4 scale-transition** is exactly the canonical territory Sahely's sheaf-machinery formalizes: scale-transition failures are *gluing failures across scales*. The Sahely framing offers `epistemic-gap` (ADR-0076 slug, F5 trigger-surface) a deeper structural interpretation — gaps as cohomological-obstruction-signatures rather than as bare divergences.
- **F6.8 meta-pattern-composition** maps to Sahely's framing of failure-as-non-trivial-cohomology-across-the-whole-system — a structural shape that propagates rather than localizes.

What Spore F6 carries that Sahely does NOT cover:

- **Governance-side failure-shapes** (F6.7 actor-capture; F3 actor-governance §4.6 remediation doctrine). Sahely's framework is algebraic-substrate; it does not engage governance-as-canon-object the way F3 does.
- **Reproductive-labor failure-shapes** (F9 maintenance-economics; ADR-0002 reproductive-commoning). Sahely's framework is structurally silent on reproductive substrate.

### 4.4 The triadic-closure layer (C2 closure-uniqueness; C11 seven Fano-roles)

Sahely's argument for triadic-closure-as-minimal-consistency-unit uses a *closure-uniqueness* derivation (C2, C10): pairwise relations cannot achieve closure; triadic relations can; the Fano plane is the *unique* finite structure satisfying the closure requirement.

Spore's joint-commitment primitive (ADR-0050) was admitted at the *joint/collective-compositional layer* via a structurally different earning-test shape — cross-tradition convergence (Gilbertian + List/Pettit + Thompson + …) + Federicist-articulation-vs-invisibilisation logic + parsimony-as-earning-test-outcome (ADR-0048). **Different earning-test shape at the same primitive layer.** Sahely's closure-uniqueness argument is not Spore-canonical evidence-shape; Spore primitives are not derived from closure-uniqueness arguments, and the parsimony-at-9 is earning-test-outcome rather than axiom.

The Fano seven-role taxonomy (C/M/X/D/P/R/O) operates at a *fundamentally different abstraction layer* than Spore's 9-primitive grammar:
- Sahely's roles are **minimal-relational-roles forced by triadic-closure under Fano geometry**.
- Spore's primitives (3 structural — field / holon / membrane — + 6 verbs — intent / commitment / joint-commitment / evidence / signal / reproduction) are **operational-coordination-grammar primitives earned via cross-tradition convergence**.

The two grammars are *compositionally-comparable but structurally-distinct*. A forced 1:1 mapping (e.g., Field ↔ Constraint, Holon ↔ State, Signal ↔ Perception, …) would *appear* coherent but would erase the layer-distinctness honest reading requires. Bridge note declines that forced mapping per `feedback_audit_then_propose.md` discipline.

### 4.5 The triality layer (C14 state/process/constraint perspective-rotation)

Sahely's triality framing — *"the system is not fixed; it is perspectival"* (C14) — has descriptive resonance with two Spore canon objects:
- **ADR-0058 graph-projections**: multiple projections of the same coordination substrate (constitutional / commitment / epistemic primaries + 5 view-templates).
- **ADR-0048 power-expressive-constructed-modes**: `expressive-power` + `constructed-power` admitted as *modes-across-primitives* — qualities of operation rather than operations themselves.

What Spore *doesn't* have, and Sahely's triality *might* offer: an *algebraic* substrate for why perspective-rotation between view-modes is structurally legitimate rather than ad-hoc. Per `feedback_audit_then_propose.md`, this is a descriptive observation only — Spore's operational-coordination-grammar layer does not need algebraic substrate, and adding one without operational pressure would be a substitution-trap (per ADR-0048 mode `substitution-trap`).

### 4.6 The E7 quartic invariant layer (C3 candidate global-coherence scalar)

§8.7 introduces the E7 quartic invariant as **candidate global-coherence scalar** — a single numerical quantity computed from the full structural state, where preservation under transformation indicates viability. *"The invariant sits at the top: It integrates all structural information into a single quantity"* (Sahely §8.9, pdf-p81).

**Spore has nothing analogous and does not currently claim to need one.** Canonical-viability-scalar is a future-state mathematical-formalism; Spore canon operates without single-scalar-viability and uses honest-rigor cluster-counting + earning-tests + decline-with-triggers as the operational discipline for canon-state assessment. R-E7-viability-scalar carries `decline-with-trigger` disposition; re-opening trigger requires emergent operational pressure for cross-federation viability comparison metric (currently absent).

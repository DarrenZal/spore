## 2. C-claims (verbatim from extraction record)

All quotes verbatim from the extraction record §"Verbatim key claims". RID `orn:source:bsahely-2026-02-09-a-single-grammar-across-scale-invariant`; page anchors per the extraction record's `pdf-p<N>` notation.

- **C1** [anchor: §Abstract · orn:source:bsahely-2026-02-09-a-single-grammar-across-scale-invariant · pdf-p6] **Central thesis (LOAD-BEARING):** "This white paper articulates a single viability grammar governing systems across scale: invariants constrain matter, energy enacts those constraints, affect feels their pressure, cognition buffers risk, cultures symbolize regulation, and ethics emerges wherever systems recognize — or refuse to recognize — the limits that keep viable futures open."

- **C2** [anchor: §Abstract · orn:source:bsahely-2026-02-09-a-single-grammar-across-scale-invariant · pdf-p6] **Convergent-failure-mode reframing (LOAD-BEARING):** "the paper reframes chronic disease, psychological distress, institutional failure, ecological overshoot, and moral injury as convergent failure modes of the same underlying grammar: the erosion of margins and the mistaken belief that buffering confers exemption from constraint."

- **C3** [anchor: §Abstract · orn:source:bsahely-2026-02-09-a-single-grammar-across-scale-invariant · pdf-p6] **What-this-paper-is-not (methodological framing):** "This is not a reductionist theory, a moral ideology, or a speculative metaphysics. It is a diagnostic framework — testable, cross-disciplinary, and practical — that clarifies why intelligence and optimization often accelerate collapse when decoupled from viability, and how ethics emerges not from preference or authority, but from lived recognition of non-negotiable limits."

- **C4** [anchor: §Keywords · orn:source:bsahely-2026-02-09-a-single-grammar-across-scale-invariant · pdf-p6] Keywords (verbatim): "viability; invariants; optionality; affect; cognition; consciousness; ethics; systems theory; chronic disease; moral injury; institutional failure; ecological limits; artificial intelligence; life-value"

- **C5** [anchor: §Executive Summary "The Problem" · orn:source:bsahely-2026-02-09-a-single-grammar-across-scale-invariant · pdf-p6-7] **Failure-as-loss-of-alignment:** "The core failure is not lack of knowledge, effort, or optimization. It is loss of alignment with the conditions that make systems viable in the first place."

- **C6** [anchor: §Executive Summary "The Core Discovery" · orn:source:bsahely-2026-02-09-a-single-grammar-across-scale-invariant · pdf-p7] **6-rung Master Sequence Across Scale (LOAD-BEARING):** "Invariants constrain matter — only certain relations persist. Energy enacts constraints — margin enables persistence under change. Affect feels viability — global control signals arise when failure threatens the whole. Cognition buffers risk — time, simulation, and symbols expand optionality when margins allow. Culture symbolizes regulation — shared norms and institutions encode collective constraint memory. Ethics emerges from limits — obligation arises when systems recognize the consequences of violation. This sequence is ordered, non-arbitrary, and unavoidable. Higher layers buffer lower ones but cannot override them indefinitely. Collapse occurs when buffering is mistaken for exemption."

- **C7** [anchor: §Executive Summary "Why This Matters" · orn:source:bsahely-2026-02-09-a-single-grammar-across-scale-invariant · pdf-p7] **Intelligence-as-liability (LOAD-BEARING):** "Institutional and economic failure follow from optimization detached from invariants. ... Intelligence becomes a liability when it accelerates action faster than feedback can arrive."

- **C8** [anchor: §Executive Summary · orn:source:bsahely-2026-02-09-a-single-grammar-across-scale-invariant · pdf-p7] **Consciousness reframing:** "Consciousness itself is recontextualized: not as a metaphysical anomaly, but as an interface-bound necessity wherever global coordination under uncertainty and bounded computation make feeling unavoidable."

- **C9** [anchor: §Executive Summary "What This Paper Is — and Is Not" · orn:source:bsahely-2026-02-09-a-single-grammar-across-scale-invariant · pdf-p7] **Non-reductive + non-ideological framing:** "This work does not propose: a new ideology, a reduction of ethics to biology, a universal moral code, or a theory of consciousness divorced from lived reality."

- **C10** [anchor: §Cover subtitle · orn:source:bsahely-2026-02-09-a-single-grammar-across-scale-invariant · pdf-p1] **Title-derived thesis:** "Invariant Constraints, Viability, and the Emergence of Value from Matter to Civilization. Diagnosing the Rupture, Naming the Limits We Live Within, and Designing for Viable Futures."

- **C11** [anchor: §TOC §4.7 · orn:source:bsahely-2026-02-09-a-single-grammar-across-scale-invariant · pdf-p4 (TOC)] **Sequence-non-reversibility (LOAD-BEARING; title-surfaced, body-unread):** "Why the Sequence Cannot Be Reversed" — the master sequence is a causally-grounded ladder in which higher layers (cognition, culture, ethics) emerge from lower (invariants, energy, affect) but do not invert.

- **C12** [anchor: §TOC §14.4 · orn:source:bsahely-2026-02-09-a-single-grammar-across-scale-invariant · pdf-p4 (TOC)] **AI-as-amplifier-of-failure (title-surfaced, body-unread):** "Intelligence as an Amplifier of Failure" (Ch 14.4) — names the C-7 puzzle that "intelligence becomes a liability when it accelerates action faster than feedback can arrive" as a chapter-level theme.

## 4. Substrate-resonance map (descriptive)

This section reads the pp 6-8 / TOC substrate against Spore's post-Bundle-α canon (14 foundation docs / 9 primitives / **4** cross-cutting doctrines / 2 modes / 2 properties; concepts-yaml v24; 40 canon-decisions). Descriptive resonance only — no admission proposals.

### 4.1 The 6-rung Master Sequence vs Spore's foundation-doctrine cascade (shape-parallel, not 1:1)

Sahely's C-6 ladder (Invariants → Energy → Affect → Cognition → Culture → Ethics) is *non-reversible* (C-11): higher layers buffer lower ones but cannot override them indefinitely. Spore's foundation-doc cascade (F1 sensor-oracle-governance → F4 representation-authority → F6 failure-modes → F5 actuator-logic → F3 actor-governance → F8 external-validation) shares the *cascading-dependency / non-reversible* shape. **Crucially this is NOT a 1:1 mapping** — Sahely's rungs are operational-layers of *matter/energy/affect/cognition/culture/ethics*, whereas Spore's foundations are *coordination-grammar-layers* (sensor / representation / failure / actuator / actor-governance / validation). The contents map cross-categorically; only the *ordered-non-reversible-ladder* shape parallels. The non-reversibility claim (C-11, §4.7) is the same epistemic move as ADR-0042's `dag-delete-structural-legitimacy-promote` DAG-of-authority direction discipline at a different abstraction layer.

### 4.2 Margin / buffering-as-exemption ↔ margin-as-reserve (ADR-0089)

C-2's "erosion of margins and the mistaken belief that buffering confers exemption from constraint" and C-6 rung-2's "margin enables persistence under change" sit in the canon slot Bundle-α ADR-0089 scope-conditioned onto F9 maintenance-economics as **margin-as-reserve**. Sahely's framing (margin / reserve / slack as the precondition for persistence-under-change, whose erosion is the convergent failure mode) is operationally adjacent. Resolved by ADR-0089; cited descriptively, not as reinforcing pressure (single-source).

### 4.3 Intelligence-outruns-feedback ↔ sensor + representation doctrine weight (ADR-0073 / ADR-0074)

C-7 "intelligence becomes a liability when it accelerates action faster than feedback can arrive" articulates *why* sensor-grade attestation discipline (F1, ADR-0073) and representation-precedence (F4, ADR-0074) carry their canon-doctrine weight: when optimization decouples from invariants and acts faster than feedback returns, only sensor-input discipline + representation-precedence prevent the accelerated collapse. The C-12 chapter title "Intelligence as an Amplifier of Failure" + the AI-design applications (Ch 17, body-unread) are squarely in `agents-and-canons.md` positioning territory — descriptive resonance with Spore's agent-canons discipline, no canon-shift.

### 4.4 Convergent failure-modes ↔ F6 (cross-cut, not 1:1) + golden-calf-trap shape (ADR-0085)

C-2's 4-domain convergent-failure typology (chronic disease / psychological distress / institutional failure / ecological overshoot / moral injury) **cross-cuts** Spore's F6 8-category failure-modes (ADR-0075) rather than mapping 1:1 — Sahely cuts by life-domain, F6 cuts by coordination-grammar-locus. The *buffering-mistaken-for-exemption* mechanism (C-6 closing) is structurally a `golden-calf-trap`-shaped failure (the *shape-of* ADR-0048 substitution-trap, per ADR-0085 — NOT equivalent-to): an instrument (buffering / intelligence / the grammar itself) mistaken for the thing it was meant to serve (viability / the limits). C-3's "ethics emerges not from preference or authority, but from lived recognition of non-negotiable limits" + the implied discipline of holding optimization accountable to invariants resonates with `recursive-audit-method` (ADR-0085) — the discipline of applying the grammar's own question back to itself.

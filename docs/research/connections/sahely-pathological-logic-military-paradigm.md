### 4.2 McMurtry life-value lineage ↔ life-value-doctrine (ADR-0086)

C5 makes the lineage explicit: the "fallacies of the military paradigm" method (1989) is the precursor of the "fallacies in capitalist value-syntax" method (*Cancer Stage of Capitalism*). ADR-0086 admitted `life-value-doctrine` (McMurtry Life-Value Onto-Axiology) as the **4th cross-cutting doctrine** (sibling to `reproductive-commoning` / `boundary-commoning` / `care-commoning`), grounded in McMurtry's multi-book, multi-decade corpus operationalized across six Sahely papers. *Understanding War* (1989) is the earliest visible node of that corpus — the 35-year arc the extraction record surfaces (1989 → 1999/2014 *Cancer Stage* → 2003 *War State*). ### 4.3 "Self of National Self-Defence?" ↔ civil-commons (ADR-0087)

C6 interrogates the ontological status of the "self" that defends itself militarily — *who/what social subject is actually being secured?* The extraction record flags this as prefiguring McMurtry's later Civil-Commons-as-Social-Subject framing. ADR-0087 admitted `civil-commons` as a derived-glossary slug (alias `civil-commons-substrate`): McMurtry's term for the socially-constructed life-good-provisioning systems that constitute the primary economic/social infrastructure. The 1989 self-of-self-defence question is the early form of asking *what life-bearing social subject the security apparatus claims to protect* — descriptive resonance at the question level, with the answer (Civil Commons as the real social subject) crystallizing in later McMurtry work that ADR-0087 already cites.

### 4.4 Violence-triangle precursor ↔ perception-as-power (ADR-0090)

C3 (Sahely-curator) positions the 1989 book as a precursor to Galtung's 1990 violence-triangle; C2's "deep structure of war-making" and "war-making mentality so central to our culture" is exactly the *what-becomes-visible-as-legitimate-violence* concern that ADR-0090 scope-conditions onto F4 representation-authority (perception-as-power). ### 4.5 Persistence ≠ legitimacy ↔ structural-legitimacy (ADR-0042)

C1 distinguishes a "cooperative way of understanding war" from the persisting "defense and security" paradigm the book critiques — a paradigm can dominate the culture (persist) while failing to be the legitimate way of understanding the problem. This parallels Spore's `structural-legitimacy` foundation doc (ADR-0042): operational persistence is not legitimacy.

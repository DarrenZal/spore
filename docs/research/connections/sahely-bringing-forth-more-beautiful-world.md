## 4. Substrate-resonance map (descriptive)

Reads the pp 1-16 + TOC substrate against post-Bundle-α canon (14 foundation docs / 9 primitives / **4** cross-cutting doctrines / 2 modes / 2 properties / derived glossary slugs incl. `civil-commons` + `golden-calf-trap` + `recursive-audit-method`; concepts-yaml v24). Descriptive only.

### 4.1 Life-value / life-capital / civil-commons → `life-value-doctrine` (ADR-0086) + `civil-commons` (ADR-0087)

C3's "Answered Life = Civil Commons + Life Capital + Justice + Peace + Enoughness" and C12's "Civil Commons as Answering Field" are the McMurtry life-value onto-axiology and civil-commons frameworks recast in Sahely's call-and-answer semiotic grammar. Spore's Bundle-α admissions already absorb exactly this McMurtry territory: `life-value-doctrine` is the **4th cross-cutting doctrine** (ADR-0086, sibling to `reproductive-commoning` / `boundary-commoning` / `care-commoning` — a doctrine that operates *across* primitives, not a glossary slug), and `civil-commons` is the derived-glossary slug (ADR-0087, alias `civil-commons-substrate`) admitted under the McMurtry/Ostrom/Bollier cluster. So this treatise's central life-value crosswalk resonates with already-admitted canon at the doctrine layer (life-value) and the glossary layer (civil-commons) simultaneously. No canon-shift; independent-treatise reinforcement only.

### 4.2 Money-as-damaged-language → `golden-calf-trap` shape (ADR-0085)

C4's semiotic critique — price/equivalence/exchange as the "one-word language" that misnames value, "Money Becomes the Master Verb," "Colonization of Need," "Monetization of Care" — is a *misnaming / substitution* shape: a thin representational token (money) displaces the richer life-value it was meant to serve. Spore's `golden-calf-trap` (ADR-0085) names exactly this shape — the SHAPE-OF ADR-0048's `substitution-trap` (a simple-substitution of a sacralized token for the living value), surfaced by the `recursive-audit-method`. The resonance is at the trap-shape layer: money-as-master-verb is a textbook golden-calf substitution where the medium-of-exchange is mistaken for the value it represents. Descriptive; `golden-calf-trap` is the shape-of, NOT equivalent-to, the underlying `substitution-trap` mode.

### 4.3 Maturana bringing-forth-worlds + interbeing → care-cluster scope-condition (ADR-0088)

C2 elevates the Maturana-Dávila "bringing forth worlds in language" substrate to first position, and C8 adopts Eisenstein's Interbeing as the mythic-affective bridge. Both engage the *legitimacy-of-the-other / love-as-structural-coupling* territory that Spore's care-cluster scope-condition (ADR-0088, scoping ADR-0045 `care-commoning`) already covers. Maturanan love-as-legitimate-other-in-coexistence and Eisenstein's interbeing-as-relational-ground converge on the relational doctrine layer that `care-commoning` occupies. No canon-shift; the Bundle-α scope-condition already accommodates the Maturanan substrate.

### 4.4 Conservation-or-transformation + languaging → rule-in-use (ADR-0046) + participatory-sense-making (ADR-0063)

C2's "choose whether to conserve or transform [worlds-in-language]" resonates at substrate-philosophy layer with ADR-0046's `rule-in-use` vs rule-in-form distinction (operational conservation as the locus of identity). The languaging-as-world-bringing-forth claim sits inside the participatory-sense-making scope-condition (ADR-0063) on the Signal primitive. C5's Homo-Donans (giver-receiver as the human anthropology *replacing* Homo Economicus) reinforces the relational (not atomistic) reading of the Holon — adjacent to `relational-identity` (ADR-0051) and the Holon-irreducibility framing (ADR-0050). Descriptive; all already-scoped.

### 4.5 Positive-peace + enoughness → adjacencies, not pressure

C9's positive-peace-as-more-beautiful-world is civilizational-design framing that sits *adjacent* to `structural-legitimacy` (a peaceful-persistence-that-is-also-legitimate reading) but does not exert canon-pressure on its own. C3's "Enoughness" and C11's "life-value budgeting" touch reserve/sufficiency themes that ADR-0089's `margin-as-reserve` scope-condition on F9 maintenance-economics already frames at the maintenance layer — but enoughness-as-economic-wisdom is a *sufficiency-ceiling* claim, distinct from margin-as-resilience-reserve; the resonance is partial and descriptive. Note: this post's peace theme is *positive peace* (presence of life-coherent conditions), NOT Galtung *structural violence* / perception-as-power; ADR-0090 (perception-as-power scope-condition on F4) is the standpoint-epistemology / what-becomes-visible decision and is **not** the primary fit for this post's peace material. It is listed in `relates_to` only because §4.6 of the Phase-2 viability grammar (C-11 "Relevance asks: What becomes visible…") is the perception-thread Sahely's broader corpus carries; this treatise itself does not press it.

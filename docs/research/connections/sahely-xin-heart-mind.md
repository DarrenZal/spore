## 2. C-claims (verbatim, from the extraction record)

Anchors carry RID `orn:source:bsahely-2018-06-20-xin-the-heart-mind-and-feeling-tones` and `[html-section:<...>]`. All quotes verbatim from the extraction record. (Full sampled set C1–C19 lives in the extraction record; the load-bearing subset for substrate-resonance is reproduced here.)

- **C2** [LaViolette 1986; feeling tones as experiential units] [html-section:Teaching-with-Feeling]
  > "This theory conceives creative thoughts as emerging spontaneously from a fabric of sensation and emotion composed of experiential units called 'feeling tones.'… basic feeling tones, like the primary colors of the rainbow, combine in a nearly infinite number of ways to form a whole spectrum of shades and these represent all aspects of our internal and external experience."

- **C3** [LaViolette 1986; brain as self-weaving loom] [html-section:Teaching-with-Feeling]
  > "the brain may be conceived as a kind of sophisticated loom… these feeling tone tapestries have the unique ability of themselves being able to run the controls of the loom. Thus the fabric is able to weave itself in a self-determining fashion!"

- **C4** [LaViolette 1986; agrarian not programming model] [html-section:Teaching-with-Feeling]
  > "Teaching is not a programming process, the feeding of information into computer-like brains. Rather, it is an agrarian process — the acquisition of knowledge being likened to the raising of crops."

- **C5** [Gray 1979; repairing the Cartesian split] [html-section:Gray-abstract]
  > "This theory posits an integrating relationship between emotion and cognition, which repairs the age old Cartesian split between feeling and thinking."

- **C8** [Gray 1979; emotional-cognitive structure as stabilized pattern of flow] [html-section:Gray-IV]
  > "emotional-cognitive structures are essentially 'fliess-gleichgewichts'… introduced by Ludwig von Bertalanffy to describe the character of open systems, and may be translated as meaning 'stabilized pattern of flow'. This term captures the essential difference between living and non-living systems."

- **C9** [Gray 1979; feeling-drained cognition as dangerous] [html-section:Gray-VI]
  > "when ideas are drained of their feeling element they become pathological and dangerous… [permitting] man to manipulate symbols in such a way so as to allow him to carry out actions based purely on logical connectedness, without regard as to whether the results are destructive to society."

- **C10** [LaViolette 1979; cognition as order-through-fluctuation] [html-section:EPC-emergence-of-cognition]
  > "It is this spontaneous self-simplifying process which, I believe, constitutes cognition and which accounts for the emergence of creative thought in man."

- **C11** [LaViolette 1979; emergence is bottom-up] [html-section:EPC-emergence-of-cognition]
  > "cognition is not a top-down process; it is a bottom-up process. It is a phenomenon of emergence, of microlevel phenomena producing macrolevel effects. So, cognition is inherently stochastic."

- **C12** [LaViolette 1979; concepts freely created, not transmitted] [html-section:EPC-thought-and-symbolism]
  > "Concepts cannot be transmitted from one person's mind to another's like TV signals. They cannot be replicated, code and all, from the top-down as in programming a computer. They must be learned from the bottom-up. They must by freely created."

- **C13** [LaViolette 1979; the sick society / left-brain overdose (quoting Hazel Henderson)] [html-section:EPC-the-sick-society]
  > "Today we see our culture suffering from an overdose of left-brain hemisphere Cartesian cognition… we reward analysis and punish synthesis, while the interesting problems exist at the interfaces between the disciplines."

- **C14** [LaViolette 1979; comprehension as time-requiring system formation] [html-section:EPC-the-sick-society]
  > "Comprehension, being a process of system formation, requires a certain length of time to occur. Without taking time to contemplate and to let feelings organize cognitions into structured and meaningful forms, one is left basically with just a collection of cognitions."

- **C15** [LaViolette 1979; mind as autopoietic / self-producing] [html-section:EPC-thoughts-that-go-round]
  > "the fabric controls the loom and the loom weaves the fabric. The mind, therefore, is self-producing, or, in systems theoretic terminology, the mind is an autopoietic system. It is a reality system which creates its own form."

- **C16** [LaViolette 1979; mind evolving within body; mind-body problem] [html-section:EPC-thoughts-that-go-round]
  > "Mind may be regarded as evolving within body (or brain). The physical architecture of the brain and the biochemical behavior of nerve cells serve as a womb in which feelings may differentiate, combine, self-organize, and relationally increase in complexity."

- **C17** [LaViolette 1980; feeling-tone/waveform correlate; non-interreducibility] [html-section:Aha-feeling-tones]
  > "the basic dual entity: feeling tone/waveform may be regarded as the basic psycho-physical correlate of mental/physical experience… the feeling tone/waveform correlation thesis recognizes the non-interreducibility of mind and brain phenomena."

- **C19** [Sahely framing banner] [html-section:title]
  > "Xin (The Heart-Mind) and Feeling Tones: A Unifying Systems Theory Framework - TOWARD LIFE-KNOWLEDGE"

### 4.1 The core engagement — autopoiesis-enactive substrate (foundation-era anticipation)

The CORE Spore-canon engagement is that this 2018 assemblage gives an **early (foundation-era) articulation of the autopoiesis-as-self-production thesis** that Spore canon later acknowledged via the SCOPE-CONDITION triad ADRs 0062/0063/0064 (Membrane production-mode / Signal sense-making-mode / Field co-presence-mode):

- **ADR-0062 (Membrane-as-self-produced).** C15 ("the mind is an autopoietic system… a reality system which creates its own form") + C3 (the self-weaving loom: "the fabric is able to weave itself in a self-determining fashion") articulate the autopoiesis-active reading at the cognitive-system scale. This is the **same Maturana-Varela-lineage autopoiesis** Spore's ADR-0062 scope-conditioning was driven by — LaViolette cites Varela explicitly (source references include "Varela, F.G., Maturana, R., and Uribe, R."). The resonance is descriptive: Spore canonicalizes the dual production-mode of Membrane; LaViolette's mind-as-autopoietic is one early instance of the autopoiesis-active reading.

- **ADR-0063 (Signal participatory-sense-making).** C10–C12 (cognition as bottom-up, stochastic emergence; concepts as "freely created" not "transmitted… like TV signals") articulate the constitutively-interactive-emergence reading of meaning-making over the sender-receiver-transmission reading. C1 + C5 (the Chinese *xin* heart-mind / Gray's repair of the Cartesian split: cognition-and-emotion-coextensive) align with the **languaging/emotioning** frame the citation-map routes to ADR-0063. Descriptive resonance only.

- **ADR-0064 (Field co-presence-mode).** C3 + C16 (mind "evolving within body"; the brain as a "womb"/medium that provides "conditions such that complex emotional fabrics may weave themselves") articulate the medium-constitutes-the-system reading — the structural-coupling history that makes a medium into a niche. This is the **organism-medium** shape the citation-map routes to structural coupling (ADR-0050 + ADR-0062). Descriptive only.

### 4.2 Structural-legitimacy: stabilized-pattern-of-flow / persistence ≠ legitimacy

C8 ("emotional-cognitive structures are essentially 'fliess-gleichgewichts'… 'stabilized pattern of flow'… the essential difference between living and non-living systems") is a Bertalanffy open-systems claim. It resonates with **`spore.structural-legitimacy` (ADR-0042)** at the canon-discipline layer: Spore's structural-legitimacy doctrine holds **persistence ≠ legitimacy** (a structure that persists is not thereby legitimate; legitimacy is constituted, not merely endured). LaViolette/Gray's "stabilized pattern of flow" is the *open-systems* counterpart — a thing whose persistence is itself a dynamic achievement (flow-equilibrium), not a static given. Same generative posture (living systems persist by continuous re-constitution, not by inert durability), different register (cognitive-systems vs coordination-legitimacy). Resonance: descriptive; not admission-relevant.

### 4.3 Conservation-through-change → rule-in-use

C8 + C18 (gradual evolution "in which existing elements of consciousness would be for the most part preserved yet at the same time altered by the continually changing feeling tone content") articulate **conservation-through-change** — the organization is conserved while the structure changes. The citation-map routes conservation-through-change to **`rule-in-use` (ADR-0046 field-rule-level-stratification)**: the *rule-in-use* (what is actually enacted) can shift while the *rule-in-form* (the declared organization) is conserved, mirroring the structure-changes/organization-conserved distinction. Descriptive resonance at the substrate layer.

### 4.4 Feeling-as-expressive / what-becomes-visible → perception-as-power (ADR-0090)

C2 + C5 + C6 (feeling tones as the organizing/expressive substrate of thought; the primacy of feeling) resonate with **`expressive-power` (ADR-0048 mode; cited via ADR-0090 territory)** — "life articulating meaning, desire, perception" — at the modal layer. More pointedly, C9 + C13 (feeling-drained cognition becomes "pathological and dangerous"; the "overdose of left-brain Cartesian cognition" that "reward[s] analysis and punish[es] synthesis") articulate a **what-becomes-visible** claim: a mode of cognition that filters out the feeling-element renders certain destructive consequences invisible-to-the-actor. This is the structural shape **ADR-0090 (perception-as-power on F4 representation-authority)** scope-conditions — perception is power because what-becomes-visible is itself a contested, power-laden achievement (`constructed-power`). Resonance: descriptive; the 2018 cognitive-science framing is a foundation-era precursor of the perception-as-power concern, not a vocabulary candidate.

### 4.5 Feeling-drained-cognition as a trap-shape (ADR-0085) / substitution-trap

C9 ("when ideas are drained of their feeling element they become pathological and dangerous… manipulate symbols… purely on logical connectedness, without regard as to whether the results are destructive to society") describes a **failure-mode**: a cognition-system that substitutes symbol-manipulation for life-answerable judgment. This resonates with **`substitution-trap` (ADR-0048)** and with the trap-shape vocabulary of **ADR-0085** (golden-calf-trap as the shape-of substitution-trap; symbol-displaces-reality). Per the citation-map, trap-shape themes cite ADR-0085 (NOT equivalent-to ADR-0048's substitution-trap, but shape-of). The resonance is at **F6 failure-modes** (a feeling-drained symbol-manipulation regime is an F6-shape failure: the map displaces the territory). Descriptive; this is a foundation-era articulation of the symbol-displaces-reality trap, not new failure-language.

### 4.6 Agrarian teaching / comprehension-needs-time → maintenance-economics (ADR-0089)

C4 ("Teaching is not a programming process… it is an agrarian process — the acquisition of knowledge being likened to the raising of crops") + C14 ("Comprehension, being a process of system formation, requires a certain length of time") articulate a **margin/reserve/timing** claim: understanding requires slack-time (margin) and cannot be force-accelerated. This resonates with **`reproduction-continuity` (ADR-0049)** (the cross-episode reproductive labor of cultivating understanding over time) and with **ADR-0089 (margin-as-reserve on F9 maintenance-economics)** — the agrarian metaphor is precisely a maintenance-economics frame (sunlight/fertilizer/water/freedom-from-predators = the reproductive-infrastructure conditions for growth; rushing = margin-exhaustion). The "sick society" that "demand[s] efficiency [and] gets only shallowness" (C13–C14) is a margin-collapse diagnosis. Descriptive resonance at the F9/ADR-0089 layer.

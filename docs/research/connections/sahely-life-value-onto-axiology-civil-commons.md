## 2. C-claims (verbatim, McMurtry-primary)

All quotes verbatim from the extraction record §"Verbatim key McMurtry-primary claims." RID `orn:source:bsahely-2026-02-22-life-value-onto-axiology-civil-commons-mcmurtry-essays`. Attributed to **McMurtry** per §1.

- **C1** [anchor: §V "Being Human" · orn:source:bsahely-2026-02-22-life-value-onto-axiology-civil-commons-mcmurtry-essays · pdf-p29] **Life-value efficiency (LOAD-BEARING):** "A society is *life-value efficient* only so far as its rule system is structured to provide for access of its members to life goods through generational time. This structure of provision for the life capacities of its members to be fully alive rather than deprived is, in turn, what life-value theory designates as **the civil commons**."

- **C2** [anchor: §V "The Civil Commons" · …mcmurtry-essays · pdf-p29] **Canonical Civil Commons definition (HIGHEST LOAD-BEARING):** "The civil commons is defined as any and all social constructs which enable universal access to human life goods without which people's capacities are always reduced. Thus defined in logico-scientific principle, the civil commons can be recognised as the unseen *differentia* of the species which first emerges in language itself and distinguishes all subsequent supra-genetic evolution."

- **C3** [anchor: §V "The Civil Commons" · …mcmurtry-essays · pdf-p29] **Civil-commons ubiquity:** "Civil commons functions sustain the human condition remaining everywhere we look beneath the surface of corporate occupation and commodification for profit."

- **C4** [anchor: §V "The Civil Commons" · …mcmurtry-essays · pdf-p29-30] **Phenomenological argument:** "As I sit in my home, there is nothing I experience and need that is possible *without* the civil-commons infrastructures enabling it. The electricity flow to my word-processor, lamp and space-heater are built and guaranteed by publicly built public hydro sources, lines and safety regulations … could not occur without long civil planning, contribution and struggle to develop and retain every moment of it against privatization-for-profit forces."

- **C5** [anchor: §V intro · …mcmurtry-essays · pdf-p28] **Human Vocation (CANONICAL):** "A human vocation comes in as many forms as there are ways of contributing one's share to society by expressing one's own capabilities as a human being. It enables and obliges the provision of universal life necessities for others, and realizes oneself as human. It is the ultimate human life good and necessity because it links the rights of people to universal human goods to their obligation to contribute to them — the bridge from individual to society, is to ought, and humanity's development to better."

- **C6** [anchor: §V intro · …mcmurtry-essays · pdf-p28] **Disutility-of-work critique (LOAD-BEARING):** "Work is a 'disutility' to all ruling economic doctrine since Adam Smith, and only its selling for a price counts as work. … Grotesque consequences of economic organisation follow such as disqualifying the most basic economic contributions such as homecare and child upbringing as *not* work because it has not been bought and sold."

- **C7** [anchor: §V "Corporate-Rich War" · …mcmurtry-essays · pdf-p30] **Katrina / civil-commons destruction (CANONICAL example):** "New Orleans could not withstand an offshore hurricane with its stripped civil commons leaving nothing intact in its natural and built sea-delta infrastructures or community resources of rescue, health and housing to protect human life through natural time. The hurricane Katrina was not a natural disaster. It was a man-made one from the destabilization of hydrological cycles behind these events …"

- **C8** [anchor: §V "Corporate-Rich War" · …mcmurtry-essays · pdf-p30] **"Efficiency"-by-money-metric (LOAD-BEARING):** "In California private-profit control bankrupted the wealthiest state of the U.S. in the short and long term, and treated its citizens to endless blackouts and extortionate prices up to *200 times* the normal. … Higher efficiency in this system means more money for private money-sequencers than what they put in, the higher the ratio of self-take the more 'efficient'."

- **C9** [anchor: §V (Polanyi engagement) · …mcmurtry-essays · pdf-p32] **Polanyi-vs-McMurtry on civil-commons (LOAD-BEARING):** "Karl Polanyi's classic work, *The Great Transformation* (1944), is a compelling exception, but his emphasis is on *pre-market* community and stop-gap social measures since. … Marx and Marxism focus on class divisions and technological powers with no civil commons recognition anywhere."

- **C10** [anchor: §IV "Sufficiency/Insufficiency by Life Capacity Margins" (TOC pp 23-27) · …mcmurtry-essays · pdf-p23-27] **Life-capacity margins (TOC-attested; chapter body not read at extraction):** §IV enumerates "Universal Life Needs and Goods / Beyond the Invisible Hand / How to Test Universal Life Goods / Sufficiency/Insufficiency by Life Capacity Margins." ## 4. Substrate-resonance map (descriptive; cites admitted ADRs/slugs)

Read against resolved canon (concepts-yaml v24; 4 cross-cutting doctrines; foundation docs 14; canon-rebuild arc 40 decisions).

1. **C2 Civil Commons definition ↔ `civil-commons` slug (ADR-0087).** McMurtry's "any and all social constructs which enable universal access to human life goods" (C2) is the **canonical-original** of the slug admitted at ADR-0087. The slug's `one_line_definition` already names this McMurtry framing and cites it via Sahely-secondary notes (`sahely-money-growth-to-life-coherence` W4.1; `sahely-ethics-as-science-of-viability` W2.6); this PDF is the McMurtry-PRIMARY source those secondary citations resolve to. `civil-commons` is a **derived-glossary slug** (alias `civil-commons-substrate`), NOT a doctrine — the doctrine is `life-value-doctrine`.

2. **C1 + C5 life-value / human-vocation ↔ `life-value-doctrine` (ADR-0086).** McMurtry's "life-value efficiency" criterion (C1) and the Human Vocation bridge "from individual to society, is to ought" (C5) are McMurtry-primary substrate for the **4th cross-cutting doctrine** admitted at ADR-0086 — sibling to `reproductive-commoning` / `boundary-commoning` / `care-commoning`. The doctrine operates as a **lens over all nine primitives** (not a 10th primitive); its economics application-domain (W4.1) cites exactly this McMurtry corpus. No primitive-elevation; resolved.

3. **C3 + C4 + C6 civil-commons-as-reproductive-infrastructure ↔ F9 `maintenance-economics` (ADR-0079).** McMurtry's "everywhere we look beneath the surface" (C3), the phenomenological hydro-infrastructure argument (C4), and the disutility-of-work critique disqualifying homecare/child-upbringing as "not work" (C6) all sit on F9's `reproductive-infrastructure` + `compensation-pattern-layer` substrate. The `civil-commons` slug already records F9 as a `relates_to`-layer composition (no F9 narrative-extension required). Descriptive reinforcement; no canon-shift.

4. **C1 + C8 persistence-/profitability-vs-legitimacy ↔ `structural-legitimacy` (ADR-0042) + F4 `representation-authority` (ADR-0074).** "Life-value efficiency" (C1) is a structural-legitimacy criterion (a rule-system can persist while failing to provide life-goods access → persistence is not legitimacy, the ADR-0042 canon-work at economic-system scale). C8's "higher efficiency means more money for money-sequencers … the more 'efficient'" is a precise **declared-state vs instance-state divergence**: the declared metric ("efficient") diverges from the instance reality (citizens' life-capacity ruined) — exactly F4 representation-authority's (ADR-0074) fact-vs-specification territory. Both already-canonical; descriptive.

5. **C9 Polanyi/Marx civil-commons-blindness — single-source rigor anchor.** McMurtry positions Polanyi as a "compelling exception" and Marx/Marxism as having "no civil commons recognition anywhere" (C9). In the resolved cluster-counting, Polanyi composes with the broader McMurtry substrate under `life-value-doctrine` (C2 PARTIAL → folded into doctrine breadth per DECISION-BRIEF §6.2), while Bollier-Helfrich-Linebaugh is FULL for `civil-commons` standalone. This note does **not** re-run that count; it records that McMurtry himself frames his civil-commons concept as distinct from (not derivative of) Polanyi/Marx — which is why the resolved ADRs count McMurtry as a distinct C1 cluster.

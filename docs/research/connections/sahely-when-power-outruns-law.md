## 2. C-claims (verbatim from extraction record)

All quotes verbatim from the extraction record §"Verbatim key claims". RID `orn:source:bsahely-2026-01-04-when-power-outruns-law-venezuela-the`; page anchors per extraction record.

- **C1** [anchor: §Author's Foreword · orn:source:bsahely-2026-01-04-when-power-outruns-law-venezuela-the · pdf-p4] **LOAD-BEARING — medical-diagnostic framing of system failure:** "In medicine, we are taught to distinguish symptoms from causes, urgency from emergency, and intervention from harm. … When power overrides process in the name of necessity, the immediate outcome may feel decisive, but the long-term damage often goes unseen until it is too late to reverse."

- **C2** [anchor: §Author's Foreword · orn:source:bsahely-2026-01-04-when-power-outruns-law-venezuela-the · pdf-p4] International law as protective infrastructure: "Small states do not have the luxury of abstraction when international norms weaken. We experience disruption not as theory, but as grounded flights, disrupted supply chains, strained hospitals, sudden migration pressures … For us, international law is not an academic discipline; it is **protective infrastructure**."

- **C3** [anchor: §1 · orn:source:bsahely-2026-01-04-when-power-outruns-law-venezuela-the · pdf-p7] **LOAD-BEARING — law fails by drift not collapse:** "What we are witnessing now is not the sudden collapse of that order, but something subtler and more dangerous: the gradual normalization of exceptions. … Legal boundaries are not openly rejected; instead, they are reinterpreted, stretched, or bypassed in ways that preserve the language of law while hollowing out its function. … law does not fail all at once. It fails by drift."

- **C4** [anchor: §6.1 · orn:source:bsahely-2026-01-04-when-power-outruns-law-venezuela-the · pdf-p17] Law as restraint, not decoration (friction-as-feature): "Law was never meant to eliminate power. It was meant to **shape** it. Law introduces friction — procedures, thresholds, justifications — that slow decisions down and force reasons into the open. This friction is not a flaw; it is the point. … When power outruns law … it ceases to function as a restraint. … legality shifts from a guide to a gloss."

- **C5** [anchor: §6.3 · orn:source:bsahely-2026-01-04-when-power-outruns-law-venezuela-the · pdf-p17] Semantic drift as failure mode: "One of the most insidious effects of power outrunning law is the corruption of language. … 'Self-defense' no longer refers strictly to response against armed attack; it expands to include prevention, deterrence, and even punishment. 'Terrorism' shifts from a description of methods to a label for enemies. … When words lose stable meaning, argument becomes impossible. Disagreement is no longer about interpretation; it is about allegiance. At that point, law cannot mediate conflict — it can only rationalize it."

- **C6** [anchor: §6.4 · orn:source:bsahely-2026-01-04-when-power-outruns-law-venezuela-the · pdf-p17] **LOAD-BEARING — order without legitimacy is brittle:** "Power can create order without law. … The problem is that order without legitimacy is never self-sustaining. It relies on continuous enforcement, escalating coercion, and constant vigilance against dissent. Legitimacy, by contrast, allows order to reproduce itself with less force. People comply not only because they must, but because the system makes sense to them. When legitimacy erodes, enforcement costs rise."

- **C7** [anchor: §7 · orn:source:bsahely-2026-01-04-when-power-outruns-law-venezuela-the · pdf-p17] **LOAD-BEARING — small states as canary / early indicator:** "When power begins to outrun law, its earliest effects are rarely felt where decisions are made. They are felt where consequences land. Small states occupy that downstream position in the international system. They experience first what others will experience later."

- **C8** [anchor: §8.4 · orn:source:bsahely-2026-01-04-when-power-outruns-law-venezuela-the · pdf-p20] Truth as restraint; evidence as de-escalation infrastructure: "The discipline of truth does not guarantee justice. It does not prevent conflict. But it does impose restraint. It forces decision-makers to confront uncertainty rather than erase it. It creates space for alternatives to force — diplomacy, mediation, containment, or patience."

## 4. Substrate-resonance map (descriptive)

Read against the resolved post-Bundle-α canon (14 foundation docs / 9 primitives / 4 cross-cutting doctrines / concepts-yaml v24). Descriptive observations, not admission proposals.

### 4.1 "Law fails by drift" ↔ sensor-oracle slow-signal-degradation (ADR-0073, F1)

C-3's core diagnostic — *law does not fail all at once; it fails by drift; boundaries are reinterpreted, stretched, or bypassed in ways that preserve the language of law while hollowing out its function* — is the legal-order articulation of exactly the slow-signal-degradation concern F1 sensor-oracle-governance was admitted to govern (ADR-0073). F1's `longitudinal-attestation` slug exists precisely because a single point-reading can look healthy while the long-run attestation pattern degrades. Sahely's "drift" is the same shape at the international-law layer: the form persists (`language of law`) while the function (`restraint`) decays. No canon-shift; reinforcing cross-domain resonance.

### 4.2 "Order without legitimacy is brittle" ↔ structural-legitimacy (ADR-0042)

C-6 is the strongest resonance in the essay. *Order without legitimacy is never self-sustaining … legitimacy allows order to reproduce itself with less force* is — almost line-for-line — the persistence-is-not-legitimacy doctrine that Spore's `structural-legitimacy` foundation doc canonizes (ADR-0042). Sahely adds an operational-economics gloss Spore's foundation doc gestures at but does not foreground: *enforcement costs rise as legitimacy erodes*. The "reproduce itself with less force" framing also brushes against `replication-regime` (the labor by which patterns persist across time) — illegitimate order requires continuous re-enforcement labor; legitimate order reproduces cheaply. Reinforcing; no canon-shift.

### 4.3 "Small states as canary" ↔ external-witness + sensor early-indicator (ADR-0081, F8 / ADR-0073, F1)

C-7's *small states experience first what others will experience later; they occupy the downstream position where consequences land* is structurally the external-witness role F8 external-validation-loop admits (ADR-0081) plus the early-indicator framing in F1's sensor-failure taxonomy. The canon-shape match: a witness positioned downstream of where decisions are made detects systemic degradation before the decision-makers do — which is exactly why F8 admits external witnesses as a meta-canon feedback channel and why F1 cares about sensor placement. Reinforcing; no canon-shift.

### 4.4 "Law as friction" ↔ rule-stratification + rule-in-use/rule-in-form (ADR-0046)

C-4's *law introduces friction — procedures, thresholds, justifications — that slow decisions down and force reasons into the open … legality shifts from a guide to a gloss* maps cleanly onto ADR-0046's rule-stratification (the constitutional-rule level is the slow, deliberate, justification-forcing tier) AND onto the `rule-in-use` vs rule-in-form distinction: Sahely's "gloss" is precisely a rule-in-form that has detached from any rule-in-use. When law "remains present as language, as ritual, as paperwork" but "ceases to function as a restraint," the rule-in-form persists while the rule-in-use has evaporated. Reinforcing; no canon-shift.

### 4.5 "Semantic drift / what becomes visible" ↔ scope-conditioning discipline (ADR-0033) + perception-as-power (ADR-0090, weak)

C-5's *corruption of language — terms stretch and blur until disagreement is about allegiance not interpretation* resonates with the scope-conditioning discipline Spore canonized to stop its own vocabulary from over-claiming (ADR-0033 dual-axis pattern-recursion + the ADRs-0031/0032/0044 universality-overreach repairs). There is also a **weak** brush against ADR-0090's perception-as-power scope-condition on F4: C-5 ("words lose stable meaning") and C-7 ("consequences land downstream / first felt where they land, not where decided") both touch *what-becomes-visible-to-whom* and *whose standpoint registers the harm first*. This is held as a weak resonance only — Sahely's frame is semantic-capture-as-power, which is adjacent to but not the same operational concern as ADR-0090's representation-authority-and-standpoint scope-condition. No canon-shift; flagged as weak per `feedback_surface_vocabulary_vs_operational_concern.md` (surface-vocabulary brush, not operational-concern identity).

### 4.6 "Truth as restraint" ↔ representation-authority fact-vs-specification + validation-loop (ADR-0074, F4 / ADR-0081, F8)

C-8's *the discipline of truth … forces decision-makers to confront uncertainty rather than erase it; it creates space for alternatives to force* resonates with F4 representation-authority's fact-vs-specification text-type distinction (ADR-0074: factual reports are instance-authoritative, not declaration-overridable) and with F8's validation-loop (evidence subjected to external scrutiny constrains action). Sahely's "truth as restraint" and Spore's "facts cannot be specification-overridden" are the same discipline at different layers: you cannot legislate the territory by editing the map. Reinforcing; no canon-shift.

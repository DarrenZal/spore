## 2. C-claims (verbatim from extraction record)

All quotes verbatim from the extraction record's "Verbatim key claims" section. Anchored by `[anchor: §… · <RID> · pdf-p<N>]` with RID `orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-28-the-life-coherence-clinical-assessment-a`.

- **C-1** [anchor: §Abstract · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-28-the-life-coherence-clinical-assessment-a · pdf-p4] Complementary-not-replacement thesis: *"This white paper proposes the Life-Coherence Clinical Assessment as a complementary renewal of the clinical method. It does not replace biomedical diagnosis, urgent intervention, physical examination, investigation, or evidence-based treatment. Rather, it widens clinical attention from disease entities alone to the patterns through which adaptive margin, functional capacity, agency, relational participation, and practical possibility are progressively constrained."*

- **C-2** [anchor: §Executive Summary · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-28-the-life-coherence-clinical-assessment-a · pdf-p4] Disease-as-loss-of-life-capacity (central): *"Disease is not only a pathological entity located in an organ, biochemical pathway, or diagnostic code. It is also experienced as a loss of life-capacity: a narrowing of adaptive margin, function, agency, participation, rhythm, relationship, and possibility within a lived field."*

- **C-3** [anchor: §Executive Summary · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-28-the-life-coherence-clinical-assessment-a · pdf-p5] Patient-as-structurally-coupled-being: *"The patient is not an isolated disease-bearing body, nor merely a self-managing consumer of interventions. The patient is a living being structurally coupled to a world."*

- **C-4** [anchor: §2 What Is Life-Coherence Medicine · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-28-the-life-coherence-clinical-assessment-a · pdf-p10] Organism-niche miscoupling + explicit autopoiesis citation: *"In this sense, illness can be understood as a pattern of organism-niche miscoupling. The organism is not a machine operating in isolation. It is a living being structurally coupled to a niche: food systems, family systems, work demands, housing, climate, culture, institutions, technologies, social expectations, and histories of care or harm. Maturana and Varela's account of autopoiesis and structural coupling provides one biological lineage for understanding living beings not as detached mechanisms, but as organisms continuously constituted in relation to their worlds (Maturana & Varela, 1980, 1992)."*

- **C-5** [anchor: §2 What Is Life-Coherence Medicine · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-28-the-life-coherence-clinical-assessment-a · pdf-p10] Coherence ≠ perfection/harmony: *"The word coherence does not mean perfection, harmony, or the absence of struggle. Living systems are never static. They survive through continuous regulation, adaptation, repair, and reorganization. Coherence refers instead to a sufficiently viable alignment among the processes that allow life to continue... When these processes are sufficiently aligned, the person has margin. When they are persistently misaligned, adaptive capacity narrows."*

- **C-6** [anchor: §2 What Is Life-Coherence Medicine · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-28-the-life-coherence-clinical-assessment-a · pdf-p10] Anti-reductionism guardrail (does not dissolve biology into biography): *"This does not mean that all disease is caused by social context, lifestyle, or psychology. Such a view would be both clinically false and morally dangerous... Life-coherence medicine does not dissolve biology into biography. Rather, it asks how biology and biography meet in the living person."*

- **C-7** [anchor: §3 Definition and Scope · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-28-the-life-coherence-clinical-assessment-a · pdf-p12] Disease pattern vs constraint pattern (load-bearing distinction): *"A disease pattern explains what is biologically wrong. A constraint pattern explains why adaptive capacity is narrowing, why treatment is failing, why symptoms persist, why risk is escalating, or why healing has not become possible. Good clinical care often depends on seeing both."*

- **C-8** [anchor: §7.2 The Least-Forcing Intervention · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-28-the-life-coherence-clinical-assessment-a · pdf-p26] Least-forcing intervention (does not mean weak): *"A central principle of the Life-Capacity Repair Plan is the least-forcing intervention. This means choosing the smallest, most precise, feasible action that can restore margin without overwhelming the patient or destabilizing the system of care. Least-forcing does not mean weak treatment. In an emergency, the least-forcing intervention may be immediate hospitalization, oxygen, antibiotics, insulin, anticoagulation, surgery, or intensive care."*

- **C-9** [anchor: §7.3 Restoring Margin Before Demanding Change · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-28-the-life-coherence-clinical-assessment-a · pdf-p26] Sequence reversal (margin before demand): *"Life-coherence medicine reverses the sequence. Before demanding self-management, it asks whether the patient has enough margin to act. What must be relieved before this patient can participate in care? What burden is preventing change?... What is the first repair that makes the next repair feasible?"*

- **C-10** [anchor: §7.4 Feasibility as a Clinical Criterion · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-28-the-life-coherence-clinical-assessment-a · pdf-p26] Feasibility as a clinical criterion: *"In life-coherence medicine, feasibility is not an afterthought. It is a clinical criterion. A treatment plan should be evaluated by asking whether the patient can understand it, afford it, access it, remember it, tolerate it, fit it into daily life, return for monitoring, recognize danger signs, and survive contact with the patient's world. A plan that fails these questions may still be medically correct, but it is not yet coherent."*

- **C-11** [anchor: §10.1 Preserve Diagnostic Discipline · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-28-the-life-coherence-clinical-assessment-a · pdf-p31] Recursive-misuse safeguard (load-bearing): *"Every life-coherence assessment must begin with the possibility of disease, danger, and treatable pathology. The rule is simple: Life-field understanding must never be used to explain away danger. Context enriches differential diagnosis; it does not replace it."*

- **C-12** [anchor: §10.6 Respect Scope · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-28-the-life-coherence-clinical-assessment-a · pdf-p31] Bounded-responsibility clause: *"The clinician is not responsible for solving every determinant of health, but is responsible for not designing care as if those determinants did not exist."*

- **C-13** [anchor: §11 Conclusion · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-28-the-life-coherence-clinical-assessment-a · pdf-p32] Rigor-and-wholeness synthesis / completes-not-displaces (load-bearing closing thesis): *"Its central question — what pattern is constraining this person's capacity to live, adapt, heal, and participate? — does not displace the conventional clinical question, 'What disease is present?' It completes it... medicine does not have to choose between rigor and wholeness. The best medicine requires both. Rigor without wholeness can become fragmented, burdensome, and miscoupled from the patient's life. Wholeness without rigor can become unsafe."*

- **C-14** [anchor: §Glossary · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-28-the-life-coherence-clinical-assessment-a · pdf-p33] Adaptive Margin (glossary): *"The reserve that allows a person to absorb disturbance without collapse. Adaptive margin may be biological, emotional, cognitive, social, financial, relational, or practical."*

## 4. Substrate-resonance map (descriptive; cites resolved ADRs)

This reads the clinical paper against the **post-Bundle-α** canon (14 foundation docs / 9 primitives / **4 cross-cutting doctrines** / concepts-yaml v24 / 40 canon-decisions). Descriptive observations only.

1. **Disease-as-loss-of-life-capacity ↔ life-value-doctrine (ADR-0086).** C-2's reframe of disease as "loss of life-capacity... within a lived field" is the clinical-bedside instantiation of the **life-value-doctrine** admitted at ADR-0086 as the **fourth cross-cutting doctrine** (sibling to `reproductive-commoning` / `boundary-commoning` / `care-commoning`). This is doctrine-layer resonance, already canonical — no admission pressure. (Note: `life-value-doctrine` is a doctrine that the concepts-yaml governs as a `concepts:` reference; it is **not** a glossary slug.)

2. **Adaptive margin ↔ margin-as-reserve scope-condition on F9 (ADR-0089).** C-5 / C-8 / C-9 / C-14 articulate "adaptive margin" as the reserve that lets a living system "absorb disturbance without collapse." This is the clinical-domain instantiation of the **margin-as-reserve** scope-condition that ADR-0089 placed on the F9 maintenance-economics foundation doctrine. Sahely's "restore margin before demanding change" (C-9) is margin-as-reserve operating at the patient-care scale; Spore canon already holds the reserve concept at the maintenance-economics layer. `margin-as-reserve` is an ADR-0089 scope-condition, not a glossary slug — cited as ADR only.

3. **Care / margin-before-demand ↔ care-cluster scope-condition (ADR-0088).** C-3 (patient as structurally-coupled being, not self-managing consumer) and C-9 (relieve burden before demanding self-management) instantiate the asymmetric-care relation that ADR-0088 scope-conditioned onto `care-commoning` (ADR-0045). Maturana's biology-of-love / legitimacy-of-the-other substrate is the cited lineage; the care-cluster scope-condition is the resolved Spore home for it.

4. **Recursive-misuse safeguard ↔ trap-shape vocab + recursive-audit-method (ADR-0085).** C-11 ("life-field understanding must never be used to explain away danger") plus §10's five named dangers (vagueness, moralism, impracticality, overreach, therapeutic drift) are the clinical articulation of a grammar-must-not-be-misused discipline. This is adjacent to the **trap-shape vocabulary** and **recursive-audit-method** admitted at ADR-0085 — and to `golden-calf-trap` (the *shape-of* ADR-0048 `substitution-trap`, **not** equivalent-to it). Sahely's safeguard is a domain-specific clinical boundedness rule; it is **not** a new trap-shape and surfaces no admission pressure. The "apply the method's own discipline reflexively" move parallels `recursive-audit-method`.

5. **Organism-niche coupling + conservation-through-regulation ↔ Holon / Membrane / rule-in-use.** C-4's explicit Maturana/Varela autopoiesis citation reinforces Holon-irreducibility (ADR-0050) and the membrane-as-self-produced scope-conditioning (ADR-0062 R-Mem-1); this is the same Maturanan substrate as the 2026-05-21 viability-grammar anchor (C-9 there), here applied to clinical medicine rather than a diagnostic grammar.

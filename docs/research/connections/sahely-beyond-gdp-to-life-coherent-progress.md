## 2. C-claims (verbatim from extraction record)

All quotes verbatim from the 11-fact anchor-hand-curated extraction. RID `orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-13-from-beyond-gdp-to-life-coherent-progress`.

- **C1** [anchor: §Part VIII §8 · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-13-from-beyond-gdp-to-life-coherent-progress · pdf-p55] Four-substrate integration: "This framework rests on four integrated foundations. First, the UN Beyond GDP agenda establishes the global measurement opening… Second, McMurtry's life-value onto-axiology provides the evaluative criterion: what enables life-capacity is value; what reduces, disables, or destroys life-capacity is disvalue (McMurtry, 2011a, 2011b, 2018). Third, Galtung's peace theory deepens the moral and political field: peace requires the reduction of avoidable life-harm across direct, structural, cultural, ecological, institutional, commercial, and digitally mediated domains (Galtung, 1969, 1990). Fourth, Maturana's relational biology provides the epistemic and relational guardrail: worlds are brought forth through distinctions, language, emotion, and recurrent structural coupling."

- **C2** [anchor: §Part VIII §8.1 · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-13-from-beyond-gdp-to-life-coherent-progress · pdf-p56] The life-master question: "What must be recognized, named, measured, exposed, de-implemented, restored, redesigned, protected, coordinated, and learned so that life-capacity flourishes for all, within the web of life, now and for future generations?" — an 11-verb operational action stack.

- **C3** [anchor: §Part VIII §8.4 · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-13-from-beyond-gdp-to-life-coherent-progress · pdf-p59] Generative logic: "Exposure → Repair → Margins → Life-Capacity → Flourishing"; "Progress is real when harmful exposures are reduced, necessary exposures are made meaningful and supported, repair systems are accessible, margins are protected, and life-capacity expands."

- **C4** [anchor: §Part IX §9.1-§9.5 · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-13-from-beyond-gdp-to-life-coherent-progress · pdf-p63-68] 5-Layer Life-System Diagnostic refining (not replacing) the UN Beyond GDP Dashboard: "(1) Life-Value Foundation Layer / (2) Planetary Boundary Layer / (3) Life Capital Layer / (4) Civil Commons Layer / (5) [continued]". Each layer is an interpretive overlay on the existing UN dashboard.

- **C5** [anchor: §Part XV §15.1-§15.5 · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-13-from-beyond-gdp-to-life-coherent-progress · pdf-p104-107] Reframing the five governance verbs in life-coherent form: "Progress = expansion of life-capacity… Wealth = life capital, NOT accumulation… Peace = active reduction of avoidable life-harm… Efficiency = increasing provision of life goods with diminishing life-loss… Governance = coordination of life-enabling conditions through legitimate coexistence."

- **C6** [anchor: §Part XIV §14.4 · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-13-from-beyond-gdp-to-life-coherent-progress · pdf-p102] "Performative transformation" failure mode: "A society can speak of well-being while preserving austerity. / It can speak of sustainability while expanding extractive industries… / It can speak of equity while leaving power untouched. / It can speak of participation while consulting without transferring influence… / It can speak of peace while ignoring structural violence. / It can speak of capital while accumulating life-incoherent capital…"

- **C7** [anchor: §Part XIV §14.5 · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-13-from-beyond-gdp-to-life-coherent-progress · pdf-p103] Anti-utopian framing: "This is not utopian in the weak sense. It is practical because every policy already affects life-capacity. Every budget already distributes exposure, repair, and margins. Every institution already enables or reduces access to life goods… The life-coherent framework simply makes these consequences explicit and answerable."

- **C8** [anchor: §Part XIV §14.5 + §15.5 · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-13-from-beyond-gdp-to-life-coherent-progress · pdf-p103] Institutional "return to proper place": "The economy should provision life. / Technology should expand agency and care. / Law should protect dignity and commons. / Education should develop life-capacity. / Medicine should support healing and repair. / Governance should coordinate legitimate coexistence. / Measurement should reveal what life needs and whether it is being secured."

- **C9** [anchor: §Part XV closing · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-13-from-beyond-gdp-to-life-coherent-progress · pdf-p108] Inversion-of-justification: "Let us no longer ask life to justify itself before the economy. Let the economy, technology, law, policy, medicine, education, and governance justify themselves before life."

- **C10** [anchor: §Part XV §15.6 · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-13-from-beyond-gdp-to-life-coherent-progress · pdf-p107] Three-question measurement hierarchy: "GDP asks: How much economic activity is occurring? / Beyond GDP asks: Are well-being, equity, sustainability, and resilience being measured? / Life-coherent progress asks: Are the relations of society organized so that life can live, heal, participate, repair, and flourish?" — hierarchical-not-replacement (each layer expands the prior).

- **C11** [anchor: §Appendix A · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-13-from-beyond-gdp-to-life-coherent-progress · pdf-p109-110] Glossary defines 10+ core terms (Beyond GDP / Life-Coherent Progress / Life-Coherence / Life-Capacity / Life-Value / Life-Value Onto-Axiology / Life Goods / Universal Human Life Necessities / Life Capital / Life-Incoherent Capital / Civil Commons), with explicit vocabulary-evolution acknowledgement: "Earlier drafts used 'false capital'; the final framing uses 'life-incoherent capital' as the primary term, while retaining 'false capital' only where McMurtry's contrast is being explained."

## 4. Substrate-resonance map (descriptive)

### 4.1 Life-value onto-axiology ↔ `life-value-doctrine` (ADR-0086) — the load-bearing resonance

C1's second foundation and C5's reframing of Wealth as "life capital, NOT accumulation" are textbook McMurtry life-value onto-axiology: *what enables life-capacity is value; what reduces/disables/destroys it is disvalue*. This is precisely the territory Bundle α admitted as the **fourth cross-cutting doctrine**, `life-value-doctrine` (ADR-0086), sibling to `reproductive-commoning` / `boundary-commoning` / `care-commoning`. Per the consistency map, this is a **doctrine** (a lens operating across all nine primitives without primitive-elevation), **not** a glossary slug — even though the slug-token is registered in concepts-yaml v24, its canon-object-class is doctrine. The paper's C5 five-verb reframing (Progress/Wealth/Peace/Efficiency/Governance) is exactly the lens-across-domains shape the doctrine names; C11's glossary (Life-Value / Life-Capacity / Universal Human Life Necessities / Life Capital / Life-Incoherent Capital) is the doctrine's own vocabulary. No canon-shift: the doctrine already exists, and this paper is one of the seven Sahely bridge notes ADR-0086 cites in its substrate-citation UNION-set.

### 4.2 Civil Commons ↔ `civil-commons` derived-glossary slug (ADR-0087)

C4's "Civil Commons Layer" (diagnostic layer 4) and C11's glossary entry are the McMurtry-coined *socially-constructed life-good provisioning systems* that Bundle α admitted as the `civil-commons` **derived-glossary slug** (ADR-0087, alias `civil-commons-substrate`). Per the consistency map, this is a **slug**, NOT a doctrine — the doctrine is `life-value-doctrine`; `civil-commons` is one operational substrate the doctrine operates over at the economics and clinical-policy application-domains. No canon-shift.

### 4.3 "Performative transformation" ↔ `golden-calf-trap` + `recursive-audit-method` + F6 actor-capture

C6's nine-line catalogue of pathological transformations — *speak of equity while leaving power untouched; speak of participation while consulting without transferring influence; speak of peace while ignoring structural violence* — names institutions adopting reform-language while preserving extractive relations. This is the **`golden-calf-trap` shape** (ADR-0085): a corrective distinction captured, hardened, ritualized, and turned into a selection-mechanism, here at the policy layer where the very Beyond-GDP reform vocabulary becomes the cover for extraction. Per the consistency map, `golden-calf-trap` is the SHAPE-OF ADR-0048 `substitution-trap`, not equivalent-to it. The paper's antidote — C7's "make these consequences explicit and answerable" and C10's self-interrogating final measurement question — is the operational stance `recursive-audit-method` (ADR-0085) names: apply the grammar's own question to itself. At foundation layer this also composes with Spore F6 actor-capture (`spore.failure-modes`), generalized from institutional self-protection to **language-capture**. All already-canonical; this paper supplies a fresh policy-layer illustration, not new canon-pressure.

### 4.4 Margins-as-reserve and perception-as-power ↔ F9 / F4 scope-conditions (ADR-0089 / ADR-0090)

C3's "margins are protected" in the Exposure→Repair→Margins→Life-Capacity→Flourishing chain sits inside the **margin-as-reserve scope-condition on F9 maintenance-economics** (ADR-0089). Both already-resolved.

### 4.5 Care-coupling, conservation, legitimacy ↔ ADR-0088 / ADR-0046 / ADR-0042

## 2. C-claims (verbatim from extraction record)

All quotes verbatim from the extraction record §"Verbatim load-bearing claims". RID `orn:source:bsahely-2026-02-23-life-value-and-social-justice-the-civil` per the extraction record. Anchored `pdf-p<N>` per intake protocol. Each claim is attributed to its **original author** per the REPOST discipline in §1.

- **C1 (Noonan 2011 — Rawls life-blindness)** [anchor: §Noonan-introduction · orn:source:bsahely-2026-02-23-life-value-and-social-justice-the-civil · pdf-p2-3] "Rawls confuses the system-values of a liberal-capitalist society with primary life-values, and normalizes the pathological demand for endless accumulation as 'rational' regardless of the actual extent of one's own or others' need. Instead of defining and explicating what is actually primary to embodied rational life—the resources, relationships, institutions, and practices which support life and enable the development of its vital capacities—Rawls assumes as primary the prevailing system-values, even though these are demonstrably destructive of the natural system of life-support and billions of peoples' lives within different human societies."

- **C2 (Noonan 2011 citing McMurtry 1998 — life-ground)** [anchor: §Noonan-introduction · orn:source:bsahely-2026-02-23-life-value-and-social-justice-the-civil · pdf-p3] "The life-ground of value, in the most general terms, is everything that is required for the survival and development of human and ecological life and their life-support systems. Subjectively, it is 'the connection of life to life's requirements as a felt bond of being' (McMurtry, 1998, p. 23)."

- **C3 (Noonan 2011 — N-criterion: needs vs demands)** [anchor: §Noonan-introduction · orn:source:bsahely-2026-02-23-life-value-and-social-justice-the-civil · pdf-p3-4] "Life-requirements may be distinguished from consumer demands according to what McMurtry has called the N-criterion: those resources, relationships, practices and institutions whose deprivation causes harm in the form of loss of organic life-capacity. (McMurtry, 1998, p. 164)."

- **C4 (McMurtry 2010 via Noonan — Primary Value Axiom)** [anchor: §Noonan-introduction · orn:source:bsahely-2026-02-23-life-value-and-social-justice-the-civil · pdf-p4] "X is of value if and only if and to the extent that x consists in or enables a more coherently inclusive range of thought/experience/action (McMurtry, 2010, p.73)."

- **C5 (McMurtry 2010 via Noonan — two forms of life-value)** [anchor: §Noonan-introduction · orn:source:bsahely-2026-02-23-life-value-and-social-justice-the-civil · pdf-p4] "There are two essential forms of life-value: the instrumental or ultimate value of that which sustains and enables life, and the intrinsic value of the enjoyed expressions of the life-capacities of feeling, thought, and action (McMurtry, 2010, p.74)."

- **C6 (Noonan 2011 — life-coherence principle limits capacity-realization)** [anchor: §Noonan-introduction · orn:source:bsahely-2026-02-23-life-value-and-social-justice-the-civil · pdf-p6] "Life-grounded social justice therefore requires what McMurtry calls the life-coherence principle to establish the materially rational limits to individual and system demands. Hence social justice does not create the conditions for unlimited capacity expression, but capacity expression which enables planetary and human life as a whole (McMurtry, 2010, p. 97)."

- **C7 (Noonan 2011 — McMurtry's 40-year intellectual arc)** [anchor: §Noonan-introduction · orn:source:bsahely-2026-02-23-life-value-and-social-justice-the-civil · pdf-p6] "[The life-coherence principle] is the outcome of a philosophical struggle that McMurtry began 40 years ago through overland travels across over 80 countries in Africa, the Middle East and Asia. … The actual turn towards an explicit theorization of the life-ground of value, the real origin of life-value onto-axiology, did not occur until 1998 and the publication of Unequal Freedoms."

- **C8 (Noonan 2011 — critique of Sen's capability approach)** [anchor: §Noonan-introduction · orn:source:bsahely-2026-02-23-life-value-and-social-justice-the-civil · pdf-p5-6] "[Sen's] position is thus left open to the objection that not all lives that people have reason to value are good lives, either because the life contributes nothing good to the world or because it actively makes things worse for others."

- **C9 (Noonan 2011 — common wealth thesis)** [anchor: §Noonan-introduction · orn:source:bsahely-2026-02-23-life-value-and-social-justice-the-civil · pdf-p5] "Socially produced wealth is a collective creation of human beings labouring in a natural world they did not create. As a collective product, social wealth is properly understood as common wealth to be used for the sake of universal and comprehensive satisfaction of life-requirements."

## 4. Substrate-resonance map (descriptive)

This section reads the source's Noonan-introduction substrate against Spore's post-Bundle-α canon (14 foundation docs / 9 primitives / **4 cross-cutting doctrines** / 2 modes / 2 properties / concepts-yaml **v24** / 40 canon-decisions through ADR-0090). Layer-distinctness preserved — these are descriptive resonance observations, not admission proposals.

### 4.1 McMurtry life-value onto-axiology ↔ `life-value-doctrine` (ADR-0086)

C4's Primary Value Axiom (*"X is of value if and only if and to the extent that x consists in or enables a more coherently inclusive range of thought/experience/action"*) and C5's two-forms-of-life-value (instrumental + intrinsic) are the **canonical academic statement** of the doctrine Spore admitted at ADR-0086 as `life-value-doctrine` — the fourth cross-cutting doctrine, sibling to `reproductive-commoning` (ADR-0002), `boundary-commoning` (ADR-0003), and `care-commoning` (ADR-0045). The yaml definition's "value = enablement of range of being / doing / feeling / thinking" is a near-direct paraphrase of C4–C5. This 2011 Noonan introduction is one of the McMurtry-LVOA corpus sources the doctrine's C1 cluster already cites (multi-book multi-decade McMurtry corpus operationalized across the Sahely papers). **No canon-shift needed; the doctrine is resolved and this is its upstream anchor.**

### 4.2 N-criterion needs-vs-demands ↔ life-value-as-lens-over-primitives

C3's N-criterion (*"those resources, relationships, practices and institutions whose deprivation causes harm in the form of loss of organic life-capacity"*) is the operational test that distinguishes life-requirements from consumer demands. In Spore terms this is exactly the *evaluative substrate* `life-value-doctrine` was admitted to carry as Option C (lens-over-all-nine-primitives, not a 10th primitive): coordination outcomes are readable through the life-value lens — do they serve life-capacity (life-value) or cumulative monetary indicators decoupled from life-coherence (money-value)? The N-criterion is the discrimination rule the lens applies. **Descriptive resonance; no canon-shift.**

### 4.3 Civil Commons substrate ↔ `civil-commons` slug (ADR-0087) + common-wealth thesis

Sahely's added subtitle reads the issue as *"The Civil Commons Paradigm"* — curator framing (extraction R-3), but it points at a real substrate: McMurtry's life-support-systems (the *"resources, relationships, institutions, and practices which support life"* of C1; the *"life-support systems"* of C2) are precisely what Spore's `civil-commons` derived-glossary slug (ADR-0087) names — socially-constructed systems (public water, sanitation, public health, education, legal frameworks, environmental protections) enabling universal access to life-goods. C9's common-wealth thesis (*"social wealth is properly understood as common wealth to be used for the sake of universal and comprehensive satisfaction of life-requirements"*) sits inside that slug's public-infrastructure + legal-institutional layers. The slug's yaml already cites the McMurtry *Cancer Stage of Capitalism* (2013) + Ostrom + Bollier-Helfrich-Linebaugh cluster; this 2011 issue is an earlier point in the same McMurtry corpus. **No canon-shift; reinforcing the resolved slug at its academic-anchor source.**

### 4.4 Rawls/Sen critique + life-coherence limit ↔ structural-legitimacy + doctrine property

C1's Rawls critique (system-values normalizing endless accumulation while destructive of life-support) is a *persistence-is-not-legitimacy* claim at the political-philosophy layer: an arrangement (liberal-capitalist system-values) can persist and be called "rational" while failing the life-ground test. Spore's `structural-legitimacy` foundation doc (ADR-0042) does the same canon-work at the federation-governance layer. C6's life-coherence principle (capacity-expression bounded by *"what enables planetary and human life as a whole"*) is the materially-rational-limits property already folded into `life-value-doctrine` — the doctrine does not license unlimited capacity expression. C8's Sen critique (not all lives people have reason to value are good lives) is the life-ground correction the doctrine makes to capability-approach (the doctrine's C3 cluster explicitly includes Sen-Nussbaum capabilities via cross-paper density). **All three are resolved-canon reinforcements; no new pressure.**

### 4.5 Care-cluster touch (light) ↔ ADR-0088 scope-condition on care-commoning

C2's life-ground includes *"the connection of life to life's requirements as a felt bond of being"* and counts *relationships* among life-requirements. This lightly touches the care-cluster that ADR-0088 scope-conditioned onto `care-commoning` (ADR-0045). The touch is genuine but secondary: this issue's substrate is **life-value-economic** (McMurtry onto-axiology + civil-commons + common-wealth), not **care-relational-primary** (Maturana biology-of-love / legitimacy-of-the-other, which is where ADR-0088 lives). Recorded descriptively so the cross-reference is legible; not a load-bearing resonance for this note.

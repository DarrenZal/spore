## 2. C-claims (verbatim from extraction)

C-claim anchors carry RID `orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-20-biology-of-living-coordination` and `[pdf-p<N>]` per the canonical convention. All quotes are verbatim from the Phase 1 extraction record.

- **C-1** [LOAD-BEARING; methodological discipline] [pdf-p1, Cover epigraph]
  > "The organism does not belong to our framework. Our framework must remain answerable to the organism."

- **C-2** [central proposal] [pdf-p2, Abstract]
  > "This white paper proposes **the biology of living coordination** as a generative domain of inquiry for understanding life without reducing it to mechanism, physiology, subjective experience, emotion, culture, or observer-made categories alone. Its central concern is how living systems conserve organization, change structure, enact worlds, evaluate what matters, coordinate action, and generate explanations within histories of organism-medium coupling."

- **C-3** [LOAD-BEARING; Maturana canonical] [pdf-p2, Abstract]
  > "Humberto Maturana's biology of autopoiesis and cultural biology discloses the living being as a molecular autopoietic system that exists only in the conservation of its relation with a dynamic ecological niche."

- **C-4** [Noble canonical] [pdf-p2, Abstract]
  > "Denis Noble's Biological Relativity provides the causal architecture: no biological level has causal sovereignty, because living function is realized through reciprocal boundary conditions across molecular, cellular, tissue, organ, organismic, ecological, and social levels."

- **C-5** [LOAD-BEARING; Kauffman emotional-sentience] [pdf-p2, Abstract]
  > "Katherine Peil Kauffman's work on emotional sentience restores the affective-evaluative dimension of living agency, showing emotion as a self-regulatory sense through which organisms feel, evaluate, approach, avoid, preserve, and develop."

- **C-6** [five-domain enumeration] [pdf-p2, Abstract]
  > "The paper organizes this inquiry around five domains of distinction: **living constitution**, **organism-medium coupling**, **multi-level causal realization**, **affective valuation and emotioning**, and **observer languaging and distinction-making**."

- **C-7** [CANONICAL OPERATIONAL CLAIMS; health-disease-healing reframe] [pdf-p3, Abstract close]
  > "Health is reframed as coherent transition; disease as discoordination, narrowing, or locked transition; healing as restored movement; care as structural coupling; public health as protection of living conditions; and civilization as an extended niche that may become salugenic or pathogenic."

- **C-8** [inquiry commitment] [pdf-p3, Abstract close]
  > "The aim is not to offer a closed theory of life, but a disciplined grammar for inquiry. Its guiding commitment is that living systems must not be forced to fit our distinctions; our distinctions must remain answerable to life."

- **C-9** [LOAD-BEARING; dynamic ecological niche; organism-niche unity] [pdf-p4, Executive Summary]
  > "More radically, Maturana shows that the organism does not live in an abstract environment. It lives only in a dynamic ecological niche that arises with its living. Organism and niche are therefore understood as a dynamic ecological organism-niche unity. This makes life fundamentally relational without dissolving the organism into its surroundings."

- **C-10** [Maturana observer-discipline] [pdf-p4, Executive Summary]
  > "Maturana also gives the inquiry its epistemological discipline. Observers do not stand outside life looking at it from nowhere. They make distinctions in language, within emotioning, for purposes of action and explanation. Scientific categories are therefore not rejected, but they must be held with humility. They are instruments, not possessions."

- **C-11** [3-thinker synthesis disclosure] [pdf-p4, Executive Summary]
  > "Together, these three thinkers disclose a wider field. Maturana shows that life is autopoietic and organism-niche constituted. Noble shows that living is causally realized through multi-level biological relativity. Kauffman shows that living agency is affectively valenced: the organism does not merely interact with its world; it feels, evaluates, approaches, avoids, remembers, and develops through what matters to it."

- **C-12** [five-domain definitions] [pdf-p4, Executive Summary]
  > "The first is **living constitution**. This asks what must be conserved for a living unity to continue living. The central distinction is that structure changes, while organization must be conserved."
  > "The second is **organism-medium coupling**. This asks how the organism exists in relation with the medium that makes its living possible. The environment is not a passive external container; it becomes a niche through the organism's living, sensing, acting, and history of structural coupling."
  > "The third is **multi-level causal realization**. This asks how living is enacted through molecular, cellular, tissue, organ, organismic, ecological, and social levels. This is where physiology, systems biology, and medicine remain indispensable."
  > "The fourth is **affective valuation and emotioning**. This asks how the organism feels, evaluates, and enacts what matters. Emotioning is not treated as a secondary mental event. It is the valenced relational tone through which worlds of action become possible or impossible."
  > "The fifth is **observer languaging and distinction-making**. This asks what distinctions are being made, from what emotioning, for what action, and with what consequences. This domain keeps the inquiry reflexive and prevents 'living coordination,' 'life-coherence,' or any other term from becoming a new rigidity."

- **C-13** [LOAD-BEARING; wu-wei as practical posture; NOVEL CANONICAL TERM] [pdf-p5, Executive Summary close]
  > "The paper also introduces **wu-wei** as a practical posture of non-forcing participation. Wu-wei does not mean biological non-intervention. It means action proportionate to the living situation, guided by the system's constraints, margins, timing, and next possible transition. The principle is to use the minimum sufficient force required to protect living and open the next coherent movement."

- **C-14** [LOAD-BEARING; methodological commitment, repeated] [pdf-p5, Executive Summary close]
  > "Its deepest methodological commitment is this: **Life must not be forced to fit our distinctions; our distinctions must remain answerable to life.**"

- **C-15** [LOAD-BEARING; central reframing question] [pdf-p11, Part I §1]
  > "From this starting point, the central question changes. We no longer ask only, 'How does the body work?' We ask a wider question: **How does living remain possible through changing coordinations of body, medium, feeling, action, language, and distinction?**"

- **C-16** [LOAD-BEARING; five-domain hierarchy refusal] [pdf-p12, Figure 1 caption]
  > "The biology of living coordination is organized around five distinguishable but inseparable domains: living constitution, organism-medium coupling, multi-level causal realization, affective valuation and emotioning, and observer distinction-making. The figure avoids a hierarchy: no domain is sovereign. Instead, each domain helps disclose how living systems conserve organization, change structure, enact worlds, evaluate what matters, coordinate action, and generate explanations within histories of organism-medium coupling."

## 4. Substrate resonance against Spore canon

This is the descriptive map of how Sahely's apparatus engages Spore's post-Phase-4 canon. Per `feedback_intake_to_vocab_admission_program.md` 3-layer separation: cluster-counting verdicts belong to Layer 2 DECISION-BRIEF, not here.

### 4.1 The core engagement — autopoiesis-enactive substrate

The CORE Spore-canon engagement of this paper is that it provides **Sahely's canonical articulation of the autopoiesis-enactive substrate** that Spore canon already acknowledged via three SCOPE-CONDITION ADRs in the autopoiesis-enactive-tension canonical-resolution triad:

- **ADR-0062** (Membrane-as-self-produced) added a primitive-bullet scope sub-bullet to Membrane naming the dual production-mode (canonical-layering vs autopoiesis-active). C-3 ("the living being as a molecular autopoietic system that exists only in the conservation of its relation with a dynamic ecological niche") + C-9 (organism-niche unity) operationalize the autopoiesis-active reading at a depth Spore canon-body did not articulate: the *unity* claim — organism and niche co-arising — is the substrate for what "autopoiesis-active Membrane" *is* when applied to commons / governance / coordination instances. Sahely's substrate is Maturana-Varela-Thompson canonical; Spore's scope-conditioning was driven exactly by this enactive tradition (per ADR-0062 §"Tradition citation breadth").

- **ADR-0063** (Signal participatory-sense-making) added a primitive-bullet scope sub-bullet to Signal naming sender-receiver-transmission vs constitutively-interactive-emergence. C-10 (observer as distinction-maker in language within emotioning) + C-12 (domain 5: observer languaging and distinction-making, "what distinctions are being made, from what emotioning, for what action, and with what consequences") + C-16 (five-domain non-hierarchical inseparability) operationalize the constitutively-interactive-emergence reading. Sahely's reflexivity-as-discipline framing ("Scientific categories are therefore not rejected, but they must be held with humility. They are instruments, not possessions" — C-10) extends Spore's scope-conditioning without contradicting it.

- **ADR-0064** (Field co-presence-mode) added a primitive-bullet scope sub-bullet to Field naming text-authoritative vs co-presence-requiring. C-9 (organism-niche unity arising with the organism's living) + C-12 (domain 2: organism-medium coupling, "the environment is not a passive external container; it becomes a niche through the organism's living, sensing, acting, and history of structural coupling") operationalize the co-presence-requiring reading. The structural-coupling history that makes an environment into a niche is the substrate for what "co-presence-requiring Field" *is* in commons/coordination instances.

The autopoietic-active-vs-canonical-layering tension Spore canonicalized at ADRs 0062/0063/0064 is *exactly* the substrate Sahely operationalizes via "**The organism does not belong to our framework. Our framework must remain answerable to the organism.**" (C-1). Spore's three SCOPE-CONDITION ADRs and Sahely's methodological discipline are **mutually reinforcing** at the canon-discipline layer: Spore's discipline says "don't collapse the dual production-mode / sense-making-mode / co-presence-mode into either single reading," and Sahely's says "don't force life to fit your distinctions; let distinctions remain answerable to life." The same generative posture in two registers.

### 4.2 Multi-level causal realization → foundation-doctrine layering

C-4 + C-11 + C-12 (domain 3) articulate Noble's biological-relativity claim: no biological level has causal sovereignty; living function is realized through reciprocal boundary conditions across molecular / cellular / tissue / organ / organismic / ecological / social levels. This engages **F4 representation-authority** (the fact-vs-specification text-type distinction and inter-layer precedence doctrine; no layer is sovereign) at a substrate-resonance layer, not at a vocabulary-admission layer. The two share a generative posture (multi-level realization without single-level sovereignty) without claim-level overlap. F4's appeal-protocol §5.3 has its own scope (text/graph/sensor/attestation/agent-summary layers in coordination-grammar context), distinct from Noble's biological-causal layers. Resonance: descriptive; not admission-relevant.

### 4.3 Affective valuation → expressive-power mode

C-5 + C-11 + C-12 (domain 4) restore "the affective-evaluative dimension of living agency" via Kauffman: emotion as self-regulatory sense through which organisms feel / evaluate / approach / avoid / preserve / develop. This is structurally homologous to **ADR-0048 Johar expressive-power mode** ("life articulating meaning, desire, perception, dissent; vitalizes"). Spore's expressive-power is a mode-across-primitives (a quality-of-operation) not a primitive itself; Kauffman's affective-valuation is similarly a *dimension of living agency*, not a separate thing. Same shape; different substrate (Kauffman biological-organism; Johar political-social-institutional). The resonance is at modal-articulation layer.

### 4.4 Health-disease-healing operational reframe

C-7 articulates clinical operationalization in six paired definitions: **Health = coherent transition / Disease = discoordination, narrowing, or locked transition / Healing = restored movement / Care = structural coupling / Public health = protection of living conditions / Civilization = extended niche (salugenic or pathogenic)**. Each definition engages Spore canon-territory:

- *Health-as-coherent-transition* resonates with **ADR-0049 reproduction-continuity** (the cross-episode time-scale primitive verb: coherent transition is the operational shape reproduction-continuity preserves).
- *Disease-as-narrowing / locked-transition* resonates with **F6 failure-modes §6.4 scale-transition** and **§6.7 actor-capture** (narrowing and lockup are F6-shape failure-categorizations) plus the substitution-trap concept (`substitution-trap` slug from ADR-0048).
- *Healing-as-restored-movement* resonates with **F5 actuator-logic response-categories** R1-R4 (acknowledge, contest, amend, escalate — operational responses that restore movement).
- *Care-as-structural-coupling* — **specific canon-pressure candidate** because ADR-0045 admitted `care-commoning` as cross-cutting doctrine via Held / Tronto / Kittay / Federici lineage; Sahely adds **Maturana's structural-coupling** as a tradition-anchor for care that ADR-0045 does not cite. The substrate match is operational (care AS the practice through which structural coupling is sustained), not merely vocabulary.
- *Public health = protection of living conditions* engages **F3 actor-governance §4.5/§6** (governance-bodies receive feedback) and **F8 external-validation-loop** (meta-canon receiving feedback from external witnesses) at the framing-note layer.
- *Civilization as extended niche (salugenic or pathogenic)* engages **F6 failure-modes** at scale (civilization-scale niche can fail to coordinate / can narrow / can lock); resonates with `substitution-trap` named as canon-legible failure mode complementing ADR-0005 power-capture.

### 4.5 The wu-wei practical posture

C-13 introduces *wu-wei* as a practical posture of non-forcing participation with the principle "minimum sufficient force required to protect living and open the next coherent movement." This is a NOVEL canonical term in the Sahely corpus, not directly mapped to existing Spore vocabulary. It engages **F5 actuator-logic** (response-doctrine when sensor-evidence disagrees with declared intent) and **F4 representation-authority** (appeal-protocol with proportionate intervention) at the **canon-method** layer — Spore's foundation-docs already specify proportionate response, but do not name a posture-term for the *minimum-sufficient* discipline. Honest-rigor note: *wu-wei* is single-source (Sahely-only at present, with deep historical roots in Daoist tradition but not as articulated for biological-coordination); cluster-counting at Layer-2 DECISION-BRIEF would likely surface ≤1 non-Sahely full cluster within Spore's substrate-relevant traditions (the Daoist tradition itself is rich, but its operational mapping to biological-coordination grammar is Sahely-original).

### 4.6 The 3-thinker synthesis as such

C-11 makes the synthesis claim: "Together, these three thinkers disclose a wider field." The Maturana / Noble / Kauffman 3-thinker synthesis is **Sahely-original integration**; each underlying tradition is independently citable (autopoiesis is widely cited in Spore's vocabulary already — `autopoiesis` slug at concepts-p2p-wiki.yaml entry; biological-relativity is mature in Noble's own corpus; emotional-sentience is canonical in Kauffman's own corpus), but **the synthesis as such** is Sahely-only at this moment of writing. This is load-bearing for any DECISION-BRIEF Layer-2 cluster-counting on Sahely-substrate canon-pressure candidates: per ADR-0054 (rewilding-decline-with-triggers) and ADR-0069 (four-enabling-conditions-decline-with-triggers) precedent, single-source-from-primary-inspiration is honestly handled as decline-with-triggers in the *first* admission attempt, with re-opening triggers when ≥2 non-Sahely full clusters articulate the same synthesis-shape independently.

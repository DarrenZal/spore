## 4. Substrate-resonance map (descriptive)

This reads the extraction-record substrate against Spore's post-Bundle-α canon (concepts-yaml v24; 4 cross-cutting doctrines; 40 canon-decisions through ADR-0090; 14 foundation docs). Descriptive only.

### 4.1 Pathological vs enabling war ↔ F6 failure-modes + F4 representation-authority (already-resolved)

C-3's distinction between **pathological war** (organized killing of humans) and **enabling war** (organized campaigns against disease, destitution, ecological degradation) maps cleanly onto two Spore foundation surfaces. *Enabling war* is **failure-aware civilizational repair** — it engages F6 failure-modes (ADR-0075) directly: it is a coordinated response to recognized failure-categories (disease, destitution, environmental collapse) rather than a homicidal frame. *Pathological war* is the inverse: a Field-level rule-set that conserves homicidal social patterns and represents them as legitimate, which Spore reads through F4 representation-authority — the authority by which a homicidal social pattern declares itself a "just war." This is exactly the descriptive overlap the extraction record's R-3 already flagged; nothing new is proposed.

### 4.2 Constructed enemies ↔ perception-as-power scope-condition on F4 (ADR-0090, already-resolved)

C-4 — the national enemy as *"a political construction used to justify militarization"* — is a perception-as-power claim: what **becomes visible** as a threat, and to whom, is itself an exercise of power that governs which coordination (militarization) gets authorized. Spore already scope-conditioned this concern onto F4 representation-authority at ADR-0090 (perception-as-power). McMurtry's constructed-enemy analysis is a war-domain instance of the same Galtung-adjacent structural-violence / standpoint-epistemology concern that ADR-0090 admitted. The claim that the constructed enemy *"frequently poses a greater threat to a nation's own citizens than any foreign power"* is the perception-as-power inversion in its sharpest form: the perceptual frame, not the foreign actor, is the load-bearing threat.

### 4.3 Military-paradigm-as-mythic-frame ↔ golden-calf-trap SHAPE + recursive-audit-method (ADR-0085, already-resolved)

C-1 (military paradigm *falsely equates* war with mass homicide) + C-4 (constructed enemies) describe a self-justifying frame that substitutes a partial idol (the homicidal paradigm) for the life-protective end it claims to serve. This is **SHAPE-OF** the `golden-calf-trap` vocab admitted at ADR-0085 (the golden-calf-trap names the shape of ADR-0048's substitution-trap; it is **not equivalent-to** it). McMurtry's whole method — interrogating whether the war-frame conserves or negates life — is a `recursive-audit-method` instance: applying the life-ground question to the paradigm itself. Per the SHAPE-OF discipline, this is descriptive resonance, not an admission that McMurtry's war-paradigm critique IS the golden-calf-trap; it shares the shape.

### 4.4 Life-value substrate ↔ life-value-doctrine (ADR-0086) + civil-commons (ADR-0087) (already-resolved)

C-2 (structures "locked" into a homicidal framework because it serves ruling elites) and C-5 (the arms race as an economic engine profiting from high per-unit prices, obsolescence, and state-guaranteed financing) are McMurtry's **life-value-vs-money-value** substrate in the war domain: money-value indicators (arms profitability) decoupled from and predating life-value (the lives the structure destroys). This is the **same McMurtry life-value cluster** that served as the primary admission substrate for `life-value-doctrine` (ADR-0086, the 4th cross-cutting doctrine sibling to reproductive-commoning / boundary-commoning / care-commoning) and for `civil-commons` (ADR-0087, the derived-glossary slug for life-protective collective provisioning). C-3's *enabling war* is civil-commons-as-life-protection in McMurtry's own register. Because this is the same author-substrate already admitted, it adds **no independent cluster weight** — it is a facet of the cluster, not a second cluster.

### 4.5 Agent/structure distinction ↔ structural-legitimacy (ADR-0042) (descriptive)

C-6's claim that a just response should *"target disabling social patterns and coercive structures rather than the individual human beings … who are often involuntary participants"* is a structural-legitimacy move: it locates legitimacy in the pattern, not the individual, and refuses to treat persistence of a coercive structure as legitimacy. Combined with C-2 ("locked" structures persisting because they serve elites), this parallels Spore's `structural-legitimacy` framing that a structure may persist while failing the legitimacy test. Cross-domain reinforcement only.

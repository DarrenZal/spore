## 2. C-claims (verbatim from extraction record)

All quotes verbatim from the extraction record. These are **curated third-party text** (CARTA editorial / journal abstracts), anchored by `[html-section:]`. They represent Sahely's curation signal, not Sahely-authored claims.

- **C1** [html-section:CARTA — Domestication and Human Evolution] "The domestication of other species has played an undeniably central role in the evolution of modern humans, and in our planetary dominance and success."

- **C2** [html-section:CARTA — Domestication and Human Evolution] "Recently, a convergence of views has led to the notion that the study of animal domestication may tell us something not only about our relationship with domesticated species since perhaps at least the Pleistocene, but also about our own evolution as a species in the more distant past."

- **C3** [html-section:Robert Franciscus — Craniofacial Feminization] **LOAD-BEARING — social-tolerance / reduced-aggression:** "He then describes a promising model for the advent of facial diminution, which suggests that anatomically modern humans represent a ‘self-domesticated’ species where selection for increased social tolerance led to growth and developmental alterations producing craniofacial “feminization,” which itself results in a phenotypic signal of reduced aggressiveness."

- **C4** [html-section:Tecumseh Fitch — Domestication Syndrome and Neural Crest Cells] "Tecumseh Fitch (Univ of Vienna) hypothesizes that the selection for tameness during early stages of domestication led to delayed maturation and reduced output of the adrenal component of the “fight or flight” response, via reduced neural crest input."

- **C5** [html-section:Kazuo Okanoya — Vocal Behavior in Finches] **LOAD-BEARING — language ↔ socio-emotional:** "He shows that evolution of song complexity involves not only factors related to sexual selection and species identification, but also to socio-emotional factors due to domestication. He then speculates that language evolution in humans might also be based on sexual selection and self-domestication."

- **C6** [html-section:Richard Wrangham — Did Homo Sapiens Self-Domesticate?] **LOAD-BEARING — self-domestication definition:** "He defines “self-domestication” as the evolution of a reduced propensity for reactive aggression (compared to an immediate ancestor), without the active involvement of another species."

- **C7** [html-section:Richard Wrangham — Did Homo Sapiens Self-Domesticate?] **LOAD-BEARING — communal-sanction mechanism:** "He then shows that communal sanctions practiced by hunter-gatherers, which depend on proactive aggression, provide a leading candidate mechanism selecting against high levels of reactive aggression."

- **C8** [html-section:Richard Wrangham — Did Homo Sapiens Self-Domesticate?] **LOAD-BEARING — cooperation underpinning:** "He therefore proposes that human self-domestication is an ironic consequence of a particularly well-developed facility for proactive aggression, and concludes that humans did indeed self-domesticate, providing a critical underpinning for inter-individual tolerance and cooperation."

- **C9** [html-section:Further Resources — Theofanopoulou et al. 2017 abstract] "The results obtained suggest that (paleo-)genomic data can be exploited to complement the fossil record and support the idea of self-domestication in Homo sapiens, a process that likely intensified as our species populated its niche."

### 4.1 Self-domestication / cooperation ↔ structural-coupling (ADR-0050 + ADR-0062)

The post's core curated thesis — Homo sapiens self-domesticated toward *reduced reactive aggression, increased social tolerance, and cooperation* (C3+C6+C8) — is the biological seed of the Maturanan structural-coupling substrate that Spore canon already engages at two layers: ADR-0050 (joint sociality / Side-B at the operational layer) and ADR-0062 (Membrane-as-self-produced, organism co-constituting its medium). Self-domestication is precisely organism-medium co-constitution: the species reshapes its own *social niche* (communal sanctions, C7) which in turn selects the species. The resonance is genuine but already-admitted substrate; nothing new is implicated.

**Lossy bits:** Spore's `structural-coupling` is engaged at the coordination-grammar layer (how holons and their media co-constitute through ongoing operations). The post's structural coupling is evolutionary-developmental (neural crest, craniofacial feminization, adrenal "fight or flight" attenuation). The two converge on *self-shaping cooperative organism* from different substrates; the convergence is cross-tradition reinforcement, not new pressure, and one curation does not move any cluster count.

### 4.2 Communal sanctions ↔ rule-in-use (ADR-0046)

The post supplies a paleo-anthropological instance of the same shape (norm enforced *in practice* selects against reactive aggression).

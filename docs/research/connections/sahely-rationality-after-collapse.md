## 2. C-claims (verbatim from extraction record)

All quotes verbatim from extraction record §"Verbatim key claims". RID `orn:source:bsahely-2025-12-28-rationality-after-collapse-upgrading`; page anchors per the extraction record. **LOAD-BEARING** flags mark the claims most engaged by §3.

- **C-2** [anchor: §Abstract · orn:source:bsahely-2025-12-28-rationality-after-collapse-upgrading · pdf-p3] **LOAD-BEARING — axioms-not-misapplication thesis:** "This white paper argues that the problem lies not in misapplication or moral failure, but in the **axioms of rationality embedded in dominant decision models themselves**. By auditing the hidden assumptions of game theory, the paper shows that it is structurally blind to life necessities, commons, prevention, and long-term viability. As a result, it cannot detect the conditions of its own failure."

- **C-3** [anchor: §Abstract · orn:source:bsahely-2025-12-28-rationality-after-collapse-upgrading · pdf-p3] **LOAD-BEARING — life-range-expansion redefinition:** "Drawing on John McMurtry's Life-Value Onto-Axiology, the paper proposes a constructive upgrade: redefining rationality in terms of **life-range expansion** — the preservation and growth of the coherent capacities for thought, felt being, and action across time. It replaces equilibrium with viability as the primary success criterion and introduces universal life necessities as non-negotiable constraints on rational choice."

- **C-4** [anchor: §Abstract · orn:source:bsahely-2025-12-28-rationality-after-collapse-upgrading · pdf-p3] **LOAD-BEARING — central claim:** "Its central claim is practical and urgent: **rational systems that cannot see life cannot sustain it — and therefore cannot sustain themselves**."

- **C-5** [anchor: §Executive Summary · orn:source:bsahely-2025-12-28-rationality-after-collapse-upgrading · pdf-p4] **LOAD-BEARING — life-blind-by-construction:** "The paper's core diagnosis is that dominant models of rational choice — especially game theory — are **life-blind by construction**. They assume self-maximizing agents, fixed preferences, and payoff optimization within given rules, while excluding from consideration the life-support conditions that make rational agency possible in the first place. Universal life necessities, commons, prevention, and long-term viability are treated as externalities rather than governing constraints."

- **C-6** [anchor: §Executive Summary · orn:source:bsahely-2025-12-28-rationality-after-collapse-upgrading · pdf-p4] **LOAD-BEARING — cannot-recognize-own-failure:** "As a result, these models cannot recognize when 'rational' strategies cumulatively destroy the conditions of life. Collapse appears as an external shock rather than a predictable outcome. This structural blindness explains repeated failures in pandemic preparedness, climate mitigation, financial stability, and environmental protection."

- **C-7** [anchor: §Executive Summary · orn:source:bsahely-2025-12-28-rationality-after-collapse-upgrading · pdf-p4] Four-axiom upgrade: "Within this framework: Value is defined as that which enables a more coherently inclusive range of thought, felt being, and action. Universal life necessities function as non-negotiable constraints, not tradeable preferences. Rational action is redefined as that which preserves and expands future life-range. **Equilibrium is replaced by viability as the primary criterion of success.**"

- **C-12** [anchor: §10 · orn:source:bsahely-2025-12-28-rationality-after-collapse-upgrading · pdf-p14] **LOAD-BEARING — life-support conditions as explicit model state:** "A life-value-coherent model begins by redefining the game space itself. Instead of assuming a fixed arena with given rules and payoffs, the model explicitly represents the **life-support conditions that make the game possible**. These conditions — public health capacity, ecological regeneration rates, social trust, knowledge commons — are not background constants. They are dynamic stocks of life capital that can grow, degrade, or collapse depending on collective behavior."

- **C-13** [anchor: §10 · orn:source:bsahely-2025-12-28-rationality-after-collapse-upgrading · pdf-p14] **LOAD-BEARING — viability thresholds as invalid-strategy filters:** "Agents still pursue goals, but their strategy sets are constrained by viability thresholds. Strategies that undermine essential life capacities are excluded as invalid, just as strategies that violate physical constraints are excluded in engineering models. This is not moral censorship; it is **rational boundary setting**."

- **C-14** [anchor: §10 · orn:source:bsahely-2025-12-28-rationality-after-collapse-upgrading · pdf-p14] Cooperation as rational default: "When life conditions are explicit, cooperative behavior often emerges as the rational default, because the preservation of shared life capital directly benefits all agents' future agency. Institutions, regulations, and commons governance appear not as distortions of rationality, but as **rational infrastructure**."

- **C-15** [anchor: §10 · orn:source:bsahely-2025-12-28-rationality-after-collapse-upgrading · pdf-p14] **LOAD-BEARING — asymmetric power-responsibility:** "Such a model also incorporates asymmetry and power explicitly. Actors with disproportionate capacity to damage life systems are subject to stricter constraints, reflecting the reality that **unequal power entails unequal responsibility**. Rationality is thus re-anchored in real-world conditions rather than abstract symmetry."

- **C-16** [anchor: §10 · orn:source:bsahely-2025-12-28-rationality-after-collapse-upgrading · pdf-p14] **LOAD-BEARING — new metric:** "In this framework, strategic intelligence is measured not by winning the game, but by **keeping the game alive**."

- **C-18** [anchor: §11 · orn:source:bsahely-2025-12-28-rationality-after-collapse-upgrading · pdf-p15] Conservative-in-deepest-sense reframe: "It is conservative in the deepest meaning of the word: it seeks to conserve the conditions of life. The choice is not between markets and planning, competition and cooperation, freedom and constraint. The choice is between **a rationality that cannot see life and one that cannot ignore it**."

- **C-19** [anchor: §Afterword · orn:source:bsahely-2025-12-28-rationality-after-collapse-upgrading · pdf-p16] **LOAD-BEARING — AI systematically dangerous within a life-blind game:** "An AI that optimizes within a life-blind game does not become neutral or objective; it becomes **systematically dangerous**, precisely because it cannot recognize the conditions of its own failure."

## 4. Substrate-resonance map (descriptive)

Read against the resolved post-Bundle-α canon (concepts-yaml v24; 14 foundation docs; 9 primitives; **4** cross-cutting doctrines; 2 modes; 2 properties). Descriptive resonance only — no admission proposals.

### 4.1 Life-blind-by-construction → ADR-0085 trap-shape + ADR-0090 perception-as-power

C-2/C-5/C-6 diagnose game theory as "life-blind by construction" — it "cannot detect the conditions of its own failure" because life necessities are treated as externalities rather than governing constraints. This is a clean cross-tradition exemplar of the **trap-shape vocabulary** admitted at ADR-0085: the diagnosis names a grammar (game-theoretic rationality) that systematically excludes the conditions of its own validity — the `golden-calf-trap` shape (the SHAPE-OF ADR-0048's `substitution-trap`, not equivalent-to it). The proposed fix — auditing the hidden axioms so the model can see what it excludes — instantiates `recursive-audit-method`. The perceptual register of the diagnosis ("structurally blind"; "cannot recognize"; "collapse appears as an external shock") engages **ADR-0090 perception-as-power** (scope-condition on F4 representation-authority): what a decision model renders visible or invisible is itself a distribution of power, and a model that cannot represent life-support conditions cannot govern by them.

### 4.2 Life-range expansion / universal life necessities → ADR-0086 life-value-doctrine

C-3/C-7 carry the McMurtry substrate directly: rationality redefined as "life-range expansion — the preservation and growth of the coherent capacities for thought, felt being, and action across time," with "universal life necessities" as "non-negotiable constraints, not tradeable preferences." This is precisely the substrate ADR-0086 admitted as Spore's **4th cross-cutting doctrine** (`life-value-doctrine`), sibling to `reproductive-commoning` / `boundary-commoning` / `care-commoning`. `life-value-doctrine` is a doctrine (operating across primitives), not a glossary slug — this post is one of the source articulations that the doctrine admission rests on. No re-admission; the doctrine is already canon.

### 4.3 Commons as rational infrastructure → ADR-0087 civil-commons + ADR-0089 margin-as-reserve

C-12/C-14 frame "public health capacity, ecological regeneration rates, social trust, knowledge commons" as "dynamic stocks of life capital" and re-cast "institutions, regulations, and commons governance" as "rational infrastructure" rather than distortions of rationality. The commons register engages the `civil-commons` derived-glossary slug (ADR-0087, alias `civil-commons-substrate`). The "dynamic stocks that can grow, degrade, or collapse" + C-13 "viability thresholds" + C-7 equilibrium→viability engage **ADR-0089 margin-as-reserve** (scope-condition on F9 maintenance-economics): viability thresholds are the reserve/margin below which essential capacities collapse, and strategies that breach them are excluded as invalid ("rational boundary setting," not moral censorship).

### 4.4 Asymmetric power-responsibility + cooperation-as-default → ADR-0047 / ADR-0050

C-15 "unequal power entails unequal responsibility — actors with disproportionate capacity to damage life systems are subject to stricter constraints" maps to the **ADR-0047 Layer-2** power decomposition (`asymmetric-commitment`; the Kittay/Folbre/Baier asymmetric-vulnerability cluster already supports this layer). C-14 "cooperation emerges as the rational default" reinforces **ADR-0050 joint-commitment**: cooperation is not an anomaly requiring special explanation once shared life capital is in the model. Both are already-canonical; Sahely supplies an independent-tradition (life-value game-theoretic) articulation, not new pressure.

### 4.5 Keeping the game alive → ADR-0049 reproduction-continuity; governing-frame → ADR-0046; persistence≠legitimacy → ADR-0042

C-16 "strategic intelligence is measured not by winning the game, but by keeping the game alive" is the operational-pressure cognate of the **reproduction-continuity** 8th primitive (ADR-0049) — coordination success measured by the cross-episode persistence of the conditions of coordination, not within-episode payoff. C-11/C-18 "what changes is the governing frame within which these tools operate" parallels **ADR-0046** rule-in-use vs rule-in-form (the frame-in-use is the operational locus, not the formal tool-set). C-4 "rational systems that cannot see life… cannot sustain themselves" parallels **ADR-0042 structural-legitimacy**: operational persistence is not legitimacy, and a system that persists by eroding its own life-ground is not thereby legitimate.

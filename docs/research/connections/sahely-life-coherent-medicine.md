## 2. C-claims (verbatim from extraction record)

All quotes verbatim from the extraction record §"Verbatim key claims". Each carries RID `orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-28-life-coherent-medicine-healing-the-organ` and the `pdf-p<N>` anchor.

- **C1** [anchor: §Abstract · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-28-life-coherent-medicine-healing-the-organ · pdf-p6] Central claim: "The central claim is that medicine becomes life-coherent when it remains scientifically disciplined while becoming answerable to the living capacities it exists to protect."

- **C2** [anchor: §Preface · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-28-life-coherent-medicine-healing-the-organ · pdf-p4] **LOAD-BEARING — the guiding question of the whole manuscript:** "Does this expand or reduce the conditions required for life to live, heal, participate, and flourish? That is the question life-coherent medicine asks medicine to carry."

- **C3** [anchor: §Executive Summary §2 · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-28-life-coherent-medicine-healing-the-organ · pdf-p7] Health / Healing / Flourishing as life-capacity: "Health as life-capacity enabled. Healing as life-capacity restored. Flourishing as life-capacity expressed in dignity, relation, meaning, participation, and ecological belonging."

- **C4** [anchor: §Appendix A Glossary · Life-Capacity · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-28-life-coherent-medicine-healing-the-organ · pdf-p304] **LOAD-BEARING — canonical definition:** "Life-Capacity: The capacity of persons, communities, and living systems to live, repair, regulate, relate, learn, participate, belong, and flourish within the web of life."

- **C5** [anchor: §Executive Summary §3 · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-28-life-coherent-medicine-healing-the-organ · pdf-p7] **LOAD-BEARING — organism–niche structural coupling:** "The patient is an organism-person structurally coupled with food, air, water, microbes, housing, work, family, care, culture, law, economy, technology, climate, ecology, and meaning. ... It means that disease is embodied in living systems that are continuously shaped by relations with their worlds."

- **C6** [anchor: §9 Capture Modes · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-28-life-coherent-medicine-healing-the-organ · pdf-p69] **LOAD-BEARING — capture-mode definition:** "A capture mode occurs when a system's tools, incentives, language, measures, procedures, or power relations become detached from the life they are supposed to serve. Something designed to help begins to dominate."

- **C7** [anchor: §9 Capture Modes · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-28-life-coherent-medicine-healing-the-organ · pdf-p69] Capture does not require bad intentions: "Capture does not require bad intentions. That is why it is so dangerous. ... Life-coherent medicine must therefore ask not only: What disease is present? But also: What system is making this disease easier to conserve?"

- **C8** [anchor: §9 Capture Modes · Metric Capture · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-28-life-coherent-medicine-healing-the-organ · pdf-p71] **LOAD-BEARING — "the metric becomes the master":** "Metric capture occurs when institutions begin optimizing for indicators rather than for life-capacity. ... The metric becomes the master. ... If the measurable drives out the meaningful, care becomes life-blind."

- **C9** [anchor: §9 Capture Modes · Measurement Violence · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-28-life-coherent-medicine-healing-the-organ · pdf-p70] Measurement violence — "the map injures the territory": "measurement becomes violent when it reduces living reality to what the system can count, then treats everything outside the measure as secondary, irrelevant, or nonexistent. ... Measurement violence occurs when the map injures the territory."

- **C10** [anchor: §Appendix A Glossary · Civil Commons · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-28-life-coherent-medicine-healing-the-organ · pdf-p306] **LOAD-BEARING — McMurtry lineage:** "Civil Commons: Shared life-support systems that enable access to life goods without requiring private wealth, privilege, or market power as the condition of access."

- **C11** [anchor: §Appendix A Glossary · Commons Enclosure · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-28-life-coherent-medicine-healing-the-organ · pdf-p306] The counter-concept: "Commons Enclosure: The privatization, degradation, commodification, or restriction of shared life-support systems such as water, food, care, education, public knowledge, ecological stability, and public space."

- **C12** [anchor: §Appendix A Glossary · Miscoupling · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-28-life-coherent-medicine-healing-the-organ · pdf-p305] **LOAD-BEARING — persistence ≠ life-coherence:** "Miscoupling: A recurrent organism–niche relation that preserves short-term viability while progressively reducing life-capacity."

- **C13** [anchor: §Appendix A Glossary · Boundary-Coherence · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-28-life-coherent-medicine-healing-the-organ · pdf-p306] Boundary-coherence (identity-with-openness): "Boundary-Coherence: The organism's capacity to conserve identity while remaining open to nourishment, microbes, relation, learning, repair, and ecological exchange."

- **C14** [anchor: §Appendix A Glossary · Minimum Sufficient Force · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-28-life-coherent-medicine-healing-the-organ · pdf-p308] Minimum-sufficient-force ethic: "Minimum Sufficient Force: The clinical ethic of acting strongly enough to protect life and support healing, while avoiding unnecessary burden, coercion, overmedicalization, or treatment-related miscoupling."

- **C15** [anchor: §Appendix D Minimum Sufficient Force Table · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-28-life-coherent-medicine-healing-the-organ · pdf-p317] Margin / sufficiency maxim: "Under-force abandons life to danger. Over-force makes care itself a burden. Minimum sufficient force protects life while preserving the conditions for healing."

- **C16** [anchor: §Executive Summary §5 · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-28-life-coherent-medicine-healing-the-organ · pdf-p8] Exposure / repair / margins principle: "Health is sustained when exposure remains within restorative capacity. ... Disease, distress, dysfunction, and breakdown become more likely when exposure repeatedly exceeds repair and margins collapse."

- **C17** [anchor: §20 The CARE Method · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-28-life-coherent-medicine-healing-the-organ · pdf-p179] **LOAD-BEARING — "beautiful but unusable":** "Without method, the framework risks becoming beautiful but unusable. ... Patients do not need a framework that names complexity but leaves them without a next step. Health systems do not need language that expands vision without helping action."

- **C18** [anchor: §1 Why Medicine Needs a Life-Coherent Turn · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-28-life-coherent-medicine-healing-the-organ · pdf-p17] Power is not orientation: "Medicine has never been more powerful. ... But power is not the same as orientation. A medicine may become technically powerful while losing sight of the living whole it exists to serve."

- **C19** [anchor: §Appendix E Six-Level Evidence Ladder · Responsible Translation Rule · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-28-life-coherent-medicine-healing-the-organ · pdf-p318] Epistemic safeguard ("coherence is not proof"): "Responsible Translation Rule: Say clearly whether a claim is established, probable, plausible, uncertain, contested, or metaphorical."

2. **Civil commons ↔ civil-commons slug (ADR-0087).** C-10 ("shared life-support systems that enable access to life goods without requiring private wealth, privilege, or market power as the condition of access") and its counter-concept C-11 (commons enclosure) map 1:1 to the `civil-commons` **derived-glossary slug** (alias `civil-commons-substrate`) admitted via ADR-0087 from the McMurtry/Ostrom/Bollier cluster. This manuscript's Appendix A is among the densest single-source articulations of the civil-commons concept in the corpus.

3. **Capture modes ↔ golden-calf-trap + recursive-audit-method (ADR-0085).** C-6's capture-mode definition ("something designed to help begins to dominate") and its seven-mode taxonomy (Measurement Violence / Metric Capture / Implementation Violence / Commercial Capture / Epistemic Capture / Algorithmic Capture / Commons Enclosure) are shape-of the `golden-calf-trap` admitted via ADR-0085 — which is itself the **SHAPE-OF** ADR-0048's substitution-trap (a grammar/tool designed to serve becoming an object of worship/domination), **not equivalent-to** it. C-8 ("the metric becomes the master") is the metric-capture face of that shape. C-7's diagnostic move ("What system is making this disease easier to conserve?") and the Responsible-Translation discipline (C-19) are kin to the `recursive-audit-method` admitted in the same ADR — the practice of turning a system's own diagnostic question back on the system. Both are already canonical; the manuscript adds a clinical-systems enumeration of capture forms.

4. **Margins + measurement ↔ ADR-0089 + ADR-0090 (Bundle-α scope-conditions).** C-15/C-16 + the restorative-margins triad ("the reserves that allow persons, families, institutions, and ecosystems to absorb disturbance without collapse") map to the `margin-as-reserve` scope-condition on F9 maintenance-economics (ADR-0089). Both Bundle-α scope-conditions already carry this territory; the manuscript supplies medicine-domain exemplars.

5. **Organism–niche coupling + persistence≠legitimacy ↔ ADR-0050/0062/0042/0046.** C-5 organism–niche structural coupling and C-13 boundary-coherence ("conserve identity while remaining open") reinforce Holon-irreducibility (ADR-0050) + Membrane-as-self-produced scope-conditioning (ADR-0062) — already-canonical structural-coupling language. No canon-shift; reinforcing cross-domain articulation only.

### 4.1 Galtung structural-violence at peace-civilization scale ↔ C1 R-Trap-cluster (honest 6th-cluster question)

DECISION-BRIEF §4.2 enumerated **5 full clusters** for C1 R-Trap-cluster admission: (1) Johar/Sufi via ADR-0048 existing; (2) Sahely-Maturana school; (3) Post-Marxist critical theory (Marx → Lukacs → Frankfurt → Gramsci → Foucault → Bourdieu, treated as 1 genealogical cluster per honest-rigor aggregation); (4) Postcolonial critique (Fanon → Spivak → Said → Mbembe → Quijano → Mignolo); (5) State-legibility / bureaucratic-form critique (Scott + Graeber + Illich). The honest question this anchor raises: **does Galtung's direct/structural/cultural violence triad applied at peace-civilization scale constitute a genuinely independent 6th cluster, or is it absorbable into Post-Marxist Cluster 3?**

**Per `feedback_surface_vocabulary_vs_operational_concern.md` + anti-enthusiasm-override discipline, honest per-Cluster verification:**

- **Lineage-genealogy**: Galtung founded the Peace Research Institute Oslo (PRIO) in 1959 and the *Journal of Peace Research* in 1964. His Norwegian-peace-research tradition (extending through Brian Martin's later work + Kevin Clements + Olivia Rutazibwa's decolonial peace work + the broader Nordic + UN-system peace-studies subfield) is **genealogically distinct** from the Frankfurt-school continental Marxist tradition. Galtung engaged Marx's thought but built peace-research as a distinct discipline with its own institutional infrastructure, journal, methods (peace-by-peaceful-means; conflict-transformation; positive peace), and successor scholars. The direct/structural/cultural violence triad (Galtung 1969 *"Violence, Peace, and Peace Research"* + 1990 *"Cultural Violence"*) is the field's foundational typology. **Distinct lineage** by standard academic-history criteria.

- **Operational-concern match**: Galtung's *structural violence* names categorization-becomes-life-destruction at population-scale (categories of personhood + entitlement embedded in institutional arrangements → measurable shortfall in human flourishing relative to potential). Galtung's *cultural violence* names the symbolic-layer that legitimizes structural-violence (religion, ideology, language, art, science used as moral-cover for harm). Both are **operationally identical** to the substitution-trap shape: corrective-distinction (the institutional category that was introduced to enable coordination) becomes the domination it was introduced to correct (the categorization-becomes-violence-distribution-mechanism). At the peace-civilization scale this anchor operates, Galtung's triad is the precise cross-tradition articulation of the operational concern that ADR-0048 substitution-trap names at coordination-grammar scale.

- **6th-cluster verdict (honest)**: **Yes**, on lineage-genealogy + operational-concern-match grounds. Norwegian-peace-research is a recognized academic subfield with multi-decade independent corpus (Galtung *Peace by Peaceful Means* 1996; *A Theory of Conflict* 2010; Lederach *Building Peace* 1997; Galtung-Webel *Handbook of Peace and Conflict Studies* 2007). It is not Sahely-Maturana (Cluster 2); not Johar/Sufi (Cluster 1); not Frankfurt-derived continental Marxism (Cluster 3, which Galtung pre-dates by ~30 years in publication terms and whose institutional infrastructure is German/French not Norwegian/Nordic). It is genuinely distinct.

- **What this means for canon**: nothing yet, because the Layer-2 C1 verdict was ADMIT at 5-cluster cluster-counting; adding a 6th cluster does not change the verdict (it strengthens it). But if operator opens DECISION-BRIEF refinement, the 6-cluster math could be canonicalized in the eventual C1 ADR's §Context as additional substrate-citation. Per operator dispatch §"Post-Wave-3" (operator-elective), this bridge note flags the observation; no self-litigation.

**Disposition stance**: `framing-note-only` for R-Trap-cluster-deepen-Galtung; **explicit DECISION-BRIEF-refinement flag** on the 6th-cluster question. The Layer-2 C1 verdict stands; the substrate-citation is enriched for the future Layer-3 ADR.

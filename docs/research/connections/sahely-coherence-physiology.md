## 2. C-claims (verbatim from extraction record)

All quotes verbatim from the extraction record §"Verbatim key claims". RID is `orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-29-coherence-physiology-the-embodied-substr` throughout. Claims flagged **LOAD-BEARING** are those most engaged by §3/§4.

- **C-1** **LOAD-BEARING** [anchor: §Abstract · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-29-coherence-physiology-the-embodied-substr · pdf-p3] "The living organism is too often treated as an assemblage of discrete organs, pathways, and molecular targets rather than as a nested continuum of dynamically coupled processes."

- **C-6** **LOAD-BEARING** [anchor: §Executive Summary · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-29-coherence-physiology-the-embodied-substr · pdf-p4] "It is a living field of interdependent relations within which organs, tissues, cells, vessels, nerves, immune sentinels, connective matrices, and metabolic regulators arise as differentiated expressions of a larger organized whole."

- **C-9** **LOAD-BEARING** [anchor: §Executive Summary · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-29-coherence-physiology-the-embodied-substr · pdf-p5] "The fragmentation of physiology is not only a scientific accident. It is partly reinforced by institutional and commercial incentives that favor modular, targetable, patentable, and specialty-compatible explanations. ... A renewed physiology commons is needed so that micro-coherent research streams can be assembled transparently without premature dismissal or overclaiming."

- **C-12** **LOAD-BEARING** [anchor: §1 Introduction · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-29-coherence-physiology-the-embodied-substr · pdf-p9-10] "Coherence, as used in this paper, does not mean uniformity, perfection, equilibrium, symmetry, or vague holism. It refers to the successful coupling of differentiated processes such that the organism can sense, respond, adapt, repair, and participate without becoming trapped in defense."

- **C-14** [anchor: §2 From Organ Assemblage to Living Continuum · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-29-coherence-physiology-the-embodied-substr · pdf-p10] "Organ boundaries are real, but they are not absolute. They are local differentiations within a shared field of life."

- **C-21** [anchor: §7 Boundary Intelligence · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-29-coherence-physiology-the-embodied-substr · pdf-p17] "Biological boundaries are active zones of discernment, not inert walls. ... A chronically alarmed boundary can keep the organism in a state of vigilance even when no single dominant threat remains."

- **C-25** **LOAD-BEARING** [anchor: §9 Defensive Lock-In · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-29-coherence-physiology-the-embodied-substr · pdf-p19] "Defensive lock-in is a self-stabilizing physiological state in which multiple layers of regulation become mutually entrained around protection, containment, and reduced adaptive openness. It is not a single disease, marker, pathway, or lesion. It is a pattern of organization."

- **C-29** **LOAD-BEARING** [anchor: §11 Clinical Translation · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-29-coherence-physiology-the-embodied-substr · pdf-p23] "The central clinical shift is from pathway suppression alone to field restoration. ... Field restoration means restoring the conditions under which salugenesis can proceed. ... It is not vague holism; it is a physiological strategy."

- **C-31** [anchor: §12 Prevention · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-29-coherence-physiology-the-embodied-substr · pdf-p25] "A society that treats sleep as expendable is degrading one of the central conditions of coherence. Sleep is not a private luxury; it is a physiological commons within the organism: a recurring window for autonomic recalibration, immune resolution, endocrine rhythm, memory processing, brain clearance, mitochondrial repair, tissue recovery, and emotional integration."

- **C-32** **LOAD-BEARING** [anchor: §13 Epistemic Closure · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-29-coherence-physiology-the-embodied-substr · pdf-p26] "Epistemic closure occurs when a knowledge system becomes unable or unwilling to recognize phenomena that do not fit its preferred categories. ... A clinician may dismiss a patient not from lack of compassion, but because the available map has no legitimate place for the pattern."

- **C-34** **LOAD-BEARING** [anchor: §14 Life-Coherent Medicine · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-29-coherence-physiology-the-embodied-substr · pdf-p27] "This framework is aligned with Maturana's biology of living. The organism is not a passive object acted upon by external causes. It is a self-producing system structurally coupled with its medium, bringing forth and conserving a world through its organization (Maturana & Varela, 1980, 1987)."

- **C-35** **LOAD-BEARING** [anchor: §14 Life-Coherent Medicine · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-29-coherence-physiology-the-embodied-substr · pdf-p28] "Life-coherent medicine is also aligned with McMurtry's life-value onto-axiology. Value must be grounded in what enables life-capacity. ... Galtung adds a further layer. Violence is not only direct harm; it includes avoidable impairment of life. ... When such impairment is avoidable or worsened by social neglect, environmental exposure, fragmented care, medical dismissal, or economic conditions that deny recovery, it becomes part of structural violence (Galtung, 1969, 1990)."

- **C-36** [anchor: §14 Life-Coherent Medicine · orn:source:bsahely-2026-05-29-coherence-physiology-the-embodied-substr · pdf-p28] "The clinical ethic that follows is non-forcing but not passive. Wu-wei medicine means action aligned with the organism's own conditions of viability. ... In all cases, the question is the same: does this action serve the organism's movement toward coherence?"

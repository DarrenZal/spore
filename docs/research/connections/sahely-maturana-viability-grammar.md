### Cover-page visualization note

The paper's cover (p1) presents the 8 primitives diagrammatically as nodes circling a single central node labeled **"LOVE — The Relational Ground of Humanness"**. The central node is structurally distinct from the eight primitive-nodes: it is the GROUND of the grammar, not one of the reflective questions. This is canonically important for the resonance map in §4 below — Sahely's grammar has the shape *8 primitives + 1 central relational ground*, not *9 flat primitives*. Read in Spore terms, this most closely resembles a *cross-cutting doctrine* sitting beneath/across primitives, rather than a primitive among primitives.

## 4. Substrate resonance against Spore canon (descriptive map)

This section reads Sahely's pp 1-5 substrate against Spore's post-Phase-4 canon (14 foundation docs / 9 primitives / 3 cross-cutting doctrines / 2 modes / 2 properties / 6 derived glossary slugs / concepts-yaml v21). Layer-distinctness preserved — these are descriptive resonance observations, not admission proposals.

### 4.1 Grammar-shape distinction (load-bearing for the rest)

Sahely's grammar is **diagnostic** at the level of *reflective questions a living system can pose about its own manner of living*. Each of the 8 primitives in C-11 is grammatically a question-form ("Conservation asks: What is being conserved?"). The grammar's purpose per C-1 + C-12 is to *help living see what it is conserving* and to apply the same question to itself recursively.

Spore's grammar is **operational** at the level of *coordination acts and structures that constitute action-situations across instances of a federation*. The 9 primitives are 3 structural (Field, Holon, Membrane) + 6 verbs (Intent, Commitment, Joint-commitment, Evidence, Signal, Reproduction) — these are coordination operations and the structures hosting them, not reflective questions.

These two grammars occupy **adjacent but not coextensive** canon-object classes:

- Sahely's 8 primitives are closest in Spore terms to a **diagnostic-rubric pattern** (per the M4 framework in ADR-0065 — a design-criteria-pattern or catalog-pattern variant) — questions to ask about an operational arrangement to assess whether the arrangement preserves life-coherence.
- Sahely's central LOVE node is closest in Spore terms to a **cross-cutting doctrine** (sibling to `reproductive-commoning` / `care-commoning` / `boundary-commoning` in ADRs 0002/0003/0045) — a relational ground that operates across primitives rather than as a primitive itself.

So the Sahely → Spore composition shape is not "8 + 1 Sahely primitives → 9 Spore primitives" (different layers) but rather "Sahely's diagnostic-grammar of life-coherence → a diagnostic rubric Spore could compose over its operational primitives". This is structurally similar to how a code-review rubric is not "the same kind of thing" as the programming language being reviewed.

### 4.2 LOVE as relational ground ↔ care-commoning doctrine (ADR-0045)

C-6 names love in Maturana's precise sense as *"the relational domain in which the other arises as legitimate in coexistence"*. This is not sentimental love — it is a structural relational-domain claim. Spore's `care-commoning` doctrine (ADR-0045, third cross-cutting doctrine sibling to `reproductive-commoning` and `boundary-commoning`) was admitted to capture exactly this layer of relational-asymmetric labor that operates *across* primitives rather than *as* a primitive.

Resonance observation: Maturanan "love-as-legitimate-other-in-coexistence" and Spore's `care-commoning` are doing the same canon-layer work (relational doctrine across primitives) under different vocabulary. C-7's derivation chain (suffering = conserved negation of love; healing = restoration of trust + self-respect + respect for the other; ethics = care for consequences in coexistence; responsibility = answerability for participation) reinforces the same canon-layer convergence — these are doctrinal operations on the cross-cutting relational ground, not primitive-level operations.

**Lossy bits:** Spore's `care-commoning` was admitted from the Federici / Bhattacharya / Tronto / Gibson-Graham / Indigenous-pluriversal cluster — care as gendered, asymmetric, reproductive, often-invisibilized labor. Maturanan love is articulated from a biology-of-cognition cluster (organism-medium congruence + structural coupling generating the relational domain). These two clusters converge on *legitimate-other-in-coexistence* but arrive there from different argumentative substrates. The convergence is real; the substrate-traditions differ. Whether this constitutes a non-care-commoning-cluster supporting evidence for `care-commoning` admission (it was already admitted) or rather an independent reinforcement-from-another-tradition is a DECISION-BRIEF question.

### 4.3 Recursive safeguard ↔ substitution-trap (ADR-0048)

C-12 — *"A viability grammar becomes life-incoherent if it is used to dominate, accuse, or negate. Therefore, the grammar must apply its own question to itself: What manner of living does this grammar conserve when it is used?"* — is the Maturanan articulation of a discipline Spore already canonizes as `substitution-trap` (ADR-0048 modes-across-primitives substrate; failure mode where a grammar designed to liberate becomes the instrument of capture-via-language).

The substantive convergence is exact: both Sahely and Johar identify that a coherence-grammar's *use* is the operational site of its life-coherence-or-incoherence, not the grammar's *content*. Sahely articulates this as recursive self-application of the grammar's own diagnostic question; Johar (ADR-0048 substrate) articulates this as the structural risk that liberatory vocabulary will be captured into domination-vocabulary if not held under reflective discipline.

This is a strong substrate-resonance candidate for DECISION-BRIEF cluster-counting against `substitution-trap`'s existing cluster support. Convergence-from-an-independent-tradition (Maturanan biology-of-cognition rather than Johar-native critical practice) is precisely the kind of evidence that strengthens an existing admission per `feedback_surface_vocabulary_vs_operational_concern.md` — operational-concern match (life-incoherent use of the grammar itself) is satisfied; surface-vocabulary differs (recursive question vs substitution-trap) but the operational concern is shared.

### 4.4 Organism-medium structural coupling ↔ Holon-irreducibility + Membrane scope-conditioning

C-9's joint rejection of individualism + environmental determinism, with structural coupling as the affirmative alternative, sits structurally underneath two existing Spore canon decisions:

- **ADR-0050 Holon-irreducibility** ratified that Spore Holons are not reducible to their parts (Side-B+ joint-commitment substrate; "the whole is not just the sum of the participating individuals"). Maturanan organism-medium structural coupling articulates the *adjacent* claim: the organism is not reducible to a substrate of which the medium would be the environment-only-cause. Both claims live on the same anti-reductionist axis.
- **ADR-0062 Membrane-as-self-produced scope-conditioning** preserved the autopoietic reading of Membrane as a co-admitted scope (alongside the boundary-rule reading) — admitting that for certain instance-classes the Membrane is constituted *by the ongoing operations of the living system itself*, not given exogenously. This is structural-coupling-language inside Spore canon already.

Resonance observation: Sahely strengthens the operational-concern substrate for both ADR-0050 and ADR-0062 by importing the full Maturanan derivation of structural coupling (`a history of recurrent congruent change between living system and medium`). No canon-shift needed — both ADRs already admit the substrate; Sahely's articulation adds independent-tradition reinforcement that DECISION-BRIEF can read into the cluster-counting record if any future scope-conditioning ADR cycles back through this territory.

### 4.5 Languaging-as-recursive-consensual-coordination-of-doings ↔ Signal sense-making-mode (ADR-0063)

C-10's articulation of languaging as *"recursive consensual coordination of doings"* and culture as *"a conserved network of conversations"* sits inside the scope ADR-0063 already canonized: Signal primitive admits a sense-making-mode reading alongside the message-passing reading (the participatory-sense-making scope-conditioning that resolved ADR-0053's R-Sig-1 residue). Sahely's articulation reinforces the substrate that justified ADR-0063 — languaging is not a one-way Signal-send-and-receive operation; it is a coordination-of-doings whose recursivity is constitutive of culture.

**Lossy bits:** Sahely's *consensual* qualifier in "recursive consensual coordination of doings" is doing work that Spore's Signal-as-sense-making-mode does not currently isolate. Maturanan consensuality is about the structural-coupling-history that makes coordination possible — not procedural consent. Whether this consensuality-substrate deserves separate canon-articulation under ADR-0063 or operates as an internal mode of the existing scope-conditioning is a DECISION-BRIEF question that Anchor #2 (Biology of Living Coordination) is better positioned to surface. Held descriptive here.

### 4.6 Conservation-through-change ↔ rule-in-use vs rule-in-form (ADR-0046)

C-8's axiom *"Living is conservation-through-change. A living system continues only while its organization and its operational congruence with the medium are conserved"* parallels at substrate-philosophy layer the distinction ADR-0046 imported as `rule-in-use` vs `rule-in-form`: rules-in-use are what is operationally conserved through the ongoing flow of action-situations; rules-in-form are static-text declarations that may or may not match the rules-in-use. Both Sahely's conservation-through-change and Ostrom-via-ADR-0046's rule-in-use share the discipline of *operational conservation as the locus of identity*, not formal-statement conservation.

Resonance observation: cross-tradition convergence (Maturanan biology of cognition + Ostrom institutional economics) on operational-conservation-as-identity-locus. No canon-shift needed; framing-note value for any future ADR-0046 work that wants to invoke independent-tradition substrate.

### 4.7 Viability-rejection clause ↔ structural-legitimacy

C-4 is doctrinally striking: *"viability cannot be reduced to survival, adaptation, stability, resilience, or functional persistence. A manner of living may persist while conserving fear, domination, humiliation, extraction, self-negation, or ecological destruction."* This separates *persistence* from *legitimacy* at the philosophical layer. Spore's `structural-legitimacy` foundation doc (admitted at ADR-0042) does the same canon-work at the federation-governance layer: a federation arrangement may operationally persist while failing the legitimacy test that makes its persistence ethically meaningful.

Resonance observation: cross-domain convergence (Maturanan civilizational-viability + Spore federation-governance) on persistence-is-not-legitimacy. No canon-shift needed; framing-note value for any future structural-legitimacy work.

### 4.8 Maturanan "ecological coherence" keyword — surface vs operational match

Sahely's keyword list (p3) names *ecological coherence* among the paper's terms. This is surface-vocabulary that Spore canon does not directly admit. Per `feedback_surface_vocabulary_vs_operational_concern.md`, surface-vocabulary citation alone is not canon-pressure; the operational concern (whether a federation arrangement preserves the ecological conditions through which the federation's instances remain livable) is partly engaged by `reproduction-continuity` (ADR-0049) and `maintenance-economics` foundation doc but is not a primary Spore-canon emphasis. Bregion canon arm is the more natural locus for ecological-coherence operational-pressure. Held descriptive here; flagged in §5 for bregion cross-repo context.

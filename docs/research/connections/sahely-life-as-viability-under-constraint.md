## 2. C-claims (verbatim from extraction record)

All quotes verbatim from the extraction record §"Verbatim key claims (~12 claims from pages 1-9)". RID and page anchors carried per intake protocol.

- **C1** [anchor: §Abstract · orn:source:bsahely-2026-01-25-life-as-viability-under-constraint · pdf-p6] Constraint-first framework: "This paper develops a constraint-first framework that treats life as the capacity to remain within a bounded region of state space under non-equilibrium conditions."

- **C2** [anchor: §Abstract · orn:source:bsahely-2026-01-25-life-as-viability-under-constraint · pdf-p6] **LOAD-BEARING — build-up sequence:** "Starting from non-equilibrium thermodynamics, the framework introduces regulation, information, and control as physical necessities for stability under disturbance. These elements are integrated into a geometric account of viability, in which persistence depends on the simultaneous satisfaction of multiple necessary conditions. From this geometry emerge universal invariants of living systems, conjugate pairings governing trade-offs, a triadic closure linking energy, information, and viability constraints, and a multiplicative structure that explains weakest-link failure and nonlinear collapse."

- **C3** [anchor: §Abstract · orn:source:bsahely-2026-01-25-life-as-viability-under-constraint · pdf-p6] **LOAD-BEARING — stability vs intrinsic health:** "The framework distinguishes present stability from intrinsic health, defined as distance from absorbing boundaries and preservation of future option space. It further shows how a minimal notion of normativity and responsibility arises naturally from action in constrained viability space, without moral presupposition."

- **C4** [anchor: §Executive Summary · orn:source:bsahely-2026-01-25-life-as-viability-under-constraint · pdf-p7] Core question: "This paper proposes a unifying framework that treats life as **viability under constraint**. Rather than defining life by complexity, function, or optimization, it asks a more basic question: what conditions must be continuously satisfied for a system to persist at all?"

- **C5** [anchor: §Executive Summary · orn:source:bsahely-2026-01-25-life-as-viability-under-constraint · pdf-p7] Physical-necessity derivation: "Because living systems operate far from equilibrium, they require continuous energy throughput. Because they exist under constant disturbance, they require regulation and control. Because regulation is costly and environments are variable, they require information, memory, and prediction. These necessities lead naturally to a geometric conception of viability: a bounded region of state space within which recovery remains possible, surrounded by absorbing boundaries corresponding to irreversible failure."

- **C6** [anchor: §Executive Summary · orn:source:bsahely-2026-01-25-life-as-viability-under-constraint · pdf-p7] Invariant capacities: "Persistence depends on a small set of invariant capacities — such as flux adequacy, boundary integrity, regulatory capacity, informational continuity, adaptive plasticity, and preservation of future option space. These capacities are not independent; they are coupled through conjugate pairings that enforce trade-offs. Survival is conjunctive rather than compensatory, giving rise to a multiplicative viability structure dominated by weakest-link dynamics. As a consequence, collapse often appears abrupt even when underlying degradation has been gradual."

- **C7** [anchor: §Executive Summary · orn:source:bsahely-2026-01-25-life-as-viability-under-constraint · pdf-p7] **LOAD-BEARING — compensated fragility:** "A central contribution of the framework is the distinction between **stability** and **intrinsic health**. Systems may remain stable while silently consuming reserves and narrowing future options, a condition termed **compensated fragility**. Recognizing this distinction allows earlier detection of collapse risk and more effective intervention timing across domains, from intensive care medicine to institutional governance and ecological management."

- **C8** [anchor: §Executive Summary · orn:source:bsahely-2026-01-25-life-as-viability-under-constraint · pdf-p7] **LOAD-BEARING — responsibility from geometry:** "Finally, the framework shows that once action is admitted, a minimal notion of responsibility follows inevitably. Actions differ in whether they preserve or undermine viability across relevant scales and time horizons. This ordering is not moral or ideological; it is imposed by the geometry of persistence itself."

- **C9** [anchor: §1.2 · orn:source:bsahely-2026-01-25-life-as-viability-under-constraint · pdf-p8] **LOAD-BEARING — life as constraint satisfaction:** "What distinguishes living systems is not the presence of any single activity, but the requirement that **multiple conditions be satisfied simultaneously and continuously**. From this perspective, life is more naturally understood as a **constraint satisfaction problem**. A living system must maintain itself within a bounded region of state space despite ongoing perturbations from its environment and internal dynamics. Failure is not defined by the absence of activity, but by the violation of constraints that make continued activity possible at all."

- **C10** [anchor: §1.2 · orn:source:bsahely-2026-01-25-life-as-viability-under-constraint · pdf-p9] **LOAD-BEARING — multiplicative interaction:** "This reframing shifts attention away from cataloguing functions and toward identifying **necessary conditions for persistence**. It also clarifies why living systems exhibit sharp transitions between apparently stable operation and rapid collapse. Constraint violations do not accumulate linearly; instead, they interact multiplicatively, producing threshold effects and irreversible regime shifts."

- **C11** [anchor: §1.2 · orn:source:bsahely-2026-01-25-life-as-viability-under-constraint · pdf-p9] Non-presupposition of purpose: "Crucially, this approach does not presuppose purpose, intention, or value. It relies only on the minimal observation that some systems persist while others do not, and that persistence requires remaining within specific bounds. The normative appearance of 'success' and 'failure' emerges later, as a consequence of these constraints, rather than as an assumption built into the framework."

- **C12** [anchor: §1.3 · orn:source:bsahely-2026-01-25-life-as-viability-under-constraint · pdf-p9] Persistence not complexity: "The defining feature that distinguishes living systems from other complex processes is **their ability to remain viable over time**. Viability here refers to the capacity to continue operating as a system of the same kind, rather than merely undergoing change. This capacity must be maintained despite energy dissipation, component turnover, noise, and environmental variability."

## 4. Substrate-resonance map (descriptive)

This reads the pp 1-9 substrate against Spore's **resolved** post-Bundle-α canon (14 foundation docs / 9 primitives / **4** cross-cutting doctrines / 2 modes / 2 properties; concepts-yaml v24; 40 canon-decisions through ADR-0090). Descriptive only.

### 4.1 Compensated fragility ↔ the trap cluster (substitution-trap ADR-0048 + golden-calf-trap ADR-0085)

C-7 names **compensated fragility**: a system "may remain stable while silently consuming reserves and narrowing future options." This is the formal-thermodynamic restatement of two resolved canon objects. (i) `substitution-trap` (ADR-0048) is the failure mode where surface operation continues while the underlying capacity that made it possible is consumed — Sahely's compensated fragility is precisely the *operational signature* of that trap, stated in viability-geometry terms. (ii) `golden-calf-trap` (ADR-0085) is the SHAPE-of substitution-trap where a proxy is worshipped in place of the thing it stands for; Sahely's stability-metric-tracked-while-intrinsic-health-degrades is a textbook instance of mistaking the proxy (present stability) for the substance (distance from absorbing boundaries). The matching discipline `recursive-audit-method` (ADR-0085) is what C-7's "earlier detection of collapse risk" calls for: audit the gap between the proxy and the substance rather than trusting the proxy. Note that `golden-calf-trap` is the *shape-of* ADR-0048's substitution-trap, **not** equivalent-to it. No canon-shift; resolved.

### 4.2 Stability vs intrinsic health ↔ structural-legitimacy (ADR-0042)

C-3 + C-4 separate **present stability** from **intrinsic health** ("distance from absorbing boundaries and preservation of future option space"). This is the viability-geometry form of the persistence-≠-legitimacy axiom that Spore's `structural-legitimacy` foundation doc (ADR-0042) canonizes at the federation-governance layer: an arrangement may operationally persist while failing the test that makes its persistence meaningful. Sahely's contribution is a formal substrate — *legitimacy-as-distance-from-absorbing-boundary* — for a claim Spore already holds. Cross-domain convergence (non-equilibrium physics + federation governance); framing-note value for any future structural-legitimacy work; no canon-shift.

### 4.3 Preservation of future option space ↔ margin-as-reserve (ADR-0089) + maintenance-economics (ADR-0079)

C-3/C-6/C-7's sixth invariant — **preservation of future option space**, with reserves consumed silently — is exactly the territory ADR-0089 scope-conditioned onto F9: `margin-as-reserve` names the slack/reserve that maintenance-economics must protect against silent depletion. The `reproductive-infrastructure` slug (F9, ADR-0079) is the standing capacity whose erosion *is* the narrowing of future options. Sahely supplies the formal-substrate reading (option-space contraction as measurable distance-to-boundary); F9 + ADR-0089 supply the instance-doctrine reading (margin must be funded, not assumed). Mutually supporting at different layers; no canon-shift.

### 4.4 Multiplicative / weakest-link collapse ↔ failure-modes (ADR-0075) + sensor/actuator loop (ADR-0073/0076)

C-10's "constraint violations interact multiplicatively, producing threshold effects and irreversible regime shifts" gives the formal account of why F6 `failure-mode-class` shapes manifest as abrupt rather than gradual. Read through the F1→F6→F5 loop: the six invariants (C-6) define a sensor-set-completeness criterion (F1 `sensor-oracle-governance`, ADR-0073), where `longitudinal-attestation` is needed because the dangerous degradation is *gradual and silent* (C-7); the multiplicative structure names the F6 failure shapes (one weak invariant dominates); and the gap between a stability-metric and an intrinsic-health-metric is an F5 `epistemic-gap` (ADR-0076) that should trigger a `response-doctrine` action. This is descriptive substrate-resonance: Sahely's geometry is a coherent formal story under which the already-admitted F1/F5/F6 doctrines compose. No canon-shift.

C-2/C-6/C-10's conjugate-pairings + symplectic geometry of trade-offs + multiplicative-quartic viability function (Appendix A formal apparatus, deferred-read) is the mathematical-grammar-substrate layer. Per the C7 future-ADR-shape (graph-substrate-of-canon, DECLINE-with-triggers, 6 re-opening triggers), this layer is **declined for admission** and held framing-note-only: Spore does not admit a mathematical formalism as canon object on single-source resonance.

A layered violence architecture in which cultural narratives legitimize structural harm. 2. A conflict grammar oriented toward binary opposition and annihilation (Dualism–Manicheism–Armageddon). 3. An economic value code defining rationality as self-maximizing growth. 4. A feedback system in which crises reinforce rather than disrupt underlying incentives."*

- **C5** (Sahely citing Galtung 1990) [anchor: §II Violence Architecture · …same RID · pdf-p11] — Galtung violence-triangle import: *"Johan Galtung's formulation of direct, structural, and cultural violence provides a foundational analytical model (Galtung, 1990). - **C6** (Sahely citing Galtung 1990) [anchor: §II Violence Architecture · …same RID · pdf-p13] — violence as reduction of basic-need satisfaction: *"Galtung's typology identifies four domains of basic needs (Galtung, 1990): Survival, Well-being, Identity, Freedom. Violence exists when these are avoidably reduced."*

- **C7** (Sahely) [anchor: §II Violence Architecture · …same RID · pdf-p12] — self-sealing-system mechanism (5-step closed loop): *"1. Cultural narratives define what is rational and legitimate. 2. These narratives shape institutional design. 3. Institutional design produces structural outcomes. 4. Structural pressures escalate into direct violence. 5. Direct violence reinforces cultural narratives ('We were right to act'). The system stabilizes itself."*

- **C8** (Sahely citing Galtung 2013) [anchor: §III DMA · …same RID · pdf-p16] — DMA archetype: *"Galtung identifies two archetypal conflict grammars that have shaped civilizations: Dualism–Manicheism–Armageddon (DMA) and Holism–Dialectics–Transcendence (HDT) (Galtung, 2013). … The DMA script encodes three linked assumptions: 1. The world is divided into Good and Evil. 2. Conflict between them is inevitable. 3. Resolution occurs through decisive victory of one over the other. It is binary, moralized, and terminal."*

- **C9** (Sahely citing Galtung 1996) [anchor: §III DMA · …same RID · pdf-p17] — projection-as-stabilizer: *"In Global Projections of Deep-Rooted U.S. Pathologies, Galtung argues that systems often project their own violent tendencies onto external enemies (Galtung, 1996)."*

- **C10** (Sahely citing McMurtry 2014) [anchor: §IV Ruling Value Code · …same RID · pdf-p22] — cancer analogy as structural description: *"McMurtry characterizes deregulated self-multiplying money sequences as structurally analogous to cancer (McMurtry, 2014). Cancer cells: Multiply without reference to the organism's life. Appropriate resources without systemic accountability. Destroy host systems through unchecked expansion. … This is not metaphorical rhetoric. It is structural description."*

- **C12** (Sahely) [anchor: §IV Ruling Value Code · …same RID · pdf-p24] — violence-without-villains: *"When self-maximizing growth is institutionalized: Ecological destruction is incentivized. Inequality is rewarded. Public systems weaken. Militarization becomes protective. No conspiracy is required. Individuals operating rationally within the code reproduce structural violence. The system selects for actors who comply with its incentives."*

- **C14** (Sahely) [anchor: §VI Risks of Misdiagnosis · …same RID · pdf-p31-32] — **critique-replicates-DMA (recursive diagnostic):** *"If the critique of systemic violence is framed as: Evil elites vs virtuous people, Corrupt insiders vs innocent outsiders, Then the critique itself adopts DMA logic. Binary moralization reappears. Annihilation rhetoric follows. The grammar remains unchanged — only the sides shift. This is how revolutions replicate the structures they overthrow."*

- **C15** (Sahely citing McMurtry 2003) [anchor: §VI Risks of Misdiagnosis · …same RID · pdf-p32] — ruling group-mind as distributed cognition: *"McMurtry refers to a 'ruling group-mind' dynamic … Group-mind does not imply unified malicious intent. It refers to shared premises embedded within institutions. Individuals operating within institutions internalize the prevailing grammar."*

- **C16** (Sahely) [anchor: §Executive Summary · …same RID · pdf-p6-7] — five constructive entailments incl. **civil commons:** *"[The viability principle] entails: Redefining security as ecological and infrastructural resilience. Aligning economic incentives with regenerative capacity. Embedding ecological accounting into fiscal policy. Reframing conflict through integrative rather than annihilative logic. Securing essential life goods as civil commons rather than unregulated commodities."*

- **C17** (Sahely) [anchor: §VI Risks of Misdiagnosis · …same RID · pdf-p33] — methodological-integrity disclaimer (anti-totalizing): *"The claim of this white paper is not: 'Everything is controlled.' It is: 'When similar harm patterns recur across independent domains, and those patterns align with a shared value grammar, systemic causation is the most parsimonious explanation.' This is structural reasoning, not ideological assertion."*

### 4.1 Cancer-economy + critique-replicates-DMA ↔ trap-shape vocabulary (ADR-0085)

ADR-0085 admitted `golden-calf-trap` (the SHAPE-OF ADR-0048's `substitution-trap`, **not** equivalent-to) and `recursive-audit-method` — naming the trap-shape taxonomy and the discipline of applying a grammar's own question to itself. Three of this post's claims sit squarely in that territory:

- **C-10** McMurtry cancer/self-multiplying-money "multiplies without reference to the organism's life" is a civilizational-scale class-trap instance — money-sequence growth decoupled from life-coherence. The ADR-0085 substrate explicitly maps McMurtry's *Cancer Stage of Capitalism* to the Golden-Calf class-trap (per the concepts-yaml `golden-calf-trap` lineage). This post is one more Galtung+McMurtry-sourced articulation of that already-named shape.
- **C-14** critique-replicates-DMA — *"the grammar remains unchanged — only the sides shift"* — is the recursive failure mode the `recursive-audit-method` discipline guards against: a liberatory critique that re-instantiates the structure it opposes. It is the Galtung-side homolog of the substitution-trap (ADR-0048): substitute the contents (good/evil sides) while preserving the dominating grammar.
- **C-17** the anti-totalizing methodological guardrail (*"not 'Everything is controlled'"*) is itself an instance of recursive-audit-method applied by Sahely to his own diagnosis — a self-limiting move that prevents the diagnosis from collapsing into the DMA grammar it analyzes.

No canon-shift: ADR-0085 already carries the shape and the discipline. This post adds a Galtung+McMurtry articulation of the same shape — and per single-source rigor, that does not add independent cluster weight.

### 4.2 Viability principle + life-cost externalization ↔ life-value-doctrine (ADR-0086)

C-3's viability principle (*"rational iff it preserves and regenerates the life-support conditions that enable its continuation"*) and C-1's *"externalization of life costs"* are McMurtry Life-Value Onto-Axiology substrate. Spore admitted `life-value-doctrine` as the **4th cross-cutting doctrine** (ADR-0086; sibling to `reproductive-commoning` / `boundary-commoning` / `care-commoning`) precisely as the lens that evaluates whether coordination outcomes serve life-value or money-value. C-3 is Sahely's *reformulation/naming* (R-2) of that substrate; C-10's cancer-economy and C-16's "regenerative capacity / ecological accounting" are the same money-value-vs-life-value distinction the doctrine canonizes. ### 4.3 "Securing essential life goods as civil commons" ↔ civil-commons slug (ADR-0087)

### 4.4 Grammar-determines-the-legitimate + Galtung structural violence ↔ perception-as-power (ADR-0090)

### 4.5 Self-sealing system + ruling group-mind ↔ structural-legitimacy + Holon/Membrane (ADR-0042/0050/0062)

- **C-15 + C-12:** McMurtry's "ruling group-mind" as *distributed cognition* (shared premises embedded in institutions; "the system selects for actors who comply") resonates with Holon-irreducibility (ADR-0050; the institutional whole is not reducible to malicious individual intent) and Membrane-as-self-produced (ADR-0062; the boundary that selects/conserves is produced by the system's own ongoing operation). C-7's narrative-loop (cultural narratives → institutional design → structural outcomes → reinforcement) also brushes the rule-in-use vs rule-in-form distinction (ADR-0046, §4 R-Conservation): value-codes are what is *operationally* conserved as legitimate, regardless of formal declaration.

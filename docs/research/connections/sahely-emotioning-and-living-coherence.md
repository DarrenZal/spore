## 4. Substrate-resonance map (descriptive)

Read against post-Bundle-α canon (concepts-yaml v24; 4 cross-cutting doctrines; 40 canon-decisions through ADR-0090). Descriptive only.

### 4.1 Emotion-as-configurator-of-possible-action ↔ expressive/constructed-power modes (ADR-0048)

C1's exact claim — emotions "configure domains of possible action" and "alter perception, bodily readiness, relational orientation, and the range of actions that become possible" — is structurally the same move Spore canonized in ADR-0048 as **modes-across-primitives**: `expressive-power` (life articulating meaning/desire/perception) and `constructed-power` (situational agency emerging dynamically). The extraction record's own R1 names this parallel: "emotion as Johar-style mode rather than primitive." Emotion is not a coordination primitive; it is a mode-of-operation that conditions the action-space across primitives. The Maturanan derivation (biology-of-cognition) is an independent argumentative substrate arriving at the operational concern ADR-0048 already holds.

### 4.2 Non-forcing affective care ↔ care-cluster scope-condition (ADR-0088 on ADR-0045)

C6's non-forcing affective care ("emotional change cannot be commanded from outside; the clinician participates in conditions for the patient's own re-organization") and C7's social-baseline-of-safety both sit in the care territory ADR-0088 scope-conditioned onto `care-commoning` (ADR-0045): love-as-structural-coupling, legitimacy-of-the-other-in-coexistence, care as conditions-for-another's-own-reorganization rather than imposed change. Maturanan "biology of love" is a distinct argumentative cluster from the Federici/Tronto/Bhattacharya care-feminist cluster that originally supported ADR-0045; here it operates as independent-tradition reinforcement of the already-scope-conditioned care doctrine. No canon-shift — ADR-0088 already carries this scope.

### 4.3 Disease as costly conserved viability ↔ margin-as-reserve (ADR-0089) + structural-legitimacy (ADR-0042)

C5's pathology-grammar — emotional patterns becoming "conserved attractors that preserve short-term affective viability at cost of wider coherence" — is a two-edged resonance. First, it engages ADR-0089's `margin-as-reserve` scope-condition on F9 maintenance-economics: a system spending its coherence-margin to conserve a short-term pattern is the affective-biology instance of margin-depletion. Second, it instances the structural-legitimacy discipline (ADR-0042): operational *persistence* of a conserved pattern is not its *legitimacy* — a disease-attractor persists precisely while negating wider coherence. Both readings are descriptive; neither requires canon-shift.

### 4.4 Organism-medium structural coupling ↔ Holon-irreducibility + Membrane-as-self-produced

C3 (emotional sentience as embodied evaluative system reporting on "organism–environment relations") and C4 (interoception + allostasis as the organism-medium physiological bridge) restate the anti-reductionist structural-coupling axis already admitted at ADR-0050 (Holon-irreducibility) and ADR-0062 (Membrane-as-self-produced scope-conditioning — Membrane constituted by the ongoing operations of the living system itself). Sahely adds an interoception/allostasis-grade physiological articulation of the same substrate. No canon-shift.

### 4.5 Emotioning/languaging ↔ Signal sense-making-mode (ADR-0063)

The affective framing of emotioning as the relational domain that "specifies possible behaviors" (per the Maturana substrate this paper extends) sits inside ADR-0063's participatory-sense-making scope-conditioning on Signal — coordination-of-doings rather than one-way message-passing. Descriptive reinforcement; no canon-shift.

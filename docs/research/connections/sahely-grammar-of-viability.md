## 4. Substrate-resonance map (descriptive)

Read against Spore's post-Bundle-α canon (14 foundation docs / 9 primitives / **4** cross-cutting doctrines / 2 modes / 2 properties / concepts-yaml v24 / 40 canon-decisions through ADR-0090). Descriptive observations, not admission proposals.

### 4.1 Grammar-shape distinction (load-bearing for the rest)

Sahely's grammar is **diagnostic-ontological** — viability as a *unifying invariant* (C-1/C-4) that a living system, person, institution, or civilization either remains-within or loses contact with. Spore's grammar is **operational-coordinational** — primitives and structures that constitute coordination action-situations across a federation. These are **adjacent but cross-layer**. The honest composition is: Sahely's viability-grammar could compose *over* Spore's operational primitives as a diagnostic lens (does this coordination arrangement keep futures open?), without either subsuming the other. This is structurally the same point made for the 2026-05-21 Maturana-anchor: diagnostic grammar ≠ the operational grammar it can be applied to. (`spore.connection.sahely-maturana-viability-grammar` §4.1.)

### 4.2 Viability-as-life-capacity ↔ life-value-doctrine (ADR-0086)

C-1's "capacity to remain within the constraints that keep futures open" and C-9's spirit-as-sensitivity-to-trans-personal-constraints (ecological limits, trust, intergenerational responsibility) sit in the canon-shape slot of `life-value-doctrine` — the 4th cross-cutting doctrine (ADR-0086; sibling to reproductive-commoning / boundary-commoning / care-commoning, NOT a glossary slug). Sahely's "viability not flourishing or optimization, but continued possibility" (C-4) is a McMurtry-adjacent life-capacity framing per the extraction record's implicit-substrate note. The doctrine already engages this canon-layer; Sahely is independent-tradition reinforcement, not new pressure. (`civil-commons` slug, ADR-0087, is not engaged at the pp 1–5 substrate read here.)

### 4.3 Constraint-loss diagnosis ↔ recursive-audit-method + golden-calf-trap (ADR-0085)

C-12/C-13 diagnose a *reduction-habit* — "treat what cannot be isolated as secondary or subjective"; reductions that "severed explanation from the conditions that make systems endure." This is the **shape of a trap**: a successful method (variable-isolation) becomes a habit of thought that quietly negates the very constraint-substrate it depends on. Spore's `golden-calf-trap` (ADR-0085; the SHAPE-OF ADR-0048 substitution-trap, *not equivalent-to*) and `recursive-audit-method` (the discipline of applying the grammar's own question to itself) are the canonical handles for catching exactly this severance. The convergence is at the *operational-concern* layer (a coherence-method becoming the instrument of its own incoherence), per `feedback_surface_vocabulary_vs_operational_concern.md`. No canon-shift — Bundle α already admitted this vocabulary.

### 4.4 Persistence-severed-from-enduring-conditions ↔ structural-legitimacy (ADR-0042) + collapse ↔ failure-modes (F6)

C-4's "viability is not flourishing or optimization, but continued possibility across time" and C-13's diagnosis that reductions "severed explanation from the conditions that make systems endure" separate *persistence* from *the conditions that make persistence legitimate/durable*. Spore's `structural-legitimacy` foundation doc (ADR-0042) does the same canon-work: a federation arrangement may operationally persist while failing the legitimacy test. C-8's collapse-as-delayed-feedback ("appear stable until correction becomes catastrophic") resonates with F6 `failure-modes` (`spore.failure-modes`) — the delayed-feedback / coupling-breakdown class where decision-makers lose contact with the consequences that would correct them. Both already-canonical; reinforcing.

### 4.5 Ethics-as-coordination-of-viability ↔ joint-commitment (ADR-0050)

C-7 ("ethics arises from the need to coordinate viability among multiple agents across time; violations of ethics are violations of future possibility") is a societal-scale framing of `joint-commitment` (ADR-0050) — coordination of obligation across multiple agents, irreducible to a sum of individual commitments. Sahely adds the across-generations time-axis explicitly; Spore's `reproduction-continuity` (ADR-0049) carries the cross-episode/intergenerational substrate at the primitive layer. No canon-shift; framing-note value if any future joint-commitment / reproduction-continuity work wants an across-generations independent-tradition citation.

### 4.6 Memory-without-a-single-address ↔ rule-in-use (ADR-0046)

C-6 ("recurring forms, skills, and archetypes arise from attractor landscapes shaped by repeated success under constraint, not from localized storage") parallels ADR-0046's `rule-in-use` vs `rule-in-form` distinction: what is operationally conserved (the lived pattern, the attractor) is the locus of identity, not the static formal record. Cross-tradition convergence (dynamical-systems/attractor framing + Ostrom institutional-economics) on operational-conservation-as-identity-locus. Held descriptive.

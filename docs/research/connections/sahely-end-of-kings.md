## 4. Substrate-resonance map (descriptive)

Read against the resolved post-Bundle-α canon (14 foundation docs / 9 primitives / 4 cross-cutting doctrines / concepts-yaml v24). Descriptive observations, not admission proposals. All concept-claims attribute to **Caitlin Johnstone (the original author)**, not Sahely.

### 4.1 "The impulse found a different way of manifesting" ↔ structural-legitimacy persistence≠legitimacy (ADR-0042)

C2 is the strongest and cleanest resonance. Johnstone's whole allegory is built on the observation that *a form can disappear (kings, crowns, thrones) while the underlying domination it expressed persists in a new guise (money + media + economic infrastructure)*. That is the persistence-is-not-legitimacy doctrine `structural-legitimacy` canonizes (ADR-0042): the survival/continuation of an order is not evidence of its legitimacy. Johnstone runs the inverse direction too — the *visible* monarchy ended but the *function* survived — which is exactly why Spore separates "this persists" from "this is legitimate." Reinforcing; no canon-shift.

### 4.2 "Keep the people from realizing they were being ruled" ↔ illegitimate order maintained by concealment (structural-legitimacy + `asymmetric-membrane`)

C4 + C8 (rule-from-the-shadows) describe order sustained not by consent but by *hiding the fact of rule*. This brushes structural-legitimacy (concealed order is order-without-legitimacy that must hide because exposure would erode compliance) and the concealment/gatekeeping axis of `asymmetric-membrane` (ADR-0047 L3) — the new kings operate a one-way membrane: they see and shape the public sphere while remaining unseen. Reinforcing; no canon-shift.

### 4.3 "Control the stories the people tell themselves" ↔ perception-as-power on F4 (ADR-0090)

C6 — media capture as control over *the stories the people tell themselves about what's going on in their society* — is almost a definition of the perception-as-power scope-condition on F4 representation-authority (ADR-0090). ADR-0090's concern is precisely that *what becomes visible / sayable / representable* is itself an instrument of power, not a neutral substrate. Johnstone's claim that whoever owns the media owns the public's model of reality is the popular-political articulation of that scope-condition. This is the post's second-strongest resonance. Reinforcing; no canon-shift.

### 4.4 "Humanity into a funnel … the earth stripped bare" ↔ life-value-doctrine money-vs-life (ADR-0086)

C9 — wealth-extraction as an end in itself, with the earth "stripped bare to quench their insatiable lust for more and more control" — is the money-value-decoupled-from-life-coherence contrast the 4th cross-cutting doctrine (life-value-doctrine, ADR-0086) evaluates. Johnstone names cumulative monetary accumulation pursued *against* life-coherence (planetary stripping, human funnelling); the life-value doctrine is the lens Spore admitted to evaluate exactly whether coordination outcomes serve life-value or money-value. Note the attribution care: this is Johnstone's political framing, read into the doctrine descriptively — the doctrine itself was admitted on McMurtry Life-Value Onto-Axiology substrate, not on this repost. Reinforcing; no canon-shift.

### 4.5 "We can all be kings / collectively change our minds / heal our planet together" ↔ constructed-power + expressive-power + civil-commons (ADR-0048, ADR-0087)

C10–C12 turn from diagnosis to emancipation: shared information dissolves the kings' power; the people *collectively change their minds about how money works*; and a "humanity that works in collaboration with itself and with its ecosystem" emerges. The agency-from-below register — situational capacity emerging in collective response — is what `constructed-power` and `expressive-power` name (ADR-0048). The end-state ("collaboration with itself and with its ecosystem") brushes `civil-commons` (ADR-0087), the life-enabling commons-structure that is the constructive counter-pole to extractive domination. Reinforcing, descriptive; no canon-shift. (Per `feedback_surface_vocabulary_vs_operational_concern.md`, these are surface-vocabulary brushes onto Spore's agency vocabulary, not operational-concern identity — a political allegory is not an operational coordination grammar.)

### 4.6 The "gold on their heads" symbol ↔ golden-calf-trap (ADR-0085, weak)

C1's image — *kings were just regular people with gold on their heads* — and C2's continuation are a symbol-mistaken-for-substance shape: the crown/gold is the visible symbol whose removal does not remove the thing it stood for. This weakly brushes `golden-calf-trap` (ADR-0085), the shape (NOT equivalent-to ADR-0048 substitution-trap) in which a symbol displaces the reality it should serve. Held as weak — Johnstone's point is closer to *the symbol was never the substance* than to *the corrective became the new idol*. No canon-shift; flagged weak.

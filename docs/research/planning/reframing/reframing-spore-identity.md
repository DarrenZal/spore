# Reframing Proposal: Spore Identity — Coordination Grammar

## Problem

Spore's identity sentence appears in two surfaces, with verified current wording:

- **`README.md:7`** (standalone tagline): *"An infrastructure for collective agency — a common grammar for plural, sovereign coordination across scales and scopes."*
- **`docs/project-vision.md:10`** (the constitutional identity sentence, `"Spore is"`-prefixed, mid-paragraph, continuing into the normative-commitments clause): *"Spore is an infrastructure for collective agency — a common grammar for plural, sovereign coordination across scales and scopes. It operates across the normative commitments that shape how agents see, value, know, and act…"*

The current line leads with **"infrastructure for collective agency"** — an *agency-enablement* frame. It gestures at the grammar role in its second clause (*"a common grammar for plural, sovereign coordination"*), but the lead under-names what the work across the repo constellation has revealed Spore actually to be: **the upstream coordination grammar that everything else instantiates** — Layer 1 in the four-layer architecture, the substrate the constellation orients around — the grammar that lets local parts keep their own meaning while still composing into a whole that holds together (local sovereignty and global coherence at once).

This is not a contradiction the way `reframing-pm-canon-scope` was a contradiction. It is an **identity that the work has outgrown.** The 2026-06-02 repo-constellation strategy work (`~/.claude/plans/so-i-had-this-proud-kahn.md` Track B; `docs/research/synthesis/coherence-without-collapse-and-its-projections.md`) established that Spore is turning out to be upstream of Intelligence Commons, bregion, BKC, bioregional-economics, Poietic Match, *and* the non-canon applications (CIE, the RC financing facility, the GPU node network) — each consuming Spore's grammar. The four-layer integration architecture names Spore as **Layer 1 — Grammar**, the source the rest of the constellation orients around. The identity sentence should name that role plainly, in the register the operator already uses for it externally (sheaf.lol's *"coordination grammar for plural / sovereign / local-to-global coherent agentic systems"*; the Schmidt grant bio's *"shared coordination substrates"*).

**Honest scope caveat.** This is at the *lighter* end of foundational reframing — a self-description sharpening, not a concept dissolution, rename, re-layer, or topology change. It is routed here (not to a canon-review ADR) for two reasons given in §"Why ADR scope is insufficient": FR-2 sends identity-altering wider-scope changes here, and the operator elected the heavier ceremony deliberately because the identity sentence is the one line the entire constellation orients around, and Spore's repositioning as upstream grammar is exactly the kind of self-conception change that earns a proposal layer + cooling-off rather than a quick edit. This proposal states that honestly rather than inflating the change.

## Proposed reframing

Reframe both identity surfaces toward a **math-quiet "coordination grammar / local-to-global coherence" framing (Option A).** The wording leads with *coordination grammar* (operator lean, 2026-06-02 — the most continuous choice, since "grammar" is already canon's own term and matches "Layer 1 — Grammar" in the architecture) and names *holding local sovereignty and global coherence at once* as what the grammar is for.

**Math-quiet by deliberate exclusion (Option B excluded).** No sheaf / topological / cohomology vocabulary enters the identity line. The companion synthesis note's honest ledger (`coherence-without-collapse-and-its-projections.md` §7) records *"local-to-global coherence as plain language for what the grammar already does"* as **earned (frontstage-ready now)** and *"the math-forward identity claim ('topological engine') stays out until the testbed earns it"* as **parked.** This proposal admits only the earned framing.

This proposal fixes the **direction + constraints**, not the final glyph; the exact wording is finalized at ADR-drafting (mirroring `reframing-pm-canon-scope`, which left the exact origin-doc choice to its ADR). Candidate wordings, for reviewer reaction:

**README.md:7 (standalone tagline):**
> "Spore is a coordination grammar for collective agency across plural, sovereign systems — for local-to-global coherence at the scales it has reached."

**docs/project-vision.md:10 (`"Spore is"`-prefixed, continues into the existing normative-commitments clause — preserved):**
> "Spore is a coordination grammar for collective agency across plural, sovereign systems — for local-to-global coherence at the scales it has reached. As shared infrastructure for that agency, it operates across the normative commitments that shape how agents see, value, know, and act (ontological, axiological, epistemological, practical)…"

**README.md:13 (the "Why Spore?" paragraph — light echo-harmonization only):**
> "Spore follows the same logic — a shared coordination grammar that can land across projects, grow in different forms, and remain interoperable and stay coherent across them without requiring centralization."

(Resolved during drafting, 2026-06-02, with Codex x-high review applied: lead is *"a coordination grammar for collective agency"* — grammar as the lead noun, with collective agency named as the grammar's canon-stated purpose (project-vision.md:132) rather than demoted out of the identity; the overclaiming *"holds … global coherence at once"* is replaced by the purpose/orientation *"for local-to-global coherence at the scales it has reached,"* echoing the canon's own scope-discipline (project-vision.md:128 *"a scoped grammar, not a universal account"*; :130 *"each scale Spore has reached … a research question, not a demonstrated property"*); the README "Why Spore?" harmonization keeps *"remain interoperable"* and adds *"and stay coherent across them"* rather than replacing it.)

**Constraints on the final wording (binding regardless of exact glyph):**

1. **Math-quiet** — no sheaf / topology / cohomology / "engine" terms in the identity line (Option B excluded).
2. **Preserve `"plural, sovereign"`; scope the coherence claim to *"the scales it has reached"*** — continuity with the current line, echoing the canon's own scope-discipline (project-vision.md:128 *"a scoped grammar, not a universal account"*; :130 *"each scale Spore has reached … a research question, not a demonstrated property"*; ADR-0031 / ADR-0032 / ADR-0044). The reframe must not re-introduce a universal-pattern claim — see Constraint 6.
3. **Preserve the Spore / Agent Commons distinction** — the reframe touches the *identity sentence* only; the Spore-as-project / Agent-Commons-as-protocol-family distinction (README.md:11; project-vision.md:12, which phrase it differently) is untouched.
4. **Three distinct edit sites** (not one find-replace — the F1 correction from the strategy-plan self-review): README.md:7 (standalone tagline) and project-vision.md:10 (`"Spore is"`-prefixed, continuing into the normative-commitments clause) carry the reframed identity sentence; README.md:13 (the "Why Spore?" paragraph) is a *light echo-harmonization only* (keep *"remain interoperable,"* add *"and stay coherent across them"*).
5. **`"coordination grammar"` is the lead noun; `"collective agency"` is named as the grammar's purpose in the lead** (resolved 2026-06-02; reinforced by Codex review) — collective agency is the canon's stated purpose (project-vision.md:132 *"The grammar's purpose is collective agency"*), so it stays in the identity line rather than being demoted out of it. The lead is *"a coordination grammar for collective agency across plural, sovereign systems."* The phrase *"infrastructure for collective agency"* survives as a secondary clause in the project-vision paragraph (*"As shared infrastructure for that agency, it operates across the normative commitments…"*); the README tagline stays lean.
6. **No achieved/universal coherence claim (anti-overclaim)** — the identity line states the grammar's *purpose/orientation* (*"for local-to-global coherence"*), scoped to *"the scales it has reached,"* and must NOT assert that global coherence is achieved or holds universally. *"holds local sovereignty and global coherence at once, across scales and scopes"* was an overclaim (it asserts an achieved, universal property) and is excluded; the §7 ledger earns *"local-to-global coherence"* only as plain language for what the grammar does, not as an achievement promise (project-vision.md:128 / :130; ADR-0031 / ADR-0032 / ADR-0044).

## Why ADR scope is insufficient

This change exceeds ordinary canon-review ADR scope under **FR-1 criterion 5 (changes the definition of canon scope), read at the identity layer.** The opening sentence of `project-vision.md` (a `doc_kind: vision` constitutional doc) is the canon's self-definition. Repositioning Spore from *"infrastructure for collective agency"* to *"a coordination grammar (local-to-global coherence)"* changes what the canon says it **is**, and its role relative to the downstream-aligned (IC, PM) and peer-instance-family (bregion, BKC, bioregional-economics) constellation that now treats Spore as upstream. **FR-2 (ambiguity rule)** is explicit: a change that *"alters a layer's identity"* defaults to foundational reframing, *"not downward."*

Per FR-2, settling the constitutional *"Spore is…"* lead is an identity-level change that belongs at the proposal layer; the ADR then *implements* the ratified wording. (The honest off-ramp in the Execution gate covers the case where consultation concludes a doc-local canon-review ADR is sufficient after all.)

**Honest narrowing.** This is the *narrowest* canon-scope sub-case — the self-description wording, not the governed-surface boundary that `reframing-pm-canon-scope` re-drew (which re-layered a doc out of canon). It is therefore lighter than that precedent. It still belongs here because (a) FR-2 routes it here, (b) the operator elected the heavier ceremony for the constitutional identity line, and (c) the cost of getting the one orienting sentence wrong is high: it propagates to the README, sheaf.lol, grant materials, and every downstream *"Spore is…"* citation. The 7-day cooling-off is cheap insurance against a hasty edit to the line the whole constellation reads first.

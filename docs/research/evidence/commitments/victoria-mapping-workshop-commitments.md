## Source Basis

**This is a seed record, not operational evidence.** It summarizes
the batch of commitments documented through BKC mapping workshops
at the process level. No individual workshop commitment records were
observed or imported. It exists to validate the batch-level schema
shape and start the annotation discipline.

**Repo sources cited:**
- `docs/protocols/claims-evidence-attestation.md`, line 50:
  `"Mapping workshop output: 23 commitments documented"` (example of
  "Deliverable" evidence type)
- `docs/patterns/commitment-pooling.md` — describes mapping workshops
  as the upstream source feeding commitment proposals into pools
- `docs/synthesis/coordination-grammar.md`, line 249:
  `"Mapping workshops surface needs/offers/gaps across landscape groups"`
  (BKC coordination loop, Sense phase)

**What is known (illustrative, from an example cell and design descriptions; not observed):**
- Mapping workshops are designed to be the primary source of commitment proposals
- At least 23 commitments were documented through this process (in fact the 23 were minted in the hackathon demo from a scripted transcript and other demo inputs, not from a workshop)
- Commitments enter the pool via steward-review verification (as designed)
- The process surfaces needs, offers, and gaps across landscape groups (as designed)

**What is unknown:**
- Individual commitment details from any specific workshop
- How many workshops produced the 23+ commitments
- Per-commitment epistemic mode breakdown
- Which commitments have progressed through the lifecycle
- Renegotiation or scope change history
- Per-commitment consequence bearers
- Standing conditions of workshop participants

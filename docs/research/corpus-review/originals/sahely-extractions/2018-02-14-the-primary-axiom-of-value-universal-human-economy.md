## Verbatim key claims

All quotes are reproduced verbatim from the source text. The axiom and principles are **McMurtry's** formulations, curated/explicated by Sahely. Anchored by nearest section heading.

- **C1** [html-section:body] *"I endorse, outline and apply John McMurtry's life-value onto-axiology, which is in all probability the most articulate theory of value developed by any philosopher in the 21st century."* (Sahely's own endorsement frame — the one clearly Sahely-authored line.)

- **C2** [html-section:The Primary Axiom of All Value] *"X is value if and only if, and to the extent that, x consists in or enables a more coherently inclusive range of thought/feeling/action than without it."*

- **C3** [html-section:The Primary Axiom of All Value] *"Conversely: x is disvalue if and only if, and to the extent that, x reduces/disables/destroys any range of thought/experience/action."*

- **C4** [html-section:Definitions of the three ultimate fields of value] *"the true = progressive consistency with the P-Axiom, or the life coherence principle"*

- **C5** [html-section:unlimited validity of the P-Axiom] *"(7) sovereignty in that it overrides any other value in cases of conflict;"*

- **C6** [html-section:Principle 1] *"The ultimate organising principle of any life-coherent society or economy through generational time is secure access to means of life/life goods otherwise in short supply (ie., the production and distribution of goods and the protection of ecosystem services in accord with Principles 2 through 7)."*

- **C7** [html-section:Principle 2] *"A means of life is a means of life if and only if it enables life capacities/abilities not possible without it (eg., food, water, shelter and literacy education)."*

- **C8** [html-section:Principle 3] *"The complete and universal set of means of life which all humans require to flourish as human are: breathable air, sense-open space, and daily light … clean water, nourishing foods and self-waste disposal … shelter space from the elements … environmental surroundings whose elements and contours contribute to the whole … intimate love, social inclusion, safety and healthcare when ill or infirm … activities of language-logos/art-play to choose and learn from … meaningful work or service to perform … self-governing choice in each's enjoyment consistent with each's provision."*

- **C9** [html-section:Principle 3 Converse] *"Any priced commodity which does not directly or indirectly provide means of life/life goods for these needs is uneconomic, and anti-economic to the extent of life resources wasted on the commodity's production and consumption (eg., non-growth addictive commodities)."*

- **C10** [html-section:Principle 4 Converse] *"Willingness or ability to pay prices for commodities does not measure their life requirement, but increasingly the opposite (e.g., fast-foods, leisure motor vehicles)"*

- **C11** [html-section:Principle 5] *"The true measure of the overall performance of any society or economy is its civil commons development or the full access of its members to these life goods (including the work share required to provide them) in comparison to a previous state of the society or economy, or to another socioeconomic order …"*

- **C12** [html-section:Principle 5 Converse] *"Growth of aggregate commodities sold in a society (GDP) is never an accurate or reliable measure of a society or economy's development unless it is made to correspond to the access of its members to life goods as defined …"*

- **C13** [html-section:Principle 6] *"The primary capital of any society or economy is Life Capital (LC→LC1→LC n) = the wealth of means of life/life goods that produce more without loss in cumulative yield through time (eg., species/ecological, social, and knowledge capital which reproduce and grow if not deprived by life-blind mechanisms of private money growth-predating them in carcinogenic self-multiplication)."*

- **C14** [html-section:Principle 7] *"The efficiency of any product, tool or process increases, and only increases, to the extent that: i: inputs and throughputs function to enable the provision of life goods with diminishing waste and externalities … = Ecological Efficiency … ii: reduced inputs of materials/energy/space/mandatory work time produce same or greater means of life outputs … = Physical Input-Output Efficiency … iii: capability development of productive agents enables more life goods, life-time, and/or life-range choices than before … = Human Development Efficiency."*

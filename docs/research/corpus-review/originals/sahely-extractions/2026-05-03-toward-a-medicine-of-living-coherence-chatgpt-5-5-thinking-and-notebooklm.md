## Diagnostic primitives / framework structure

**Seven Relational Patterns of Living Coherence (THE CENTRAL DIAGNOSTIC GRAMMAR)**:

| # | Pattern | Operational question |
|---|---------|----------------------|
| 1 | Boundary / Self-Production | Does the system conserve itself as a unity? |
| 2 | Exchange / Provisioning | Does the system access what it needs? |
| 3 | Perturbation Sensing / Distinction | Does the system register relevant difference? |
| 4 | Context Interpretation / Valuation | Does the system evaluate what matters? |
| 5 | Regulation / Proportionate Response | Does the system respond at right magnitude/direction? |
| 6 | Memory / Historical Readiness | Does the system carry forward life-serving learning? |
| 7 | Resolution / Repair / Regeneration | Does the system complete cycles and restore? |

**Scale-jumping consistency**: same 7 patterns operate at clinical level (§10) AND policy level (§25). This is a **fractal grammar** — same primitives, different operationalization at each scale.

- Disease = stabilized loss of living coherence (§11)
- Health = recoverable life-capacity (§12)
- Diagnosis = life-serving distinction (§15)
- Treatment = minimum-sufficient, condition-restoring action (§17)
- Clinical encounter = structural coupling (§16)

## 10-fact hand-curated PRIMARY-anchor extraction

1. **Central proposal: living coherence as synthetic extension of autopoiesis** [pdf-p3 abstract; p5-6 §3] — "The term **living coherence** is introduced here as a synthetic concept. It does not replace autopoiesis. Rather, it extends the implications of autopoiesis into contemporary biological domains: mitochondria, redox regulation, immunity, microbiomes, stress physiology, affective life, development, behavior, and ecological relations." Health = dynamic conservation of viable coupling; disease = costly conserved drift / loss of congruence; healing = restoration or reorganization of viable structural coupling across organism–niche unity. This is the **canonical definitional move** for Sahely's May-arc; subsequent papers (#5 emotioning, #6 evolutionary, #7 governance, #8 peace) all build on living-coherence-as-extension.

2. **Maturanan biological grammar enumerated as 8 linked distinctions** [pdf-p5 §2] — autopoiesis (self-producing organization) / structural determination (system changes according to own structure, not external instruction) / structural coupling (recurrent congruent interactions with medium) / cognition (effective action in domain of existence) / emotioning (configuring domains of possible action) / languaging (recursive consensual coordination of consensual coordinations of behavior) / love (relational domain in which the other arises as legitimate in coexistence) / natural drift (lineages conserve ways of living through systemic reproductive conservation of organism–niche relations). The full grammar is here — the primary citation site for Sahely's substrate. Cites Maturana & Varela (1980) Autopoiesis and Cognition; Maturana (2002); Maturana & Mpodozis (2000) origin of species by means of natural drift.

3. **Six-stage grammar of chronic disease as costly conserved drift** [pdf-p13 §9] — verbatim: "First, the organism is perturbed... Second, the organism compensates... Third, cost accumulates [allostatic load]... Fourth, the compensatory pattern may become an attractor... Fifth, the attractor narrows... Sixth, the cost manifests as chronic disease, premature aging, or collapse." Maps allostatic-load logic onto Maturana's natural-drift substrate; chronic disease (hypertension / insulin resistance / chronic inflammation / autoimmune activation / obesity / sleep disruption / burnout) interpreted as conserved pattern that once supported viability but became costly and incongruent. Cites McEwen 1998/1999 allostatic load.

4. **Disease as costly conserved drift — non-deficit framing** [pdf-p4 abstract continuation; p6-7 §3; p13 §9] — "Disease is interpreted not only as defect, lesion, pathogen, mutation, or biochemical abnormality, but also as a costly conserved pattern of living. In many chronic conditions, the organism preserves short-term viability through compensatory patterns that may become self-reinforcing and increasingly incongruent with wider organismic coherence." Important: framework does NOT reject biomedical intervention — situates it within wider biological grammar. Antibiotics, surgery, insulin, antihypertensives, immunotherapy all valid; they participate in (do not replace) restoration of viable coupling.

5. **Non-forcing action doctrine across 5 domains** [pdf-p15-16 §11] — verbatim: "**Non-forcing action is therefore not inaction. It is disciplined participation in the autonomy of living systems.**" Applied across: (a) **medicine** = support sleep, rhythm, nutrition, movement, microbial ecology, metabolic flexibility, immune tolerance, meaning, relational safety alongside necessary technical intervention; (b) **education** = transformation of learner's domain of distinctions, not information transfer; (c) **parenting** = child is accompanied, not manufactured; (d) **governance** = institutions conserve dignity / autonomy / reciprocity / repair rather than enforce compliance at cost of life-capacity; (e) **ecology** = restoration is participation in renewed co-drift, not domination. **Connects structurally to Spore's substitution-trap discipline (ADR-0048)**: forcing produces substitution-trap pattern. Cross-tradition convergence; descriptive only.

6. **Organism–microbe–niche unity as holonic relation** [pdf-p10-11 §6] — verbatim: "The organism–microbe–niche unity is a **holonic relation** in the precise sense that it is both a distinguishable whole and a participant in wider ecological and evolutionary coherences. Its wholeness is not static or hierarchical; it is relational, historical, and operational." Dysbiosis reframed as "disturbed pattern of host–microbe–niche coupling" — not simply "bad bacteria." Healing may require microbial intervention BUT also restoration of diet, rhythm, movement, sleep, relational safety, ecological exposure, immune tolerance. **Connects to Spore's Holon primitive (ADR-0044) + holon-irreducibility property (ADR-0050)**: structural-isomorphism with whole-not-reducible-to-aggregation-of-parts. Descriptive only.

7. **Immunity as relational identity conservation** [pdf-p9 §5] — verbatim: immune system "conserves the organism's biological identity through tolerance, surveillance, and response to perturbation" — not merely defense against external threats but ongoing distinction-making between what is conservable-as-self and what perturbs viable organization. Autoimmunity reframed as **conserved disharmony** between immune distinctions and viable coupling (not failed self/non-self discrimination per orthodox immunology). Sister concept to #2's Social Ecology of Immune Disease paper.

8. **Mitochondria as energetic-relational participants (not powerhouses)** [pdf-p7-8 §4] — verbatim: "Mitochondria are not the command center of the cell. They are participants in the bioenergetic and signaling conditions through which cellular viability is conserved." Cites Picard et al. 2019 mitochondrial psychobiology. Sister concept to #3's Mitochondria, Structural Coupling, and Intrinsic Health paper — where #3 develops the Maturana–Picard dialogue at length (48pp), this anchor distills the principle in one section. Mitochondria + redox + immune + microbiome + endocrine + affective systems are "biological distinctions made within the living organization itself" not external explanations imposed.

9. **Emotioning as biological configurator of possible action** [pdf-p12 §8] — verbatim: "Maturana's concept of emotioning is biological. **Emotions configure domains of possible action**. To change emotion is to change the actions, relations, and worlds that become available." Cites Panksepp 2010 primary-process emotional systems; Damasio & Carvalho 2013 feelings-as-life-regulation. Fear / love / shame / curiosity / grief / play "configure the organism's world of possible action" — not internal psychological states alone. Sister to #5 (Emotioning and Living Coherence) which develops this at full white-paper length. Recurring Wave-10 entity Katherine Peil Kauffman (emotional sentience) cited.

10. **Natural drift as alternative to gene-centered explanation** [pdf-p11-12 §7] — verbatim: "Maturana and Mpodozis's theory of natural drift emphasizes that lineages conserve ways of living through the systemic reproductive conservation of organism–niche relations. In this view, the organism's behavior is not secondary to evolution; it is central to the niche relation that is conserved or lost." Cites Maturana & Mpodozis 2000. Evolution is "the historical conservation and transformation of organism–niche relations through lineages" — natural selection is consequence of historical drift, not the generative mechanism. Sister to #6 (From Natural Drift to Evolutionary Living Coherence) which extends this to evo-devo, niche construction, symbiosis, inclusive inheritance, population genetics.

## Key concepts and structural moves

- **Health as system stability (not state)**: Health = dynamic balance between system load, capacity, and adaptive response. Disease = loss of coherence under constraint, not isolated abnormalities. Direct application of Wave-9 framework to cardiovascular medicine.
- **Life-course determinants framework**: Developmental + social + commercial + economic + environmental factors jointly determine system load / capacity / response across the life course. Multi-scale integration: in-utero conditioning → childhood social environment → adult labor + commercial determinants → late-life planetary determinants.
- **DOHaD substrate (Barker, Gluckman, Hanson)**: Developmental Origins of Health and Disease — fetal and early-life conditions shape adult cardiovascular vulnerability via developmental conditioning of allostatic regulation. Sahely CITES Barker 1995 BMJ + Gluckman-Hanson-Cooper-Thornburg 2008 N Engl J Med + Hanson-Gluckman 2014 Physiol Rev.
- **Allostatic load (McEwen, Seeman)**: Cumulative biological cost of chronic stress mediators. Sahely CITES McEwen 1998 N Engl J Med + Seeman-McEwen-Rowe-Singer 2001 PNAS (MacArthur studies of successful aging).
- **Early-warning signals from complex-system dynamics**: Sahely CITES Scheffer et al 2009 Nature paper on early-warning signals for critical transitions + Folke et al 2010 resilience-thinking + Kauffman 1993 Origins of Order. These are NOT new citations introduced here; they reinforce the existing Wave-9 framework with clinical-grade literature.
- **Reframing SDG 8 (work + growth + physiological load)**: Sahely's most operationally-novel move — economic and labour systems (SDG 8) are central regulators of chronic physiological load. The "decent work + economic growth" SDG target is read as a structural variable in the cardiovascular-coherence-system, not as an external policy area. SDG 8 ≡ physiological-load determinant.
- **From disease management → condition design**: Policy paradigm shift. Health systems are designed to manage downstream disease; the framework calls for upstream **condition design** — shaping the multi-domain conditions under which circulatory coherence persists across the life course.
- **One Health perspective (cross-system integration)**: Health depends on coherent circulation across human + societal + planetary systems. Cross-scale integration is the structural concern; cardiovascular disease in any one scale is a localization of multi-scale incoherence.
- **Crosswalk table (Table 1)**: Maps the coherence-framework dimensions onto SDGs / WHO frameworks / One Health domains. Operational discipline: cross-domain mapping enables translation across policy levers.

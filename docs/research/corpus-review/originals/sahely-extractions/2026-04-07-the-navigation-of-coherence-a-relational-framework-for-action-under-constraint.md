## Key concepts and structural moves

- **Master Diagram as 11-layer architecture**: First explicit articulation of Sahely's full architectural stack — from relational field substrate up to operational imperative. Each layer is a structural component; system viability depends on integrity of the whole stack, not any single component.
- **Distortion layer (4 transformation modes)**: Filtering (exclusion of non-conforming signals) / Reinterpretation (reframing anomalies to fit narratives) / Delay (postponement of action until thresholds are crossed) / Suppression (discouragement of disconfirming information). These produce **epistemic closure** at field-level.
- **Constraint layer (3 friction sources)**: Institutional inertia (resistance to deviation) / Incentive locking (alignment with proxies rather than conditions) / Penalty asymmetry (immediate cost for deviation, delayed cost for misalignment). The Constraint layer is the **what-resists-correction** layer.
- **Control vs Navigation distinction**: Traditional control assumes sufficient visibility + stable causality + neutral intervention — all fail in distorted systems. **Navigation** replaces optimization with continuous alignment: "the capacity to act within partially observable, dynamically evolving relational fields while maintaining sensitivity to feedback and preserving adaptive capacity."
- **Four modes of navigation**: 
  - **Stealth Navigation** — acting in alignment with underlying conditions while remaining legible within constraints
  - **Local Coherence Building** — creating aligned micro-domains within broader distortion
  - **Signal Proxying** — routing signals through trusted pathways when formal channels are blocked
  - **Optionality Preservation** — maintaining future action capacity by avoiding irreversible commitments
- **Trust as threshold condition**: Signals are actionable only when trusted; below-threshold ignored, above-threshold coordination becomes possible. Loss of trust fragments the system into isolated interpretive domains, collapsing the epistemic commons. Restoration of coherence requires reconstruction of trust pathways, **not merely improved information**.
- **Tool mediation as active component (not neutral)**: Perception is mediated through tools that transform signals: Reality → Data → AI Processing → Dashboard → Decision. Tools compress complexity, stabilize narratives, filter signal diversity. When aligned, they enhance signal fidelity; when misaligned, they amplify distortion and reinforce epistemic closure. **Tools are not neutral — they are active components of the relational field.** Particular implication: AI/dashboard infrastructure can be either coherence-supporting or epistemic-closure-amplifying.
- **The Viability Invariant** (first explicit articulation): *"A system remains viable if and only if the combined integrity of signal fidelity, trust thresholds, and optionality is sufficient to enable adaptive response before irreversible transition."* This invariant is scale-independent, domain-agnostic, relational in nature. **Pre-figures #5's E7 quartic invariant formalization.**
- **Operational Imperative**: The central challenge is not prediction or control but "the disciplined navigation of coherence under conditions of constraint and resistance." Effective intervention requires acting without full certainty, responding to weak signals, operating within friction rather than assuming its absence.

## Final statement (verbatim from Executive Summary)

> Coherence is not a state to be achieved. It is a condition to be continuously maintained.

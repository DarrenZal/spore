## Key concepts and structural moves

- **Perception as structurally conditioned**: Drawing on Maturana's biology of cognition, perception is not direct access to reality but is conditioned by the structure of the perceiver. No privileged observer exists.
- **Distributed observers and divergence**: When multiple observers see a single system through different conditioning structures, their reports diverge — this divergence is structurally constitutive, not a measurement error.
- **Three-lineage synthesis (Maturana / Galtung / McMurtry)**:
  - **Maturana**: structural coupling + biology of cognition → epistemic dimension
  - **Galtung**: structural violence + structural analysis → structural dimension
  - **McMurtry**: life-value onto-axiology + life-ground → axiological dimension
  
  These three are integrated into a unified account where coherence requires the joint maintenance of epistemic alignment + structural integrity + life-aligned value.
- **Epistemic closure as failure mode**: Distortion can propagate within the relational field, leading to "epistemic closure" — where a system loses the capacity to recognize that misinterpretation is occurring. Systems remain *internally coherent while becoming misaligned with the requirements of sustaining life*. This is the core failure pattern.
- **Value inversion as failure mode**: When epistemic closure compounds with structural distortion, values invert — life-displacing operations come to be enacted as if they were life-supporting. The McMurtry "value-program" critique applied at field-level.
- **Three-axis viability invariant (signal integrity + life-capacity + coordinated action)**: A minimal architecture is proposed in which system viability depends on the joint maintenance of these three — a triadic invariant pre-figuring #5's E7 quartic invariant.
- **Early warning as recognition of relational patterns (not prediction of discrete events)**: Reframes early-warning epistemology away from variable-threshold-watching toward pattern-noticing across the relational field.
- **Action as navigation within constraint-field (not control of components)**: Action-discipline reframed; control assumes external observer and decomposability, both of which fail in the relational-field frame.

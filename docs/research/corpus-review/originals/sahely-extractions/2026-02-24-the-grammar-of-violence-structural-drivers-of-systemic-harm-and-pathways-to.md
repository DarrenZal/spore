## Verbatim key claims (anchor depth — 15+ load-bearing)

### C-1 (Sahely, integrating Galtung + McMurtry) — Central thesis: structural homology across domains
> "Modern systemic instability is not accidental or episodic, but generated by a layered architecture in which cultural narratives legitimize institutional designs that reward extraction, escalation, and externalization of life costs. Crisis events reinforce rather than destabilize this architecture through feedback loops of moral framing and security expansion." `[pdf-p5]`

### C-2 (Sahely) — Four-element core diagnosis
> "Modern instability is not a sequence of unrelated failures. It is the coherent output of: 1. A layered violence architecture in which cultural narratives legitimize structural harm. 2. A conflict grammar oriented toward binary opposition and annihilation (Dualism–Manicheism–Armageddon). 3. An economic value code defining rationality as self-maximizing growth. 4. A feedback system in which crises reinforce rather than disrupt underlying incentives." `[pdf-p5-6]`

### C-3 (Sahely) — The viability principle (substrate-claim for Wave-1 anchor sibling)
> "A system is rational if and only if it preserves and regenerates the life-support conditions that enable its continuation." `[pdf-p6]`

### C-4 (Sahely) — Grammar-level vs event-level analysis
> "Events are surface phenomena. Institutions are mid-level structures. Value codes are deep-level grammars. A grammar determines what counts as rational, legitimate, necessary, or inevitable within a system." `[pdf-p9]`

### C-5 (Sahely citing Galtung 1990) — Galtung violence-triangle import
> "Johan Galtung's formulation of direct, structural, and cultural violence provides a foundational analytical model (Galtung, 1990). It shifts the focus from isolated acts to layered architecture." `[pdf-p11]`

### C-6 (Sahely citing Galtung) — Violence as reduction of basic-need satisfaction
> "Galtung's typology identifies four domains of basic needs (Galtung, 1990): Survival, Well-being, Identity, Freedom. Violence exists when these are avoidably reduced." `[pdf-p13]`

### C-7 (Sahely) — Self-sealing-system mechanism (5-step closed loop)
> "1. Cultural narratives define what is rational and legitimate. 2. These narratives shape institutional design. 3. Institutional design produces structural outcomes. 4. Structural pressures escalate into direct violence. 5. Direct violence reinforces cultural narratives ('We were right to act'). The system stabilizes itself." `[pdf-p12]`

### C-8 (Sahely citing Galtung 2013) — DMA archetype definition
> "Galtung identifies two archetypal conflict grammars that have shaped civilizations: Dualism–Manicheism–Armageddon (DMA) and Holism–Dialectics–Transcendence (HDT) (Galtung, 2013). … The DMA script encodes three linked assumptions: 1. The world is divided into Good and Evil. 2. Conflict between them is inevitable. 3. Resolution occurs through decisive victory of one over the other. It is binary, moralized, and terminal." `[pdf-p16]`

### C-9 (Sahely citing Galtung 1996) — Projection-as-stabilizer
> "In Global Projections of Deep-Rooted U.S. Pathologies, Galtung argues that systems often project their own violent tendencies onto external enemies (Galtung, 1996)." `[pdf-p17]`

### C-10 (Sahely citing McMurtry 2014) — Cancer analogy as structural description not metaphor
> "McMurtry characterizes deregulated self-multiplying money sequences as structurally analogous to cancer (McMurtry, 2014). Cancer cells: Multiply without reference to the organism's life. Appropriate resources without systemic accountability. Destroy host systems through unchecked expansion. … This is not metaphorical rhetoric. It is structural description." `[pdf-p22]`

### C-11 (Sahely citing McMurtry 2003) — War state as economic-coupling mechanism
> "McMurtry's analysis of the war state (McMurtry, 2003) identifies how: Security rhetoric protects resource interests. Military intervention aligns with corporate expansion. Public funds subsidize private accumulation. When growth is sacralized and conflict is moralized, militarization becomes rationalized as necessary for economic vitality. Security and growth merge." `[pdf-p23]`

### C-12 (Sahely) — Violence without villains thesis
> "When self-maximizing growth is institutionalized: Ecological destruction is incentivized. Inequality is rewarded. Public systems weaken. Militarization becomes protective. No conspiracy is required. Individuals operating rationally within the code reproduce structural violence. The system selects for actors who comply with its incentives." `[pdf-p24]`

### C-13 (Sahely) — Anti-conspiracy diagnostic guardrail
> "Conspiracies require secrecy and coordinated intent. Structural grammars require neither. They reproduce through: Educational systems, Economic theory, Political rhetoric, Media framing, Institutional incentives. They persist not because a hidden group enforces them, but because they are normalized." `[pdf-p9]`

### C-14 (Sahely) — Critique-replicates-DMA risk (recursive diagnostic)
> "If the critique of systemic violence is framed as: Evil elites vs virtuous people, Corrupt insiders vs innocent outsiders, Then the critique itself adopts DMA logic. Binary moralization reappears. Annihilation rhetoric follows. The grammar remains unchanged — only the sides shift. This is how revolutions replicate the structures they overthrow." `[pdf-p31-32]`

### C-15 (Sahely citing McMurtry 2003) — Ruling group-mind as distributed cognition
> "McMurtry refers to a 'ruling group-mind' dynamic in analyzing war-state projection (McMurtry, 2003). … Group-mind does not imply unified malicious intent. It refers to shared premises embedded within institutions. Individuals operating within institutions internalize the prevailing grammar." `[pdf-p32]`

### C-16 (Sahely) — Five constructive entailments of viability architecture
> "[The viability principle] entails: Redefining security as ecological and infrastructural resilience. Aligning economic incentives with regenerative capacity. Embedding ecological accounting into fiscal policy. Reframing conflict through integrative rather than annihilative logic. Securing essential life goods as civil commons rather than unregulated commodities." `[pdf-p6-7]`

### C-17 (Sahely) — Methodological integrity disclaimer (anti-totalizing claim)
> "The claim of this white paper is not: 'Everything is controlled.' It is: 'When similar harm patterns recur across independent domains, and those patterns align with a shared value grammar, systemic causation is the most parsimonious explanation.' This is structural reasoning, not ideological assertion." `[pdf-p33]`

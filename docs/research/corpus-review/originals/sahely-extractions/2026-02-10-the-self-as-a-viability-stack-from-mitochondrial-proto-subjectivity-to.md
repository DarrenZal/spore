## Verbatim key claims (12 claims; high-fidelity)

### C-1 (p5, Abstract) — Central thesis (LOAD-BEARING)
> "This white paper proposes a unifying framework in which the self is understood as a **stacked architecture of viability-regulating interfaces**, rather than a unitary entity or representational construct."

### C-2 (p5, Abstract) — Damasio-grounded architecture
> "Building on Antonio Damasio's typology of proto-self, core self, and autobiographical self, the paper anchors the proto-self in mitochondrial psychobiology, identifies molecular signaling (neuropeptides, cytokines, hormones, and mitokines such as GDF15) as the primary translation layer from cellular viability to felt experience, and situates consciousness in brainstem–hypothalamic affective circuits."

### C-3 (p5, Abstract) — Life-regulating-itself-from-inside framing
> "The result is a rigorously naturalistic, non-reductive account of the self as **life regulating itself from the inside**, with direct implications for psychiatry, trauma, ethics, and integrative medicine."

### C-4 (p6, Executive Summary) — Central organizing claim (LOAD-BEARING)
> "The paper advances a single organizing claim: **the self is not a thing but a viability stack** — a layered architecture through which life regulates itself under uncertainty, with each layer solving a distinct regulatory problem and inheriting constraints from below."

### C-5 (p6, Executive Summary) — Consciousness-as-felt-enforcement
> "**Core self** emerges at the level of brainstem–hypothalamic integration, where deviations from homeostasis become felt as valence, arousal, and motivation. Consciousness, on this account, is not cortical awareness but the **felt enforcement of viability constraints**."

### C-6 (p6, Executive Summary) — Trauma as interface injury (LOAD-BEARING)
> "It reframes trauma and psychopathology as **interface injuries** — persistent constraints on regulatory flexibility — rather than as failures of insight or will. It also clarifies why body-based and relational therapies are often necessary precursors to narrative repair."

### C-7 (p7, §1.1 The Persistent Confusion) — Structural-error diagnosis
> "The difficulty is not lack of data or sophistication. It is a structural error: the self is repeatedly approached as a *thing* to be located, rather than as a **process that spans interfaces**."

### C-8 (p7, §1.2 The Central Proposal) — Layered descriptions of same architecture
> "Each 'self' described in the literature — proto-self, core self, autobiographical self — corresponds to a **distinct interface depth** at which life must regulate itself under uncertainty. These are not competing theories. They are *layered descriptions of the same architecture*, seen from different observational standpoints. At each interface: intrinsic constraints must be satisfied, information is compressed, and global control variables become *felt*."

### C-9 (p8, §1.2 closing) — Single organizing principle: viability from the inside
> "What philosophers call subjectivity, neuroscientists call affect, clinicians call agency, and traditional systems call vitality are different projections of a single organizing principle: **viability from the inside**."

### C-10 (p9, §2.1 Viability Before Representation) — Viability precedes representation (LOAD-BEARING)
> "Any account of the self that begins with representation begins too late. Long before a system can model the world, describe itself, or narrate experience, it must *remain viable*. Viability here does not mean mere persistence. It means maintaining a bounded, coherent organization in the face of perturbation, uncertainty, and limited information. This requirement is not optional. It is the precondition for everything that follows."

### C-11 (p9, §2.1) — Value is implicit in continued existence (LOAD-BEARING for Spore ADR-0049 reproduction-continuity)
> "From this standpoint, value is not something added by cognition, culture, or language. Value is implicit in the very act of continued existence. States are already better or worse for the system before they are named, categorized, or understood. A system that could not register this difference would not survive long enough to think about it. This is the first unifying move: **viability precedes representation**."

### C-12 (p10, §2.4 Qualia as Interface Variables) — Qualia definition (LOAD-BEARING; companion to Wave-4 #3)
> "These experienced variables are what we call *qualia*. They are not mental objects or private decorations. They are **felt global control signals** that encode the system's current relationship to its own constraints. This is why qualia are: ineffable (they are already maximally compressed), private (they are relative to the system's own viability), unified (they govern the whole, not a part), motivational (they bias action directly)."

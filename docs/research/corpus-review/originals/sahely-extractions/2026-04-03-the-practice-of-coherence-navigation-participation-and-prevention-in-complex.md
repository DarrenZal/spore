## Key concepts and structural moves

- **Seven relational variables (C/M/X/D/P/R/O)**: Constraints, Margins, State, Disturbance, Perception, Regulation, Options — irreducible grammar of any complex system. Foreshadows the seven primitives that #5 formalizes as Fano-plane geometry.
- **Coherence as relational, not state-level**: Failure begins when coherence across the C/M/X/D/P/R/O relationships degrades, not when any single variable crosses a threshold. Visible variables are lagging indicators of deeper structural change.
- **Distortion as structural (not error)**: Signals are filtered, delayed, reinterpreted, suppressed via incentive structures, institutional pressures, cognitive limits, political pressures — distortion is intrinsic, not anomalous.
- **Observer-inside-the-system**: Decision-makers are not external; they are embedded with constrained perception, conditioned action. Effective practice requires self-mapping of one's own position within the system. Pre-figures the "embedded observer" theme that #2/#3 amplify.
- **Four early warning signals**: Path dependence (same input → different outcome) / Cross-channel divergence (subsystems fall out of alignment) / Increasing variability / Delayed recovery. These four form a structural-stability monitoring lattice.
- **Five principles for decision-under-distortion**: Act on structure not variables / Preserve margin / Minimize forcing / Interrogate apparent stability / Prefer reversible actions. Re-frames "Wu-Wei" (per tag-cluster) as operational discipline rather than mystical posture.
- **Prevention as coherence**: Five-tier prevention taxonomy (Primordial → Primary → Secondary → Tertiary → Quaternary) — shifts focus from reaction to upstream coherence maintenance. Quaternary specifically guards against harmful over-intervention.
- **The Altimeter**: Operational early-detection tool tracking the four warning signals via proxies (path-dependence → input/output drift; divergence → correlation breakdown; variability → rising variance; recovery → time to baseline). Pre-figures the formal "E7 quartic invariant altimeter" of #5/#6.
- **Minimal Intervention Principle**: "Act only to the extent necessary to preserve coherence — and no further." Avoids force, margin depletion, structural damage.
- **Cross-domain validation**: Medicine (early detection beyond vital signs; avoiding over-treatment) / Infrastructure (process stability beyond compliance metrics; avoiding over-control) / Economics (recognizing instability beneath growth; avoiding policy overreach). Same patterns hold across domains.
- **Reframe of system engagement**: From control / reaction / variable-management → to participation / anticipation / structural awareness.

## Final statement (verbatim from Executive Summary §"Final Statement")

> Coherence is not achieved by controlling systems from the outside, but by acting within them — early, precisely, and with restraint.

## Verbatim load-bearing claims (curator-extracted; concepts attributed to McMurtry substrate)

### C-1 (Sahely-abstracting-McMurtry) — Cancer as isomorphic explanatory model (not metaphor)
> "In the onto-axiological diagnosis of our global condition, the 'Cancer Stage of Capitalism' is not a mere descriptive analogy; it is a rigorous isomorphic explanatory model. The mutation occurs when the 'money-sequence' of value—the drive to turn money into more money (M-M')—becomes autonomous, uninhibited, and entirely disconnected from any productive or life-serving function." `[pdf-p8]` (attribution: McMurtry 1999/2014)

### C-2 (Sahely-abstracting-McMurtry) — 1973 as systemic mutation turning-point
> "This systemic mutation reached a decisive turning point in 1973. With the unilateral abandonment of the gold standard and the subsequent oil crisis, the global medium of exchange was 'ungrounded' from any real value base. This facilitated a shift toward a 'supranational sovereignty of bankers,' where money is printed at will and leveraged exponentially by private interests, uncoupled from the life-host's regulatory requirements." `[pdf-p8-9]` (attribution: McMurtry)

### C-3 (Sahely-abstracting-McMurtry) — Four metastasis pathways
> "1. Austerity Programmes (Bleeding the Host): … Public life-capacities—healthcare, education, and pensions—are liquidated to service 'debts' to private banks. … 2. Junk-Food Claws & Life-Killing Commodities: … By conflating 'life-needs' with 'commodity desires,' the value-code protects disease-causing products as 'goods' simply because they are priced. … 3. The 9-11 & Security Strategy: … functions as a 'meta-programme' designed to 'reset world uprising to no resistance.' … 4. Ecological Depredation as 'Externality': … the M-M' circuit has no allowed corrective for the despoliation of air, water, or soil." `[pdf-p9-10]` (attribution: McMurtry — these pathways are McMurtry's 2014 Cancer Stage thesis)

### C-4 (Sahely-abstracting-McMurtry) — Civil Commons as Social Immune System
> "The Civil Commons is the underlying defense system of society—the 'Life-Ground' of socially constructed rules and institutions that protect the shared requirements of human existence. It is the primary target of the Cancer System, which seeks to dismantle any collective barrier to money-sequencing." `[pdf-p10]` (attribution: McMurtry)

### C-5 (Sahely-abstracting-McMurtry) — Three Universal Life-Requirements
> "The Civil Commons exists to protect the three pillars of human health that no 'invisible hand' can provide: Income Security: The guaranteed continuity of life-means … for all members of society. Human Vocation: The protection of a citizen's right to make a functioning contribution to society's real life-needs … Environmental Life-Host: The preservation of the life-carrying capacities of the earth." `[pdf-p10-11]` (attribution: McMurtry)

### C-6 (Sahely-abstracting-McMurtry) — Life-Capital ontological first principle
> "**The Ontological First Principle**: 'Life-Capital is the wealth that creates more life-wealth without loss.'" `[pdf-p11]` (attribution: McMurtry direct quote)

### C-7 (Sahely-abstracting-McMurtry) — Dodge v. Ford as legal substrate of cancer-stage
> "Money-Capital Sequence (M-M'): Unlawful to serve life. Per Dodge v. Ford, directing money to social well-being is a violation of stockholder priority." `[pdf-p11]` (attribution: McMurtry citing legal case)

### C-8 (Sahely-abstracting-McMurtry) — Money-creation as private debt-issue
> "**The Smoking Gun**: 97% of all circulating money is created as debt-issue by private banks for profit (IMF data)." `[pdf-p11]` (attribution: McMurtry citing IMF data; this is McMurtry's signature critique of fractional-reserve banking)

### C-9 (Sahely-abstracting-McMurtry) — Public Banking as recovery mechanism
> "Public Banking: Reclaiming sovereign control over currency and credit from the private bank displacement. Life-Principled Investment: Directing public spending toward enabling people's lives (e.g., Brazil's Bolsa Familia) rather than subsidizing corporate money-sequences." `[pdf-p6]` (attribution: McMurtry)

### C-10 (Sahely-abstracting-McMurtry) — Zero Bad Growth distinction
> "A life-coherent economy distinguishes between 'Zero Growth' and 'Zero Bad Growth.' A healthy system regulates its own expansion, rationing resources toward life-need while aggressively inhibiting the growth of carcinogenic waste and toxic commodities." `[pdf-p12]` (attribution: McMurtry)

### G-1 (Galtung 1996, abstracted) — CMT Syndrome (Emotive subconscious)
> "Myths: Narratives of a glamorous future (e.g., Manifest Destiny) or the 'Trinity' of Judeo-Christianity, Free Markets, and Democracy. ### G-2 (Galtung 1996, abstracted) — DMA Syndrome (Cognitive structure)
> "Dichotomy: Dividing the world into two watertight parts (Us vs. Them). Manicheism: Defining those parts as Good vs. Evil. Armageddon: The belief that the tension between Good and Evil must be resolved through a final, violent confrontation." `[pdf-p5]` — DMA framework is Galtung's substrate; anchor #6 §III invokes it directly via C-8 (Galtung 2013) but origin-traces to this 1996 work

### G-3 (Galtung 1996, abstracted) — U.S. Psychogram: 21 archetypes
> "The document outlines a specific 'code' or 'patriotic program' consisting of 21 archetypes that drive U.S. behavior. Categories: Chosenness (covenanted people; sacred land; U.S. lives are of a higher kind), Myths (Melting Pot; Trinity; Manifest Destiny; Invincibility), Traumas (Immigrant trauma; Genocide of Native Americans; Slavery; Civil War; Wars Not Won), DMA (Two-ness; God vs. Satan; Violence as final arbiter), RP Complex (Repression of Self's violence; Projection of violence on the 'Other')." `[pdf-p5-6]`

### G-4 (Galtung 1996, abstracted) — War Participation Index (WPI) ranking
> "WPI Top 10 Ranking (as of 1985/1996 data): 1. United States of America (.3040), 2. Israel (.1842), 3. Ottoman Empire/Turkey (.1552), 4. England/Great Britain (.1277), 5. South Africa (.1165). The U.S. average is calculated at more than one military 'use' abroad per year since 1798, with the frequency of use increasing over time." `[pdf-p6-7]`

### G-5 (Galtung 1996, abstracted) — Isomorphism mechanism (South Africa / Israel support)
> "Support for these regimes is attributed to 'isomorphism'—a recognition of the Self in others. The U.S. identified with Boers and Zionists as fellow 'covenanted nations' fighting 'pagan' or 'inferior' others." `[pdf-p8]`

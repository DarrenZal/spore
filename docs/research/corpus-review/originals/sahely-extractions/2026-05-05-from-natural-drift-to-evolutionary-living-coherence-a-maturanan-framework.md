## 7-fact moderate-selective extraction

1. **Core thesis: evolution as historical conservation of viable organism-niche relations** [pdf-p4] — verbatim: "**Evolution is the historical conservation, transformation, and diversification of viable organism–niche relations through reproduction, development, behavior, plasticity, niche construction, symbiosis, inclusive inheritance, and population-level genetic sorting.**" Maturana provides "the evolutionary unit of meaning: the conserved way of living"; contemporary evolutionary biology provides the mechanisms. Develops anchor #4 §7 at full white-paper length.

2. **Three-synthesis integration: Modern + Extended + Maturanan** [pdf-p4 + p27 conclusion] — preserves: (a) Modern Synthesis (population-genetic mechanisms — allele-frequency change, selection, drift, mutation, recombination, gene flow, quantitative traits, pleiotropy, epistasis); (b) Extended Evolutionary Synthesis (evo-devo, plasticity, niche construction, inclusive inheritance, reciprocal causation); (c) Maturana–Mpodozis natural drift (organism-niche-relations as evolutionary unit). Synthesis-claim: each addresses different layer; together they form complete evolutionary biology.

3. **Maturana–Mpodozis natural drift as evolutionary grammar** [pdf-p7 §2 + p27 conclusion] — verbatim conclusion: "Maturana and Mpodozis's theory of natural drift remains deeply relevant because it asks the right biological question: **what is conserved through reproduction?** Their answer — the ontogenic phenotype–ontogenic niche relation — provides a relational grammar for integrating recent developments in evolutionary biology." Foundational citation: Maturana-Mpodozis 2000 origin of species by means of natural drift.

4. **Eco-evo-devo bridge** [pdf-p11 §5 + p15 §7] — Eco-Evo-Devo (Gilbert et al. 2015) brings development + symbiosis + plasticity into evolutionary explanation. "Developmental plasticity and genetic accommodation" (Gilbert et al.) + "Niche construction and reciprocal causation" — central EES contributions.

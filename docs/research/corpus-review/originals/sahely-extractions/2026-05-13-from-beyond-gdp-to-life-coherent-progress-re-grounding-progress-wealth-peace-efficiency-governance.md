5. **Reframing 5 governance verbs in life-coherent form (Part XV conclusion)** [pdf-p104-107 Part XV §15.1-§15.5] — five-axis reframing:
   - **Progress** = expansion of life-capacity (§15.1)
   - **Wealth** = life capital, NOT accumulation (§15.2)
   - **Peace** = active reduction of avoidable life-harm (§15.3)
   - **Efficiency** = increasing provision of life goods with diminishing life-loss (§15.4: includes ecological efficiency + physical input-output efficiency + human development efficiency)
   - **Governance** = coordination of life-enabling conditions through legitimate coexistence (§15.5)
   This is the title's operational payload. Reframes 5 high-stakes policy-verbs that GDP-centric governance currently anchors elsewhere. Most-explicit Sahely policy-integration to date.

6. **"Performative transformation" as failure-mode of Beyond-GDP-adoption** [pdf-p102 Part XIV §14.4] — verbatim 9-line list of pathological transformations: "A society can speak of well-being while preserving austerity. / It can speak of sustainability while expanding extractive industries. / It can speak of resilience while abandoning vulnerable communities to climate risk. / It can speak of equity while leaving power untouched. / It can speak of participation while consulting without transferring influence. / It can speak of innovation while expanding surveillance and attention capture. / It can speak of peace while ignoring structural violence. / It can speak of capital while accumulating life-incoherent capital. / It can speak of efficiency while shifting costs onto families, workers, ecosystems, and future generations." **Performative transformation** is novel failure-mode-naming: institutions adopting reform-language while preserving extractive-relations. Compatible with Spore F6 actor-capture (institutional self-protection) generalized to **language-capture** at policy layer.

7. **Life-coherence ≠ utopia; life-coherence = practical-because-already-affecting** [pdf-p103 Part XIV §14.5] — verbatim: "This is not utopian in the weak sense. It is practical because every policy already affects life-capacity. Every budget already distributes exposure, repair, and margins. Every institution already enables or reduces access to life goods. Every technology already shapes attention, agency, and relation. Every economy already provisions some things and neglects others. The life-coherent framework simply makes these consequences explicit and answerable." Anti-utopian framing: life-coherence-as-explicit-naming-of-already-operating-consequences (NOT introduction of new aspirational concepts).

8. **Institutional-role-respect "return to proper place" framing** [pdf-p103 Part XIV §14.5 + §15.5] — verbatim 7-line list: "**The economy should provision life. / Technology should expand agency and care. / Law should protect dignity and commons. / Education should develop life-capacity. / Medicine should support healing and repair. / Governance should coordinate legitimate coexistence. / Measurement should reveal what life needs and whether it is being secured.**" Pattern: each institution returns to its **proper role as instrument of life** rather than master of life. Parallels Wave-12 #4 "great inversion" framing (institutions originally meant to serve life come to require life to serve them).

9. **Final principle: "Let us no longer ask life to justify itself before the economy"** [pdf-p108 Part XV closing] — verbatim closing: "**Let us no longer ask life to justify itself before the economy. Let the economy, technology, law, policy, medicine, education, and governance justify themselves before life.**" Most compact statement of the entire Sahely framework: relation-of-justification-inverted. The economic-evaluation-of-life relation must invert. **Closes the great inversion** from Wave-12 #4 — and is the operational payload of "Re-Grounding [5 verbs] in Life" (the paper subtitle).

10. **Final synthesis: 3-question hierarchy GDP / Beyond GDP / Life-Coherence** [pdf-p107 Part XV §15.6] — verbatim:

    > **Beyond GDP asks:** Are well-being, equity, sustainability, and resilience being measured?

    Three-layer measurement-purpose escalation: output → multidimensional-well-being → relation-organization-for-flourishing. GDP measures activity, Beyond GDP measures more dimensions, Life-coherent progress measures whether relations enable life. Hierarchical-not-replacement: Life-coherence integrates Beyond GDP integrates GDP (each layer expands the prior without abandoning it).

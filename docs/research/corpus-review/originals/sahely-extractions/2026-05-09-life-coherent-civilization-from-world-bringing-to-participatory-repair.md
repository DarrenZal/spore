## 7-fact moderate-selective extraction

1. **The "great inversion" as civilizational pathology** [pdf-p31 Executive §3] — verbatim: "The book names the dominant civilizational disorder as **the great inversion**. The great inversion occurs whenever systems originally justified as serving life come to require life to serve them. Economy no longer serves provisioning; provisioning becomes subordinate to growth, profit, and debt. Law no longer serves justice and repair; justice becomes subordinate to procedure, property, and institutional self-protection. Education no longer serves world-opening and human development; learning becomes subordinate to credentialing, competition, and labour-market sorting." Canonical naming of a structural-pathology category (precedent for Spore F6 failure-mode-class additions: institutional-purpose-inversion as a new failure-shape).

2. **Three foundational correctives: Maturana + McMurtry + Galtung triad** [pdf-p28 Abstract] — verbatim: "The argument is built around three foundational correctives. From Humberto Maturana and Francisco Varela, it takes the insight that cognition is not passive representation of an independent world, but embodied, relational, and enacted world-bringing by living systems structurally coupled to their media. From John McMurtry, it takes the life-value principle... From Johan Galtung, it takes the expanded account of violence as direct, structural, and cultural life-disablement, and peace as the active creation of conditions for human and ecological flourishing." Most explicit Sahely articulation of the triad-as-foundation. **Same triad as Wave-11 #7 governance-paper** (May 5) but now book-length integration.

3. **The "life-coherent vessel" framework** [pdf-p28 Abstract + p32 Executive §4] — verbatim: "The proposed alternative is the **life-coherent vessel**: a framework for reordering civilization around the conditions that make life possible. The vessel is not a utopian blueprint, ideological program, or technocratic master plan. It is a diagnostic and practical architecture for asking: What world is being brought forth? Whose life is enabled or disabled? What forms of violence are hidden in normal arrangements? What civil commons are being protected or destroyed? What feedback is being ignored? What forms of repair are now required?" 6-question diagnostic-not-prescriptive architecture; reframes "framework" from blueprint-shape to question-asking-shape.

4. **Three foundational claims (Executive Summary §2)** [pdf-p30] — verbatim 3-statement list: "(1) **Worlds are brought forth.** (2) **Worlds must answer to life.** (3) **Avoidable life-disablement must be named as violence.**" Compact philosophical position-statement. Statement (1) names Maturana-substrate; (2) names McMurtry-substrate; (3) names Galtung-substrate. Triad-collapse into three sentences.

5. **"World-bringing animal" as foundational anthropology** [pdf-p3 ToC §1.1-1.10 + Part I title "The Foundational Grammar"] — verbatim Chapter 1 title: "**The World-Bringing Animal**". 10 sub-sections covering: "1.1 The human being as an unfinished biological-cultural being / 1.2 Distinction as the beginning of a world / 1.3 Language and the coordination of worlds / 1.4 Institutions as stabilized distinctions / 1.5 Tools, technologies, and the extension of world-bringing / 1.6 The social construction of normality / 1.7 World-bringing and the body / 1.8 World-bringing and ecology / 1.9 The danger of world-blindness / 1.10 From world-bringing to responsibility". Foundational anthropology framing: human beings as participants-in-world-bringing (NOT observers of pre-given world). Maturana's enactive-cognition extended into civilizational diagnosis.

6. **"Civil-coherent civilization does not abolish conflict"** [pdf-p29 Keywords + Conclusion] — verbatim: "The book concludes that a life-coherent civilization is not one that abolishes conflict, uncertainty, plurality, suffering, or error. It is one that learns to organize its institutions around the continuous detection and repair of avoidable life-disablement." Anti-finalist conclusion: life-coherence = ongoing institutional-learning, not utopian endpoint. Honors recursive-repair-principle from Wave-11 #8 §12 fifth-safeguard.

7. **Book aim: usable framework, not theoretical addition** [pdf-p29 abstract closing] — verbatim: "**Its aim is not to add another theory to the existing archive of civilizational critique, but to offer a usable framework for seeing, judging, and acting differently: a way of bringing forth worlds that remain answerable to life.**" Method-precedent: explicit refusal of theoretical-addition-shape in favor of usable-framework-shape. Aligns with Spore canon-rebuild arc's distinction between abstract-frameworks vs operational-grammars.

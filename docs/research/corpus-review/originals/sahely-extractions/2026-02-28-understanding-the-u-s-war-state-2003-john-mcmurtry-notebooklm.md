### M-2 (McMurtry 2003, abstracted) — U.S. as "rogue state" via international-law violations
> "The U.S. is depicted as a 'rogue state' that systematically bypasses international legal frameworks while accusing others of doing the same." `[pdf-p4]`

### M-3 (McMurtry 2003, abstracted) — Specific legal violations
> "Specific Legal Violations: Nuremberg Charter (Principles 1, 2, and 6(a)): Planning and executing a war of aggression is defined as the 'supreme international crime.' Geneva Convention (Article 54): Violated by the destruction of civilian infrastructure (electricity, sewage, and water) during military assaults. U.N. Resolutions: While the U.S. cited Iraqi violations of 12–18 U.N. resolutions to justify war, it simultaneously ignored the 64 U.N. resolutions violated by its partner, Israel." `[pdf-p4]`

### M-4 (McMurtry 2003, abstracted) — Humanitarian consequences (load-bearing empirical claims)
> "Humanitarian Consequences: Child Mortality: Over 500,000 Iraqi children died as a result of the first Gulf War and subsequent sanctions. Technological Testing: Military operations like 'Operation Shock and Awe' are described as high-tech 'science experiments' tested on third-world populations." `[pdf-p4]`

### M-5 (McMurtry 2003, abstracted) — Ruling Group-Mind: 5 presuppositions (anchor #6 C-15 substrate)
> "The U.S. War State is sustained by a 'group-mind' that operates on five core presuppositions. These principles regulate perception at a preconscious level, making dissent appear as heresy. Presupposition 1: The U.S. national security state is synonymous with 'America.' Opposition to the security apparatus is thus framed as anti-Americanism. Presupposition 2: America is the ultimate source of freedom and goodness, effectively 'God's material embodiment on earth.' Presupposition 3: America is always and necessarily right in all conflicts. Facts that contradict this (e.g., the International Court conviction regarding Nicaragua) are ignored. Presupposition 4: Any force or nation that does not side with the U.S. is 'evil' by definition. Presupposition 5: 'Evil' must be attacked by any means, including pre-emptive force, even if no real threat has materialized." `[pdf-p5]` — this is THE substrate-claim that anchor #6 C-15 cites as "ruling group-mind" via "(McMurtry, 2003)"

### M-6 (McMurtry 2003, abstracted) — Media complicity ("Lapdog Press" + "Big Lie" of WMDs)
> "The Lapdog Press: Media outlets act as 'war entertainment,' using a 'drum-beat' for war to divert the masses from domestic failures, environmental issues, and corporate corruption. The 'Big Lie' of WMDs: The justification for the Iraq war—weapons of mass destruction—is characterized as a 'criminal big lie,' noting that the U.S. itself had previously sold such weapons to Saddam Hussein for profit." `[pdf-p5]`

### M-7 (McMurtry 2003, abstracted) — UN impotence + "ceremony of avoidance"
> "U.N. Impotence: The Security Council is described as being 'in thrall' to U.S. intimidation, ultimately permitting the 'lawless usurpation' of its own processes. … [a] 'ceremony of avoidance' among international bodies and the press that enables the War State's agenda." `[pdf-p5]`

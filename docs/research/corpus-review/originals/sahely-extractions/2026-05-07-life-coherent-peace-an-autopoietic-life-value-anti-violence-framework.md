## 7-fact moderate-selective extraction

1. **Two methodological tools: Life-Coherence Test + Life-Coherence Arbitration Protocol** [pdf-p4 intro] — verbatim: "The paper therefore proposes two methodological tools. **The Life-Coherence Test** asks whether a policy or institution expands or contracts life-capacities, secures or deprives the means of life, strengthens or weakens civil commons, reduces or reproduces violence, protects or degrades ecological life-support, arises from mutual legitimacy or domination, and subordinates money-sequences to life-sequences. **The Life-Coherence Arbitration Protocol** addresses the harder problem of competing life-needs, where different life-enabling claims come into conflict." Operational complement to anchor #7's 10-point diagnostic dashboard.

2. **8-step Arbitration Protocol** [pdf-p2 §9 TOC] — sub-steps 9.1-9.8: (1) Identify the living unities affected; (2) Map affected life-capacities; (3) Distinguish needs from wants and means from substitutes; (4) Identify thresholds of irreversibility and non-substitutability; (5) Seek compossible options before sacrificial trade-offs; (6) Use minimum sufficient force; (7) Require participatory languaging; (8) Monitor, repair, and revise. Operational procedure for ethical-political conflict-resolution under life-coherence framework.

3. **Conclusion: Life-Coherent Peace = "disciplined, dialogical, life-grounded practice of social learning under constraint"** [pdf-p4 + p25] — verbatim conclusion: "Life-Coherent Peace is neither technocracy nor moral absolutism. It is a disciplined, dialogical, life-grounded practice of social learning under constraint." Five safeguards: dialogical / participatory / recursive-repair / accountability / repair. **Sister to "social learning"** framing in Wave-7 + Wave-10; lineage continues.

4. **Five safeguards including recursive repair** [pdf-p25 §12] — verbatim: "The fifth safeguard is **recursive repair**. A policy does not become life-coherent because it is declared so at the moment of design. It becomes life-coherent only through ongoing monitoring, correction, accountability, and repair." Anti-finalist principle: peace is not a state but ongoing practice.

5. **From "non-war to life-coherence" reframe** [pdf-p4 §1 title] — verbatim section title: "**From Non-War to Life-Coherence**." Same positive-not-negative-definition discipline as #3 ("intrinsic health from disease absence to conserved viability") and anchor #7 ("peace not merely absence of violence but organized presence of life-enabling conditions"). Pattern-consistent across Wave-11.

6. **Worked Case Study: Water Privatization and Civil Commons Alternative** [pdf-p2 §11 + p21] — operational application of Life-Coherence Test to specific policy case. Methodologically demonstrates the test in use rather than only theoretical statement.

7. **Conclusion subtitle: "A Secular Covenant for Life on Earth"** [pdf-p25 §13 title] — verbatim: "Life-Coherent Peace is not a final state without conflict. Such a condition would be neither biologically plausible nor politically desirable. Living beings perturb one another. Communities disagree. Needs collide. Worlds are brought forth through different histories of language, memory, ecology, suffering, and aspiration. A peace framework that imagines the disappearance of conflict would confuse peace with..." Honest-rigor on conflict-irreducibility: peace ≠ conflict-absence, peace = ongoing life-coherent practice of conflict-navigation.

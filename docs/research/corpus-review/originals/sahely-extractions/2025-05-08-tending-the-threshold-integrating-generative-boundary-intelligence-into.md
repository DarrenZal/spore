## Verbatim key claims

### C-1 (p5, Abstract) — Central diagnosis (LOAD-BEARING)
> "Integral Theory has profoundly shaped the landscape of developmental frameworks with its elegant mapping of levels, quadrants, lines, states, and types. Yet it exhibits a critical blind spot: the underemphasis of **thresholds**, **transitional coherence**, and **generative boundaries** as vital to safe and meaningful development."

### C-2 (p5, Abstract) — Reframing thesis
> "This paper addresses that omission, proposing a reframing of Integral Theory that centers the **liminal**, the **symbolic**, the **somatic**, and the **semiotic** infrastructure of transformation."

### C-3 (p5, Abstract) — Boundary reconceptualization (LOAD-BEARING for Spore Membrane primitive)
> "Drawing on interdisciplinary insights from biosemiotics, teleodynamics, rites of passage, fascia science, mythopoetics, and regenerative systems theory, we articulate a threshold-enriched model that recognizes boundaries not as barriers but as **sacred, semi-permeable membranes of becoming**."

### C-4 (p6, Executive Summary) — Generative boundary intelligence
> "This paper introduces the concept of **generative boundary intelligence**, positioning thresholds not as mere gaps between stages but as **sacred coherence fields** where transformation is metabolized."

### C-5 (p6, Executive Summary) — Failure modes from un-tended thresholds
> "Using case studies, biological analogies (e.g., fascia, glycocalyx, myelin), and frameworks such as **teleodynamics**, **rites of passage**, and **life-value ethics**, the paper demonstrates how untended thresholds can lead to fragmentation, spiritual bypass, institutional failure, and civilizational incoherence."

### C-6 (p6, Executive Summary) — Developmental-logic revision (LOAD-BEARING — operational doctrine shift)
> "A revision of 'transcend and include' to 'tend, align, transcend, and integrate'"

### C-7 (p6, Executive Summary) — Quadrant reframing (LOAD-BEARING for Spore Holon-Membrane composition)
> "Reframing of AQAL quadrants as relational membranes"

### C-8 (p6, Executive Summary) — Threshold stewards
> "A call for **threshold stewards** to midwife regenerative coherence across domains"

### C-9 (p6, Executive Summary) — Paradigm-shift summary
> "In sum, this paper transforms Integral Theory from a map of altitude into a **living membrane of attuned becoming**. It proposes a model for practitioners, educators, policymakers, and healers to restore coherence where it breaks down most often: **at the edge of transformation**."

### C-10 (p7, Preface) — Membrane-as-not-barrier (cell-biology grounding, LOAD-BEARING for ADR-0062 R-Mem-1 membrane-as-self-produced scope-conditioning)
> "In biology, the cell membrane is not just a boundary — it is the interface of life itself. It selects, filters, signals, and coordinates. It is not a *barrier to overcome*, but a *field of coherence to maintain*. Likewise, in human development, there are emotional, symbolic, relational, and even somatic membranes — thresholds that shape the coherence of the next stage."

### C-11 (p7, Preface) — Crisis-diagnosis as failed transitions
> "In my own experience as a physician, systems thinker, and spiritual seeker, I've come to see that many of the crises we now face — individually and collectively — are not due to a lack of stages, states, or meta-maps. They are due to *ruptured transitions*, incoherent crossings, and misperceived thresholds. The rush to transcend has often meant a bypassing of the sacred edge."

### C-12 (p7, Preface) — Loving-enrichment stance toward Wilber (NOT rejection)
> "This paper is an invitation to re-member the wisdom of *tending the threshold*. It is not a rejection of Integral Theory, but a loving enrichment — a calling-in from the edge."

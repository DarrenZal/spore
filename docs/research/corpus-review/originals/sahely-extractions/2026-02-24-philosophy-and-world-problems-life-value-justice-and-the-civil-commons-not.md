## Verbatim load-bearing claims (curator-extracted; concepts attributed to McMurtry substrate)

### C-1 (Sahely-abstracting-McMurtry) — Civil Commons definition
> "The Civil Commons refers to the long-evolved social structures, life-protective norms, and organized human agency that allow life to be protected and developed. It is the social life-fabric—from public education and universal healthcare to common sewers and the rule of law—built through social cooperation to ensure universal access to the means of life." `[pdf-p8]` (concept attribution: McMurtry)

### C-2 (Sahely-abstracting-McMurtry) — Two-logic collision
> "At the heart of our global crisis is a collision between two irreconcilable logics. The dominant 'Money-Sequence System' (M → M') views money as the beginning and end of all value, seeking only to maximize private accumulation. In contrast, the 'Life-Sequence of Value' (L → M → L') uses means (including money) to serve the ultimate end: the protection and development of life itself." `[pdf-p8-9]` (concept attribution: McMurtry — M-M' / L-M-L' formalisms)

### C-3 (Sahely-abstracting-McMurtry) — Two-rights-systems war
> "There is an 'unseen war' between two rights systems: 1. Corporate Person Rights: Inhuman, money-focused, and increasingly unaccountable. 2. Living Person Rights: Grounded in human life development and biological requirements. The text argues that corporate rights currently override life-protective international norms and the 'Social State.'" `[pdf-p5]` (concept attribution: McMurtry)

### C-4 (Sahely-abstracting-McMurtry) — Life-Coherence Principle (epistemic version)
> "**Life-Coherence Principle**: Requirement: Any rational or scientific system must be consistent with the requirements of life support systems. Correction of 'Cooked Science': Scientific method must be released from 'private financial subjugation' and returned to the public purpose of understanding and protecting the life-ground. Epistemic Shift: Moving from 'analytical epistemology' (which abstracts away from life) to a 'life-responsible' scientific method." `[pdf-p5-6]` (concept attribution: McMurtry — life-coherence principle is McMurtry's term)

### C-5 (Sahely-abstracting-McMurtry) — Self-Maximizing Fallacy
> "Modern rationality is often defined as 'self-maximizing choice with no life base.' This logic leads to the 'Prisoner's Dilemma' and other models where cooperative, life-coherent choice space is erased a priori." `[pdf-p5]` (concept attribution: McMurtry)

### C-6 (Sahely-abstracting-McMurtry) — Language-as-paradigm-of-commons
> "Language is the ultimate paradigm of the Civil Commons. It is a form of wealth that defies the logic of scarcity and exclusionary ownership, serving as a model for how all social resources should be governed. … Non-Depletable Life-Capital: Unlike the Money-Sequence, where resources are consumed or extracted, language grows more powerful the more it is shared. It is a 'social life-host' that expands the internal thought-field of every participant." `[pdf-p10]` (concept attribution: McMurtry)

### C-7 (Sahely-abstracting-McMurtry) — Meta-choice framing
> "Humanity faces a choice between 'predatory' development and 'Civil Commons overcoming,' where the point of life is to live fully the capacities of one's being in a way that is life-coherent." `[pdf-p7]` (concept attribution: McMurtry)

### C-8 (Sahely-abstracting-McMurtry) — Closing thesis
> "The synthesis concludes that the current global crisis—characterized by planetary ecocide, increasing life insecurity, and system blindness—cannot be solved by the same logic that created it. The 'Life-Value Compass' and the 'Life-Coherence Principle' are presented not merely as philosophical ideals, but as global system imperatives necessary to ensure the continued existence of life support systems and the advancement of human civilization." `[pdf-p7]` (concept attribution: McMurtry's UNESCO essay conclusion)

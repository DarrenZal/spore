## Verbatim key claims (anchor-fidelity, 18 claims; emphasizes sheaf-substrate)

### C-1 (p15, Abstract) — Central derivation thesis (LOAD-BEARING)
> "This work develops a unified framework for understanding persistence and failure in complex systems by deriving, rather than assuming, the minimal structures required for relational coherence."

### C-2 (p15, Abstract) — Triadic closure → Fano plane uniqueness (LOAD-BEARING for Spore primitive-substrate comparison)
> "Beginning from the requirement that viable systems must resolve interactions beyond pairwise relations, it is shown that triadic closure is the minimal unit of consistency. The unique finite structure satisfying this requirement is the Fano plane, which organizes seven irreducible relational roles into a closed configuration."

### C-3 (p15, Abstract) — Lifting chain: Fano → octonions → Jordan J3(O) → Freudenthal → E7 (LOAD-BEARING — full mathematical derivation chain)
> "When these relations are required to support directed interaction, the structure lifts necessarily to the octonion algebra, introducing non-associativity as a measure of contextual inconsistency. The need to represent structured states leads to the exceptional Jordan algebra J3(O), whose cubic norm captures minimal global consistency. Further lifting to the Freudenthal triple system introduces symplectic duality and yields a quartic invariant preserved by the exceptional group E7, providing the first candidate for a global coherence measure across relational transformations."

### C-4 (p15, Abstract) — **Sheaf-substrate explicit declaration (LOAD-BEARING; sheaf-theory cluster anchor)**
> "To account for the distinction between observable variables and underlying structure, the framework incorporates fiber bundle theory, where measured states are projections of higher-dimensional relational configurations. Sheaf theory and cohomology formalize the transition from local consistency to global coherence, with failure arising as obstruction to the existence of a global section. This yields a structural interpretation of early warning signals as the accumulation of unresolved inconsistencies prior to observable collapse."

### C-5 (p15, Abstract) — Domain mapping (medicine, ecology, economics, governance)
> "The resulting framework is shown to apply across domains. In physics, it aligns with relational interpretations of quantum mechanics and entanglement. In medicine, disease is reinterpreted as loss of relational coherence preceding measurable dysfunction. In ecology, collapse emerges from breakdown of interaction networks before changes in indicators. In economics, crises reflect incoherence between financial and real systems. In governance, policy failure arises from optimizing projections rather than preserving structural integrity."

### C-6 (p15, Abstract) — Central claim: viability = coherence of relations, not properties of components (LOAD-BEARING — Spore primitive-substrate framing match)
> "The central result is that viability is not a property of components but of the coherence of their relations, and that this coherence is governed by invariant structures arising from minimal mathematical constraints. Action within such systems must therefore shift from control of variables to preservation of relational coherence under constraint."

### C-7 (p16, Keywords) — Verbatim keyword list (sheaf-substrate cluster present)
> "relational systems, coherence, Fano plane, octonions, non-associativity, Jordan algebra, Freudenthal triple system, E7 invariant, fiber bundles, sheaf theory, cohomology, complex adaptive systems, systems biology, ecological resilience, economic stability, governance, viability, invariants, triality"

### C-8 (p18, Exec Summary §5) — Failure mechanism three-stage chain (LOAD-BEARING for Spore failure-modes F6 comparison)
> "Failure occurs in three stages:
> 1. Local inconsistency (captured by non-associativity)
> 2. Cross-domain incompatibility (failure of relational 'gluing')
> 3. Global obstruction (loss of coherent structure)
>
> Collapse is the final projection of accumulated hidden incoherence."

### C-9 (p18, Exec Summary §6) — Early warning = cohomological obstruction (LOAD-BEARING)
> "Early warning signals are not primarily changes in variables but: loss of adaptive capacity, increasing variability, divergence between subsystems, delayed recovery from disturbance. Formally, these correspond to rising cohomological obstruction."

### C-10 (p38, Ch 3.2) — **Fano plane formal definition (7 points / 7 lines)**
> "The Fano plane is the smallest possible projective plane. It consists of:
> - 7 points
> - 7 lines
> - Each line contains exactly 3 points
> - Each point lies on exactly 3 lines
>
> Most importantly: Every pair of points is contained in exactly one line.
>
> Translated into our language:
> - Points → relational roles
> - Lines → triadic closure relations
>
> This is precisely the structure we required. No redundancy. No missing relations. Complete closure."

### C-11 (p39-40, Ch 3.4) — **The seven roles (C/M/X/D/P/R/O) — Spore-grammatically-comparable cover-page heptad**
> "Each of the seven roles:
> - Constraint (C)
> - Margin (M)
> - State (X)
> - Disturbance (D)
> - Perception (P)
> - Regulation (R)
> - Options (O)
>
> maps onto one point of the Fano plane.
>
> Each line (triad) corresponds to a functional channel. For example:
> - (C, M, X) → Geometry of viability
> - (X, D, R) → Response channel
> - (X, P, O) → Possibility mapping
> - (M, O, R) → Adaptive capacity
> - (C, D, O) → Pressure vs possibility
> - (C, P, R) → Governance
> - (M, D, P) → Early warning
>
> Each role participates in exactly three triads. Each triad intersects others in a structured way. Nothing is arbitrary. Everything is constrained."

### C-12 (p43, Ch 3.8) — Fano structure carries deeper algebraic content (octonions / triality)
> "At first glance, the Fano plane appears simple. Seven points. Seven lines. Minimal structure. But this simplicity is deceptive. The same structure: Encodes the multiplication rules of octonions. Underlies non-associative algebra. Supports triality in 8 dimensions. Forms the foundation of exceptional mathematical objects."

### C-13 (p53, Ch 5.2) — Octonions = R/C/H/O hierarchy; Fano encodes octonion multiplication
> "Octonions are an extension of number systems: Real numbers (R), Complex numbers (C), Quaternions (H), **Octonions (O)**. They consist of: One real unit. Seven imaginary units. Each imaginary unit corresponds to a point in the Fano plane. Multiplication is defined by the oriented triads: If (A → B → C) follows the arrow, then A · B = C. Reversing the order introduces a sign change. This structure is: Non-commutative (order matters). Non-associative (grouping matters). And crucially: It is the largest normed division algebra that exists. There is nothing beyond it of the same kind."

### C-14 (p57, Ch 5.7) — Triality maps onto viability framework (LOAD-BEARING for Spore Holon irreducibility comparison)
> "Each triad can be viewed in three ways: As state-centered (X-focused). As process-centered (R or P focused). As constraint-centered (C or M focused). These are not different systems. They are different projections of the same structure. Triality allows us to rotate between them. Thus: The system is not fixed. It is perspectival."

### C-15 (p68, Ch 7.1) — **Sheaf substrate motivation: local-to-global problem (LOAD-BEARING)**
> "How do we determine whether a system is globally coherent when we only have local information? In every real system: We observe parts, not wholes. We measure locally, not globally. We intervene in fragments, not totalities. Thus, coherence must be inferred from local consistency. But local consistency does not guarantee global coherence. To formalize this distinction, we introduce the concept of a sheaf."

### C-16 (p68-69, Ch 7.2-7.3) — **Sheaf operational machinery: local section / overlap / gluing / global section (LOAD-BEARING — Spore sheaf-bridge target)**
> "A sheaf provides a disciplined way of tracking: What exists locally. How local pieces relate on overlaps. Whether they can be combined into a global whole. In simple terms: A **local section** is what holds in a region. An **overlap** is where regions meet. **Gluing** is the process of combining local sections. A **global section** is a fully coherent system. A sheaf answers one question: Do locally consistent pieces form a globally coherent structure?"

### C-17 (p70, Ch 7.4) — **Sheaf reinterpretation of Fano triads (LOAD-BEARING — direct mapping triad↔local-section)**
> "We can now reinterpret our structure in sheaf terms:
> - Each **triad (Fano line)** → a local section
> - Each **shared element between triads** → an overlap
> - The entire system → a candidate global section
>
> For coherence: Triads must agree on shared elements. Their relations must be compatible. The system must 'glue' consistently. If this holds, the system is viable. If not, something deeper is wrong."

### C-18 (p71-74, Ch 7.5-7.10) — **Cohomology = measure of obstruction; failure as non-trivial cohomology (LOAD-BEARING)**
> "When local sections cannot be combined into a global whole, we encounter: An **obstruction**. This is not a visible failure. It is a structural incompatibility. Each part may appear correct. But their combination is impossible. This is the key insight: Failure begins as an inability to glue.
>
> To quantify this, mathematics introduces: **Cohomology**. Cohomology measures: The degree to which local consistency fails to produce global coherence. The presence of structural 'holes'. The impossibility of extending local solutions. In our framework: A **zero cohomology class** → full coherence. A **non-zero class** → obstruction present. Thus: Cohomology is a measure of hidden structural failure. [...] **Viability is the ability of a system to maintain a globally coherent section across all local relational domains.** And: **Failure is the emergence of non-trivial cohomology.** This is not metaphorical. It is structural."

## Mathematical framework hierarchy (Ch 8 unification, p81)

The author explicitly chains the framework's levels of structural depth (Fig. 15, p79; quadratic → cubic → quartic):

- **Level 1 — Quadratic (Pair)**: pairwise interactions, insufficient for closure
- **Level 2 — Cubic (Triangle)**: triadic closure via Fano plane (7 roles); cubic norm of Jordan algebra J3(O) captures minimal global consistency
- **Level 3 — Quartic (Network)**: Freudenthal triple system; quartic invariant preserved by E7 exceptional group — first candidate for *global coherence measure* across relational transformations

Final unification (§8.9, p81) — chains all 6 mathematical-substrate layers explicitly:
> "Fano plane → defines triadic closure. Octonions → encode relational multiplication. Associator → detects local inconsistency. Sheaves → track local-to-global coherence. Cohomology → measures obstruction. Quartic invariant → measures total coherence. Each layer builds on the previous. The invariant sits at the top: It integrates all structural information into a single quantity."

The chapter closes (§8.12, p82) with explicit E7 → E8 transition framing:
> "The quartic invariant is associated with E7. But mathematics tells us: There is one structure beyond this. Larger. More symmetric. More constrained. **E8**. The question is not what E8 is. The question is: What does it mean for the limits of coherence, structure, and knowledge?"

This positions the paper as part of a deeper E8-lattice-implicated research arc (consistent with the cover-page E6/E7/E8/F4/G2 labeling).

## Verbatim key claims

All quotes are the ORIGINAL authors' words (Maturana & Dávila), reproduced verbatim by Sahely in the repost. Anchored by nearest section heading.

- **C1** [html-section:The responsibility for education] *"We live in a culture centered on relations of domination and submission."*

- **C2** [html-section:The responsibility for education] *"Control is the relational dynamic from which oneself and others arise denied capacities and talents, narrowing viewpoints, intelligence, and creativity, generating dependence, from which autonomy and self-respect are not possible."*

- **C3** [html-section:The responsibility for education] *"educating is a transformational process in how all involved actors interrelate and, if we wish our children to grow up as autonomous beings respecting themselves and with a social conscience, we must live with them respecting them and respecting ourselves in the continuous creation of a relationship based on mutual trust and respect"*

- **C4** [html-section:WHAT DO WE DO] *"To educate is to do in an artificial manner something that should occur spontaneously within the family and community."*

- **C5** [html-section:WHAT DO WE DO] *"The student does not learn a subject, but rather learns a way of living with others; a way of becoming a human being."*

- **C6** [html-section:WHAT DO WE DO] *"Genetics is the initial condition, a point of departure. From this point of departure it is possible to grow in one way or another, depending on the environment."*

- **C7** [html-section:WHAT DO WE DO] *"Children are not the future of the human community. It is we adults who are. We are the future of our children because how they will be depends upon how they live with us. The future lies in our present."*

- **C8** [html-section:DEMOCRACY] *"Collaboration exists only when relations are based on legitimacy of being, which implies respect for oneself and respect for others."*

- **C9** [html-section:DEMOCRACY] *"Love is the emotion that constitutes and makes possible living together democratically."*

- **C10** [html-section:DO WE EDUCATE FOR SELF-CONTROL] *"Well-being is the sensation of being in coherence with one's circumstances."*

- **C11** [html-section:DO WE EDUCATE FOR SELF-CONTROL] *"We are agents of change of the world in which we live, and we will generate a world of well-being or of unease according to whether we generate venues of autonomy or dependence in our students."*

- **C12** [html-section:WHERE DO WE EDUCATE] *"The biosphere is finished; now we live in the homosphere. We are destroying our environment and transforming it so that living in it will be impossible."*

- **C13** [html-section:VIRTUAL EMOTIONS] *"Emotions are not virtual because they correspond to what occurs internally in the organism as a relational basis."*

- **C14** [html-section:VIRTUAL EMOTIONS] *"Emotions are not changeable by reason. Only emotion changes emotion."*

- **C15** [html-section:GLOBALIZATION] *"Listening consists of hearing and attending to what we hear and in which what the other says is valid."*

- **C16** [html-section:EPISTEMOLOGY] *"The enemy of reflection is the knowledge that we know. if I know, I don't look because I already know."*

## 9-fact anchor-hand-curated extraction

1. **Dashboard NOT score: anti-reductionism as foundational principle** [pdf-p5 Executive Summary] — verbatim: "**A score compresses. A dashboard discloses. A score ranks. A dashboard reveals patterns, thresholds, distributional burdens, narratives, and repair obligations. A score invites technocratic control. A dashboard, if properly designed, can support participatory judgment.**" Operational anti-reductionism: refuses single-score Life-Coherence Index in favor of multidimensional disclosure. First-principles distinction between technocratic-evaluation-shape and participatory-disclosure-shape.

2. **10-Domain Life-Coherence Dashboard architecture** [pdf-p1 cover + p5 Executive Summary + pdf-p13-19 §5] — Ten domains arranged radially around "LIFE-COHERENT PEACE: Enable Life-Capacities Without Destroying the Life-Ground of Others" central node (verbatim from cover infographic):
   1. Basic life necessities
   2. Health and embodied viability
   3. Thought, education, and sense-making
   4. Felt being and psychosocial security
   5. Action, agency, and participation
   6. Relational legitimacy and non-domination
   7. Civil commons and universal access
   8. Ecological life-ground
   9. Cultural-linguistic worlds
   10. Value sequence and institutional accountability
   The Galtung-derived (typed-violence) and McMurtry-derived (civil-commons, value-sequence) and Maturana-derived (cultural-linguistic-worlds, relational-legitimacy) layered together.

3. **5 Indicator Types (per-domain)** [pdf-p2 TOC §6 + pdf-p21-22] — (1) **Signal indicators** (quantitative trend); (2) **Threshold indicators** (red-line non-substitutable boundaries); (3) **Distribution indicators** (who bears burdens); (4) **Narrative indicators** (qualitative testimony + lived-experience disclosure); (5) **Repair indicators** (recursive-repair-cycle tracking). Each of 10 domains specifies all 5 types. Operational anti-reductionism: numbers + thresholds + distribution + narrative + repair-tracking together (NOT just numbers).

4. **7 Measurement Principles** [pdf-p2 TOC §4.1-4.7] — (1) Multidimensionality; (2) Life-necessity primacy; (3) Threshold sensitivity; (4) Distributional visibility; (5) Participatory interpretation; (6) Ecological grounding; (7) Recursive repair. Each principle constrains indicator-selection per domain. **Principle 7 "recursive repair"** repeats Wave-11 #8 §12 fifth-safeguard ("recursive repair").

5. **6-step Dashboard Interpretation Cycle** [pdf-p2 TOC §7] — (1) Observe; (2) Interpret with affected communities; (3) Identify thresholds and red lines; (4) Decide and act; (5) Repair; (6) Revise. Embeds participatory-interpretation (step 2) directly into the measurement workflow (NOT a separate stage after measurement). Cycle is recursive: each Revise step feeds back to a new Observe step.

6. **Tiered Dashboard usage (Minimum / Expanded / Full)** [pdf-p2 TOC §11] — three implementation tiers enable graduated adoption: **Minimum** dashboard for resource-poor settings (subset of domains + signal-indicators only); **Expanded** for partial institutional capacity; **Full Diagnostic** for high-capacity governance bodies. Parsimony-via-tiered-implementation (matches Spore ADR-0072 B2 parsimony-first integration shape).

7. **7 Risks-and-Safeguards Against Metric Capture** [pdf-p2-3 TOC §10.1-10.7] — (1) Gaming; (2) False commensurability; (3) Data colonialism; (4) Technocratic capture; (5) Depoliticization; (6) Administrative burden; (7) Institutional self-protection. Each risk paired with safeguard. Honest acknowledgment that indicators-as-instrument-of-life can themselves capture and reduce. **§10.3 data colonialism** is a Sahely contribution to dashboard-design discipline.

8. **NotebookLM debate transcript credited inside text** [pdf-p5 Executive Summary] — verbatim: "**The NotebookLM debate transcript** identified the central measurement danger: under administrative pressure, institutions may default to what is easiest to count—hospital beds, gallons of water, budget lines—while ignoring relational legitimacy, felt being, cultural continuity, and ecological thresholds." First Sahely paper to acknowledge NotebookLM's role at SUBSTANTIVE-CONTRIBUTION layer (not just acknowledgement-section "thanks for the tool"). NotebookLM's debate transcript identified the central failure-mode the paper is designed to prevent. Pattern: tool-as-substantive-collaborator continues from Wave-11.

9. **Conclusion: indicators-as-instruments-of-life-disclosure** [pdf-p3 TOC §12] — closing framing: "Indicators as Instruments of Life-Disclosure". Pattern-consistent with Wave-11 #8's "From Non-War to Life-Coherence" positive-not-negative-definition discipline applied at measurement-layer: indicators are not for technocratic-ranking but for collective-disclosure of what is being enabled, disabled, or repaired.

## 6-fact moderate-selective extraction

1. **Misrelevance as wisdom-failure** [pdf-p2 Acknowledgements + Part I + cited by Post 9 §11.4] — verbatim (per Post 9 citation): "misrelevance [is] the condition in which attention, emotion, measurement, incentives, identities, technologies, institutions, or sacred narratives organize life around signals that do not protect, repair, or expand life-capacity. Its central conclusion is that wisdom is not knowing more, but becoming less capturable by what does not serve life." Wisdom-as-uncapture-discipline rather than wisdom-as-additional-knowledge. Method-precedent for redefining philosophical-concept (wisdom) via life-coherent criterion (capturability).

2. **Peil Kauffman: Emotion as Viability Sensing** [§4 p32+] — first dedicated chapter on Katherine Peil Kauffman's substrate (introduced in #4 Acknowledgements; foregrounded here). Emotion treated NOT as cognitive-noise-to-be-managed but as **embodied viability-sensing** — pre-rational signal about whether the relational/biological/situational coupling is supporting or harming life. Reframes emotion-regulation literature: the goal is not to suppress emotion but to **recover its life-coherent signal-fidelity**. NEW substrate-attribution given full doctrinal status.

3. **Living Triad of Wisdom: Relevance + Emotion + Relation + Repair** [Part II] — 4-term operational architecture (despite "triad" framing): relevance-discrimination (knowing what matters) + emotion-as-viability-sensing (Kauffman) + relational-attunement (Maturana) + repair-capacity (McMurtry-derived). Each is a distinct wisdom-dimension; together they constitute integrated life-coherence wisdom. Operational-architecture analog of Wave-12 anchor #6 §8.1 11-verb stack.

4. **Salience capture as failure-mode** [§12] — attention/emotion/measurement systems can be captured by signals optimized-for-engagement rather than life-relevance. Algorithmic-engagement-capture is the canonical example. Failure-mode-naming inherits ADR-0075 F6 failure-modes-foundation discipline.

5. **Where Each Thinker Corrects the Others** [§7] — explicit cross-substrate-discipline: each foundational thinker (Maturana / McMurtry / Galtung / Kauffman) is shown both as contributor AND as having limits that the other thinkers correct. Method-precedent: cite-with-corrective-discipline rather than synthesis-as-summation. Generalizes ADR-0048 honest-rigor cluster-counting to multi-thinker substrate-integration.

6. **6 application-domains, single pattern** [Part VI §23-29] — Clinical wisdom + Educational wisdom + Public Health wisdom + Governance wisdom + AI/Digital wisdom + Peacebuilding/Spiritual-Political wisdom — each applied as **same Life-Coherence Wisdom Compass** to different domains. §29 names the shared pattern: each domain currently operates with misrelevance-capture; each requires the same 3-stage cycle (salience→insight→repair) applied to its specific signals + measurements + emotional-textures.

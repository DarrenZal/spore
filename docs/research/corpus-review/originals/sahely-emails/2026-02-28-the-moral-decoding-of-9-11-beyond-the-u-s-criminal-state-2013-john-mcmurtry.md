Executive Summary

Introduction and Core Thesis

"The Moral Decoding of 9-11: Beyond the U.S. Criminal State" provides a deep-structural moral and geopolitical analysis of the September 11 attacks. The author, moral philosopher John McMurtry, argues that 9-11 cannot be understood through the "official conspiracy theory" of an external terrorist attack, but must be decoded as a deliberate operation by the covert U.S. state. The event was designed to serve as a "catastrophic and catalyzing event" — a new Pearl Harbor — required to bypass democratic constraints and initiate a permanent war for global resources.

The Supreme Moral Doctrine

The paper identifies the driving force behind 9-11 as a "supreme moral doctrine" of limitless private capital accumulation, championed by global elites and intellectuals. This ideology seeks to replace the self-determination of nations with the "supranational sovereignty of an intellectual elite and bankers," enabling transnational corporations and Wall Street to override all human and environmental life requirements for maximum profit. The U.S. military and covert state act as the enforcement arm for this money-sequence system, utilizing pretexts to launch wars of dispossession against societies (like Iraq) that maintain independent, state-owned social infrastructures.

Strategic Execution and the Cover-Up

McMurtry highlights the vast disconnect between the physical evidence of 9-11 and the official narrative. He points to the physical impossibility of fireproofed steel buildings collapsing at near free-fall acceleration due to jet fuel, indicating instead that a highly sophisticated controlled demolition took place. He exposes a systematic cover-up, noting that crime-scene evidence was rapidly confiscated and destroyed. Furthermore, he critiques the official investigations:

* The 9-11 Commission: Directed by Philip Zelikow, a national security strategist who had ironically theorized the exact scenario of a "catastrophic terrorism" event years prior to justify expansive state powers. The Commission structurally presupposed the official story and erased conflicting facts.
* NIST (National Institute of Standards and Technology): Accused of producing "cooked corporate science" by deliberately restricting its computer models to the period of "collapse initiation," purposefully ignoring the physical data of the explosions and free-fall that contradict the fire-collapse theory.

Cui Bono? (Who Benefits?)

Applying the primary forensic question of criminal investigation — cui bono? — the author notes that not a single element of the transnational money-sequence system lost power; rather, they gained spectacularly. The 9-11 event reversed the crisis of legitimacy facing the Bush Jr. administration, provided the pretext for seizing control of oil-rich regions in the Middle East and Central Asia, enriched the military-industrial complex, and allowed the implementation of police-state legislation (like the Patriot Act) to crush global anti-corporate resistance.

Conclusion and Call to Action

The paper concludes that the U.S. has morphed into a criminal state that operates above national and international law, committing supreme crimes of war and crimes against humanity with total impunity. To dismantle this life-devouring system, citizens must pierce the "ruling group-mind" perpetuated by the media, recognize the institutional agency behind 9-11, and demand independent legal accountability under the established frameworks of international criminal law.

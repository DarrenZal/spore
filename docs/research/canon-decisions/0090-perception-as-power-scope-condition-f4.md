# ADR-0090 — Perception-as-Power scope-condition substrate-strengthening to F4 representation-authority (Sahely-Perception + Galtung structural/cultural-violence + Standpoint-epistemology tradition-anchors)

## Status

active (authored + activated 2026-05-29 under `~/.claude/plans/adr-0090-perception-as-power-scope-condition-f4.md`; atomic-bundle 2-commit ceremony per Bundle α discipline; FINAL Bundle α ADR — closes Bundle α at 6/6)

## Context

`F4 representation-authority` (`docs/foundations/representation-authority.md`; admitted via ADR-0074 `2026-04-25` Phase 4 Tier A SECOND admission) carries the inter-layer representation-precedence doctrine across the full five-layer canon representation surface (text / graph / sensor / attestation / agent-summary), extending ADR-0041 text-authoritative-representation from the 2-layer text-vs-graph case. Its load-bearing principled-rule is the fact-vs-specification text-type distinction; its §5 machinery is default + context-overrides + appeal-protocol + held-epistemic-tension. F4 already names the power-laden dimension of representation-authority in several places: §4.3 sensor-selection (who has standing to declare a sensor canonical; "which signals count"), §4.4 attester-eligibility (who is eligible to attest in which role), §4.5 the AI-summary asymmetry-acknowledgment (model-providers outside instance governance reach), §5.2 the pluriversal-context-override (held-tension per ADR-0001), and §5.4 unresolved-disagreement-as-held-epistemic-tension. But F4 does NOT currently ground this power-laden dimension in a cross-tradition substrate-evidence cluster.

The Sahely corpus Phase 2 (closure 2026-05-22) surfaced a substantive cross-tradition cluster that F4 does NOT currently cite at substrate-evidence layer: **the assignment of representation-authority is itself power-laden** — Sahely *Architecture of Viability* §2.5 Perception (W2.3 C19 at `spore.connection.sahely-architecture-of-viability`): *"Perception therefore depends not only on sensors, but on trust, categories, language, and power. Who is allowed to signal? Which signals count? Which forms of evidence are recognized? ... A civilization can be rich in data and poor in perception."* + Galtung structural-violence + cultural-violence (W3.1 + W4.2) + Standpoint-epistemology (Harding / Haraway / Hartsock), substrate-strengthened at the peace-civilization + War-State diagnostic scope by W3.1 + W4.2 per DECISION-BRIEF §17.4.

The Sahely DECISION-BRIEF §12.5 evaluated this surfaced substrate against three possible Spore-canon dispositions:

- **(i) NEW slug admission** (`perception-as-power` / `voice-asymmetry`): rejected — F4's `epistemic-gap` (ADR-0076) + `external-witness` (ADR-0081 F8) slugs already cover the perception-authority territory; a new slug would duplicate. Rejected DESPITE the cluster-count (3 FULL) being sufficient for a derived-glossary slug, per parsimony-as-earning-test-outcome.
- **(ii) Multi-foundation scope-condition** (F1 sensor-oracle-governance + F3 actor-governance §4.5/§6 in addition to F4): rejected as cleanest — DECISION-BRIEF §12.5 named the multi-foundation reach but recommended F4-single-foundation-touch as cleanest (perception-as-power's representation-authority concern is most squarely F4 territory; F1/F3 already canonicalize the intra-modality + actor-governance dimensions and need no edit).
- **(iii) Scope-condition substrate-strengthening to existing F4**: ratified per DECISION-BRIEF §12.5 honest-rigor cluster-counting + §17.4 W3.1 + W4.2 substrate-strengthening + §17.8 operator final disposition.

Operator selected (iii). This ADR executes the disposition.

**Why scope-condition is the cleanest shape**: the Sahely-Perception + Galtung + Standpoint-epistemology cluster is a substrate-evidence enrichment of F4's existing perception-authority doctrine, not a category-shift and not new vocabulary. F4's doctrine territory (five-layer inter-layer precedence; fact-vs-specification text-type distinction; default/context-override/appeal-protocol/held-tension machinery) remains unchanged. What changes is the substrate-evidence base: Sahely's "Perception depends not only on sensors, but on trust, categories, language, and power... Who is allowed to signal? Which signals count?" + Galtung's structural-violence (categorization-becomes-life-destruction embedded in institutional arrangements) and cultural-violence (the symbolic-layer legitimizing structural-violence) + Standpoint-epistemology (whose situated standpoint counts as authoritative) provide a cross-tradition grounding for the doctrinal commitment that representation-authority *assignment* — who selects sensors/attesters/summary-processes, who declares precedence, who has standing in appeal — is power-laden and must be canon-legible. Both F4's existing precedence machinery and the perception-as-power substrate articulate the same operational concern from different tradition-angles per ADR-0064 honest-rigor.

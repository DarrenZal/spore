## Decision

ADMIT `civil-commons` as derived-glossary slug in `docs/research/concepts-p2p-wiki.yaml` (v23 → v24).

**Slug articulation**: `civil-commons` is McMurtry-coined term for socially-constructed systems that enable universal access to life-goods and constitute primary economic infrastructure — the base layer upon which all market activity depends (per *Cancer Stage of Capitalism* 2013 + civil-commons-specific literature). Specific instances include public water systems, sanitation and waste management, public health infrastructure, education systems, legal and institutional frameworks securing rights and trust, and environmental protections preserving shared life conditions. Orthodox economics treats these as "public expenditures" or "externalities"; McMurtry's framing inverts this — markets and private enterprise *presuppose* the civil commons; the erosion of civil commons is operationally identical to contraction of life capital, which violates the life-coherence constraint.

The slug operates as a **derived-glossary canon-method anchor** — NOT a primitive (fails earning-test (a): civil-commons is a substrate-noun, not a coordination operation), NOT a doctrine (the doctrine is `life-value-doctrine` per ADR-0086; civil-commons is one operational-substrate the doctrine operates over), NOT a mode (modes are property-shapes-across-primitives per ADR-0048; civil-commons is not a mode-shape), NOT a property (properties are sub-specifications of structural primitives per ADR-0051; civil-commons is not a sub-specification), NOT a pattern (patterns are composition / design-criteria / catalog under ADR-0065 M4 sub-class framework; civil-commons is not pattern-shaped). Canon-object-class inventory PRESERVED at 4 categories (primitives 9 / doctrines 4 / modes 2 / properties 2) + 7 patterns.

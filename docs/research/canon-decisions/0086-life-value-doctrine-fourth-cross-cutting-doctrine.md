## Context

Phase 2 closure of the Sahely corpus intake (DECISION-BRIEF §17.5 Bundle α architecture; §17.8(1)+(4) operator final dispositions) ratified admission of `life-value-doctrine` as the fourth member of Spore's cross-cutting-doctrine canon-object-class. The doctrine sits sibling to `reproductive-commoning` (ADR-0002), `boundary-commoning` (ADR-0003), and `care-commoning` (ADR-0045) — each anchoring a different tradition-cluster and addressing a distinct evaluative concern over Spore's nine-primitive coordination grammar.

The substrate-pressure comes from McMurtry's life-value onto-axiology (LVOA) tradition operationalized across six Sahely papers (the foundational-triad W4.2 manifesto CAPSTONE + W2.3 architecture-of-viability + W3.1 toward-life-coherent-peace, plus extensions W4.1 money / W2.6 ethics / W4.3 medicine) and composed with Ruddick's Civil Commons protocol substrate via the 3-layer bridge note (McMurtry → Ruddick → BKC). The core LVOA claim — *value = enablement of the range of being / doing / feeling / thinking* — provides an evaluative substrate over coordination outcomes that Spore's nine-primitive grammar could not previously name canonically without a doctrine-layer admission.

Sequencing per Layer 3 handoff §6: ADR-X1 (ADR-0085 trap-shape vocab + recursive-audit method, ACTIVE at HEAD `b44ac28`) landed first, establishing the C1 R-Trap-cluster substrate this admission extends from. ADR-0085 line 59 explicitly forward-refs the present ADR: *"life-value-doctrine extends from C1 substrate."* The substitution-trap territory (golden-calf-trap shape; recursive-audit-method discipline) underwrites life-value evaluation by giving Spore canonical machinery for naming when corrective distinctions become captured into money-value substitutions for life-value.

Per-axis disposition was RATIFIED via handoff §3 + DECISION-BRIEF §17.5 + §17.8(4); this ADR does not re-design but executes a 5-file atomic-bundle + 1 narrative-ext (extend-via-prose) to ADR-0042 §Consequences. Wave-N+1 alignment (ic + pm doctrine-references) is EXPLICITLY DEFERRED to operator-elective separate-session work per `feedback_workstream_scope_discipline.md`; narrow Spore-only scope.

## Decision

ADMIT `life-value-doctrine` as the **fourth cross-cutting doctrine** in Spore's canon-object-class inventory.

**Doctrine articulation**: life-value-doctrine is a lens applied across primitives that evaluates whether coordination outcomes serve **life-value** (range of being / doing / feeling / thinking enabled, per McMurtry LVOA) versus **money-value** (cumulative monetary indicators decoupled from life-coherence). Like its sibling doctrines, it applies as a lens over the existing nine-primitive grammar — `commitment` reads differently under each doctrine, `evidence` reads differently under each, `field` reads differently under each. The doctrine does NOT admit a new primitive; it does NOT decompose into property-of-Commitment + lens-on-Evidence + Field-condition; the nine-primitive parsimony established by Phase 3b is preserved.

**Four doctrine-application-domains**, each anchored to a specific Sahely substrate paper:

- **Ethics** — W2.6 ethics-as-science-of-viability: ethics evaluated as viability-of-life-conditions; aligns Sen-Nussbaum capabilities-of-flourishing.
- **Economics** — W4.1 money-growth-to-life-coherence: money-value vs life-value distinction; six reconstruction pillars; Civil Commons as core economic infrastructure; Polanyi-substantive-economy substrate.
- **Clinical-policy** — W4.3 medicine-of-living-coherence: Seven Policy Domains operationalization; clinical-encounter as life-value-enabling-practice; Sen capabilities-clinical-substrate.
- **Peace** — W3.1 toward-life-coherent-peace: peace-as-life-coherence-at-civilization-scale; conflict-as-life-value-decoupling; structural-violence-as-money-value-substitution.

**Cross-tradition cluster-counting math (honest per gate (a))**, per DECISION-BRIEF §6.2:

- **C1 McMurtry (Life-Value Onto-Axiology)** — **FULL**. Multi-book multi-decade corpus (*Unequal Freedoms* 1998; *Value Wars* 2002; *Philosophy and World Problems* UNESCO 2009; *The Cancer Stage of Capitalism* 2013) operationalized across six Sahely papers.
- **C2 Polanyi (Substantive Economy)** — **FULL**. *The Great Transformation* 1944; fictitious-commodities critique; embeddedness thesis. Cross-paper substrate density verified at Step 0.5 audit (12 cross-paper hits across 5 of 7 Sahely notes; primary density W2.6 ethics).
- **C3 Sen-Nussbaum (Capabilities Approach)** — **FULL**. Sen *Development as Freedom* 1999 + *The Idea of Justice* 2009; Nussbaum *Creating Capabilities* 2011 + *Women and Human Development* 2000. Substrate density verified W2.6 ethics + W4.3 medicine.
- **C4 Max-Neef (Human-Scale Development)** — **PARTIAL**. Needs-satisfier doctrine adjacency; narrower corpus density than C1–C3.
- **C5 post-growth-Doughnut (Raworth / Jackson / Daly / Hickel)** — **PARTIAL**. Planetary-boundary + social-foundation framing adjacent to life-coherence; partial overlap with McMurtry / Polanyi.

**Verdict**: ≥3 FULL + 2 PARTIAL — meets cross-cutting-doctrine admission threshold. C6 Bollier-Helfrich is NOT counted as a 4th FULL cluster (per DECISION-BRIEF §6.2: *"C3 includes Civil Commons but is broader"*). No inflation.

## Consequences

**Canon-object-class member-count expansion**: cross-cutting doctrines 3 → 4 (`reproductive-commoning` + `boundary-commoning` + `care-commoning` + `life-value-doctrine`). The four-category canon-object inventory (primitives / cross-cutting-doctrines / modes / properties / patterns) is preserved; only the cross-cutting-doctrines category gains a member.

**Doctrine compositions** (lens-intersection without subsumption):

- **reproductive-commoning ↔ life-value-doctrine**: reproduction is a load-bearing instance of life-value enablement; reproductive-commoning frames *commoning of reproduction across time*, life-value-doctrine frames *evaluation of any coordination outcome* against life-coherence. Reproduction-continuity (ADR-0049 primitive) is the verb operating under both lenses.
- **boundary-commoning ↔ life-value-doctrine**: life-coherent boundaries are the substrate-discipline life-value-doctrine evaluates over. Civil Commons (McMurtry) names the boundary-set within which life-value is collectively secured.
- **care-commoning ↔ life-value-doctrine**: care-as-asymmetric-relational-practice is one mode through which life-value is enabled (Tronto's caring-with phase). The two doctrines intersect at the ethics-of-care-as-life-value-enablement layer but remain distinct — care-commoning operates as a relational-discipline; life-value-doctrine operates as an evaluative-substrate.

**Sequencing relationship with ADR-0085**: ADR-0085's `golden-calf-trap` shape (the 5-stage corrective-distinction-becomes-domination dynamic) and `recursive-audit-method` (the self-applying viability-grammar discipline) provide the trap-shape vocabulary and audit-method that operationalize life-value vs money-value evaluation. Specifically, McMurtry's *Cancer Stage of Capitalism* maps cleanly to the Golden Calf class-trap at civilizational scale (per ADR-0085's three substantive layer-instantiations); the recursive-audit-method's *"the grammar must apply its own question to itself"* clause provides the discipline for life-value-doctrine to operate self-reflexively without becoming itself a domination-grammar.

**C4 R-Immanent-ethics narrative-extension to ADR-0042 §Consequences**: extend-via-prose per ADR-0050/0077 precedent (frontmatter UNCHANGED). ADR-0042 (DAG-of-authority delete + structural-legitimacy foundation-level promotion) gains an explicit acknowledgment that `life-value-doctrine` admits the McMurtry immanent-ethics substrate as a doctrine-layer companion to structural-legitimacy. Structural-legitimacy thereby gains an evaluative substrate: legitimacy of coordination outcomes is readable through the life-value lens (range of being / doing / feeling / thinking enabled) without requiring primitive-grammar extension.

**Doctrine does NOT admit a new primitive**: nine-primitive parsimony (per Phase 3b admission discipline) is preserved. The doctrine operates as a lens applied across the existing nine primitives + six verbs; coordination operations remain unchanged in count, only the evaluative frame applied to them gains a fourth lens.

**Light vocab-governance load**: 1 new slug `life-value-doctrine` admitted in the doctrines section of `concepts-p2p-wiki.yaml`; yaml-line-count 78 → 79; doctrine-slug count 3 → 4; derived-glossary-slug subset count UNCHANGED (cross-cutting-doctrines and derived-glossary terms are distinct canon-object-classes per project-vision.md L23 / L111 four-canon-object-class framing). Misplacing `life-value-doctrine` into derived-glossary would be a category error.

**BKC peer-instance-family bridge candidate** (Ruddick-Civil-Commons → BKC commitment-pooling 3-layer composition) is preserved as operator-elective separate-session work. The Spore-side `sahely-ruddick-civil-commons-bridge.md` (Phase 2 closure Task A) already articulates this composition; a BKC-side reciprocal bridge could close the peer-citation gap when operator elects to author it.

**Wave-N+1 alignment for ic + pm** is EXPLICITLY DEFERRED to operator-elective separate-session work. Both IC and PM H2-decline concepts-registry (per ic:ADR-0019 + pm:ADR-0015), so propagation is prose-only doctrine-reference rather than slug propagation; ~30–60 min per repo at operator discretion.

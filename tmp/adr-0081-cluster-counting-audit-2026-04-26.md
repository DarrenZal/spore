## 6. Counter-argument for Outcome B (decline-with-triggers)

Even with cluster-counting passing, Outcome B remains valid on these grounds:

1. **Operator-energy / parsimony**: Phase 4 has produced 8 admissions in 2 days (5 Tier A+B + 3 Tier C); F8 as the final deficit could close as decline-with-triggers without harming canon-coherence (the forward-refs would gracefully resolve as "F8-forthcoming-pending-triggers" canon-state, parallel to F2's ADR-0080 disposition).
2. **Substrate-quality-tier**: most of the cluster (a) substrate lives in research planning layer (`docs/research/2026-04-03/`), not yet promoted to canon-decisions / foundations layer. A stricter reading might say "real cluster-counting requires canon-promoted substrate, not raw-research-input substrate." This is a defensible posture though not the F1 §4 precedent.
3. **Two-Johar-decline pattern**: ADR-0054 + ADR-0069 + ADR-0080 all declined-with-triggers; a fourth decline (F8) would be consistent with Phase-4-Tier-C-leans-decline pattern and would establish "Tier C carries the heavier-restraint discipline" as canonical observation.
4. **Cluster-counting threshold sensitivity**: F1 §4 lighter threshold is precedent-not-axiom; operator may prefer ≥2 non-Johar full clusters at heavier-doctrine-admission threshold (F8 would qualify under the lighter threshold but only marginally; under stricter threshold might fail).

**Counter-counter for Outcome A**:

1. **Forward-ref discharge value**: 5+ explicit F8 forward-refs across F4/F3/F7/F9 give F8-admission direct connective-tissue value (analogous to F3's multi-forward-ref cluster-discharge per ADR-0077 §233). Decline-with-triggers leaves these refs in unresolved-pending state.
2. **Operational-evidence is real**: 3 bridge notes + comparative-intake skill + active pattern of non-Spore agents reading Spore canon = honest operational substrate. Not speculative.
3. **Cluster (b) is unambiguously full**: 5 sub-traditions, ~30 distinct citations, substantive structural engagement, canonical research-input status, cited in ADR-0063. Not a marginal cluster-count.
4. **Phase 4 closure cleanness**: closing Phase 4 with 9/9 admissions (8 admit + 1 decline-F2) is a different shape than 8/9 admissions (5 admit + 4 decline). Either is honest, but admission-where-substrate-passes-honest-rigor is the discipline.

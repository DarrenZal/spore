## The organizing move (the through-line, frontstage register)
**Route / allocate / admit capital through coherence, not preference or similarity; obstruction is information; don't flatten local sovereignty.** Fund the *bundle* whose ecological + social + governance + evidence constraints actually hold together — not the highest-scoring project, not the most-similar cluster. (Backstage formalization in `spore.synthesis.coherence-without-collapse-and-its-projections`; never in funder-facing artifacts.)

## ⚠️ Two mathematical shapes — KEEP DISTINCT (the load-bearing discipline)
The operator's list spans **two different uses of sheaves in finance.** Conflating them is exactly the over-generalization we fenced. They have different math, different validation, different readiness:

- **Shape A — structural coherence / allocation / obstruction (THE LEAD).** *"Does this bundle of commitments / grants / flows cohere? where's the obstruction? route capital to what holds together."* Math: gluing / global-section / obstruction over a (directed-hyper)graph of commitments·pools·stewards·evidence·routes. Validation: localizes a known obstruction; distinguishes irreducible-vs-fixable tension better than rank/count baselines. Substrate exists: bregion-econ grammar + Ruddick route-graphs + BKC CommitmentPool + ADR-0084 (route-graph/settlement-operator).
- **Shape B — predictive sheaf-GNN on financial signals (RESEARCH-LANE).** The NVIDIA GTC line: *"sheaf graph neural network for multimodal risk and quantum portfolio optimization"* (sheaf-GNNs predicting commodity prices from geopolitical signals; GTC s82403). Math: **sheaf *neural networks* / learned diffusion on a feature graph, trained to PREDICT** prices/risk. Validation: predictive accuracy vs ML baselines — a totally different regime. **Not** the allocation wedge; needs financial-signal data + training we don't have. Genuinely real + published, but a separate thread, **not on the RC critical path.**

This distinction is the honest answer to "is this one engine for all of finance?" — **no.** It's one *design move* (coherence-not-preference) with a near-term structural program (Shape A) and a separate predictive research lane (Shape B).

## Honest framing of the breadth
The family is real — but it is a family of **instances of one design move**, not a claim that one engine or one math spans all of finance. Shape A (allocation-coherence on bioregional commitment/grant/flow substrates) is the coherent near-term program with a proven kernel and a runway-relevant use. Shape B (predictive sheaf-GNN) is a separate, legitimate research lane that is *not* on the critical path. **Generalize the principle; build per-application; keep the two shapes distinct.** That is how the breadth stays an asset instead of becoming the over-claim.

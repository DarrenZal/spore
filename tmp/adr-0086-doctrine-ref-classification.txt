docs/project-vision.md:111:**Four categories of canon objects** (ADR-0048). Spore distinguishes: (i) *primitives* — the substrate (field/holon/membrane) and operations (intent/commitment/joint-commitment/evidence/signal/reproduction) themselves; (ii) *cross-cutting doctrines* — lenses applied across primitives (reproductive-commoning, boundary-commoning, care-commoning); (iii) *modes-across-primitives* — grammatical properties or dynamics of how primitives operate (allocational/expressive/constructed power; possibly others as canon matures); (iv) *patterns* — recurring compositions mediating between containment and connection, organized as a composition-class with three sub-shapes per ADR-0065 M4 framework: **composition-patterns** (patterns composed over primitives/doctrines/modes/properties — e.g. governance-memory pattern; federation-encounter admission candidate per ADR-0055 triggers E-1..E-5), **design-criteria-patterns** (named design-criteria-clusters for field-conditions or coordination-substrate — e.g. four-enabling-conditions parking per ADR-0048), and **catalog-patterns** (patterns hosting ≥N legitimate sub-entities with host-structure rationale — e.g. view-template pattern-library doc parking per ADR-0058 over 5 demoted graph projections). Sub-class earning-tests defined in ADR-0065 §M4 Sub-class Framework (composition-AND-recurrence / design-criteria-cluster + primary-tradition + criteria-operationality / catalog-integrity + host-structure-earning-test); N-thresholds deferred to per-admission-ADR Step 0.5 with exemplar precedents (Johar-4 enabling-conditions; view-template-5 views). Modes are distinct from primitives (they are qualities of operation, not separate elements) and distinct from doctrines (they are structural properties, not lenses). Sub-classes are variations-of-pattern-family, not separate canon-object-classes — four-category inventory preserved.

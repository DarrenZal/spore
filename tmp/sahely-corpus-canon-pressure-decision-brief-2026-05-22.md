### 2.2 Cluster-counting framework

Per `feedback_surface_vocabulary_vs_operational_concern.md` + `feedback_audit_then_propose.md` + ADR-0064:

- **Full cluster** = independently-citable tradition (multi-source, multi-decade ideally) that articulates the operational-concern, not just shares vocabulary.
- **Partial cluster** = adjacent tradition with substantive resonance but narrower corpus or only-partial operational match.
- **Operational-concern match** = the candidate names a *specific* operational object/concern shared across clusters, not merely shared vocabulary.
- **Thresholds**: ≥2 full clusters for derived-glossary admission; ≥3 full clusters for primitive admission; framing-note-only fits ≥1 cluster without operational-pressure threshold; decline-with-trigger fits when cluster-counting fails + re-opening conditions can be articulated.
- **Operational-concern check**: surface-vocabulary match alone does NOT pre-confirm canon-pressure (per ADR-0064). The brief verifies operational-concern match per candidate.
- **Per-Cluster verification** (per operator §3 for C1 R-Trap-cluster; §4 for C7 R-Sheaf-graph-substrate): each candidate-cluster's claim of independent-tradition support is verified for operational-concern match individually, not lumped.
- **Honest-rigor cluster-aggregation**: tightly-genealogical lineages (e.g., Marx → Lukacs → Frankfurt → Gramsci → Foucault → Bourdieu) count as 1 full cluster, not 6, regardless of how many cite-able sub-works exist. Operator-decided aggregation can split if distinct operational-claim-shapes warrant.

### 2.3 McMurtry-vs-Sahely-only distinction (per operator §2)

- **Sahely-only synthesis candidates** (C1 R-Trap-cluster mythic-grammar; C7 R-Sheaf-application-to-coordination): Sahely-Maturana-school is 1 cluster; independent additional clusters required to reach ≥2 (derived-glossary) or ≥3 (primitive).

### 2.4 Provisional scoring conventions

Each candidate carries a likely-verdict (the brief's recommendation):
- **ADMIT-likely** = cluster-counting passes; operational-concern match strong; ADR-shape proposed.
- **scope-condition-likely** = cluster-counting passes; existing canon-object can absorb the substrate via narrative extension or sub-bullet per ADR-0062/0063/0064 precedent.
- **framing-note-only** = cluster-counting passes or marginal; already-canon-aligned; descriptive substrate-resonance enrichment without canon-shift.
- **DECLINE-with-trigger** = cluster-counting fails ≥2-cluster threshold; re-opening conditions articulated.
- **hold-as-tension** = genuine pluriversal incommensurability per ADR-0001 (not used in this brief — no candidate fits this shape).
